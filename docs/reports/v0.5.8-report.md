# Refute v0.5.8: how stable is an output of the v0.5 battery

Report of the v0.5.8 phase. **A draft for the maintainer's approval** (#218).

> **Limits label: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.** `method_status: not_validated`.
> **Exploratory.** Nothing here is a verdict on any TOI, a candidate, a disposition or a discovery.
> The numbers describe these 167 TOIs only. Nothing changed in the v0.5 battery or in any verdict,
> and nothing was submitted to any scientific body.

## The question

In v0.5.6 the battery searched each TOI's light curve for its strongest signal and attacked it.
Phase v0.5.8 reused v0.5.7's **TOI-seeded** mode for another question: **stability, not
equivalence**. The question: when the same TOI signal is refitted from another seed, how many
outputs and test statuses change? ([plan](../plans/v0.5.8-plan.md); the basis is an
[independent recommendation](../plans/v0.5.8-consensus/independent-recommendation.md), section 3.)

- **The mode.** The TOI's period and duration, and its epoch refined in a window of ± one
  duration, seed the unchanged battery, which refits that seed before any test. v0.5.7's runner
  was not edited; a new script imports its functions.
- **The set** (fixed and committed before the run, in
  [`stage1-set.json`](../validation/v0.5.8/stage1-set.json)):
  - the 107 matched and decided v0.5.6 records (28 SURVIVED, 53 WEAKENED, 26 REFUTED);
  - 60 of the 257 matched INCONCLUSIVE records, drawn with a committed seed.
- **The order:**
  - the runner, the draw, the four doors and the report script were committed before any result
    (`1f3e5f9`);
  - one Codex round on the design came next, and its five blocking items were applied
    ([record](../plans/v0.5.8-consensus/README.md));
  - then the run.
- **Not run:** the shifted second seed. Codex's round found that it changes the mode, and the
  decision allowed it only without a mode change.
- **Provenance.** Each record's downloaded files were compared by SHA-256 with v0.5.6's:
  - **83 records are `same`;**
  - **84 are `unknown`:** v0.5.6's part A stored no light-curve hashes, and 4 of the 5 failed runs
    ended before the check.
  - Every door is counted twice: in total, and only over `same`.

## The four doors

The doors and their counting rules are quoted from the recommendation and fixed in the plan
before the run. **They are exploratory marks, never verdicts.** A reseeded record that is not OK
counts against stability, as the rules state. Every rate carries its Wilson 95 % interval.

| Door (what it asks) | In total | Only provenance `same` |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Stable output:** at most 5 of the decided change output class | **not found:** 15 of 107 (0.140; 0.087 to 0.218) | **not found:** 10 of 54 (0.185; 0.104 to 0.308) |
| 2. **Flip predictable from the records:** at least 80 % of status changes were near a threshold | **not found:** 12 of 150 near (0.080; 0.046 to 0.135) | **not found:** 8 of 64 (0.125; 0.065 to 0.228) |
| 3. **More than one optimum:** at least 5 % of the TOIs refit an SNR more than 10 % away | **FOUND:** 15 of 167 (0.090; 0.055 to 0.143); 9 needed | **FOUND:** 12 of 83 (0.145; 0.085 to 0.236); 5 needed |
| 4. **The claim's events:** at least 15 of 17 FPs stay REFUTED, at most 2 planets REFUTED | **not found:** 14 of 17 FPs; 1 of 40 planets | **not found:** 14 of 17 FPs; 1 of 40 planets |

**What the doors say, read plainly:**
- **Doors 1, 2 and 4 are not found;** door 3 is found.
- The recommendation expected door 2 **not** to be found.
- **Door 1.** About one decided output in seven changed class when only the seed changed.
- **Door 2.** Most status changes were **not** near a threshold in the v0.5.6 record. So the
  records alone do not tell which outputs will move.
- **Door 3.** The refit fell on a different SNR in about one TOI in eleven.
- **Door 4.** Three of the 17 false positives that v0.5.6 had REFUTED were not REFUTED here: two
  became INCONCLUSIVE and one WEAKENED. The one part B planet that was REFUTED is still REFUTED,
  and no other part B planet became REFUTED.

**The five failed runs do not decide any mark.** As a description only, never a door, here are
the doors over the 162 runs that completed:
- door 1: 13 of 105 decided changed class (0.124; 0.074 to 0.200);
- door 2: 11 of 80 changes near (0.138; 0.079 to 0.230);
- door 3: 15 of 162 (0.093; 0.057 to 0.147);
- door 4: the three FPs above are all completed runs.

## Consumption, and what departed from the plan

- **CPU:** 3.24 CPU-h charged, against the cap of 8 (the estimate was about 4).
- **Downloads:** 52.6 GB, against the cap of 70 (the estimate was about 51).
- **Disk:** a peak of 6.60 GB, against the cap of 20. The estimate was about 2.5 GB. The likely
  reason (not measured): ten workers hold their pixel files at once.
- **Time:** 5 batches of at most 30 minutes on 2026-11-23, from 16:48 to 18:46 UTC (13:48 to 15:46
  local, business hours: 10 workers, 2 downloads, CPUs 8 to 31, BelowNormal).
- **Ledger:** [`stability-ledger.json`](../validation/v0.5.8/stability-ledger.json).
- **Files:** pixel files were deleted per target, with a manifest (167 deletion records, 50.6 GB).
  Light curves were deleted at the end:
  - 1,054 files from the manifests;
  - 2 left without a manifest, logged with their SHA-256;
  - 2.07 GB in all, inside `.cache/v058` only.
- **One departure.** In the third batch, the runner's heartbeat thread stopped on a
  `PermissionError`: Windows refused to replace `in-flight.json` with its temporary copy. The
  cause was not identified.
  - From then on, the in-flight marker's heartbeat and that batch's disk sampling stopped.
  - The records, the downloads and the caps were not affected: each batch measures the disk again
    at its end, and the next batch started a new thread.
  - The temporary file it left, 236 bytes inside `.cache/v058`, was deleted.

## What this phase does not do

- **No rule or threshold changes.** By the phase's rule, any proposal of a new threshold ends the
  phase; this report makes none.
- **No earlier verdict changes,** and no v0.5.6 record changes.
- **The numbers are exploratory.** They describe how these 167 outputs moved under one change of
  seed. They are not an error rate of the battery, and not a statement about any TOI.
