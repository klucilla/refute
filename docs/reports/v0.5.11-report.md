## 1. The question and the short answer

**The question:** can battery version 5 be specified from the primary papers and built in
`src/` so that:
- the screen (level 1) follows a frozen specification;
- level 2 stays version 2's verdict, byte for byte;
- versions 1 to 4 and every lock stay as they were?

**The short answer** (software only, exploratory):
- The specification was frozen with a non-empty core: F1, decided by OE_box alone.
- The code entered `src/` after synthetic tests written first.
- Versions 1 to 4 and the old locks were shown unchanged:
  - the local golden outputs are equal byte for byte;
  - the claim hashes are unchanged;
  - the 10 locked tags pass jointly;
  - full CI passed on a push that contains the `src/` commit.
- Nothing was measured on a real target.
- **The phase is not ready for the gate.**
  - The committed screen (`a896c49`) has a known numerical defect at the limits of float64
    (errata entry 12).
  - Its fix (E1) passed its tests, but the last Codex round refuted it (errata entry 13), so it
    was not committed.

## 6. Findings

- **F-1. Version 3's output depends on the process's state.**
  - **Where:** CI run 45514526728 (`84010d0`), test `test_versions_1_to_4_give_the_baseline_bytes`.
  - **What:** `verdict-v3.txt` differed in the last digits of floats between the pytest process
    (after thousands of tests) and a fresh subprocess, with the same code.
  - **What was done:** `64f3331` isolates the two sides.
  - **What it needs:** its cause identified before any lock. It is carried to
    [../plans/v0.5.12-proposal.md](../plans/v0.5.12-proposal.md). It was recorded by the
    maintainer's decision (comment 7149778857, item 3).
- **F-2. The machine-wide transfer bound is a loose upper bound.**
  - The counters sum every interface, virtual ones included, and the background traffic reached
    about 1.4 MB/s with no unit using the network.
  - Under amendment 15, the units without network are controlled by the ban on network instead.
- **F-3. Weights at the limits of float64.**
  - The arithmetic of the specification (1/σ²) needs a common-scale fallback when the raw sums
    overflow (E1), and an abstention when even that scale cannot be represented.
  - These are software edge cases. No real light curve with such errors is known.

## 7. Limits

- **Software acceptance only.** The calibration rule checks the F1 statistic on two fixed
  synthetic noise families; it gives no population error rate. A1 to A3 belong to v0.5.12.
- **Report-only quantities** (F1_trap, F2, F3, F4, U1) are adaptations of the papers'
  estimators. Their paper thresholds are shown and never applied.
- **The ban on network:**
  - `--disable-socket` holds inside pytest;
  - `UV_OFFLINE=1` holds for uv.

  Neither is a process-wide barrier for the Python scripts, as Codex noted.
- **The disk figure.** The charged peak disk counts growth above each unit's baseline, so it is
  not a whole-path peak. Criterion 6 is not demonstrated.
- **The limits label of level 2** applies: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.
