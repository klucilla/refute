## 1. The question and the short answer

**The question:** can battery version 5 be specified from the primary papers and built in
`src/` so that:
- the screen (level 1) follows a frozen specification;
- level 2 stays version 2's verdict, byte for byte;
- versions 1 to 4 and every lock stay as they were?

**The short answer** (software only, exploratory):
- The specification was frozen with a non-empty core: F1, decided by OE_box alone.
- The code entered `src/` after synthetic tests written first, twice:
  - the screen at `a896c49`;
  - correction 1 at `f18d6f8`.
- **The numerical defect at the limits of float64** (errata 12 and 13) is closed by an input
  rule, not by arithmetic:
  - every σ must lie in [1e-12, 1e3], otherwise F1 is `not_assessed` (`invalid_uncertainty`);
  - the weight is again the unscaled 1/σ² of the specification.
- Versions 1 to 4 and the old locks were shown unchanged after `f18d6f8`:
  - the local golden outputs are equal byte for byte;
  - the claim hashes are unchanged;
  - the 10 locked tags pass jointly (run after `a896c49`; correction 1 changed only
    `screen_d5.py`);
  - full CI on `f18d6f8` succeeded, and `ci_evidence --level full` holds (28 jobs).
- Nothing was measured on a real target.
- **The gate returned `REFUTED`** ([../gates/v0.5.11-review.md](../gates/v0.5.11-review.md), on
  `cf95b100`).
  - Criteria 1 to 5 are met. Criterion 6 is not met ("not demonstrated"), so criterion 7 is not
    met either.
  - There is no critical finding. Two important findings are tracked in #222 and #223.
- **The phase closes with declared limits** (section 7), under case (c) of the advance rule's
  amendment of 2026-11-21.
- **Version 5 is not adopted** for confirmatory use (charter).

## 6. Findings

- **F-1. Version 3's output depends on the process's state.**
  - **Where:** CI run 45514526728 (`84010d0`), test `test_versions_1_to_4_give_the_baseline_bytes`.
  - **What:** `verdict-v3.txt` differed in the last digits of floats between the pytest process
    (after thousands of tests) and a fresh subprocess, with the same code.
  - **What was done:** `64f3331` isolates the two sides.
  - **What it needs:** its cause identified before any lock. It is carried to
    [../plans/v0.5.12-proposal.md](../plans/v0.5.12-proposal.md). It was recorded by the
    maintainer's decision (comment 7149778857, item 3).
- **F-2. The machine-wide transfer bound is a loose upper bound.**
  - The counters sum every interface, virtual ones included. The background traffic reached
    about 1.4 MB/s with no unit using the network, and a burst of 58 MB in half a second
    (errata 15).
  - Under amendment 15, the units without network are controlled by the ban on network instead.
- **F-3. Weights at the limits of float64, closed by an input rule.**
  - The arithmetic of the specification (1/σ²) overflows in its sums for σ near 1e-153, while
    each weight is finite. Two fixes by arithmetic failed review: the common-scale fallback
    (errata 12) and E1 (errata 13).
  - Correction 1 refuses σ outside [1e-12, 1e3]. With the floor, no sum or weighted mean can
    overflow for up to 1e7 points and |f| up to 1e100 (its section 4).
  - The floor is about eight orders of magnitude below typical TESS errors in normalized flux,
    and the ceiling about four above. How often real curves fall outside is unknown.

## 7. Limits

### Declared limits of the close

By the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-25 (#218, comment 7165994585, item 2). v0.5.11 is
**closed with declared limits (gate REFUTED: criterion 6 not demonstrated)**. Every later use of
this phase's software carries these limits.
- **Criterion 6 is not demonstrated.**
  - The records are incomplete: gaps G1 to G12, launch.py version 1's rows, and the meter
    interruptions of errata 4, 6, 7 and 15.
  - So the caps are not shown to have held, although the charged totals stay below them.
- **Finding 1 of the gate, a known limit (#222).**
  - F1's noise for each parity, σ_w²/n + σ_r²/N, is the error of the weighted mean only when the
    mix of weights in the transit equals the baseline's.
  - **Every test curve of this phase has a uniform σ**, so unequal weights are not tested.
  - In the reviewer's exploratory check, with another implementation, F1 rejected signals of
    equal depths when one point or one parity had a different σ.
  - Until #222 is resolved, a `rejected` screen can come from heterogeneous errors.
- **Finding 2 of the gate, a known limit (#223).**
  - Step 1's synthetic cases were not delivered: the off-phase secondary, the occultation
    exception, grazing binaries and giants, the nulls, and every report-only path.
  - Only some `unavailable` paths are reached, and no test checks a `computed` report-only value.
  - So the report-only numerics (the MES grid, the events, the trapezoid, F4) are checked only for
    containment.
- **A deviation of the screen's placement.** The charter calls the screen's precedence "a new,
  domain-agnostic function of `src/refute/core/verdict.py`". It stayed in the TESS pack
  (`src/refute/packs/tess/screen_d5.py`), and `core/verdict.py` did not change. This was not
  recorded before the gate (minor finding 5, #59).
- **The two deviations already recorded:**
  - the weights adjustment of `a896c49` had no fourth diff round;
  - correction 1 and its code had no Codex round.

  Their review was the gate's.
- **The gate prompt's deviation,** recorded as the maintainer's adviser's: the prompt carried six
  focus points beyond the protocol and the phase name (charter, lines 471-472; finding 16).
- **Version 5 is not adopted** for confirmatory use (charter). #222 and #223 are preconditions of
  v0.5.12, before any calibration.

### Limits of the method

- **Software acceptance only.** The calibration rule checks the F1 statistic on two fixed
  synthetic noise families; it gives no population error rate. A1 to A3 belong to v0.5.12.
- **Report-only quantities** (F1_trap, F2, F3, F4, U1) are adaptations of the papers'
  estimators. Their paper thresholds are shown and never applied.
- **Correction 1** reads "every σ" as the σ of the cadences with finite time and flux, which is
  stricter than the frozen predicate (its section 2). The per-cadence TESS errors behind the
  range are the agent's scaling of hourly precisions under white noise, an assumption.
- **The ban on network:**
  - `--disable-socket` holds inside pytest;
  - `UV_OFFLINE=1` holds for uv.

  Neither is a process-wide barrier for the Python scripts, as Codex noted.
- **The disk figure.** The charged peak disk counts growth above each unit's baseline, so it is
  not a whole-path peak. Criterion 6 is not demonstrated.
- **The limits label of level 2** applies: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.
