## Declared limits

v0.5.12 is **closed with declared limits (gate REFUTED: criteria 2 and 6 not met)** (the
maintainer's decision, #218 comment 7202383280, item 4). Every later use of this phase's
correction, cases or tests carries that label.

1. **Criterion 2.** The inventory of #223 was committed after the first probe. The order
   inventory, case record, probe rests on the journal and local commits, not on pushed git
   history (deviation 3).
2. **Criterion 6.**
   - There are 11 meter gaps (G0 to G10).
   - The ledger's "not demonstrated" stands, because amendment 1 removed the network block
     across the process tree for offline units.
   - Repetitions were made without approval (deviations 10, 11, 13 and 14).
3. **The gate's important findings,** each an open issue:
   - **#226** (finding 3): the versions' bytes depend on the BLAS thread count. F-1's cause is a
     sufficient factor shown on one machine, not the mechanism of the historical CI difference.
     Pinning the threads is the maintainer's decision. It is a **precondition before any lock**.
   - **#227** (finding 4): the #223 oracle is not independent of `src/`'s reading of the
     specification. Assertions against the generating model are needed. It is a **precondition
     before calibrating U1**.
   - **#228** (findings 1, 2 and 13; Codex's 1 and 2): the order proof by push, the record of
     repetitions, and the origin and time of decision records. It is an input to the v0.5.13
     charter.
4. **The limits of correction 2.**
   - **Correlated noise.** F1's null calibration and sensitivity under correlated noise are not
     established (#224, a precondition before any use of F1 on real data). The gate's finding 8,
     F1 with a trapezoidal transit at very high SNR, is recorded on #224.
   - **F2 and U1 keep the old noise.** They keep the existing MES noise calculation (the
     correction's section 4, the scope). With unequal cadence uncertainties, their values carry
     the defect of #222. They stay report-only, with no effect on the screen or level 2.
5. **The BLAS dependence.** The last digits of version 3's `amplitude_snr` depend on the BLAS
   thread count, so on the machine (#226). No byte of versions 1 to 4 was changed, and nothing in
   `src/` was corrected for it.

The gate's minor findings are in #59 (comment 7202417782), except finding 8 (on #224, comment
7202416803) and finding 13 (in #228).
