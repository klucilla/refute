## 1. What the phase asked

Which route to an independent result has enough usable targets, at what cost and in what time, to
be worth a first pre-registered, locked result?
- **(e):** a prospective audit of predicted transit windows in new sectors.
- **(d1):** KELT false positives against planets found outside TESS.
- **The Kepler field:** certified false positives against Kepler planets.

The phase first recounts the diagnosis those routes rest on (a). Part (f), added by the
maintainer's decision of 2026-11-24, measures whether five third-party engines reach an outcome
where Refute's v0.5.9 battery was INCONCLUSIVE.

## 3. The routes, ranked

Ranked by usable targets, independence, cost and time to the first locked result. The counts are
those of section 2. "Usable" means SPOC 2-minute data and not exposed in any Refute record.

| Rank | Route | Usable targets | Independence | Cost before a lock | Time to a first locked result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **(d1)**: KELT-FP against planets found from the ground | **307 against 181** (2,380 if space-mission planets are allowed) | Labels from ground follow-up, independent of TESS and of Refute. The targets are unexposed, but their data are already public (retrospective). | Low: the battery runs on SPOC 2-minute data as it is, and the frame and the draw reuse the census scripts. | Shortest: the data exist now, and a pre-registered draw can be locked in the next phase. |
| 2 | **(e)**: predicted windows in sectors 110 to 120 | eumemic **205** (50 with 2-minute history); CTOIs 1,222 (682); Refute's own **0** | The strongest: the data do not exist when the prediction is locked. | Higher: the directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance must pass first (a design exists; a few tens of CPU-h). Two-minute coverage in those sectors is not determined. | Months: the lock can come early, but the measurement waits for the sectors' public release. |
| 3 | **The Kepler field**: certified false positives against Kepler planets | false positives **289** with 2-minute data; planets 2,548 | Kepler labels are independent of TESS. | Medium. | Short, but most planets are too shallow for TESS: 1,388 of the 2,548 are under 500 ppm. The two arms would differ in depth more than in nature, which favours a false separation. |

**Why (d1) first.**
- **The two arms are comparable.** KELT-FP and the ground-discovered planets have similar periods
  (medians 4.5 and 3.4 days), similar brightness (V 10.8; T 12.4), and depths of the same order
  (15.4 mmag, about 1.4 %, and 1.2 %).
- **There are enough targets for a first claim.** A 20 + 20 or 50 + 50 draw stays far inside 307 and
  181.
- **No new code is needed** before the lock.

**What would change the ranking.**
- **(d1) falls if** a pre-registered usability filter (depth detectable at the battery's SNR gate,
  sectors with data, crowding) leaves fewer usable targets per arm than the claim's pre-registered
  sample needs, or if the KELT-FP classes most like planets (the blends) turn out to be a small part
  of the usable 307.
- **(e) rises if** the directed evaluator's acceptance passes quickly, and the 2-minute target lists
  of sectors 110 to 120 put enough eumemic targets in 2-minute data (now at most 50).
- **The Kepler field rises if** TGLC or another FFI product covers the faint certified false
  positives, and the planet arm can be restricted to the depths TESS detects.

## 4. The incorporation plan (version 5)

Labels: exploratory, `method_status: not_validated`. This is a proposal for the version-5 phase:
nothing enters `src/` in v0.5.10.

| Tool and metric | Reaches an outcome where Refute was INCONCLUSIVE | Keep or drop | How it could enter version 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| TLS search (MIT) | yes, 14 of 14 | **keep as a candidate** for the search step | **Recovery here:** TLS recovered the catalog period on 24 of 24 TOIs, while v0.5.9's own search stayed on the TOI ephemeris on 20 of 24 (post hoc; different settings and binning). **How:** adapt the MIT code with its notice under a third-party folder. Only after a synthetic acceptance (injection and recovery, nulls), at a cost known in advance (about 240 CPU-s per TOI at 10-minute bins). |
| wotan biweight (MIT) | not a test | **keep as a candidate** for the detrending | Adapt with its notice. Compare with Refute's detrending on synthetic injections before any use. |
| exoVetter SWEET (BSD-3) | yes, 13 of 14 | **drop** as it is | It warns on a clean synthetic transit and on 16 of 23 TOIs, and agrees with LEO-Vetter 7 times in 22. A version would need its own threshold, calibrated on synthetic nulls. |
| exoVetter Centroid (BSD-3) | yes, 13 of 14 | **drop** as a decision | It meets its threshold on CP and KP TOIs. At most a diagnostic beside Refute's `centroid_shift`. |
| exoVetter ModShift, OddEven, phase coverage (BSD-3) | no (no published threshold) | **drop** | They add no decision. Refute's `odd_even` and `secondary_eclipse` already measure the same things. |
| exoVetter LPP | not run | **not assessed** | Needs its map file as a verified input, with its terms confirmed. |
| LEO-Vetter FP tests (GPL-3.0+) | its class, 14 of 14 | **keep the method** of its FP tests (secondary, odd/even, size, V shape) as candidates | Its FP class appears on 4 of the 10 TFOPWG false positives and on no CP, KP or PC. The code is never copied: the tests are reimplemented from the published paper, made deterministic, with declared coverage and zero coverage as INCONCLUSIVE, and calibrated on synthetic cases. |
| LEO-Vetter FA tests (GPL-3.0+) | yes | **drop** | They gave FA on 3 CP TOIs. |
| TRICERATOPS FPP and NFPP (MIT; imports PyTransit, GPL) | 11 of 14 | **drop** for version 5 | The cost is a median 431 CPU-s, with 3 timeouts. It needs Gaia DR3 rows (restricted terms here) and a GPL transit model. Its joint threshold is met by 2 TFOPWG false positives and missed by 7 CP. A later phase could reconsider a reimplementation from Giacalone et al. 2021 with a permissive transit model. |

**The licence rules apply to every row.** Permissive code (MIT, BSD-3) may be adapted into Refute's
own code with its notice under a third-party folder. GPL code is never copied: its methods are
reimplemented from the papers.

**The PyTransit licence discrepancy is recorded** (Codex's blocking item 5). Its 2.2.0 metadata and
`LICENSE` say GPLv2, while its model headers say GPLv3 or later (Codex's reading). No
GPL-dependent implementation enters an Apache-2.0 proposal.

## 5. The recommendation of the first locked result

**The first locked result should come from route (d1).** It would be a pre-registered, locked
agreement claim on unexposed targets with SPOC 2-minute data:
- **Arms:** KELT-FP (307 usable) and the planets discovered from the ground (181 usable).
- **What it tests:** whether the battery, with its v0.5 limits label or its version 5 if that phase
  comes first, keeps the planets and refuses or weakens the false positives.

**What would be fixed before the lock:**
- the frame, built again by a committed script from the census sources, with their URLs and dates;
- a usability filter, declared before the draw: the depth detectable at the battery's gate, and
  data in at least one sector;
- the sample per arm, for example 50 + 50, with its power stated;
- the drand round and the seed rule;
- the pass criteria and the classes counted.

**What would change this recommendation:** the conditions in section 3.

**Route (e) would follow** once the directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance has passed. Its
design is in [docs/plans/v0.5.10-directed-evaluator-acceptance-design.md](../plans/v0.5.10-directed-evaluator-acceptance-design.md).
