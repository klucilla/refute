## 1. What the phase asked

Which route to an independent result has enough usable targets, at what cost and in what time, to
be worth a first pre-registered, locked result?
- **(e):** a prospective audit of predicted transit windows in new sectors.
- **(d1):** KELT false positives against planets found outside TESS.
- **The Kepler field:** certified false positives against Kepler planets.

The phase first recounts the diagnosis those routes rest on (a). Part (f), added by the
maintainer's decision of 2026-11-24, measures whether five third-party tools, run as four engines, reach an outcome
where Refute's v0.5.9 battery was INCONCLUSIVE.

## 3. The routes, ranked (provisional)

Ranked by usable targets, independence, cost and time to the first locked result.
- **Usable** means SPOC 2-minute data and not exposed.
- **Exposed** means the target's TIC appears in a run record or draw anywhere in the repository
  (`recheck-after-codex.json`).
- **Counts are distinct TICs** unless "rows" or "entries" is said.
- **The routes use different eligibility rules,** so the ranking is provisional until a common
  filter (depth detectable at the battery's SNR, crowding) is applied before a lock.

| Rank | Route | Usable targets | Independence | Cost before a lock | Time to a first locked result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **(d1)**: KELT-FP against planets found from the ground | **307 against 181**; 171 without the ambiguous facility labels; 2,380 if space-mission planets are allowed | Labels from ground follow-up, independent of TESS and of Refute. The targets are unexposed, but their data are already public (retrospective). | Low: the battery runs on SPOC 2-minute data as it is, and the frame and the draw reuse the census scripts. | Shortest: the data were acquired already, and a pre-registered draw can be locked in the next phase. |
| 2 | **(e)**: predicted windows in sectors 110 to 120 | eumemic **205** (50 with earlier 2-minute history); CTOIs 1,196 (662), from 1,222 entries; Refute's own **0** | The strongest, for the sectors after the lock: their data do not exist yet. Sector 110 was already being observed at the census. | Higher: the directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance must pass first (a design exists; a few tens of CPU-h). Two-minute coverage in those sectors is not determined. | Months: the lock can come early, but the measurement waits for data still to be acquired and released. |
| 3 | **The Kepler field**: certified false positives against Kepler planets | false positives **282** with 2-minute data (289 rows); planets 1,804 (2,548 rows) | Kepler labels are independent of TESS. | Medium. | Short, but most planets are too shallow for TESS: 1,388 of the 2,548 planet rows are under 500 ppm. The two arms would differ in depth more than in nature, which favours a false separation. |

**Why (d1) first.**
- **The two arms look comparable on their marginal medians:** periods 4.5 and 3.4 days, and depths
  of 15.4 mmag (about 1.4 %) and 1.2 %.
  - The magnitudes are in different bands (KELT V 10.8, ground planets T 12.4).
  - Marginal medians do not show matched detectability. A common TESS-magnitude and depth or SNR
    basis is part of the pre-registered filter.
- **There are enough targets for a first claim.** A 20 + 20 or 50 + 50 draw stays inside 307 and
  171, unless the filter removes most of them.
- **No new code is needed** before the lock.

**What would change the ranking.**
- **(d1) falls if** the pre-registered usability filter (depth detectable at the battery's SNR gate,
  sectors with data, crowding) leaves fewer than the claim's sample per arm (for example 50), or if
  the KELT-FP classes most like planets (the blends) are a small part of the usable 307.
- **(e) rises if** the directed evaluator's acceptance passes quickly, and the 2-minute target lists
  of sectors 111 to 120 put more than about 50 eumemic targets in 2-minute data.
- **The Kepler field rises if** TGLC or another FFI product covers the faint certified false
  positives, and the planet arm can be restricted to the depths TESS detects.

reimplemented from the papers.

**The PyTransit licence discrepancy is recorded** (Codex's blocking item 5). Its 2.2.0 metadata and
`LICENSE` say GPLv2, while its model headers say GPLv3 or later (Codex's reading). No
GPL-dependent implementation enters an Apache-2.0 proposal.

## 5. The recommendation of the first locked result

**The first locked result should come from route (d1).** This recommendation is provisional. It
would be a pre-registered, locked agreement claim on unexposed targets with SPOC 2-minute data:
- **Arms:** KELT-FP (307 usable) and the planets discovered from the ground (181 usable, 171
  without the ambiguous facility labels).
- **What it tests:** whether the battery, with its v0.5 limits label or its version 5 if that phase
  comes first, keeps the planets and refuses or weakens the false positives.

**What would be fixed before the lock:**
- the frame, built again by a committed script from the census sources, with their URLs and dates;
- the exposure check over every run record and draw;
- a usability filter, declared before the draw, on a common TESS-magnitude and depth or SNR basis,
  with data in at least one sector;
- the sample per arm, for example 50 + 50, with its power stated;
- the drand round and the seed rule;
- the pass criteria and the classes counted.

**What would change this recommendation:** the conditions in section 3.

**Route (e) would follow** once the directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance has passed. Its
design is in [docs/plans/v0.5.10-directed-evaluator-acceptance-design.md](../plans/v0.5.10-directed-evaluator-acceptance-design.md).
