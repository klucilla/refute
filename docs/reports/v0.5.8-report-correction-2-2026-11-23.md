## The direction of the SNRs that moved

**The report says** (section "Flips per output", last point): "Of the 15 SNRs that moved, 12 were
lower than v0.5.6's."

**It is read as:** of the 15 SNRs that moved by more than 10 %, **13 were lower** than v0.5.6's and
**2 were higher**:

| TIC | TOI | SNR in v0.5.6 | SNR reseeded |
|---|---|---|---|
| TIC-158561566 | TOI-1267.01 | 64.39 | 86.80 |
| TIC-463681005 | TOI-3060.01 | 9.00 | 11.79 |

- **Where the 12 comes from:** the `same` panel had 12 SNRs that moved by more than 10 %, in either
  direction; 11 of them were lower (TOI-3060.01 is the one higher).
- **Door 3 does not change.** It counts SNRs that moved in either direction: 15 of 167 in total and
  12 of 83 over `same`, both found, as the report states.
- **The sentence before it stands:** the 7 SNRs that moved among the 13 decided changes in completed
  runs were all lower.
