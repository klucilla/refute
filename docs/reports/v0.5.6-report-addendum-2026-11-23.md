battery. Runs that reached the fitting step used the TOI's published ephemeris to seed the refit.
Each run record was compared with its v0.5.6 record. Its part on this report's claim:

v0.5.6's locked claim: of its 17 false-positive events (REFUTED on the TOI's own signal), 14
stayed REFUTED here, two became INCONCLUSIVE and one WEAKENED; its one planet event stayed
REFUTED, and none of the other 39 part B planet records rerun here (of the claim's 150 drawn
planets) became REFUTED. The locked verdict stands as recorded; this measures how much its
individual events depend on the fit.
