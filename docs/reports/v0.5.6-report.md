# Refute v0.5.6: the v0.5 battery applied to public TOIs, with declared limits

Report of the v0.5.6 phase, approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-23 (#218).

> **Limits label: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.** `method_status: not_validated`.
> All outcomes from the v0.5 battery carry this limits label: the battery **did not meet four of
> its own targets**. The outcomes describe the drawn samples, never the TOI population. Nothing here is a verdict on any TOI, a
> candidate, or a discovery. Nothing was submitted to any scientific body.

## What was done

Refute's v0.5 battery is a set of deterministic falsification tests for transit signals in TESS
light curves. In this phase it was run, unchanged (`discovery.version: 2`), on public TESS Objects
of Interest (TOIs), in two parts:

1. **Part A, exploratory.**
   - 240 TOIs from the frame's exploration half: 120 matched to TESS-discovered confirmed planets,
     and 120 unlabelled.
   - It looked for pre-written "doors", that is, patterns worth confirming.
   - Its outputs are exploratory: never verdicts and never candidates.
2. **Part B, confirmatory.**
   - One claim, locked before its targets were drawn or read.
   - Its 300 targets came from the confirmation half, never used in part A, and were drawn with a
     public drand random beacon round that was fixed before the lock and came out after it.
   - It ran once. Its verdict is reported here as it came.

targets are **not met** by the battery used here. The table summarizes the frozen rules for the

("Decision rules (the C1 self-claim)"), T2a from [v0.5's protocol](../../calibration/v0.5/PROTOCOL.md)
(section 4, "Targets"). Each result is v0.5's own measurement of this battery.

The frozen applicability conditions also hold:
- a C1 rule is `NOT DECIDABLE` when the rule's own eligible set or subset has fewer than 20
  curves;
- C2 applies the same minimum to its denominator, and evaluates no target while compatibility
  with the v0.4 battery is not established.

C1 is v0.5's synthetic confirmation, run once (drand round 6613448), and C2 its paired real-data
benchmark. Three later correction rounds tried other battery versions, which are not used here,
and none achieved acceptance of the outstanding targets. In the last of them, D2 kept 141 of 178
against the 156 needed, and T2a's `K_B` was 331 against 552. D4 passed in the v0.5.2 and v0.5.3
development forecasts ([v0.5.3's closing record](../validation/v0.5.3/closing-record.md), "The
state of each target").

So, in C1, the EB1 and EB2 upper Wilson bounds exceeded D1's limit. Many synthetic planet signals
in C1, and many injections into real light curves in C2, ended INCONCLUSIVE. Both limits matter for
what follows.

## Part A (exploratory)

- **Planet arm (120 TOIs):**
  - 13 SURVIVED, 16 WEAKENED, 9 REFUTED and 82 INCONCLUSIVE.
  - The signal the battery found matched the TOI in 87 of 120.
- **Unlabelled arm (120 TOIs):**
  - 4 SURVIVED, 15 WEAKENED, 9 REFUTED and 91 INCONCLUSIVE, with 1 coverage loss.
- **Doors:**
  - Door 1 (a subgroup): not found.
  - Door 2 (a systematic disagreement): not found.
  - Door 3 (a dominant cause of INCONCLUSIVE): found. Nine tests qualified.
    - In 3 of the 9, the main cause was a sensitivity refusal: `aperture_depth`,
      `event_consistency` and `systematics`.
    - In the other 6 it was structural:
      - too few transits or events (`odd_even`, `single_event`, `edge_events`);
      - a phase with no data (`secondary_eclipse`);
      - a period outside the test's domain (`sinusoid`);
      - sectors that did not qualify (`centroid_shift`).
- **Why door 3 was not pursued.** By the maintainer's decision, door 3 was not pursued. The
  independent review of 2026-11-22 found it:
  - nearly tautological, since each test builds its INCONCLUSIVE outcomes from one to four
    message templates;
  - already known, as D4 and T2a.
- **What part B tested instead.** Part B tested a claim of **agreement** with TFOPWG
  dispositions.
  - That claim was suggested by part A's descriptive table of the unlabelled arm.
  - The table uses the TOI table's disposition, as NExScI's answer of 2026-11-22 allows.
  - This origin is disclosed.
- **Consumption:** 5.49 CPU-h recorded on targets, 5.95 CPU-h charged in total, and at least
  81.75 GB of downloaded files.

Report: [part A's exploratory report](../validation/v0.5.6/part-a-report.md). The review:
[`partb-independent-review.md`](../plans/v0.5.5-consensus/partb-independent-review.md).

## Part B (confirmatory): the claim and its verdict

**The claim** ([`calibration/v0.5.6/claim.yaml`](../../calibration/v0.5.6/claim.yaml)):
- Among TOIs of the confirmation half, the labelled battery refutes, on the TOI's own signal, a
  larger share of TOIs dispositioned FP by the TFOPWG than of TOIs matched to TESS-discovered
  confirmed planets (Fisher exact, one-sided, alpha 0.05).
- Its rate on those planets stays at most 0.05, with an upper Wilson 95 % bound at most 0.10 (the
  planet guard).
- **An event** is: status OK, nonzero coverage, outcome REFUTED, and the signal matched to the TOI
  (period factor 1 within 0.001, epoch within half the TOI's duration).
- **Losses:** an ERROR counts as a non-event and as a loss. More than 15 losses per stratum would
  have made the claim INCONCLUSIVE.

**Verdict: SURVIVED** (p = 4.7e-05; the planet guard holds), under the limits label.

| Stratum | Drawn | Losses (cap) | Events | Event rate | Wilson 95 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed planets | 150 | 2 (15) | 1 | 0.007 | 0.001 to 0.037 |
| TFOPWG FP | 150 | 3 (15) | 17 | 0.113 | 0.072 to 0.174 |

**All outcomes** (descriptive, beyond the event):

| Stratum | SURVIVED | WEAKENED | REFUTED | INCONCLUSIVE | ERROR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed planets | 12 | 15 | 13 | 108 | 2 |
| TFOPWG FP | 2 | 14 | 38 | 93 | 3 |

- Of the 38 FP REFUTED, 21 were on a signal that did not match the TOI, so they are not events.
- **REFUTED planets.** 13 of the 150 confirmed planets ended REFUTED, and 12 of those 13 were on
  a signal that is not the TOI's.
  - The plain REFUTED rate on planets is 8.7 %, with an upper Wilson bound of 0.143.
  - The planet guard applies to the matched event (1 of 150), not to this plain rate.
- Of the 17 events, the first decisive fatal test was:
  - `aperture_depth` for 9;
  - `odd_even` for 7;
  - `period_alias` for 1.
- **Secondary tests, reported only:**
  - REFUTED, FP above planets: p = 9.0e-05.
  - SURVIVED, planets above FP: p = 0.0055.

### What this result says, and what it does not

- **It says** that, on these 300 drawn TOIs, the labelled battery's refutations on the TOI's own
  signal lean toward the TFOPWG's FP dispositions, and rarely fall on confirmed planets.
- **It is agreement, not independence.** TFOPWG dispositions draw in part on TESS photometry that
  overlaps the light curves the battery reads. The claim never measures whether the battery
  detects false positives on its own.
- **The two strata are different populations,** and part of the difference may come from that.
  - In part B's drawn TOIs, the FPs' median TOI depth is 1810 ppm, against 1410 ppm for the
    planets (quartiles 586 to 8620, and 709 to 6040).
  - The FPs' median period is 2.22 d, against 5.29 d (quartiles 1.04 to 5.16, and 2.74 to 11.7).
  - In part A, the open arm's FP-dispositioned TOIs and the planet arm differed in the same
    direction (3621 ppm against 2277 ppm; 2.5 d against 5.0 d; the independent review of
    2026-11-22).
  - The claim does not adjust for depth or period.
  - Part B's numbers come from the draw's TOI table values, computed by
    [`part_b_strata.py`](../validation/v0.5.6/part_b_strata.py) into
    [`part-b-strata.json`](../validation/v0.5.6/part-b-strata.json).
- **The battery decides little.** 108 of 150 planets and 93 of 150 FPs ended INCONCLUSIVE. These
  counts describe decidability on the drawn TOIs. Only 17 of 150 FPs are events.
- **The rates describe the sample,** a draw from one frame's confirmation half, not the TOI
  population.
- **Nothing is about a single TOI.** No per-target output is a verdict, a disposition, or a
  candidate.
- **No validated method.** `method_status: not_validated` stands.
