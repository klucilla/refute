# Refute v0.5.7: the TOI's own signal, closed at the pilot gate

Report of the v0.5.7 phase, approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-23 (#218).

> **Limits label: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.** `method_status: not_validated`.
> **Exploratory.** Nothing here is a verdict on any TOI, a candidate, a disposition or a discovery.
> The observations rest on two TOIs and are never a rule. Nothing was submitted to any scientific
> body.

## What was tried

In v0.5.6 the battery searched each TOI's light curve for its strongest signal and attacked that
signal. Phase v0.5.7 asked what the same tests say when the TOI's own published ephemeris is the
starting point instead
([plan](../plans/v0.5.7-plan.md); the basis is an
[independent recommendation](../plans/v0.5.7-consensus/independent-recommendation.md)).

- **Design.** Codex's design round found that the unchanged battery refits the period, the epoch
  and the duration before any test
  ([answer](../plans/v0.5.7-consensus/design-round-1-answer.md)). The maintainer then chose a
  **TOI-seeded** mode:
  - the TOI's period and duration, and its epoch refined in a window of ± one duration, seed the
    battery;
  - the battery itself is unchanged, and refits that seed before any test;
  - nothing in `src/` changed, and no rule, threshold or sensitivity refusal changed.
- **The order:**
  - the runner, the four pre-written doors (counted in total and over the records that stayed on
    the TOI ephemeris) and the report script were committed before any result;
  - then a pilot of 10 TOIs that had matched in search mode;
  - then, only if the pilot reproduced at least 9 of them test by test, the 540 TOIs of v0.5.6.

## The pilot gate, and why the phase stopped there

- **The result:** the pilot reproduced **8 of 10** TOIs test by test, against the 9 required.
- **What the 10 had in common:**
  - all 10 runs completed;
  - all 10 fits stayed on the TOI ephemeris;
  - in the two that differed, the downloaded files were identical to v0.5.6's.
- **The decision.** By the rule written before the pilot, the run stopped, and the 540 TOIs were
  not run. The maintainer kept the rule as written (no change after seeing the result) and closed
  the phase with declared limits.
- **Records:** [`seeded-pilot.json`](../validation/v0.5.7/seeded-pilot.json) and
  [`seeded-pilot-records.json`](../validation/v0.5.7/seeded-pilot-records.json).

| TOI | Search mode (v0.5.6) | TOI-seeded | What differed |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOI-1062.01 | SURVIVED | INCONCLUSIVE | `systematics` PASS -> INCONCLUSIVE |
| TOI-506.01 | INCONCLUSIVE | INCONCLUSIVE | `event_consistency` INCONCLUSIVE -> FAIL; `odd_even` PASS -> INCONCLUSIVE |

## The diagnostic

The maintainer approved one diagnostic rerun, with its procedure recorded before it ran.
- **Its question:** does the flip come from the seeding, the environment, or the battery's
  nondeterminism?
- **The runs:** each of the two TOIs ran four times, in fresh processes: search twice, with
  v0.5.6's runner unchanged, and seeded twice, with v0.5.7's runner unchanged.
- **The inputs:** the same files.
- **A recording gap.** The posted procedure called for the fitted duration and depth on every run.
  The unchanged search runner records only the fitted period, epoch and SNR, so the fitted
  duration and depth are missing from all four search records. The classification used the
  aggregate outcome and every test status.
- **Records:** [`diagnostic-rerun.md`](../validation/v0.5.7/diagnostic-rerun.md),
  [`diagnostic-rerun.json`](../validation/v0.5.7/diagnostic-rerun.json).

**Answer, by the rule fixed before running: the seeding, for both TOIs.**
- The two runs of each mode were identical: the battery was deterministic here.
- Search mode reproduced the stored v0.5.6 record: the environment did not change it.
- Seeded mode reproduced the pilot, and the two modes differed.

## An exploratory observation: a result at the sensitivity boundary

This rests on two TOIs. It is an observation, never a rule.

- **TOI-1062.01.** In search mode, the `systematics` test's power to detect a background rise of
  0.8 of the depth was **0.95, exactly the 0.95 it requires**, so it passed, and the outcome was
  SURVIVED.
  - The seeded fit was almost the same: the two fitted periods differed by about 3.45 × 10⁻⁷ d,
    and their fitted epochs by about 1.83 minutes.
  - With that fit, the power was 0.877, so the test refused to pass and the outcome became
    INCONCLUSIVE.
  - For TOI-1062.01, the background sensitivity crossed the cutoff between the search and seeded
    fits.
- **TOI-506.01.** The seeded fit was weaker than the search's: SNR 8.2 against 15.5. The two fitted
  periods differed by about 0.00133 d and their epochs by about 4.18 minutes; the seeded period
  was still within 0.001 of the TOI's. Two tests changed status, and the outcome stayed
  INCONCLUSIVE.

How many of v0.5.6's results sit near a sensitivity threshold was not measured.

## What this suggests for a next phase (a suggestion only; nothing is opened)

- **An ephemeris entry in `src/`.** A battery version that takes the TOI's ephemeris as its
  candidate, instead of refitting a seed, would let a phase like this one test the TOI's own signal
  as such. It is a change in `src/`, and so the maintainer's decision.
- **A stability check near the sensitivity boundary.** A result whose sensitivity sits near its
  threshold could be checked for stability under small changes of the fit before it is read. The
  rule would be designed and fixed before any data is read.
