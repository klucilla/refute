# How stable is a Refute output?

*Refute v0.5.8, 2026-11-23. Approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-23 (#218); its facts and
numbers were checked against the records in one Codex round
([record](../plans/v0.5.8-consensus/README.md)). The full report:
[v0.5.8-report.md](v0.5.8-report.md).*

> **Exploratory.** Limits label: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.
> `method_status: not_validated`. These numbers are not verdicts on any TOI, and not candidates,
> dispositions or discoveries. They describe 167 TOIs, each run again from its TOI ephemeris.
> Nothing was submitted to any scientific body.

Refute's v0.5 battery takes a transit signal, refits it, and runs falsification tests on it. In
v0.5.6 it started from the strongest signal it found in each TESS light curve. We asked a simple
question: **when the battery starts from the TOI ephemeris, how often does its output differ from
the v0.5.6 search-mode output?**

## What we did

- **The TOIs:** 167 TOIs selected from v0.5.6 records whose fitted signals matched the TOI
  ephemerides. The reruns used public SPOC products from MAST. File hashes matched for 83 records.
  File identity was unverified for 84. The set comprised:
  - the 107 with a decided output (28 SURVIVED, 53 WEAKENED, 26 REFUTED);
  - 60 drawn at random, with a committed seed, from the 257 that were INCONCLUSIVE.
- **The new start:** each TOI's published ephemeris, from the TOI catalog, seeded the same,
  unchanged battery. The battery refits that seed before any test.
- **Nothing in the battery changed:** no test, no threshold, no rule.
- **Fixed in advance:** the questions and the way to count them were committed before the first
  result.
- **The intervals:** every rate below is given with its Wilson 95 % interval.

## What we found

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Outputs that changed class | **19 of 167** (11 %; 7 % to 17 %) |
| Decided outputs that changed class | **15 of 107** (14 %; 9 % to 22 %) |
| Of the 13 decided changes in completed runs, moved to INCONCLUSIVE | 12 of 13 (92 %; 67 % to 99 %) |
| Refitted SNR more than 10 % away from the first fit | 15 of 167 (9 %; 6 % to 14 %) |
| Status changes that were close to a test threshold in the first run | 12 of 150 (8 %; 5 % to 13 %) |
| Runs that failed (2 downloads, 1 timeout, 2 numerical errors) | 5 of 167 (3 %; 1 % to 7 %) |

Under the predefined threshold rule, 12 of 150 status changes were near a threshold in the first
run. The denominator includes 70 changes to NOT OK from the five failed runs.

**Status changes per test** (completed runs, of 162):

| Test | Changed |
|---|---|
| `event_consistency` | 18 (11 %; 7 % to 17 %) |
| `centroid_shift` | 13 (8 %; 5 % to 13 %) |
| `systematics` | 13 (8 %; 5 % to 13 %) |
| `odd_even` | 12 (7 %; 4 % to 13 %) |
| `aperture_depth` | 10 (6 %; 3 % to 11 %) |
| `secondary_eclipse` | 7 (4 %; 2 % to 9 %) |
| `period_alias` | 2 (1 %; 0.3 % to 4 %) |
| `duty_cycle`, `edge_events`, `nearby_contamination`, `single_event`, `sinusoid` | 1 each (0.6 %; 0.1 % to 3 %) |
| `plausibility`, `snr` | 0 (0 %; 0 % to 2 %) |

## What it means, and what it does not

- **About one in seven previously decided records changed class in the rerun.** This count
  includes two runs that ended in ERROR. No output moved from SURVIVED or WEAKENED to REFUTED.
- Of the 13 decided changes in completed runs, 12 moved to INCONCLUSIVE and one from REFUTED to
  WEAKENED.
- v0.5.6's locked claim: of its 17 false-positive events (REFUTED on the TOI's own signal), 14
  stayed REFUTED here, two became INCONCLUSIVE and one WEAKENED; its one planet event stayed
  REFUTED, and none of the other 39 part B planet records rerun here (of the claim's 150 drawn
  planets) became REFUTED. The locked verdict stands as recorded; this measures how much its
  individual events depend on the fit.
- **The moves were hard to predict from the first run.** Only 8 % of the status changes came from
  tests that had been close to their threshold.
- **A single Refute output should not be read as more stable than this.** Refute never calls a
  claim "proven": a claim survives or it does not, under one stated procedure.
- **The changes concentrate in a few tests:** `event_consistency`, `centroid_shift`,
  `systematics`, `odd_even` and `aperture_depth`.
- **Nothing changes as a result.** This phase changes no test, no threshold and no earlier
  result. It measures; it does not fix.

The records, the scripts and the full report are in the Refute repository:
[`docs/validation/v0.5.8/`](https://github.com/klucilla/refute/tree/main/docs/validation/v0.5.8).
