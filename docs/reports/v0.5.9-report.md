# Refute v0.5.9: ephemeris entry, an exploratory prototype

Report of the v0.5.9 phase. **A draft for the maintainer's approval** (#218).

> **Limits label: 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'.** `method_status: not_validated`.
> **Exploratory.**
> - Nothing here is a verdict on any TOI, a candidate, a disposition or a discovery.
> - The numbers describe these 24 TOIs only. Every TOI was already exposed in v0.5.6 and v0.5.8,
>   and the 14 changes were chosen *because* they changed class. No rate here estimates a
>   population.
> - Nothing changed in `src/`, in the v0.5 battery or in any verdict. No lock and no drand round
>   were used.

## The question

When the v0.5 battery enters through the TOI's ephemeris, with a bounded and declared refit, and
its output is checked on a declared set of local perturbations, do the cases that changed class in
v0.5.8 stabilise, and at what cost in decidability and CPU?
([plan](../plans/v0.5.9-plan.md); the origin is Codex's smaller alternative to a full version 5,
[record](../plans/v0.5.9-consensus/README.md).)

- **Two entry modes on the same input bytes** (SHA-256 checked by every work unit):
  - **E**, the TOI's ephemeris: a bounded two-pass refit within ±0.001 in period and ±D/2 in
    epoch;
  - **S**, the battery's own search and refit.
- **Both outputs are evaluated at their final ephemeris,** held fixed.
- **Six local probes per decided output:** epoch ±D/20, period ±one fine step, duration ±D/20.
- **The consensus** keeps a decided class only if all six probes give it. It only demotes.
- **The set** ([`prototype-set.json`](../validation/v0.5.9/prototype-set.json), committed before
  the run):
  - the 13 decided class changes of v0.5.8;
  - the one change the other way (TOI-6450.01);
  - v0.5.8's two numerical ERRORs (TOI-734.01, TOI-5218.01);
  - 8 unchanged controls, 2 per class, drawn with a committed seed.

## The pilot: the supervisor's account against an independent measure

The first batch ran only TOI-2357.01, TOI-734.01 and TOI-5218.01: 27 dispatches. A separate
observer process held its own handle to every worker and to the driver:
- **CPU:** the observer read Windows' processor time (`GetProcessTimes`) after each worker
  exited;
- **Bytes:** it kept the largest size of every new file under the download folder.

| Downloads | 1,157,852,626 bytes | 1,155,265,920 bytes (48 files) | 0.22 % |

- **The rule** (posted before the pilot): every difference at most 10 %, every dispatch settled
  and measured, no cap passed and no stop. It held, so the other 21 TOIs ran without a new stop

  - the HTTP bytes, metered over the file sizes.

  the offline tests:

## The answers (exploratory marks, with Wilson 95 % intervals)

**Q1, the 14 changes: do E and S give the same class?**

| | Same decided class | Both INCONCLUSIVE | Same class, either kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| Without consensus | 3 of 14 (0.214; 0.076 to 0.476) | 1 of 14 | 4 of 14 (0.286) |
| With consensus | 1 of 14 (0.071; 0.013 to 0.315) | 9 of 14 | 10 of 14 (0.714; 0.454 to 0.883) |

- Over the 9 pairs whose two fits stayed on the TOI ephemeris, the same decided class was 3
  without consensus and 1 with it.
- **The changes do not stabilise as decided classes.** With the consensus, most pairs agree only
  by both abstaining.

**Q2, the 8 controls keep their class:** 8 of 8 without and with consensus, in both modes
(1.000; 0.676 to 1.000).

## Observations: exploratory and post hoc, for hypotheses only

These were read after the results. None was a question fixed before the run. Each is a
hypothesis for a later phase, never a finding.

1. **The class changes track the entry point.**
   - In all 13 changes, the S base (the battery's own search) gives the v0.5.6 class.
   - The E base (the TOI's ephemeris) gives v0.5.8's INCONCLUSIVE in 10 of the 12 cases where
     v0.5.8 was INCONCLUSIVE. The exceptions are TOI-2407.01 (SURVIVED) and TOI-3040.01
     (WEAKENED).
   - Both arms used the same bytes. The v0.5.8 changes therefore look tied to where the fit
     starts, not to the inputs.
   - The prototype does not isolate the cause. The fitted ephemeris and the simulation seeds
     (`power.candidate_seed`) move together; this confound was stated in the plan.
2. **The E entry's abstentions:**
   - 3 entries were unresolved, with the optimum on the epoch edge of the ±D/2 window (TOI-399.01,
     TOI-2111.01, TOI-2193.01);
   - 7 abstained in an undecided test: `systematics` in 4, `aperture_depth` in 3 and
     `plausibility` in 1. TOI-357.01 names three tests: `odd_even`, `aperture_depth` and
     `systematics`.
3. **The consensus demotions:**
   - of the 10 demotions (2 in E, 8 in S), 8 start at an epoch probe (p1 or p2), with a shift of
     D/20; one starts at a period probe and one at a duration probe;
   - `systematics` is among the tests that changed in 6 of the 10. Here, as in v0.5.8, the
     background sensitivity test is the most fragile part;
   - one S probe went the other way: TOI-399.01's p2 gave REFUTED, through `aperture_depth` and
     `centroid_shift`.
4. **The guard turns the numerical failure into an abstention.** The underlying condition, a sky
   covariance that is not positive definite, recurs on TOI-734.01 in most of its probes. The
   guard turns it into a reasoned abstention; it does not remove the condition.

## What this phase does not establish

- No population rate. The set is selected and exposed.
- No claim that E or S is better. There is no same-input comparator against version 2, and none
  was planned.
- No validation of the resource kill paths on real data (see the pilot).
- Nothing about planets or false positives as such: no disposition was used.
