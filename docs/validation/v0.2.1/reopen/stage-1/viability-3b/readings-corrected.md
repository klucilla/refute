# The viability diagnosis: corrected readings (2026-10-27)

**Recorded by the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-27**, on [report.md](report.md) (`a8d436a`) and
on its summary to the maintainer. These readings replace the ones they name; the report's numbers
and `diagnosis.json` are unchanged.

1. **The separations near two years** between consecutive observed years' first sectors are 321 of
   922 for the planets (about 35%): **the most frequent bin, not "almost always"** (the summary to
   the maintainer said "almost always"; the report, section 1, that "about two years" dominates).
   They are **separations, not independent targets**: one TIC contributes one separation per pair
   of consecutive observed years.
2. **For the false positives the most common pattern is a single sector** (`1`: 111 TICs), **not**
   `1-0-1` (19). `1-0-1` is the most common pattern of the planets only (49, tied with `1`), and the
   summary's sentence applied it to both classes.
3. **TESS's alternation between hemispheres is a plausible explanation** of the controls'
   incompatibility with D5, **not a universal cause** (the report, section 1, already marks it as
   an interpretation, not tested).
4. **The timing barrier of the two-year layouts does not transfer to three years or more.** With
   three observing years, each round trains on two years (for example 2018 and 2020 predicting
   2022); the outcome depends on the train's extent, on the number and quality of the transits, on
   the aliases, on the variability and on the distance of the prediction. The report's alternative
   B cited the two-year diagnosis's lever ([../viability/two-year-results.md](../viability/two-year-results.md))
   as if it applied: it does not carry over. **T4 is again a relevant hypothesis, not a demonstrated
   necessity.**
5. **Alternative A is not discarded for its utility**: the reach of `refute discover` has not been
   measured on a representative population. Removing the controls' filters changes the
   calibration's population and **needs its own approval**.
