# Pont, Zucker & Queloz 2006

- **Title:** The effect of red noise on planetary transit detection
- **Journal:** Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 373, 217-230 (2006); DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.11012.x
- **URL read:** https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0608597 (the arXiv abstract page)
- **Read on:** 2026-10-21
- **Literal excerpt (abstract):** "the detection threshold in the presence of systematics can be much
  higher"

**What it supports here:** correlated ("red") noise can raise a transit detection threshold well
above what the white-noise assumption gives. Used in X2 only as the reason to test the engine's
predicted error `e_pred` against the scatter of repeated noise realizations in the AR(1) cells, and
not to assume it. Nothing in X2 depends on the paper's method or numbers.
