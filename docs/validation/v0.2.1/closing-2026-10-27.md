## The hypotheses recorded for future research (none adopted, none authorized)

1. **The train's capacity rule** (decision 1, `s_pred,1` against the threshold at the R_min study's
   pair `b = 0.585`, `s = 2.883`) is the dominant first stopping point in three-, four- and
   five-year geometries (72.5% and 59.3% of the rounds); a recalibrated `(b, s)` or another capacity
   model is the open question. Specification C's candidates for the predicted error, at that pair,
   did not release it; the comparison does not separate the estimator from the threshold.
2. **The released rounds' measured outcomes**: the calibration records hold the measurement of the
   rounds the capacity rule stops, which a future, declared reading could use (not computed).
3. **Variability**: C's variability regime had 3 spec-B PASS of 360; its cause is not isolated.
4. **Timing (T4)**: a relevant hypothesis for three years or more (the corrected readings of the
   diagnosis), not a demonstrated need; it barely appeared as a barrier in the C/D lot.
5. **The hidden noise** (undetermined, capacity by the hidden replicas): a minor barrier in the lot,
   a structural question still open.
6. **The real controls**: their availability inside a domain (gate 3b), and the wider sources of
   alternative A, not discarded for their usefulness; a change of the controls' filters changes the
   calibration population and needs its own approval.
7. **Domains with more years** (D): more years raise the per-round PASS fraction (about 40% against
   22%) while specification B needs every year; D's lot (12 geometries, no variability) never
   replaces a full validation.
8. **Specification C's own validation** (C5 on its own new data) was never run; its models stay
   candidates.

## Corrections of 2026-10-27 (the maintainer's review of this record)

Added after the record above, which is kept as written. Where the two differ, this section is the
reading that holds.

1. **The supported conclusion on the outcomes is limited.** In this lot of synthetic planets, the
   holdout produced few PASS and no FAIL. That is a limited positive result, not a showing that the
   full battery is safe: the lot had no false positives and no curves without a transit; the
   gauntlet and the global gate were not run; the ability to reject spurious signals was not
   measured; and zero observed FAIL is not a zero error rate (the one-sided 95% upper bounds of the
   FAIL fraction were 0.008 for C and 0.025 for D, [report.md](reopen/stage-1/viability-cd/report.md),
   section 2). No text may say that the engine or the holdout is safe on this evidence.
2. **Hypothesis 1 locates a barrier; it does not establish its cause.** Decision 1 being the
   dominant first stopping point does not show, by itself, that it is wrong or too conservative: it
   can reflect an inadequate model, a badly calibrated threshold, or information that is really
   insufficient, and the lot did not separate these explanations. The statement on specification C
   is restricted to: **with the pair `(b, s)` kept, none of the twelve model pairs evaluated met this
   screening's rule.** Specification C's own validation was not run (hypothesis 8), so the comparison
   is no evidence that no capacity model would work.
3. **Hypothesis 7 does not show that more years solved the problem.** Its per-round comparison
   (about 40% against 22%) mixes regimes: C includes cases with and without variability, D has no
   variability, so it does not isolate the effect of the added years. Without variability, per
   target: white C 55 of 180, D 27 of 120; AR(1) C 51 of 180, D 38 of 120; the reported intervals
   (Newcombe, two-sided 95%: +0.081 [−0.023, 0.177] white, −0.033 [−0.140, 0.070] AR(1)) established
   no difference ([report.md](reopen/stage-1/viability-cd/report.md), section 6). D stays a
   hypothesis, not a demonstrated solution.
4. **The scope of the decision.** v0.2.1 ended as not approved: that ends this attempt; it does not
   show that the project as a whole is infeasible. Components that were built and tested (the list
   under "Preserved, unchanged") are distinct from a validated method, which v0.2.1 did not deliver.
5. **The capacity research stays recorded for the future, with no new lot now.** A resumption
   would need a narrower question: to show whether the model represents the available error and
   capacity correctly, without choosing adjustments to raise the number of PASS.
