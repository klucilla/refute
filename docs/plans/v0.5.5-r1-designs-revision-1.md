## 1. I4's convergence failures: the diagnosis (item 3a)

**A diagnosis only; no conclusion on D2** (`docs/validation/v0.5.5/i4_diagnosis.py`, with the
fix in `i4_fix.py`; `record-defects.md`, section 3, asked for the cause or the power apart).

- **The hypothesis** (the independent review's): I4 checks its analytic derivatives of the
  trapezoid against central differences over every cadence. At a cadence whose two perturbed
  evaluations fall on different sides of a corner of the trapezoid, a central difference is not
  the derivative on either side, so a few such cadences can pass I4's 1e-2 in L2 while the
  analytic derivatives are right.
- **The fix, declared before it was run:** the same check, the same steps and the same 1e-2,
  restricted to the cadences whose segment (out of transit, ramp, flat) is the same at −h, 0 and
  +h, with a guard (at most a quarter of a column's support left out, otherwise the planet stays
  invalid). The analytic derivatives, the Fisher matrix and the bound are unchanged.
- **The result on I4's two sets** (`i4-convergence-diagnosis.json`; every curve regenerated with
  I4's development seed and I4's full difference reproduced to 1e-9):

| Set | Invalid by I4 | By convergence | Reproduced | Cause confirmed | Masked check passes | Valid after the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fresh (round 3) | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| v0.5.2's 112 | 15 | 14 | 14 | 14 | 14 | 14 |

  The fifteenth of v0.5.2's set is invalid for another reason ("F is ill-conditioned
  (3.55e+14)") and stays invalid. The largest full difference was 0.060 and 0.041; the largest
  masked one about 4.2e-7; the largest share of a column left out 0.026. **The powers after the
  fix are all at least 0.985; they are reported apart and read against nothing.**

**The fix was chosen with I4's results known** (E = 15 in both sets): this is disclosed in the
amendment's table of conventions. It changes the domain of a numerical check, not a threshold.

## 2. D2-F, redesigned (item 3b): code only, not run

**What changes** (`docs/validation/v0.5.5/d2f_floor.py`):

1. **INFO with the fixed bound.** When I4's only invalid reason is the convergence check, the
   masked check of `i4_fix.py` is made; the reason is dropped only if it passes. The bound's
   other checks are I4's, unchanged.
2. **The remaining unknowns stay in the upper count**, and **their rate is disclosed** per variant
   (`unknown_rate_per_variant`: the upper count less the strict, over the eligible), with I4's
   bound tabulated (invalid by I4, by convergence, valid after the fix, invalid after it, the
   reasons left).
3. **The power of a bound that stays invalid is stored apart** (`power_of_an_invalid_bound`) and
   never counted.
4. **A fourth variant, frozen before any data:** `variant_4_combined` = DATA ∪ INFO ∪ SIN_J ∪
   RULE_3 (both method changes at once). It is read with the same rule as the other three.
5. **The comparison in the same unit.** Each set's own v0.4 kept fraction `f04` is tabulated, and
   the route threshold is the **stricter** of the fixed 14/178 and the set's own unit:
   `(n − ceil((f04 − 0.10) n))/n − 8/178` (`r1_common.d2f_route_fraction`; round 3's v0.4 kept
   173 of 178 gives exactly 14/178). The pooled reading uses the pooled `f04`. The threshold is
   never looser than 14/178.

**The decision-value test, redone** (`d2f_decision_value.py`, `d2f-decision-value.json`):

## 3. D1-P, revised (item 4a)

- **Distances for every eligible binary**, not only the battery's survivors.
- **Two views, both read:** the battery's survivors (a binary the battery denies SURVIVED counts
  0), and **every eligible binary** (as if the battery denied none). **"Route" for an exit needs
  every family to read "route" on both sets in both views.**
- **"Closed" is removed:** B0's own numbers make it practically unreachable (the review, Q2.5).
  The outcomes are "route" and "unresolved" (`r1_common.d1p_set_reading`).
- **Zero survivors:** a view with no survivor reads "INCONCLUSIVE (no survivor)", never "route".
- **Coverage:** an exit whose readings miss a view, a set or a family returns "INCONCLUSIVE";
  "route" needs the eight expected cells (two views, sets A and B, EB1 and EB2), all "route"
  (`d1p_exit_outcome`; Codex, night round 1). An exit with incomplete coverage on set A never
  justifies running set B (`d1p_futile_after_first`).
- **The exit prices** (amendment 1): WEAKENED **0.05** per new test; INCONCLUSIVE 0.05,
  conditional on D2 and D4 met in full. **Scenarios, never outcomes:** WEAKENED at 0.10 (the
  battery unit); REFUTED at step 2's strict 0.025, and 0.025 less 3/178.
- **The bridge** (amendment 1, (c)): set A's first 100 seeds per family drawn again with C1
  version 1 (the star at the catalog's centre). **It compares the same 100 attempted indices per
  family under version 1 and version 2** (set A's records of indices 0 to 99), side by side, in
  both views and at every price, with each index's eligibility and v0.5 verdict under each
  version and the count of differences; the comparison is not restricted to the binaries
  eligible under both. Never an outcome. Its own bootstrap seed (20261122, for each side) and a
  minimum of 75 eligible per family.
- **The futility rule:** after set A, set B runs only if some decisive exit reads "route" for
  every family in every view on set A; otherwise set A's record holds every exit "unresolved".
- **The decision-value test, redone.** At 0.05, B0's box on all eligible binaries left an
  expected 14.1 and 12.5 (k = 10) and the known star 4.6 and 4.1; the primary reading's distance
  is at most the known star's (the review, Q2.6), so its count is at least the known star's.
  "Route" needs at most 8 in both views. **Route: about 15 to 30 %; unresolved: about 70 to
  85 %; planning opinions, not probabilities computed from the records cited.** Each changes the next action as before (route: D1's priced route is worth designing in
  R2; unresolved: G1 weighs a pause of D1). Two plausible outcomes: **it passes**, with a lower
  prior than before (the amendment's lower price and the all-eligible view both move away from
  "route").

## 6. The code's fixes (item 4c)

- `d1p_exit_outcome` with empty maps returns "INCONCLUSIVE".
- A futile set A writes "unresolved" for every exit (D1-P) or variant (D2-F).
- The caps: section 4.
- **The frozen lists, checked by a conservative guard:** `r1_common.require_frozen` refuses (a STOP)
  when a project module outside `src/` that the check loads, or imports anywhere in its frozen
  modules (a static pass over their import statements, transitively, including folders put on
  the path inside a function), is not in its list. It found v0.5.1's `forecast.py` (through
  `d2_bounds._wp1`) and seven other modules; all are now listed. **It is a conservative guard of
  loaded modules and recognized static imports, not a general proof of the dependencies'
  closure** (Codex, night round 1); each check runs in a process of its own.
- **A stuck lock** (`ledger_v055.release_stale`): **the recovery removes only the instance of the
  lock whose identity was checked, under a mutual exclusion that creation and release also
  respect; any doubt keeps the lock and is a STOP** (Codex, night round 1). Creating, releasing
  and recovering the lock happen under an operating-system lock on `<lock>.guard` (released by the
  system if a process dies); the lock is removed only if the process recorded in it does not exist
  (or the PID now belongs to a process started after the lock) and its identity (file id, size,
  time, bytes) is unchanged just before the removal; the removal is recorded in
  `.cache/v055/stale-locks.jsonl` after it happened. A release removes only its own instance. The
  Windows calls are fully declared.
- `pinned_hash` reads `git ls-tree -z`, so a path with a space stays one path.
- **Offline tests** of the glue (`tests/v055/test_r1_glue.py`): D2-F's `categories` and
  `floors`, D1-P's `_family`, T2a-E's `shift_phases`, `placebo` (with a fake E4) and `_check_i1`,
  the caps against the projections, the frozen lists, the stale lock and `ls_tree_paths`.

## What this revision does not do

It runs nothing. The maintainer's decision (item 5) approves D1-P and, since it passes the
geometry count, T2a-E **once Codex returns SURVIVED on these fixes**: new seeds, at most 10
workers, one pre-approved resume per part, and the exact procedure posted on #218 before each run.
D2-F does not run.
