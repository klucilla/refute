# v0.5.3 stage A: bounds, audits and diagnoses (frozen design)

The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-11 (#217): stage A of round 3 approved, with caps of **50 CPU-h,
0.5 M review tokens and 4 agent-days**, in the order (a), (b), (c), (d), cutting (d) first if the cap
tightens. **Development data and development seeds only; it decides nothing about any target.** No
change to `src/`, to any rule, to T2a's fingerprint or to any earlier record: v0.5.2's scripts are
imported, never edited, and never called through an entry point that writes (`forecast_t2a.fetch`,
`manifests`, `run` and `summarize` are not called); historical records are read only through
`driver.Run(...).assemble()`, which writes nothing; the v0.5.2 ledger's SHA-256 is checked unchanged
before and after every stage A run. Every output, log and fingerprint is stage A's own (under
`docs/validation/v0.5.3/stageA/` and `.cache/v053/`), and each fingerprint covers the stage A
script, its wrappers and every v0.5.2 file it imports. `method_status: not_validated`. Each run's
approval and exact procedure are posted on #217 before it runs. This design is frozen, reviewed by
Codex and committed before stage A's first run; a change after a result is a recorded, prospective
amendment. Codex reviewed this design in three rounds (`REFUTED`, `REFUTED`, `WEAKENED`); every
finding is applied, and its round 3 found no blocking defect for (a) once the review tokens' rule is
stated; (b), (c) and (d) each need their implementation reviewed before they run.

## Accounting and the machine

A ledger of its own, `docs/validation/v0.5.3/stageA/ledger.json`: `ledger_a.py` imports v0.5.2's
`budgeted.py` and `driver.py` unchanged and points their module constants at this ledger (one line,
`stage_a`, 50 CPU-h; nothing spent before). Allocations, declared before any dispatch: (a) 4, (b) 8,
(c) 24 (the bounds 4, the background audit 2, the sample above 20 sectors 18), (d) 6, reserve 8.
**The strict cap.** The driver may pass a part's cap by up to 10 % and checks between batches, so
every part's hard cap is reserved below what is left: (what is left of the 50 CPU-h, minus the
in-flight reserve of one batch, workers times the shard limit) divided by 1.10, and never more than
1.5 times its allocation. The reserve is not automatic headroom: moving CPU between parts, or using
the reserve, is a prospective transfer recorded in the ledger's notes and on #217 before the part
runs, cutting (d) first; spent attempts stay counted (`ledger_a.transfer`; `ledger_a.declare`
initializes missing allocations only and keeps persisted ones, transfers included, and each cap is
computed from the part's current row). **Tokens and days.** Before each step, `ledger_a.check_caps`
reads the review tokens recorded in `stageA/caps.json` (an independent review session's count) and
Claude's agent-days since 2026-11-11 05:55 UTC (`.cache/v052/tools/agent_time.py`, the wall-clock
upper bound), adds the step's reservation, records the check and refuses the step if either cap
would be passed: a stop, reported. **The earlier ledger.** Its SHA-256 is checked unchanged in a
`finally` around every stage A run, covering preflight and execution failures.
The driver's machine rules hold: processors 8 to 31 only, BelowNormal, batches of at most 30
minutes, at most 10 workers on weekdays from 8 to 18 and up to 12 otherwise, atomic shards, resume
proven by the driver's tests, the 5 % checkpoint, the stops binding, the pause at 20:00 on 18
November.

## (a) A0's refusals

**Question.** Which branch of A0 refuses, with the data present or not, in every refusal of the
v0.5.2 diagnosis and forecast.

**What runs.** The 51 T2a development injections that version 4 B loses against v0.4 (the v0.5.2
diagnosis), rebuilt by `forecast_t2a_diagnosis.one` (the v0.4 battery and version 4 C, with its own
trace of every `a0_v4.a0_depth` return), and the 28 D2 development planets that version 4 loses
against v0.4 (`forecast-v4.json`), rebuilt as `forecast_v4.one` does (C1's generator and development
seed; the v0.4 battery and version 4 at stage 2; version 3 not run). In version 4, three functions
are traced, each returning the original's result unchanged:
- `a0.group_variance`: on a none, a step-by-step copy names the first refusing branch: a lag
  correlation with fewer than `MIN_PAIRS` window pairs; a lag correlation with enough pairs but a
  degenerate (zero-variance) Pearson correlation; a covariance that is not positive semi-definite
  (its smallest eigenvalue); or a variance that is not positive. Every correlation value it used is
  kept. A copy that finds no refusal quarantines the record.
- `a0_v4.empirical_events`: the events before and after (`a0_not_measured`), and each sector and
  coverage stratum's `n`, `n_eff` and lag correlations (an event is dropped when its stratum's
  `n_eff` is below 10).
- the sector selection, from the tests' metrics: the eligible events per sector, the chosen and the
  contributing sectors, the refusal.

**Checks.** A record is quarantined, not classified, unless it reproduces its v0.5.2 record: for T2a,
the candidate, every version 4 test's status and message, and the stage verdict; for D2, the v0.4
and version 4 verdicts and every version 4 test's status and class. **Output:** each refusal blocker
with a named branch and its numbers (`a0-refusals.json`); the branch totals count refusing calls,
not independently attributable lost planets. Calls made by the diagnostic copy of
v0.5.2 (`_why_none`, a replay on an A0 none) are tagged as replays and never counted; a failure of
any trace, or a copy that disagrees, quarantines the record; D2's `a0_v4.a0_depth` returns are traced
as T2a's are; each dropped event keeps its epoch and its reason. **Cost:** about 3 CPU-h.

## (b) D2's bounds

**Two quantities, kept apart.** (1) **Evidence-qualified PASS**: whether a blocking test could PASS
with its declared sensitivity under a bound on attainable precision. (2) **Kept retention**: D2 and
T2a count WEAKENED as kept, and the sensitivity rule only turns a PASS into INCONCLUSIVE, so a
**warning** test's INCONCLUSIVE can also be recovered by a warning FAIL. An upper bound on kept
retention therefore counts a warning blocker as recoverable unless both its PASS and its FAIL
routes are excluded, which no information bound does; only **fatal** blockers (`odd_even`,
`period_alias`, `aperture_depth`, the per-event tests) can be bounded as lost.

**Bounds** (every one stated with its assumptions; an unbiased-estimator Cramér-Rao bound does not
cover "any estimator", so each is a bound for the stated model):
- `odd_even` (fatal): a stage A helper computes the Fisher information of the odd and even depths
  jointly, `F = X^T Σ^-1 X`, with `X`'s columns the true template's odd transits, its even transits
  and one constant level per sector (the nuisance columns), and `Σ` the generator's covariance
  (white plus each sector's exponential term); the bound is `σ^2 = c^T F^-1 c` with
  `c = (1, -1, 0, ..., 0)`, keeping the cross-information that v0.5.2's diagnosis omitted (it added
  the odd and even informations separately). The planet passes the bound if the
  sensitivity rule's probability at that error reaches its requirement. Gaussian noise with the
  generator's covariance and the true template are assumed.
- the sinusoid (warning): for the evidence-qualified PASS only, the oracle generalized-least-squares
  standard error of the residual box depth, `σ_box = sqrt([(Z^T Σ^-1 Z)^-1]_(box, box))` with `Z` the
  box column and the joint model's columns, under the generator's covariance; the flag's power taken as 1 (no proved bound on
  it); then `small` at that error. These marginal conditions are necessary, not sufficient, for the
  joint sensitivity. For kept retention, recoverable (a warning).
- `aperture_depth` and `systematics`: from (a). A procedural refusal, a quarantine, `period_alias`
  (no bound) and any blocker without a bound count as recoverable; an input counts as absent (a
  loss) only where its necessity across the allowed procedure is shown.

**The count.** All 112 eligible D2 planets are enumerated once: a planet is kept in the upper bound
if version 4 keeps it, or if every one of its blockers is recoverable by the rules above; gains
among v0.4's 4 losses are counted the same way, never twice. **Uncertainty, kept apart from the
bound's validity:** the count is deterministic for the 112 generator draws; the population's
uncertainty is the Wilson 95 % upper end of the kept fraction, compared with D2's population
requirement, v0.4's kept fraction minus 0.10 (108/112 − 0.10 = 0.8643); the integer requirement of
this sample, 97 of 112, is stated apart. The bound's own assumptions are listed beside it. If even that upper end is
below the requirement, the draft infeasibility record of D2. **Cost:** about 6 CPU-h.

## (c) T2a: the background statistic, its bounds, and the sample above 20 sectors

**(c1) The background audit** (measurements, not bounds; no new run beyond (a)'s records and the
cached light curves): for the 23 background losses, the per-point scatter of the background series
(the median absolute deviation of first differences, divided by 0.6745 and by the square root of 2,
which may still contain variability), its white box error, A0's error, the SAP depth's error, and the
background's variability outside transit; the ratio of A0's error to that scatter; and the
unrounded necessary condition of the 3-error rule alone, error over SAP depth at most
0.8 / (3 + Φ^-1(0.95)) = 0.17223, which does not bound the compound rule with an uncertain SAP depth.

**(c2) T2a's bounds**, per injection, with (b)'s two quantities and rules. `odd_even`'s
joint-information error under white noise estimated from the data is a plug-in, not a bound (the
true error may be smaller), so it is reported as a conditional scenario only; in the upper bound,
`odd_even` blockers count as recoverable, as do warnings (`systematics`, the sinusoid), refusals,
quarantines, unfinished injections and `period_alias`. The
upper bound on T2a's kept fraction is weighted by the population's strata, with the stratified
hypergeometric interval of the forecast (alpha 0.05 over three strata), every unknown counted in its
stratum's denominator and as a success at the upper end. It does not cover the error of noise
estimation or of simulations.

**(c3) The sample above 20 sectors.** The forecast's frozen sample of that stratum (30 injections in
8 hosts, seeded `refute-v052-dev:forecast-t2a`; their light curves are already in the cache and
were checked by SHA-256 in v0.5.2, so nothing is downloaded). A stage A worker of its own,
version 4 C only (the selector treated as certified and the refusal above 20 lifted, as in the
forecast; no v0.4, version 3 or A battery), rebuilds each injection as `forecast_t2a_diagnosis.one`
does and checks the reconstructed **final gauntlet candidate** against the candidate of the
injection's v0.4 record in the v0.5 C2 records (`paired.py` records the final one); a mismatch makes
the injection unknown. **No child process** (the driver accounts only for its pool workers' CPU and
ends only them): each injection runs inside its pool worker. **(c3) is on hold until an amendment,
reviewed by Codex before it runs, gives it a batch deadline enforced while the workers run** (the
driver detects an overlong shard only after it completes), with interrupted work charged and
unfinished injections unknown, never replaced; the smoke of the sample's injection with the most
loaded sectors is a runtime proxy, not a worst case, and an outside-pool smoke needs its CPU reserved
in advance and charged even on an exception. Without that amendment, (c3) is cut and the stratum
stays bounded both ways.

**(c3) is cut by the tie-break (2026-11-11).** Codex reviewed amendment 2's controller in three
rounds, each `REFUTED` (round 3: an idempotent charge that does not reconcile the amount, a stop
persisted after its attempt is closed, a watchdog failure not persisted as a stop, a persisted
failed checkpoint not enforced on restart). By the rule, the option more conservative against a
false conclusion wins: (c3) does not run, nothing of it was executed, and the stratum above 20
sectors stays bounded both ways (T2a's kept fraction 0.909 to 1.0 under (c2)'s rules). The
controller (`above20.py`) is kept as written, never run.

**Amendment 2 (2026-11-11, before (c3) runs; reviewed by Codex, execution-blocking until it
survives):** (c3) runs under a controller of its own (`docs/validation/v0.5.3/stageA/above20.py`),
whose docstring states its guarantees: the controller enters a Windows Job Object (kill on close,
processors 8 to 31) before creating any child, so every child is created inside it; each child
pins itself to one processor at BelowNormal with one numeric thread; an attempt and its
reservation are written before each launch, each child's processor time is charged once through
its handle, and an open attempt found after a crash, or a failed measurement, is charged its full
reservation with a binding stop; a watchdog thread ends a child at 1,500 seconds or at the batch's
28-minute deadline; a timeout or interruption is authoritative and its injection stays unknown; the
accepted smoke (a clean exit within 1,200 seconds, bound to the fingerprint) gates the run; a
checkpoint of the first ceil(n / 20) injections projects the cost; the caps are checked before each
batch with the batch's own worst case reserved; no batch after 20:00 on 18 November. Their bounds follow (c2)'s rules; a raw version 4 C loss is not an attainable-retention bound. **Cost:** (c1) under 1 CPU-h; (c2) about
3; (c3) about 0.4 CPU-h per injection, about 12 for 30.

## (d) D1's geometric route (last; cut first)

The transit-geometry consistency of #215 (the stellar density implied by the transit's duration,
ingress and depth against the catalogue star), measured on C1's development binaries and planets,
recorded as a diagnostic: its separation of EB1 and EB2 from the planets at a threshold fixed before
the measurement on the planets' side (false-failure rate at most 0.0027 on P and PG). **Cost:** about
5 CPU-h. Runs only if (a) to (c) leave at least 6 CPU-h under the cap.

**Amendment 3 (2026-11-11, before (d) ran; its implementation reviewed by Codex in three rounds,
the last "acceptable to run"; posted on #217 before the run):** the implementation
(`docs/validation/v0.5.3/stageA/d1_geometry.py`) reuses v0.5.1's `d1_routes.route_one` on 1,900
new curves of C1's generator at v0.5.1's development seed (P and PG indices 150 to 949, EB1 and EB2
150 to 299; disjoint from v0.5.1's indices 0 to 149, with which they are pooled, and from every
confirmation seed; C1', C2', C3' stay undrawn). The threshold is the
larger of the P and PG empirical 99.73 % quantiles, and the counts above it are calibration-sample
fractions, without a population guarantee. The cost is revised from v0.5.1's records to about
7.7 CPU-h, inside the part's cap of 1.5 times its 6 CPU-h; the hard cap computed at launch was
8.18 CPU-h. The 5 % checkpoint covers all four families.
