# v0.5.5 R2: the frozen code's semantics (for the blind review)

**Status: a description of the frozen rules, for the blind review's bundle** (the maintainer's
decisions of 2026-11-21, 13:30, item 4; charter (d)3; amendment 1 (f)). It specifies the frozen
execution path, the expected decisive tests, their severities and roles, the coverage and
sensitivity enforcement, the parameter values and measurement definitions that the three design
questions touch, and where the proposed branch changes would act. **It contains no empirical
outcome and no reference to a record of results.** It changes nothing. The source files it cites
are in the bundle under `frozen-rules/`. `method_status: not_validated`.

## 1. The execution path

1. **A candidate** is found by the search and passes or fails the **gate** (`snr`, severity GATE).
2. **The battery** runs its tests on the candidate (`packs/tess/pipeline.py`, which builds the
   test list; the v0.5.2 battery's tests are assembled in `packs/tess/pipeline_v52.py`,
   `v052_tests`, from `battery_v5.py`, `battery_v51.py`, `battery_v51_diff.py` and
   `battery_v52.py`).
3. **Each test** returns a `TestResult` (`core/verdict.py`): a status (PASS, FAIL or
   INCONCLUSIVE), a severity (GATE, FATAL or WARNING), its metrics and thresholds, its coverage
   (with its unit) and, for a statistical test, one **sensitivity block** per sub-check.
4. **The coverage rule** (`core/verdict.py`, `enforce_coverage`): a PASS without positive declared
   coverage becomes INCONCLUSIVE.
5. **The sensitivity rule** (`core/sensitivity.py`, `enforce_sensitivity`): a fatal or warning
   PASS becomes INCONCLUSIVE unless every registered sub-check has a valid block and every
   applicable rule block is detectable (its probability of rejecting under the declared
   alternative at least the required probability). A FAIL is never changed. The gate is exempt.
   The registry of sub-checks and their kinds is `packs/tess/power.py` (`TESS_REGISTRY_V3` for
   the corrected battery): a rule's required probability is **0.95** unless the registry sets
   another (`period_alias` registers 0.9).
6. **Roles** (`core/verdict.py`, `aggregate_roles`): a claim with roles aggregates its **decisive**
   tests only; **diagnostic** tests are reported and never change the verdict.
7. **The aggregation** (`core/verdict.py`, `aggregate`), in order: a gate that did not PASS gives
   INCONCLUSIVE; any fatal FAIL gives REFUTED; a missing expected test or any INCONCLUSIVE gives
   INCONCLUSIVE; any warning FAIL gives WEAKENED; otherwise SURVIVED.

## 3. The background sub-check (question B2)

- **Where:** `systematics` (WARNING) has three sub-checks: momentum dumps (a fraction category),
  SAP (a rule, conditional) and **background** (a rule).
- **The measurement:** A0's in-transit measurements give the SAP depth (with its error) and the
  in-transit change of the background estimate (with its error); the code takes the **rise** as
  the in-transit increase of the background and its **fraction** as the rise divided by the SAP
  depth.
- **The FAIL rule:** the rise is at least **3** of its errors (`max_background_sigma`) **and** its
  fraction is at least **0.5** (`max_background_fraction`).
- **The sensitivity block** (`battery_v51_diff.py`, `background_block`): the alternative is a
  rise at the fraction **0.5 + 0.3 = 0.8** of the depth (`background_alternative_increment`); the
  depth and the rise are drawn from independent Gaussians with their errors; the probability that
  a draw lands in the FAIL region must reach 0.95.
- **Where B2's proposal would act:** only the alternative's fraction (0.8); the FAIL rule and the
  required probability are unchanged.

## 4. The sinusoid test (question B3)

- **Where:** `sinusoid` (WARNING), `battery_v52.py`; one sub-check per harmonic: P/2 (required),
  P and 2P (conditional on their period domain).
- **The fit:** a sinusoid at each applicable harmonic is fitted with the transit masked
  (inverse-variance weights); its semi-amplitude and that amplitude's error give a significance.
- **The flag:** a harmonic is flagged when its significance exceeds **15**
  (`sinusoid_min_significance`) **and** its semi-amplitude is at least **0.5** of the depth
  (`sinusoid_min_amplitude_ratio`).
- **The residual:** `r`, the box depth of the dip after the sinusoid model is removed (the box
  averages in-transit and out-of-transit cadences with uniform weights), and `sigma_r`, its error.
- **The outcomes:** no harmonic flagged: PASS (subject to the sensitivity rule); flagged and the
  residual **small**, `r + 3·sigma_r < 0.5·D` (`ATTENUATION_SIGMAS`, `ATTENUATION_FRACTION`; D the
  depth), with valid coverage and FAIL allowed: **FAIL**; flagged otherwise: **INCONCLUSIVE**
  (the ambiguous-cause branch, `AMBIGUOUS_SINUSOID`; no sensitivity blocks).
- **The sensitivity block:** per harmonic, the probability `p` that the flag is raised under the
  alternative (simulated: `battery_v5.py`, `_sinusoid_power`) and the probability `q` that the
  residual is shown small under it (Gaussian, from `sigma_r`); the block's probability is the
  Fréchet lower bound `max(0, p + q − 1)`, which must reach 0.95.
- **Where B3's proposals would act:** B3.2 replaces the Fréchet bound by a simulated joint
  probability under a specified common model (the rejection region and the alternative
  unchanged); B3.3 splits the ambiguous-cause branch: `r − 3·sigma_r > 0.5·D` would give PASS for
  this hypothesis (with valid sensitivity blocks), and only the rest would stay INCONCLUSIVE.

## 5. What the bundle's source files contain

`core/verdict.py` (the aggregation and the coverage rule), `core/sensitivity.py` (the
sensitivity rule), `packs/tess/power.py` (the registry and the v0.4 tests' blocks),
`packs/tess/params.py` (the parameter values), `packs/tess/pipeline.py` and
`packs/tess/pipeline_v52.py` (the test lists and the battery's assembly), `battery_v5.py`,
`battery_v51.py`, `battery_v51_diff.py` and `battery_v52.py` (the tests). Comments in them may
refer to the history of revisions; they hold no empirical outcome of the targets.
