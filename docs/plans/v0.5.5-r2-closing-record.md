**The decision.** The maintainer's decisions of 2026-11-21 (17:15) at R2's second stop, item 1
(#218): "R2 closes WITHOUT a freeze. Reason: by review 2 (blocker 1), B3.3 stays a registered variant
only, and the principled decisive combination (B1 (a′), B2 (a), B3.1 kept, B3.2 Fréchet, the frozen
ambiguous branch) is behaviour-identical to the frozen battery. An R3 on it opens no route for any

drawn; no lock or drand round was made.

## 1. What R2 concluded

R2 examined, on paper, three questions about the frozen test rules (B1 to B3), the data outside
development (B4) and the safeguards of a prospective version (C1 to C7), under the charter's
section (d) and amendment 1 (f): arguments written under a rule that kept every known number in a
separate disclosure, reviewed by Codex (conversations r2choice, r2args, r2t3b, r2rev) and by two
independent blind reviewers (Claude Fable 5.2, separate sessions launched by the maintainer's
adviser), each on a bundle built from an allowlist with a SHA-256 manifest written first.

**The principled primaries** ([`v0.5.5-r2-arguments.md`](v0.5.5-r2-arguments.md)):

| Question | Primary | Why |
|---|---|---|
| B1: does a warning test's INCONCLUSIVE block? | **(a′)**: keep blocking, with an INCONCLUSIVE split for reporting only ("warning-only gaps" / "fatal or gate gaps") | every verdict keeps its meaning; partial evidence is preserved by reporting; the option (e) (cap at WEAKENED) lowers the evidence a retained verdict needs and had no independent justification |
| B2: the background sub-check's alternative | **(a)**: keep it | none of the three increments (aperture, SAP, background) has a derivation on record, so none is singled out; (b) narrows what a PASS certifies and is ineligible until coupling controls exist |
| B3.1: the small-residual requirement | **keep** | it is part of the alternative's definition ("the sinusoid explains the dip") |
| B3.2: Fréchet or a joint probability | **Fréchet** | a valid conservative certificate; a joint model would need its own derivation and blind review |
| B3.3: the ambiguous-cause branch | **the frozen branch** (the evidence split a registered variant only) | its conditions were not accepted by the second blind review |

**The consequence (the second blind review, section 2):** that combination is behaviour-identical to
the frozen battery, plus a reporting label. A confirmation of it would re-test the frozen rules on
new draws; it opens no route for D1, D2, D4 or T2a. So R2 closes without a freeze, and there is no
R3.

## 2. B3.3, a registered variant, and what its promotion would need

The evidence split of the ambiguous branch (a PASS for "the sinusoid explains the dip" when the
residual is shown large) stays registered as a variant, never decisive. **Its promotion would need,
at least** (the second blind review, items 8 and 9):

- **One exact definition of `D_r` and `sigma_D`** (which components of the joint model are removed
  before the box contrast; a formula for `sigma_D` under that reading), and **the reference
  `max(D, D_r + k·sigma_D)`** in the "shown large" inequality, closing the loophole where a fitted
  sinusoid in anti-phase with the dip deflates `D_r`.
- **A VN family that leaves the fitted span** (the test also fits up to three extra frequencies, so
  harmonics 3 to 5 of the proposed VN can be absorbed), **plus an eclipse-with-phase-locked-modulation
  family** (an eclipsing binary with ellipsoidal or synchronized-spot variation at P or P/2, the
  population the evidence split would most likely turn into a PASS), and quasi-periodic and
  narrow-feature variability, each with a complete, reproducible specification (the star model,
  sizes, phase laws and conventions, draw order, seed mapping).
- **T3b and T2c** added to its dependency row (it can only add SURVIVED verdicts, and T3b counts known
  false positives SURVIVED with `sinusoid` decisive).

## 3. Lessons for any future version (the second blind review's blockers 2 to 7)

1. **The paired population and invalid pairs.** The paired population is the eligible curves; a
   "5 % invalid" rule covers only non-OK statuses, not ineligibility (a family whose gate pass rate is
   below 95 % would otherwise never be decidable); generation runs in index order to a frozen count of
   eligible pairs, with a frozen cap.
2. **A real-data paired reading needs its size first.** T2b's paired reading at about 100 real stars
   is structurally NOT DECIDABLE for a 0.02 margin (with zero discordant pairs the one-sided 95 % bound
   exceeds 0.02 until about 149 pairs); a rule that makes NOT DECIDABLE block adoption then blocks
   every change. Size the real set, or state a weaker exact rule ("no worsened pair") with its meaning,
   before the freeze.
3. **Freeze the test completely:** the ordering and algorithm; for a monotone change (one that can only
   turn INCONCLUSIVE into PASS), the exact binomial bound on `p_worse` (since `p_better = 0`); a power
   statement at a named alternative; the joint adoption power across all readings; and a justification
   of the margin (for example tied to D1's limit), beside the frozen "not above the earlier count".
4. **A code-hash lock** of the battery, of any new generator and of the analysis script, **before the
   first number of a new version and before the confirmation draw**, with a rule for defect fixes after
   numbers (a new version, or an approved and logged rerun).
5. **Dependencies are measured, not assumed:** every change that can add PASS verdicts lists T3b and
   T2c beside D1, D2, D4 and T2a.
6. **The tests that can emit FATAL are enumerated by name per battery version** (`secondary_eclipse`
   is WARNING for a candidate and FATAL without one; `period_alias` and `eb_catalog` are
   outcome-dependent), for any rule that scopes by severity.
7. **The confirmation's mechanics are pinned in advance:** the drand round number before it is emitted;
   the selection scripts and sizes for real targets; the exclusion list's hash; what a crash or a fetch
   failure allows; where the mandatory safeguards are read; the low power of a small real control set,
   stated beforehand.

The first blind review's lessons were applied in R2's revision (its ten items); the reviews, the
answers and the bundles' manifests are kept unchanged
([`v0.5.5-consensus/`](v0.5.5-consensus/README.md)).
