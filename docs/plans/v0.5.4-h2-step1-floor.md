# v0.5.4, second half, step 1: D2's joint floor and T2a's losses that a procedure cannot fix

**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (night), item 4a (#217): "passo 1 (piso
conjunto da D2 e as perdas não corrigíveis da classe (ii) da T2a): nos registros existentes,
divulgando que as margens já são conhecidas; um rascunho de inviabilidade só pode vir de sementes
novas."

The step adds Codex's conditions from the audit check
([`v0.5.4-audit-check.md`](v0.5.4-audit-check.md), step 1). `method_status: not_validated`.
Development data only. **This rule is frozen before any per-case overlap is read.**

## Disclosure: what is already known

The **marginal** counts are already published:
- D2: 8 ambiguous-sinusoid losses, 1 with too few transits, 3 REFUTED, 3 `period_alias`;
  I4's L = 2 and 6 planets below at the oracle on the fresh set.
- T2a: 20 `systematics` "not enough data" blockers and 42 grid refusals; I1's class-(ii) messages.

What is not known, and what this step reads, is how they **overlap per case**. Because the
marginals are known, any outcome here is partly predictable, so **no infeasibility draft comes
from these records**. A draft could only come from new seeds, under its own prospective rule, and
it is a non-delegated stop.

**Predictable before the reading** (Codex, `h2-step1` round 1):
- **T2a under B is exhausted under DATA whatever the overlaps.** Its 20 `systematics`
  "not enough data" blockers are in 20 distinct losses, more than the 7 allowed. That follows
  from the definitions and the published marginals; it is no new evidence of infeasibility.
- **D2 cannot reach F_strict > 22 with these marginals.** DATA (the `odd_even` case with too few
  transits, and D2's 1 `systematics` "not enough data") and I4's limits (2 and 6) add to fewer
  than 23 even if disjoint. What is new for D2 is in the unions and in F_upper.

**Integrity and joins.**
- A reader's refusal, a duplicated key, or an unexpected I1 `kind` is a stop.
- The reproduced decisive blockers per test must equal `b1-blockers-decisive.json`'s, for D2 and
  for T2a's B, or the step stops.
- A loss with no I1 or I4 record for a test that needs one gets **UNK** for that test.
- A T2a injection whose census record is unknown counts as a loss with an UNK blocker. It enters
  F_upper, never lowering the totals.

**The run** is `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -B` (no bytecode written), so nothing is written
outside the two outputs.

## The categories (a set per decisive blocker, and per test I1 classified)

**Each test gets the set of every category that applies to it**, not one: a reason that states a
procedural error and also no measurable transit is both PROC and MSG. So no cause hides another,
and no precedence is needed. A loss counts in a floor if any category of any of its blockers is
in the floor's set.

| Category | D2 | T2a |
|---|---|---|
| **DATA**: a data limit under the frozen rules | an INCONCLUSIVE with class `not_enough_data`; I1 class (ii) whose reason is too few measured transits | an INCONCLUSIVE with class `not_enough_data` |
| **MSG**: a message of no measurable event (Codex: not a proof by itself) | I1 class (ii) reasons "no transit measured …" | I1 class (ii) reasons "no transit measured in the background or SAP flux", "… in the PDCSAP flux …", "… in both …" |
| **RULE**: an outcome of a frozen rule, not of sensitivity | `sinusoid` INCONCLUSIVE in the ambiguous-cause branch: the record's `inconclusive_detail` is `AMBIGUOUS_SINUSOID`, or I1 class (ii) with the reason "does not establish the dip's cause". The class `insufficient_sensitivity` alone never decides it (`record-defects.md`, 1) | the same test on the T2a brief's own row; otherwise UNK |
| **INFO**: below the information bound under the generator's model | `odd_even` INCONCLUSIVE with I4 `power_nuisance` < 0.95 (valid); `sinusoid` INCONCLUSIVE with I4 `sinusoid_small_at_bound` < 0.95, the flag's power taken as 1 (so a lower bound on this category) | none: T2a has no information bound |
| **PROC**: a procedure or a domain a design could fix | a test row refused outside the grid; "error not measurable" | `odd_even` or `systematics` in a brief whose `selection.refused` is "outside the grid"; "error not measurable" |
| **FATAL**: a fatal FAIL (REFUTED) of the current estimator | the 3 REFUTED losses' FAILs | the 5 REFUTED losses' FAILs |
| **REC**: insufficient sensitivity where the bound allows power ≥ 0.95 | I1 class (i) with I4 valid and not INFO | — (no bound) |
| **UNK**: everything else | `period_alias`; I4 invalid (the E planets); I1 unresolved; a missing record; no class | I1 class (i) (no bound); `sinusoid` and `odd_even` sensitivity losses; `period_alias`; a missing record |

**A loss stays lost** if any one of its decisive blockers cannot be resolved: retention needs
every decisive test resolved. For a REFUTED loss, the tests I1 found hidden behind the fatal FAIL
also count, as they would block if the FAIL were removed.

## The counts and the outcomes (frozen)

For each target, three nested counts of losses, each the number of losses with at least one
blocker in the listed categories:
- **F_strict** = DATA ∪ INFO: what no estimator recovers, under the generator's model and the
  frozen rules;
- **F_rule** = DATA ∪ INFO ∪ RULE ∪ MSG: F_strict plus the frozen rules' own outcomes and the
  no-measurable-event messages taken at face value;
- **F_upper** = F_rule ∪ FATAL ∪ UNK: every loss not shown recoverable.

**F_upper is not a demonstrated upper bound on the unavoidable losses** (Codex, round 1). It
leaves out REC and PROC. Power that the bound allows, or a procedure that could be fixed, does not
show that those losses are recovered jointly. So a margin read from F_upper holds only **if every
REC and PROC loss is recovered**, and the outcome says so.

**D2.**
- Allowed: 178 − 156 = **22** losses, with the original denominator of 178. Planet 3129 stays in
  D2's 178; it is excluded only from I4's L.
- The outcome is:
  - **"a margin is left, if every REC and PROC loss is recovered (assumed)"** if F_upper ≤ 22;
  - **"the margin is exhausted under the stated assumptions"** if F_strict > 22;
  - otherwise **`INCONCLUSIVE`**, naming the counts at which it is exhausted.
- Each count is reported with the margin, 22 − F.

**T2a (configuration B).**
- Allowed: **7** losses (552 of 559). If the 5 REFUTED stay lost: **2** more.
- Scenario C, conditional on the selector's certification above 20 sectors (never a granted
  certification): **49**.
- The outcome is decided against 7:
  - **"exhausted under the stated assumptions"** if F_strict > 7, and separately if F_rule > 7
    (the message assumption, which Codex says the messages alone do not prove);
  - else **"margin left, if every REC and PROC loss is recovered (assumed)"** if F_upper ≤ 7;
  - otherwise **`INCONCLUSIVE`**.
- **The REFUTED scenario.** If the 5 REFUTED stay lost, only the floors counted **outside** the
  REFUTED losses are compared with 2. The REFUTED are never counted twice.
- **Scenario C:** the floors are compared with 49.
- The non-grid class-(ii) `systematics` blockers are tallied by their category sets.

**Never a draft.** "Exhausted" here is a reading of existing records under stated assumptions.
It is not an infeasibility record, and it changes no target, rule or verdict.

## The price

- **CPU:** below 0.1 CPU-h. The step reads JSON and shard files in one process; there is no pool
  and no driver part.
- **Tokens:** one Codex conversation for the design and the script (about 150,000).
- **Disk:** below 5 MB.
- **Agent time:** about 2h.

## What stops it

It stops, for the maintainer, in any of these cases:
- a reader's refusal, a sidecar mismatch, a duplicated key or an unexpected kind;
- T2a's I1 class-(ii) `systematics` blockers other than grid refusals not exactly 24;
- reproduced blockers differing from `b1-blockers-decisive.json`;
- the D2 population other than 178 eligible with 37 losses;
- T2a's B other than 559 with 228 losses;
- I1's or I4's reader refusing (a stop, a missing row, an open run).
