## 1. The question

Which route to an independent result has enough usable targets, at what cost and in what time,
to be worth a first pre-registered, locked result? The three routes are:
- **(e)**, a prospective audit of predicted transit windows in new TESS sectors;
- **(d1)**, KELT false positives against planets found outside TESS, in SPOC 2-minute data;
- **the Kepler field**, certified false positives against confirmed planets.

The phase also reproduces, by a committed script, the diagnosis on which those routes rest.

The phase answers with counts and a ranking, never with a scientific result. It runs no battery,
no evaluator and no search on any light curve.

## 2. Scope

**(a) Reproduce the diagnosis.**
- What: a committed script recomputes, from committed records only, every number of section 2 of
  the research ([fable-research.md](research-2026-11-23/fable-research.md), lines 20 to 79).
  - The three loss rows (C1 family P, C2 T2a, v0.5.6 part B planet arm) are recounted, with
    Codex's corrections: part B retention **23/100** and decidability **24/100**
    ([codex-answer.md](research-2026-11-23/codex-answer.md), line 27).
  - Also recounted: the INCONCLUSIVE classes of part B, the per-test pass rates and their product,
    the `systematics` counts, the SNR splits and the "what if" table.
- Output: one table, recording for each number the research's value, the recount and the
  difference.
- A difference is recorded, never explained away; no number is tuned.

**(b) Route (e), feasibility.**
- **Lists.** Three lists of predictions:
  - eumemic/exoplanets (`candidates/predictions.csv`, its commit and date recorded);
  - the ExoFOP CTOIs;
  - Refute's own candidates, the ephemerides of outputs already locked in committed records.
- **Per target, from public metadata only:**
  - whether it falls on silicon in each new sector;
  - the expected product (SPOC 2-minute target lists where published, otherwise FFI only);
  - the sector's dates;
  - the number of predicted transits inside the sector, with the prediction's stated
    uncertainty.
- **New sector.** A sector whose data are not public at the census date, per the official
  schedule (https://tess.mit.edu/observations/), with the date it was read.
- **Visibility source.** The sectors' public pointing tables and a committed geometry check with
  a declared margin, if they are enough.
  - The TESS-point Web Tool is a public web service, not code run here. Using it is logged, and
    so is each query.
  - Running `tess-point` (or any other third-party code) locally is a stop.
- **Design of the synthetic acceptance** of the directed (fixed-ephemeris) evaluator, pending
  since v0.2.1 (ROADMAP.md, "Directed mode" of the holdout;
  `docs/validation/v0.2.1/closing-2026-10-27.md`, lines 79 to 85).
  - The design specifies positives, nulls, aliases and correlated artifacts; control windows;
    timing uncertainty; minimum coverage; and the product cutoff.
  - **The design only:** nothing runs and nothing changes in `src/`.

**(c) Route (d1), census.**
- **The false-positive arm:** the KELT-FP catalog (Collins et al. 2018,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.01869), from its machine-readable table.
  - Its total of 1,128 is checked against the sum of its subtype counts, which the research
    gives as 1,099. The difference is reconciled from the table itself, or recorded as
    unresolved.
- **The planet arm:** planets whose discovery facility, in the NASA Exoplanet Archive, is not
  TESS. The arm is defined by that facility, not by the TFOPWG label `KP`.
- **Per target:**
  - whether SPOC 2-minute light curves exist in at least one sector (MAST metadata);
  - whether it is already exposed in any Refute record (a committed check against the selection
    frames and run records);
  - whether it is a TOI, and with which disposition.
- **Output:** the counts usable for a later claim (2-minute data, not exposed) in each arm, with
  the depth, period and magnitude support of each arm.

**(d) Kepler field, census.**
- **The two arms:**
  - KOIs with a certified false positive (the Certified False Positive table);
  - confirmed planets (the NASA Exoplanet Archive).
- **Per target:** the TESS products that exist: SPOC 2-minute, SPOC FFI light curves
  (TESS-SPOC) and TGLC.
- **Output:** the counts by product, by brightness (TESS magnitude bins) and by transit depth
  bins, declared in the script before it runs.

**(f) Engine trial** (exploratory, a new diagnostic under Process diet 2 item 4: it yields
quantities no recorded run yielded, is logged before it runs, and re-scores no recorded result).
Principle: measure first; keep what works, drop what does not, and only then incorporate.
- **Tools:** exoVetter (spacetelescope/exovetter) and TRICERATOPS (stevengiacalone/triceratops)
  as screen and statistical validation; wotan and transitleastsquares as detrending and search;
  LEO-Vetter (mkunimoto/LEO-Vetter) as an external comparator only, never imported into Refute's
  code.
- **Isolation:** one virtual environment under `.cache/v0510/engines/`, never `pyproject.toml`
  or `uv.lock`; each tool pinned to an exact version (PyPI with hashes, or a git commit) released
  at least 7 days before; the install commands and the resolved list with hashes committed;
  licences recorded in `docs/sources/` (URL, date, literal excerpt) before any run; GPL code runs
  as a separate process and is not distributed with Refute.
- **Targets:** only the 24 TOIs of v0.5.9 (already exposed); light curves and target pixel files
  fetched again from MAST and checked against the v0.5.9 manifests (SHA-256 where recorded).
- **Outputs per TOI and engine:** each metric and pass/fail with its threshold as published by
  the tool, coverage, and failures; a table beside Refute's v0.5.9 E and S classes and the TFOPWG
  disposition (with source and date). Questions written before the run:
  - G1, where Refute was INCONCLUSIVE, does each engine decide?
  - G2, do the engines agree with each other?
  - G3, how do they relate to the TFOPWG disposition (description only, never a verdict)?
  - G4, cost per TOI and per engine.
  - G5, what each engine does when a metric cannot be computed (pass, fail, missing), recorded
    per metric.
- **Labels:** nothing here is a verdict, a candidate or a claim; outputs carry 'exploratory', the
  v0.5 limits label where Refute's records are used, and `method_status: not_validated`.
- **Caps:** for the whole phase, 6 CPU-h, 10 GB of downloads, 10 GB disk peak (caps ledger above
  5 CPU-h); section 3.
- **Incorporation plan (deliverable):** for each tool and metric, the report states whether it
  decides where Refute was INCONCLUSIVE, whether it is worth keeping or should be dropped, and
  how it could enter battery version 5:
  - permissive licences (MIT, BSD-3, Apache-2.0) adapted into Refute's own code with the
    copyright notice and licence kept under a third-party folder;
  - GPL or unlicensed code never copied, the method reimplemented from its published paper
    instead;
  - every adapted metric made deterministic, with declared coverage, zero coverage as
    INCONCLUSIVE, and synthetic tests.

  The plan is a proposal for the version-5 phase; nothing enters `src/` in v0.5.10.
- **Its place among the rules of this charter** (the same decision): for the tools listed above
  and the 24 TOIs only, running third-party code for the first time is approved, in the isolated
  environment, and fetching their light curves and target pixel files is part of this phase's
  scope. The data rules below and the stops of section 7 hold for everything else. One Codex
  round on part (f) and its isolation (supply chain, licences, exposure) comes before the first
  run; a blocking item is applied without a new round, and a change of scope is a stop.

**Data rules.**
- **Allowed:** public catalogs and metadata only.
- **Not allowed:** no light curve, target pixel file or FFI cut-out is downloaded, and no flux is
  read.
- **Sources:** every external fact carries its URL and the date it was read.
- **Licences:** each catalog's licence or terms of use is marked **not confirmed** until a
  `sources/` record holds its URL, retrieval date and a literal excerpt (CLAUDE.md, rule of
  issue #34).

**Exposure.**
- **What does not expose a target:** reading a catalog row, a product listing or a pointing table.
  None of these reads a target's photometry, runs a test on it or produces an output for it.
- **What exposes a target:** looking at its data, which is out of this charter's scope.
- **If the census needs more:** it may define a named pool of new targets (Process diet 2,
  item 4). The pool is logged with its reason before it is used, and everything in it is recorded
  as exposed and unusable for confirmation.
- **No such pool is needed** for the census as written above.

**No rerun.** No execution gives a recorded case a new value of an output it already has. The
recounts of (a) read recorded values; they are verification, not reruns.

## 3. Resource caps

| Resource | Cap |
|---|---|
| CPU | 6 CPU-h |
| Downloads | 10 GB |
| Disk peak | 10 GB |
| Time box | 7 calendar days from the maintainer's approval of this charter |

- **Below a cap:** the phase runs within these caps, which include part (f). Above 5 CPU-h the
  caps ledger applies (Process diet 2, item 4). The runner records the CPU, the bytes downloaded
  and the disk peak in the phase log.
- **Reaching a cap:** the work stops for the maintainer; a cap is never raised by the runner.
- **Machine rules:**
  - processors 0 to 7 stay free;
  - at most 10 workers and 2 downloads in business hours, and 20 and 6 outside them;
  - BelowNormal priority;
  - batches of at most 30 minutes, every execution resumable.

## 4. Records

- **The log.** One append-only log per phase, `docs/validation/v0.5.10/log.jsonl`:
  - before each run: the question and the choices, with a machine timestamp;
  - after it: the provenance, the coverage and every outcome, including failures and abandoned
    attempts.
- **Scripts and outputs** go in `docs/validation/v0.5.10/`. A raw output larger than 5 MB never
  enters git: it is archived to the Drive with a SHA-256 manifest.
- **The report.** One phase report, `docs/reports/v0.5.10-report.md`, with an append-only errata
  log, `docs/validation/v0.5.10/errata.md`.
- **Records already approved, published, locked or hashed** are never edited. A correction is a
  separate dated file.
- **Cleanup.** Inside `.cache`, by literal path with a committed record (the rule of #220's
  finding).

## 5. Exit criteria (each checkable from the repository)

1. **The diagnosis is recounted.** A committed script and its output table reproduce every number
   of section 2 of the research, with Codex's corrections, or record each difference. Running the
   script again from a clean checkout gives the same table, byte for byte.
2. **Route (e) is counted.**
   - The output: a committed table per list (eumemic, CTOIs, Refute's own), with the new sectors
     considered and, per target, on silicon (yes, no or unknown), the expected product, the
     sector dates and the predicted transits in the sector.
   - The totals: per list, the targets with at least one predicted transit on silicon, and how
     many of them have expected 2-minute data.
   - Every source carries its URL and the date it was read.
3. **The directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance is designed.**
   - The design document answers the items of section 2 (b).
   - It names the evidence that the v0.2.1 closure found missing.
   - It changes nothing in `src/` and runs nothing.
4. **Route (d1) is counted.**
   - The KELT-FP total is reconciled, or the difference is recorded with the table's own counts.
   - Each arm's counts: total; with SPOC 2-minute data; not exposed; usable (both). Also the TOI
     overlap and the support in depth, period and magnitude.
5. **The Kepler field is counted.**
   - Each arm's counts by TESS product, brightness bin and depth bin, with the bins declared
     before the run in the log.
6. **The routes are ranked.**
   - The short report ranks (e), (d1) and Kepler by usable targets, independence, cost, and time
     to the first locked result, and recommends the first.
   - The recommendation states what would change it.
7. **The records are complete.**
   - The log covers every run.
   - CPU, downloads and disk peak are within the caps.
   - Every catalog's licence status is recorded, as "not confirmed" unless a `sources/` record
     exists.
   - Nothing in `src/`, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock` changed.
8. **The gate is reviewed.** A short gate review, by a fresh Codex session given only the dossier
   (section 6), is stored unchanged.

9. **The engine trial is run and recorded** (part (f)).
   - Isolation: the install commands and the resolved list with hashes are committed, every tool
     at an exact version or commit at least 7 days old, and each licence recorded in
     `docs/sources/` before the first run.
   - The 24 TOIs' light curves and target pixel files are checked against the v0.5.9 manifests,
     with every mismatch or missing hash recorded.
   - The per-TOI, per-engine table holds each metric, its pass or fail with the tool's published
     threshold, coverage and failures, beside Refute's v0.5.9 E and S classes and the TFOPWG
     disposition (with source and date); G1 to G5 are answered from it.
10. **The incorporation plan is written.** For each tool and metric: whether it decides where
    Refute was INCONCLUSIVE, keep or drop, and the way into version 5 under part (f)'s licence
    rules. Nothing in `src/` changed.

At the time box's end, each criterion is recorded as met, failed or not run.

## 6. Reviews

- **Codex, one round** (exploratory work), on the scripts and the report together, before the
  gate. A blocking finding is fixed or recorded; divergence ends by the most conservative option,
  recorded.
- **The gate** (Process diet 2, item 6): one fresh, read-only Codex session (a new thread, which
  has seen no other thread of the phase).
  - It receives only a clean checkout of the pushed SHA, the gate-review protocol and the phase
    name.
  - Its review is stored unchanged, with the model, the thread id, the prompt and the SHA.
  - A `WEAKENED` gate has every issue tracked; a `REFUTED` gate closes nothing.
- **No Claude subagent** without the maintainer's approval.

## 7. Stops (work halts for the maintainer)

These add to every stop of Process diet 2, item 2, and remove none.
- **Third-party code:** running any third-party code for the first time, including `tess-point`.
  The public pointing tables, or the TESS-point Web Tool used as a web service, come first.
- **Dependencies:** any change to them.
- **`src/`:** any change in it.
- **Public text:** README, the site, a release or an announcement.
- **Scope:** any work beyond this charter. That includes reading light curves or pixels, defining
  a target pool, and reaching a cap.
- **The end of the phase:** the report's approval and the close are the maintainer's.
- **Never in this phase:** locks, drand rounds, reruns, Zenodo, and changes to old targets.
