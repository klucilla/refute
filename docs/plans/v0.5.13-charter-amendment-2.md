## 3. Any other thread setting: decision identity

### 3.1 The comparison (Codex round 1, item 1)

> For each alternate setting, verify the exact golden file inventory and the SHA-256 of each raw
> file against its recorded payload. Compare the raw bytes in order. The frozen comparator may
> treat only schema-identified numeric value tokens and explicitly mapped numeric measurement
> spans in rendered reports as numeric. Require identical field locations, token counts, and all
> bytes outside those spans, including line endings. Compare IDs, paths, versions, integer
> counts, configuration, thresholds, and error records exactly. Reject malformed, missing, extra,
> ambiguous, or nonfinite numeric values. For each paired finite measurement, require
> `abs(a - b) <= 0.000001 * max(abs(a), abs(b))`, using exact decimal values from the serialized
> tokens, with zero equal only to zero.
>
> Require the exact protected claim inventory and identical resolved SHA-256 values. Preserve the
> existing `calibration/v0.5/deviation/claim.generated.yaml` error record byte for byte as a
> baseline exception. It supplies no claim hash. Require joint PASS for all ten expected
> locked-claim checks at the setting being certified, with the exact tag and claim identities and
> evidence of the setting in the verifying execution. Missing per-setting evidence leaves that
> setting unmet. Any additional verification requires its own authorization and manifest.

### 3.2 The tolerance (Codex round 1, item 2)

The earlier justification (a summation error bound with an assumed condition number) is
**withdrawn**: it did not bound the relative error of small, cancellation-prone outputs, and its
inputs were not established. In its place:

> Fix the relative tolerance at `1e-6`, a one-part-per-million engineering reproducibility budget
> for each finite reported measurement. Its purpose is to bound tolerated change in output values
> even when the decision agrees. The independent margin check separately requires clearance from
> the decision cutoffs. The scientific thresholds retain their exact values and comparison
> operators. This budget provides no floating-point error guarantee.
>
> NR-A1 and D1a, including the observed maximum relative difference of approximately `2.12e-9`,
> were known when this budget was proposed. This is a post-hoc acceptance amendment. Submit the
> one-part-per-million budget for explicit judgment on its engineering purpose and freeze it with
> the amendment before any acceptance comparison. A failed check leaves #226 unmet. Changing the
> budget requires a new dated rule proposal and fresh blind review.

The first amendment draft proposed `1e-6`; the value is not changed in response to the observed
drift.

### 3.3 The margin check (Codex round 1, item 3)

> The margin manifest covers each golden fixture, stage, test, and subcheck. Enumerate every
> operative numeric predicate that can determine a test status, eligibility for that status, or
> the aggregate verdict. Include numeric coverage and count cutoffs, compound predicates, and
> intermediate cutoffs that select the model used by a decision. Trace them from the frozen
> source and resolved plan, regardless of which fields D1a found changed. Include each
> potentially applicable predicate and record source-backed evidence for any unevaluated branch.
> A branch suppressed by short-circuiting is inapplicable only when the controlling predicate and
> its margin are established.
>
> Before running the acceptance comparator, freeze and hash the fixture list, source and resolved
> plans, raw input artifacts, comparator, and complete manifest. Each row specifies the case and
> predicate, source location, comparison operator, units, and exact operand locations or
> reconstruction formulas. Examples include `difference_sigma` versus `max_sigma`, harmonic
> significance versus `min_significance`, harmonic semi-amplitude versus
> `min_amplitude_ratio * candidate.depth`, every attempted extra peak's SNR versus
> `extra_min_snr`, and the v4 residual-box comparison. Evaluate full-precision operands with the
> source's evaluation order and resolved thresholds.
>
> In both the reference and comparison setting, require finite operands `x` and `t`, identical
> predicate outcomes, and `abs(x - t) > 0.000001 * max(abs(x), abs(t))`. Equality fails,
> including `x = t = 0`. For a case-derived threshold, evaluate both sides independently in each
> setting. If either side is computed from serialized values, establish that serialization
> uncertainty cannot change the predicate or the margin result. A missing, ambiguous, unpaired,
> or unreconstructable operand leaves the check unmet. Report the predicate, both operand pairs,
> margins, and outcome. Supply missing operands only through a separately approved measurement
> with a frozen manifest and order proof.

**Supplemental operands** (Codex round 2, item 5):

> Where a historical golden omits a required operand, any separately approved supplemental
> execution must capture the trace and its corresponding golden outputs together. Freeze the
> case, scientific source, resolved configuration, numerical environment and trace
> instrumentation in the order-proof manifest. Use the trace to assess a historical golden only
> when its corresponding raw output matches the historical output byte for byte at that setting.
> Apply this binding to the reference and each comparison setting. A missing trace or mismatch
> leaves the historical check unmet. Preserve all original records and report supplemental
> evidence separately.

**Consequence, stated plainly:** the saved goldens do not hold every operand (for example, a
rejected extra peak's SNR is not serialized). The margin check for 1 and 4 threads, and for
ID-BEFORE's side, therefore needs a separately approved measurement under the rule above before
it can pass; until then it is unmet.

## 4. Version 5 onward: one BLAS thread per worker process

### 4.1 The runner contract (Codex round 1, item 4)

> For every nonempty v5+ batch, `run_processes_pinned` partitions the indexed tasks into
> `min(workers, len(tasks))` nonempty shards and launches one fresh Python interpreter per shard.
> Pass an explicit child environment with the frozen thread variables forced to `"1"` at OS
> process creation. Preserve the caller's environment. Use this path for a single task and for
> `workers=1`. The worker entry point starts without task-module imports and checks the launch
> variables before importing the numerical stack or deserializing any task.
>
> At startup, explicitly load the approved NumPy and SciPy numerical paths, including
> `scipy.linalg`, and any other supported task-specific BLAS providers. Compare the inventory with
> the frozen backend and library manifest for that environment. Refuse if a required backend is
> missing, an unexpected backend appears, a count is unreadable, or any effective BLAS count
> differs from one. Recheck after loading each task module and before invoking it. Recheck before
> accepting the task result. An unsupported or unverifiable stack fails the batch.
>
> Each child reports its own startup check through the worker protocol, with the run and shard
> IDs, PID, launch variables, backend and library identity, version, threading layer, and
> effective counts. Bind that PID to the child the runner launched and to every task index
> returned by the child. A missing, duplicate, or mismatched startup record, incomplete shard, or
> child failure rejects the batch. Preserve task order. Persist the startup records and
> task-to-worker mapping in run evidence outside the golden payload. Record failures without
> accepting the affected batch. An empty batch records zero workers.
>
> Enable this entry point only for explicitly selected discovery versions 5 onward. Preserve the
> existing runner, dispatch, and environment behavior for versions 1 through 4. The v5 acceptance
> fixture uses the actual version-gated entry point and checks persistence of its worker evidence.

**The required implementation contract** (Codex round 2, item 1):

> Each call creates an exclusive batch directory under the supplied work directory. The protocol
> carries a run ID, batch ID, shard index and one-time shard nonce. Use JSON for the launch header
> and for startup, refusal and result-index metadata. Read the task pickle only after writing the
> verified startup record. Restrict task and result pickles to trusted local inputs and children
> in that batch directory.
>
> The parent requires exactly one valid startup record per launched child. Its run ID, batch ID,
> shard index and nonce must match the launch record, and its PID must equal `proc.pid`. Require
> the exact launch variables, a complete verified library inventory, exit status zero, and the
> exact ordered shard task indexes, each attached to the same PID. Require distinct child PIDs
> and full task coverage. Decode results only after validating the corresponding metadata.
>
> Persist startup and failure evidence independently of successful results. A missing or
> malformed record, refusal, launch failure, or partial result rejects the batch with
> `BlasPinError`. On orchestration failure, terminate and reap any children still running. Accept
> no result from an incomplete batch.

**The required launch and inventory contract** (Codex round 2, item 2):

> Launch the isolated interpreter from the frozen v5 environment. Bind its executable, installed
> Refute artifact, worker module and approved task modules to the reviewed source and artifact
> hashes. Verify the resolved installed code before dispatch and include its identity in the
> child evidence. An isolated environment containing a verified installation of the proposed
> package can exercise the patch from any checkout.
>
> Pass the pin variables at process creation. At worker entry, before reading the task payload or
> loading the numerical stack, verify the variables and refuse an environment that has already
> imported the numerical stack during startup. Load NumPy and `scipy.linalg` explicitly. Require a
> nonempty frozen manifest that identifies the approved BLAS provider for each numerical path and
> the expected loaded libraries. Bind each reported library by backend, prefix, version, threading
> layer and binary identity. Refuse an absent, unexpected or unverifiable library, or any count
> other than integer one. Recheck after task deserialization and before and after every task.
>
> The initial supported numerical paths are NumPy and SciPy. Before accepting a v5 task path, bind
> its numerical providers in the manifest. If it needs another provider, extend and verify the
> manifest before accepting that path.

**What revision 2's patches do NOT yet do** (round 2's findings; the code must be revised to the
two contracts above before any blind review):
- the parent records `proc.pid` as `launcher_pid` but never compares it with the PID the child
  reports;
- an empty `startup` inventory is accepted, and `check([])` passes on an empty inventory;
- the shard files have fixed names, so overlapping batches can overwrite each other; there is no
  run or batch ID;
- on a nonzero exit the child's refusal record is not read, failure evidence is not kept, and
  malformed output can raise an exception other than `BlasPinError`; children are not reaped on
  orchestration failure;
- the header is a pickle, not plain-data JSON;
- the library identity omits the threading layer and the binary's identity;
- the installed package, worker module and executable are not verified against reviewed hashes,
  and a numerical import during site startup is not refused.

**What revision 2's patches do** (kept for the record):
- `run_processes_pinned` refuses discovery versions below 5. It launches
  `sys.executable -I -B -m refute.core.pinned_worker <in> <out>` once per shard, with an explicit
  environment in which `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS` and
  `NUMEXPR_NUM_THREADS` are `"1"`; the caller's `os.environ` is not modified.
- The worker checks those variables before any numerical import and before reading its tasks. It
  reads a plain-data header (a per-shard nonce and the frozen library manifest), loads NumPy and
  `scipy.linalg`, and compares the `threadpoolctl` inventory (internal API, prefix, version) with
  the manifest. It refuses a missing or unexpected library and any count other than one. It
  checks again after unpickling its tasks, after each task, and before returning each result.
- The runner accepts a shard only with its nonce, its startup record, the exact task indexes of
  that shard in order, and one PID for all of them. It requires distinct PIDs across shards and
  full task coverage. Otherwise it raises `BlasPinError` and accepts nothing of the batch.
- It returns `(results, evidence)`; the evidence holds one startup record per child and the
  task-to-PID map. It has no version-gated v5 entry point and no durable evidence sink (section
  5).
