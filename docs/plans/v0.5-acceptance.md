# v0.5 acceptance documents

Written on 2026-11-03, **before any v0.5 code** ([plan](v0.5-plan.md)). For each work package:
what is accepted, the sensitivity rule of each test, and the synthetic cases that must pass and
must fail. Every case runs offline, on synthetic data or recorded fixtures (no test depends on
the network). Counts come from a JUnit XML file. Section C1 freezes the design of the synthetic
population study before any development result exists.

**Revisions.** Revision 1 (2026-11-03) applies the corrections A to H of Codex's third review of
the first text (the one revision before code that the plan's stop rule allows). Codex's
end-of-round review of the same day found A to F and H applied and G only in part: the C1
generator was not yet deterministic (the depth from the target SNR was circular, partial windows
and some families' geometry were undefined). Revision 2 (2026-11-03) completes G in section C1
only, before any C1 code; it changes no test, threshold, rule or decision rule.

## Conventions

- **Seeds.** Development seeds: `int(SHA-256("refute-v05-dev:<case>:<i>")[:16], 16)`. Acceptance
  seeds (unseen while building): `int(SHA-256("refute-v05-accept:<case>:<i>")[:16], 16)`. A test
  that reads an acceptance seed before its WP's code is frozen is a deviation, recorded in the
  tracking issue. C1 confirmation seeds come from a future drand round (section C1).
- **Binomial bands.** "FAILs at least at rate p" over N trials means a FAIL count at or above the
  lower 2.5 % quantile of Binomial(N, p). "False-failure rate at most a" means a FAIL count at or
  below the upper 97.5 % quantile of Binomial(N, a). Unless stated, N = 200 per case.
- **Noise models of the cases.** White: Gaussian. Correlated: the AR(1) series of
  `synthetic.ar1_series` with `red_noise_ppm` equal to half the white level and `tau` of one hour,
  plus a variant with a stellar trend. Injections enter before detrending.
- **"Detectable by a margin"** in a case means that the case's errors are set so that the test's
  sensitivity rule (WP1) holds; "below the rule" means that they are set so that it does not.
- **Claim schema.** Every new field and test exists only in a v0.5 claim schema
  (`discovery.version: 2`). Historical claims (v0.1 to v0.4) give exactly their recorded outputs.
- A case passes when its "Must" holds. A **sabotage** check names a patched version of the code
  (applied inside the test, never committed) and the case it must make fail.

## WP1: sensitivity rule (#185)

**Accepted when** the engine turns every PASS without the declared sensitivity into
INCONCLUSIVE, each fatal and warning test of the v0.5 battery has a registered sensitivity rule
or applicability category for every applicable sub-check, and the rules meet their cases.

**Engine.** `TestResult` gains an optional `sensitivity` block: either a rule
(`effect`, `alternative`, `unit`, `rule_id`, `probability`, `required_probability`, `detectable`)
or an applicability category (`category`, `reason`). A composite test (several sub-checks)
carries one block per sub-check. In a v0.5 claim, `enforce_sensitivity` runs after
`enforce_coverage`:
- a PASS of a fatal or warning test becomes INCONCLUSIVE when any applicable sub-check's block is
  missing, malformed (a missing key, `detectable` not a boolean, a non-finite number), says
  `detectable: false`, or declares a category that is not the one registered for that test in
  the code's registry;
- a statistical test can never obtain a PASS by declaring a category: the registry fixes, per
  test and sub-check, whether it is a rule or a category;
- a FAIL is never changed (a detected failure stands, whatever the sensitivity to other effects);
- the gate `snr` is exempt; the aggregation rules of `docs/verdicts.md` do not change.

**Categories** (registered per test; a PASS still needs positive coverage):
`deterministic-bound` (a computed bound from catalog inputs with their stated uncertainty:
`duty_cycle`; both branches of `plausibility`, radius and duration, INCONCLUSIVE when an input is
missing, as today), `catalog-lookup` (`eb_catalog`, `nearby_contamination`; coverage = rows
scanned), `fraction` (the momentum-dump sub-check of `systematics`).

**How a rule is computed.** Against the test's **full rejection region** (every condition of its
FAIL, for example significance and ratio together), under a declared **alternative strictly
beyond any effect-size boundary**:
- *sufficient-statistic simulation* (the default): the test's statistics are drawn from their
  declared error model (the measured errors, covariances where they exist) under the
  alternative, 2000 draws with a seed derived from the test name and the candidate; the full
  rejection rule is applied; `probability` = the fraction rejected; detectable iff
  `probability >= 0.95`. For a single Gaussian statistic with a one-sided threshold this equals
  the closed form `alternative / sigma >= threshold + z_power`, which may be used instead;
- *where events or sectors have unequal errors*, the alternative is placed at the **worst eligible
  location** (the event or sector whose placement gives the lowest probability);
- *full-data simulation* where the statistics are not simple (`period_alias`).

| Test (sub-check) | Rejection region (v0.5) | Alternative | Computation |

## WP2: signal versus noise (#184, #193)

**Accepted when** the three tests decide their cases with their sensitivity, a real transit is
not refuted for its nuisance, and the transformed-curve comparison appears in the case file and
report.

- `single_event` (fatal). Domain: at least 3 **covered, measured event windows** (the events of
  `per_event_depths`), else INCONCLUSIVE. Statistic: the largest single-event SNR over the total
  SNR. FAIL if above 0.88.
- `event_consistency` (fatal). Chi-square of the per-event depths about their weighted mean, with
  the red-noise error model; at least 3 measured events; FAIL if p < 0.00135. Depths are on the
  PDCSAP flux, already corrected for each sector's dilution.
- `edge_events` (warning). An event is near an edge when its mid-center lies within 2 durations
  of a segment edge or of a gap of at least 0.3 day. When at least half the measured events are
  near edges, the depth from the other events is compared with the depth from the edge events:
  FAIL (warning) if they differ at 3 sigma; INCONCLUSIVE if the other events give an SNR below
  `min_snr`. Otherwise PASS, with the fraction reported.
- **Transformed-curve comparison:** the candidate's SNR beside the largest gate SNR among the
  star's transformed curves, in the case file and report; descriptive, labelled "not a
  false-alarm rate".

|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | a planet with N equal events, N = 6, 20 and 40, SNR 12, detectable by a margin | `single_event` and `event_consistency`: false-failure rate at most 0.0027 |
| 2.2 | one 4-hour box dip on one window plus N − 1 other covered, measured windows with no dip (N = 6, 20, 40), the dip's SNR set so the rule is detectable by a margin | `single_event` FAILs at least at rate 0.95 |
| 2.3 | a planet (SNR 20, 6 events) plus one event deepened by a full depth, detectable by a margin | `event_consistency` FAILs at least at rate 0.95 |
| 2.4 | (a) 2 measured events; (b) one dip and only one covered window | INCONCLUSIVE in both tests |

| 2.6 | a spurious dip made only by edge ramps | `edge_events` FAIL (warning) or INCONCLUSIVE; never PASS |
| 2.7 | coexistence: a real transit with unequal event coverage (two partial events) | `event_consistency` false-failure rate at most 0.0027 |
| 2.8 | the comparison | present in `case.json` and `report.md` with the label |

## WP3: variability (#182, #181)

**Accepted when** a brightening at phase 0.5 is never a PASS, the sinusoid test flags strong
variability at the candidate's period, a real transit on a variable star is not refuted, and the
duty-cycle bound refutes impossible geometries.

- `secondary_eclipse`, two-sided, **warning only in v0.5**. A dip at `max_sigma`: FAIL (warning),
  with its depth ratio reported. A brightening at `max_sigma` and with an amplitude of at least
  0.1 of the primary depth: FAIL (warning), "brightening at phase 0.5: possible variability".
  The v0.4 fatal rule for a deep dip stays only in the historical compatibility path: a
  secondary's depth ratio alone does not show incompatibility with a planet while the
  occultation model is deferred (#181 stays open). The report states the limits: eccentric
  secondaries away from phase 0.5 are not covered, and a secondary alone does not classify an
  eclipsing binary.
- `sinusoid`, **warning only in v0.5**. On the normalized, undetrended PDCSAP flux, with the
  transits masked (one duration on each side), a sinusoid is fitted at P/2, P and 2P (weighted
  least squares, errors inflated by the red-noise factor). FAIL (warning) if, for P < 10 days,
  the amplitude significance of any of the three exceeds 15 and its semi-amplitude is at least
  half the depth. The box depth refitted after subtracting that sinusoid is reported; its
  non-detection is not evidence that variability explains the signal, so it never escalates the
  severity (a joint model comparison is deferred with #182).
- `duty_cycle` (fatal; category `deterministic-bound`). FAIL if duration over period exceeds 0.5;
  the circular-orbit ratio stays the `plausibility` warning.

## WP4: centroid per sector (#183, #190, #187)

**Accepted when** one sector cannot decide a fatal FAIL, the per-sector qualification cannot hide
a genuine off-target source, and the report words the result as a measurement.

- **Per-sector offset.** The source position follows `source_offset` (v0.4) in pixel coordinates.
  `PixelData` gains the sector's pixel-to-sky Jacobian (from the file's WCS, east and north per
  pixel); the offset and its 2 x 2 covariance are converted to arcsec east and north with it.
  A sector without a readable WCS does not qualify.
- **Control measurement.** In each sector, the **centroid shifts** (x and y, in pixels) are
  measured at 20 out-of-transit pseudo-epochs of the candidate's duration, at phases drawn with a
  seed in [0.15, 0.35] and [0.65, 0.85] (excluding the primary and phase-0.5 windows by at least
  one duration), never overlapping each other or a real event. Their scatter is the empirical
  noise of a shift. The shift error used is `max(formal error, empirical scatter)`: the
  empirical scatter is the total measurement noise of a shift (not an added component), so
  nothing is counted twice. It is propagated with the **actual transit depth**, as in
  `source_offset`; no pseudo-epoch depth is divided by. A sector with fewer than 10 valid
  control epochs does not qualify.
- **Qualification.** A sector qualifies when the transit is detected in its aperture flux (as in
  v0.4), it has a WCS and a CROWDSAP value, and its aperture depth agrees, at 3 sigma, with the
  light-curve depth times that sector's CROWDSAP (the dilution the PDCSAP flux removed). Missing
  dilution or WCS: not qualified. A non-qualifying sector is reported with its offset. If half or
  more of the sectors with pixel data do not qualify, the result is INCONCLUSIVE
  ("qualification failed"), never PASS.
- **Heterogeneity.** Chi-square of the qualified sky offsets about their weighted mean, with
  their covariances; p < 0.00135: INCONCLUSIVE ("sectors disagree").
- **Decision.** FAIL (fatal) if at least two sectors qualify, the sectors agree, the combined
  offset is at least `max_sigma` and at least `min_offset_pixels` (in arcsec at the TESS pixel
  scale). One qualified sector: INCONCLUSIVE ("single sector"), even with a significant offset.
  PASS only with the sensitivity of WP1.
- **Wording.** FAIL: "transit source offset X arcsec (Y px) from the target, Z sigma, from N
  sectors"; no cause. In every report next to the pixel tests: "a PASS does not exclude
  unresolved blends closer than <minimum offset>".

| # | Case | Must |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | an off-target eclipsing source 2 px away in 3 sectors with different orientations, detectable by a margin | FAIL fatal at least at rate 0.95 |
| 4.2 | the same source with one sector of data | INCONCLUSIVE "single sector" |
| 4.3 | an on-target planet in 3 sectors, one with an artifact (a brightening near one transit) shifting its offset | never a fatal FAIL |
| 4.4 | an off-target source placed so that every sector fails qualification | INCONCLUSIVE "qualification failed", never PASS (the guard) |
| 4.5 | an on-target planet in 3 clean sectors, detectable by a margin | false-failure rate at most 0.0027; PASS with sensitivity otherwise |

| 4.7 | a sector without WCS; without CROWDSAP | not qualified, reported |
| 4.8 | control epochs | never overlap a primary or phase-0.5 window or each other; fewer than 10 valid: not qualified |

**Sabotage:** removing the two-sector requirement makes 4.2 FAIL; removing the qualification guard

## C1: synthetic population study (design frozen on 2026-11-03)

### Generator

- **Sectors.** Each sector is `synthetic.sector_times(start)`: two 13.0-day orbits separated by a
  1.0-day gap, two-minute cadence. A star has 2, 3 or 4 sectors (equal probability), except PA
  (always 3). Sector starts: `2000.0 + 27.4 x j` BTJD, with the j drawn without replacement from
  0 to 29 and sorted (consecutive and multi-year spacings both occur).
- **Noise.** White Gaussian, sigma log-uniform in [300, 3000] ppm per point. Correlated noise
  (AR(1), `red_noise_ppm` = half the white level, tau one hour) in half the stars and a stellar
  trend in half the stars, drawn independently, except **NW** (white only, no trend) and **NR**
  (correlated, no trend). Trend: a sinusoid of amplitude uniform in [0, 2000] ppm, period
  uniform in [3, 10] days, random phase.
- **Star.** Radius 1.0 +- 0.05 R_sun, mass 1.0 M_sun, logg 4.44, Tmag 10.
- **Transit profile.** `synthetic.trapezoid`: radius ratio k = sqrt(depth) (no limb darkening);
  a/R_* from Kepler's third law for the star (mass 1.0, radius 1.0); total duration
  `T(k) = P/pi asin(min(1, sqrt((1 + k)^2 - b^2) / (a/R_*)))`; flat duration
  `T_flat(k) = P/pi asin(min(1, sqrt(max(0, (1 - k)^2 - b^2)) / (a/R_*)))` (zero for a V
  shape); ingress fraction `(T - T_flat) / (2 T)`. Epoch t0 uniform in the first period after
  the first sector's start.
- **Windows and expected SNR.** For a duration T, a transit window is the interval of length T
  around a predicted mid-time; it counts when it holds at least half of the cadences a full
  window would hold (the `min_coverage` of `per_event_depths`). For each counted window i with
  `n_i` cadences, `var_i = sigma_w^2 / n_i + sigma_r^2 f(T / tau)`, with
  `f(x) = 2 (x - 1 + exp(-x)) / x^2` (the variance of the mean of an AR(1) process over x
  correlation times) and `sigma_r = 0` without correlated noise. The expected SNR of a depth d
  is `d / sqrt(1 / sum_i (1 / var_i))` (inverse-variance weighting, so partial windows weigh
  less).
- **Depth from the target SNR** (deterministic, no search): start from `k_0 = 0` (the duration
  of a point planet); at step j, compute `T(k_j)`, the windows and `sigma_tot(T)`, set
  `d_{j+1} = SNR_target sigma_tot` and `k_{j+1} = sqrt(d_{j+1})`; stop when
  `|d_{j+1} - d_j| <= 1e-6 d_{j+1}`. If it has not converged after 50 steps, or no window
  counts, or `(1 + k)^2 <= b^2` (no transit), the star is a **generation failure**: recorded
  with its seed and reason, counted outside the eligible set, never redrawn.
- **Per-family geometry.** Every family that holds an eclipse takes its period, impact
  parameter, epoch and durations from the rules above, with the family's own parameters below;
  a depth chosen "by the target-SNR rule" is solved by the fixed point above.

### Sample-size rule

200 stars per family, one curve each, except PA and PX (100 each, the pixel cost): 2,400 curves.
Both batteries attack the same candidate of each curve. Before the confirmation run, the cost is
projected from the CPU time per curve measured on the development prefix only (the first 20
development seeds of each family); if the projection exceeds 30 processor-hours, every family's
size is halved, by this rule and before the run, never after.

### Seeds

Confirmation seeds: `int(SHA-256(<drand randomness> || "refute-v05-c1:<family>:<i>")[:16], 16)`,
from a drand round declared in the protocol (S2) and published after the protocol commit.

### Matching, denominators and metrics

- **Identity** (P, PG, PV, PA): the shared candidate matches the true signal when its period is
  within 1 % of the true period and its epoch, folded at the true period, within half the true
  duration of the true epoch. Candidates at another period (aliases included) and missed
  injections are reported apart, never as kept planets.
- **Eligible set.** For the planet families: curves whose shared candidate passes the gate and
  matches the identity. For the other families: curves whose shared candidate passes the gate.
  Each paired comparison uses the same eligible set for both batteries.
- **Counts.** Per family and battery: the four verdict counts in the eligible set, the number
  outside it (by reason), Wilson 95 % intervals, the paired transition table. For the
  non-planet families, SURVIVED and SURVIVED + WEAKENED are both reported.

### Decision rules (the C1 self-claim)

Each rule is reported **PASS, FAIL or NOT DECIDABLE**. NOT DECIDABLE (an eligible set with fewer
than 20 curves, counted inside the rule's own subset) never satisfies a rule. No rule blocks the
phase.

- D1. Spurious survival. In each of SE, V1, V2, EB1, EB2, ER, NW and NR: the upper Wilson bound
  of SURVIVED / eligible, v0.5, at most 0.10, and the v0.5 SURVIVED count not above the v0.4
  count on the same eligible set. SURVIVED + WEAKENED is reported beside it, with no target.
- D2. Planets kept. In P: kept (SURVIVED or WEAKENED) / eligible, v0.5, at least the v0.4 value
  minus 0.10; and REFUTED / eligible, v0.5, at most 0.05 with an upper Wilson bound at most 0.10.
- D3. Grazing, variable and artifact hosts. In PG, PV and PA, each: REFUTED / eligible, v0.5, at
  most 0.10 with an upper Wilson bound at most 0.20.
- D4. Decidability. In P, the subset of the eligible set with target expected SNR at least 15:
  INCONCLUSIVE / subset, v0.5, at most 0.30 (NOT DECIDABLE when the subset has fewer than 20).
- D5. Off-target source. In PX: REFUTED / eligible, v0.5, at least 0.80.
