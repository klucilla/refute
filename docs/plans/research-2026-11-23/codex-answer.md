## Blocking

- **Define the screen before tuning.** Specify which tests are required, the precedence of measured failures and missing core tests, and three outcomes: retained, rejected, and unassessed. Count unassessed cases in the eligible denominators, crediting neither retention nor rejection. Preserve the existing verdict on the same inputs and roles. A zero-coverage `TestResult` remains `INCONCLUSIVE`. Model-shift uniqueness, transit-to-transit SNR consistency, and shape are reasonable candidates for calibration. Adopting the screen for confirmatory selection or claims is a **rule change requiring blind review**. An exploratory field alone is permissible. Redefining `SURVIVED` independently requires blind review. [Project rules](C:/Projetos/refute/CLAUDE.md:41), [Coverage rule](C:/Projetos/refute/CLAUDE.md:113), [R2 decision](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/plans/v0.5.5-r2-closing-record.md:24)
- **Size the study by independent detections.** With two-sided 95% Wilson intervals and 250 independent cases per endpoint, the proposed floors require **224/250** retained injections and **235/250** rejected null detections. The proposed planet guard permits **12/250** refutations under its 5% point-rate cap and 10% upper-bound cap. Zero nulls retained gives a **1.51%** upper bound. Those targets are feasible at that sample size, but C2 yielded only **40/200** rotation and **24/100** inversion gate detections. One transform per 600 hosts would yield roughly **120–128** at those historical rates. More transforms on the same hosts do not give independent trials. Pilot scramble yield and residual signal leakage, split by host, account for shared sector effects, and preregister minimum yield and power. Flux injection cannot validate pixel tests. **100 CPU-h and 10 GB** are planning estimates. [C2 transform results](C:/Projetos/refute/calibration/v0.5/runs/c2/report.json:246), [Report §§5.3–5.4](C:/Projetos/refute/.cache/v059/research/fable-research.md:150)
- **Route (e) can finish first only if its directed evaluator and data qualify.** The v0.2.1 closure lacked false-positive and no-transit controls, and the roadmap still lists directed holdout as pending. Validate the exact fixed-ephemeris evaluator on positives, nulls, aliases, and correlated artifacts. Freeze windows, matched controls, timing uncertainty, minimum coverage, and the product cutoff. Verify actual future visibility, cadence, and access for each list before committing to dates. A positive result establishes recurrence at the predicted times. An eclipsing binary can satisfy that endpoint.
