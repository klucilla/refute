# v0.5.3 Battery corrections, round 3: scope proposal (for the maintainer's decision)

**A proposal, not approved; nothing runs under it.** Written 2026-11-10 at the closing of v0.5.2
([closing record](../validation/v0.5.2/closing-record.md), [independent review](../validation/v0.5.2/closing-review.md)).
Round 3 is the last correction round of the advance rule. If it also ends without success, the
maintainer decides between changing the approach, recording infeasibility, or pausing the line.
Outstanding targets: D1 for EB1 and EB2, D2, D4, T2a. No target is relaxed, and the rule of evidence
stays.

**The idea.** v0.5.2 built an estimator family and priced an acceptance of about 3,000 CPU-h before
knowing whether a battery that passes only with evidence could meet D2 and T2a. Round 3 asks that
question first, cheaply and on development data: **bounds and audits before estimators, a forecast
before any acceptance.**

**Stage A: bounds, audits and diagnoses** (development data and seeds only; no change to any rule;
estimate 20 to 50 CPU-h, unmeasured; each design frozen and reviewed by Codex before it runs)

| Item | What it answers | Worked if | Failed if |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1. Audit of the background statistic (`systematics`) on T2a's 23 background losses: SAP_BKG against SAP_FLUX units and normalization, the background-rise error against the SAP depth's error and against the background's white-noise floor | is the excess error a defect of the statistic, absorbable variability, or the data? | the ratio is explained | the error sits at its floor |
| A2. Per-injection attainable bounds: the background rise (white-noise floor or Cramer-Rao), the sinusoid's residual box error (an efficient linear model), `odd_even` (the Cramer-Rao bound in hand); then the maximum retention they imply for D2 and T2a, with sampling intervals | can any estimator meet D2 (0.864) and T2a (0.897) under the declared requirements? | the bound decides it either way | the bounds cannot be mapped to the population |
| A3. | A5. D1 for EB1 and EB2: the transit-geometry candidate (#215, #10) measured on development data, recorded as a diagnostic | is there a decisive route for folded binaries? | separation on development data with planets kept | no separation |

**The stop after stage A (for the maintainer).** The results go to the maintainer with three
options. (1) If a bound shows a target unreachable under its declared requirements, the material for
an infeasibility record exists; the record is the maintainer's. (2) The design questions only the
maintainer can decide, which round 3 does not change by itself: whether a warning-severity test's
INCONCLUSIVE should block a verdict (lesson 3), the background sub-check's alternative (a rise of 0.8
of the depth at power 0.95), and the sinusoid's small-residual requirement. (3) Stage B, if the
bounds leave room.

**Stage B (only if stage A leaves room):** only the estimators that the bounds allow (an efficient
sinusoid residual, an `odd_even` estimator close to its bound, a priced extension of A0's domain),
each frozen and recalibrated before evaluation; then **a forecast of the targets before any
acceptance is priced**; then the acceptance, calibration and confirmation of the plans of v0.5.1 and
v0.5.2, under new caps.

**Not in scope:** the centroid (#183, #200), and the other issues moved to "Deferred (no target
version)" in the triage of 2026-11-10. No v0.3 work before 2026-11-18. Tracking: the milestone
v0.5.3 (13 issues).

**Proposed caps for stage A** (the maintainer's decision): 50 CPU-h, 0.5 M review tokens, 4
agent-days, with the machine rules and the pause of 18 November at 20:00 unchanged. A STOP at each cap.
