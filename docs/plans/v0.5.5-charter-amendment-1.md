# v0.5.5 Core resolution: amendment 1 to the charter

**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-20 (night), item 2 (#218, comment
7050514004). **This amendment comes from the independent review** of the charter and of R1's
designs ([`v0.5.5-independent-review-r0-r1.md`](v0.5.5-independent-review-r0-r1.md), stored
unchanged; Fable 5.2, read only, at commit `cd92c487`). It is a new record: the charter
([`v0.5.5-charter.md`](v0.5.5-charter.md)) is not edited, and where the two differ this amendment
governs.

**Written before any R1 data.** No check of R1 had run when this was written: no D2-F, D1-P or
T2a-E shard exists (the ledger, [`docs/validation/v0.5.5/ledger.json`](../validation/v0.5.5/ledger.json),
holds only the charges of the I4 diagnosis, the cost pilot on seeds named `pilot`, the T2a-E
geometry count and D2-F's decision-value reading). The results of v0.5 to v0.5.4 were known, as
the charter already states for every one of its decisions.

`method_status: not_validated`. No target is relaxed and no frozen rule is changed: every item
below either tightens a convention the charter fixed or completes one it left open.

## (a) WEAKENED: 0.05 per new test

**The change.** The charter's price for the WEAKENED exit (charter, section (a), table, third row)
was "at most 0.10 of the true planets of each family demoted by the new test". It becomes:

- **at most 0.05 of the true planets of each family demoted to WEAKENED by each new test**, the
  per-test unit of v0.5.4's step 2
  ([`v0.5.4-h2-step2-i3-increment.md`](v0.5.4-h2-step2-i3-increment.md):62-74), which halved
  each allowance so that the other half stays for the rest of any future battery;
- **0.10 only as a published scenario**, never as an exit price.

**The unit change, declared.** The 0.10 the charter cited is a **whole-battery** unit: D2's margin
(version 5's kept fraction at least v0.4's less 0.10) and the REFUTED limit of PG, PV and PA
(`src/refute/packs/tess/calib05/c1.py:654-682`) bound the battery as a whole. The charter applied
it **per new test**, with no cumulative cap, so several new tests could each take 0.10. Step 2's
0.05 is a **per-test** unit by construction. The amendment keeps the per-test reading and takes
its unit from the per-test source. The charter's cap is not cumulative either; a cumulative
WEAKENED cap for a whole battery is a design question of R2 and is not fixed here.

**The direction.** Lowering the price lowers each new test's allowed level, so it moves D1-P's
WEAKENED exit **away from "route"**. P already had 8 of 186 WEAKENED in v0.5 (the review, Q1.1),
which the per-test price does not count.

## (b) REFUTED: step 2's strict budget

**The change.** The charter's REFUTED exit (charter, section (a), table, first row) was "the
frozen budgets for the whole battery; a new test uses only the room measured on the development
data: the budget less the battery's own REFUTED fraction there". It becomes **step 2's strict
budget per new test** (step 2, the table "The budgets (frozen here)", lines 64-69):

- **P: `r_P` ≤ 0.025, and its upper end ≤ 0.05;**
- PG, PV and PA: `r_f` ≤ 0.05, and its upper end ≤ 0.10 (the same table; the maintainer's
  decision names P's line, and the other families' lines are taken with it, the stricter reading);
- **the battery's whole point ceilings remain P ≤ 0.05 and PG, PV and PA ≤ 0.10, together with
  the Wilson upper limits** (0.10 for P, 0.20 for PG, PV and PA), as before: the per-test budget
  and the battery's ceilings hold jointly.

**Why the charter's budget was looser.** "The budget less the battery's own REFUTED fraction"
gives a new test all of the room the battery has not used. Where the battery's REFUTED fraction on
P is below 0.025, that room is larger than step 2's 0.025 (it approaches the whole 0.05 as the
fraction goes to zero) and leaves nothing for any later test; where the fraction is above 0.025,
the residual room is the smaller of the two. Both constraints are kept jointly, so the new test
gets the smaller of them in every case. Step 2 fixed half of each allowance for one test before any data and
said its budgets may be tightened, never loosened after a result (step 2:71-74). The charter
cited step 2 for the INCONCLUSIVE exit only and not for this one (the review, Q1.2). The
amendment applies the strict half here too.

**The direction.** Away from "route", or no change, for any check that reads a REFUTED exit
(the joint constraint is never looser than either alone). D1-P reads the fatal
exit as scenarios only (`fatal_strict_budget_scenario` at 0.025 and the same less the reference's
REFUTED fraction, 3/178), so its decisive outcomes do not move.

## (c) The generator: version 2 for R1's development checks only

- **Generator version 2** (`docs/validation/v0.5.5/generator_v2.py`, `c1-v2`) is used **only for
  R1's development checks** (D2-F and D1-P).
- **A future confirmation of the frozen targets uses C1's frozen design, version 1**

  radius 1.0)"; `docs/plans/v0.5-acceptance.md`, the review's Q1.4). Using version 2 for a

  design does.
- **D1-P publishes a bridge, version 1 against version 2, on the same seeds**: set A's first 100
  seeds per family drawn again with version 1 (`d1p_price.py`, set `bridge`), read in both views
  and at every price. The bridge is a comparison only: it gives no outcome.

**The direction** (the review, Q1.4): version 2 is harder than version 1 with the exact star for
any test that uses the star (its planet-side cost becomes real); generally easier than B0's ±3σ box,
with no strict ordering; near-neutral for the locked battery, whose only stellar test tolerates
1.5 times the maximum duration; nearly inert for D1-P's primary reading.

## (e) The standard of proof for an infeasibility record, completed

The charter's section (c) lists eight elements. Four are added, and the first eight stand:

9. **A fixed level of evidence.** The population bound of element 2 holds at **one-sided 95 % on
   each of the two seed sets** of element 5 (each set alone, not only pooled).
10. **The record's thresholds are frozen before its data.** Every threshold, price and reading a
    record will use is committed before any of the data it reads is generated; a threshold fixed
    after that data voids the record.
11. **A record "under the current test rules" does not by itself meet the advance rule.** The
    advance rule asks that a target be "infeasible with the available data" (ROADMAP.md:17-20). A
    record scoped to the current test rules shows that **those rules** cannot meet the target: it
    is a rule-made infeasibility. Such a record says so in its first paragraph, and it does not,
    on its own, discharge the advance rule for that target.
12. **"Admissible" was defined after the results.** A record scoped to "any admissible battery"
    states that the prices that define "admissible" (charter, section (a), and this amendment)
    were adopted with the results of v0.5 to v0.5.4 known.

## (f) For R2

The charter's section (d) keeps the prospective route open with seven safeguards. Two are made
precise for R2:

- **How the reviewer is kept blind to the forecasts.** The reviewer receives a bundle built by a
  script from an allowlist of files (the design questions, the code's semantics, the frozen rules
  and the proposed rules), with a SHA-256 manifest of the bundle recorded before the review; no
  clone of the repository, no history and no network. No forecast, count, record or report of
  v0.5 to v0.5.5 is in the allowlist. A bundle found to hold one voids that review, and a new
  reviewer receives a corrected bundle.
- **Both aggregations reported.** D2, D4 and T2a count INCONCLUSIVE, so each is reported under
  **both** aggregations: the current one (a warning test's INCONCLUSIVE blocks as it does today)
  and the one R2 proposes, side by side, for every number R2 publishes on them.

## What this amendment does not do

It changes no target, no frozen rule and no verdict; it decides no design question; it does not
amend the advance rule. It does not release any run: D2-F does not run before its redesign is
checked (the decision, 3), and D1-P and T2a-E run only on the maintainer's release (the
decision, 5).
