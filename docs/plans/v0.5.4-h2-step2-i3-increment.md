# v0.5.4, second half, step 2: I3 corrected, a rule on the increment (design only)

**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (night), item 4b (#217): "passos 2 e 3:
a I3 corrigida (sem herdar a falha da D2 da versão 5; regra sobre o incremento), em versão
populacional (uma realização por curva, níveis contínuos, orçamentos separados por família, banda
binomial ou DKW, três desfechos incluindo INCONCLUSIVE)."

This step is **design only**: it fixes the rule that step 3 computes. It also follows Codex's
conditions ([`v0.5.4-audit-check.md`](v0.5.4-audit-check.md), step 2):
- a prospective reference and the increment's budgets for REFUTED, retention losses and
  INCONCLUSIVE, overlaps included;
- version 5's lack of margin is never turned into the new test's infeasibility;
- the reference is never called an approved battery;
- without a defined incremental budget, the outcome is `INCONCLUSIVE`.

`method_status: not_validated`.

## What was wrong (the audit's point 1, accepted with a reservation)

The frozen I3 fixed every other outcome at version 5's records and checked D2's kept rule on each
draw. Version 5 already fails that rule (141 kept of 178, against 156), and a fatal test can only
lower the kept count. So the joint probability was about 0 whatever the new test did. That is a
property of version 5, not of the test.

## The rules the increment answers to (C1, `src/refute/packs/tess/calib05/c1.py:633-702`)

| Rule | Population | Frozen criterion |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | each binary family (EB1, EB2) | the Wilson upper end of the SURVIVED fraction ≤ 0.10, and SURVIVED in version 5 ≤ SURVIVED in v0.4 |
| D2 | P | kept fraction ≥ v0.4's − 0.10; REFUTED fraction ≤ 0.05; its Wilson upper end ≤ 0.10 |
| D3 | each of PG, PV, PA | REFUTED fraction ≤ 0.10; its Wilson upper end ≤ 0.20 |
| D4 | P with SNR ≥ 15 | INCONCLUSIVE fraction ≤ 0.30 |

## The reference and the increment

- **The binary side assumes the other tests reject nothing.**
  - The new test is a fatal density test. On a binary it can only turn SURVIVED or WEAKENED into
    REFUTED, which helps D1.
  - No version-5 record exists for the binaries: D1 had no forecast, and producing version 5's
    outcomes for about 800 binaries would cost about 15 CPU-h, beyond this half's room. So a
    binary counts as SURVIVED for D1 unless the new test rejects it. This is the conservative
    choice for a route: the new test must meet D1 alone.
  - D1 is checked per family with its Wilson rule. Its v0.4 clause cannot be checked without
    v0.4's records for the same binaries, which is stated.
  - **Consequence:** under this assumption no negative result can be drawn, since the other tests'
    help is unknown. The outcome is a route or `INCONCLUSIVE`.
- **The planet side is judged on the increment only.** For each planet family f, the new test's
  own outcome on each planet gives:
  - `r_f`, the fraction it fatally fails (REFUTED by the new test);
  - `i_f`, the fraction where it is INCONCLUSIVE (a failed fit, too little coverage, or an
    error; no sensitivity block). Such a planet
    is lost to D2's retention, and enters D4's subset if its SNR ≥ 15.

  These are the test's own costs. They are compared with prospective budgets, never with the room
  version 5 left. Version 5's own losses are not inherited, and the reference is not called an
  approved battery.
- **Overlaps.** A planet the reference already lost and the new test also rejects costs nothing
  new to retention, but its REFUTED still counts in `r_f` (D2 and D3 count REFUTED fractions
  regardless of what else failed). So the increment is counted on all planets, the conservative
  choice.

## The budgets (frozen here)

| Budget | Strict (decides a route) |
|---|---|
| P, REFUTED | `r_P` ≤ 0.025, and its upper end ≤ 0.05 |
| P, retention cost | `r_P + i_P` ≤ 0.05 |
| PG, PV, PA, REFUTED | `r_f` ≤ 0.05, and its upper end ≤ 0.10 |
| D4, INCONCLUSIVE added in P with SNR ≥ 15 | `i_P(≥15)` ≤ 0.05 |

These are half of each allowance (the D4 increment at 0.05), leaving the other half to the rest
of any future battery. They are a prospective choice made here, before any data; the review may
tighten them, never loosen them after a result. The whole allowances (the earlier "generous"
set) are reported beside them, for information only, since no negative result is drawn.

## The outcomes (two)

Step 3 calibrates one threshold τ* on its calibration planets (the smallest τ at which every
strict budget holds there), then evaluates it on disjoint sets. Each limit gets a one-sided
binomial (Clopper-Pearson) band, with a Bonferroni split over the eight limits. Let `S(τ*)` be
"D1 is met for both binary families by the new test alone, and the strict budgets hold".
- **Route** if, at τ*, every planet limit's band upper end is within its strict budget, and, for
  the two binary families together, the probability that D1's allowance holds at C1''s
  projection, under each band's upper end of the non-rejected fraction, is at least 0.95, by the
  conservative bound `1 − Σ (1 − p_f)`.
- **`INCONCLUSIVE`** otherwise; also under any stop, or if the calibration admits no τ*.

**The bands are not simultaneous over every threshold:** inference is at the calibrated τ* only.
The new test has no sensitivity block of its own. Its INCONCLUSIVE comes only from failed fits,
coverage and errors, so PASS means non-rejection, and a route stays conditional on that.

No negative result and no draft about the fatal class come from this step.

**The warning class** (the test as a warning, WEAKENED) is reported apart, as a question for the
maintainer's step 8. It is never a route here.

## What step 3 must fix in its own design

- the statistic, the models, the aliases, the stellar penalties, the bounds, the starts and the
  convergence;
- one independent realization per curve;
- calibration and evaluation sets that are disjoint and seeded, apart from every confirmation seed;
- the threshold range;
- what gives the test's INCONCLUSIVE (here: failed fits, coverage and errors; no sensitivity
  block);
- the envelope;
- a timed pilot before the full run, with the budget reserved.
