# v0.4 pilot plan (funnel, complete cases)

Written in WP0 on 2026-10-29, after the phase opened (P0, the maintainer's decision of
2026-10-29; ROADMAP.md, "v0.4 Discover"; tracking issue #177). It is written **before any
code** of the phase. The acceptance documents are in
[v0.4-acceptance.md](v0.4-acceptance.md). The protocol is not part of this file: it is
brought to the maintainer at P1 and committed only after that approval.

Nothing here is a validated method. Every v0.4 report carries `method_status: not_validated`
(the v0.2.1 closing record, [docs/validation/v0.2.1/closing-2026-10-27.md](../validation/v0.2.1/closing-2026-10-27.md)).

## 1. Goal and success

Complete, auditable, reproducible cases that a researcher can understand, rerun with one
command and contest, from the target to the conclusion, with every limit shown.

Success depends on completeness, reproducibility, review and an honest conclusion. It does
**not** need a candidate, a SURVIVED or anything publishable. "No significant signal in these
data", "signal with a likely alternative explanation" and "insufficient data to decide" are
valid outcomes. Zero promoted stars is an acceptable outcome of the funnel.

## 3. The funnel

**Stage 1, on every one of the N stars (light curves only).** The light-curve tests run:
`snr` (gate), `odd_even`, `secondary_eclipse`, `period_alias`, `plausibility` (with the TIC),
`systematics` and `nearby_contamination` (from the light-curve file: SAP flux, background,
CROWDSAP and momentum dumps, `src/refute/packs/tess/adapter.py`; and a TIC neighbor query).
The download without pixel files already exists (`target_pixel_files: false`,
`src/refute/packs/tess/params.py`).

- The pixel tests (`centroid_shift`, `aperture_depth`) are reported INCONCLUSIVE with zero
  coverage and the reason "not run at this stage". They are never PASS.
- A valid fatal FAIL after the gate gives REFUTED, as in `docs/verdicts.md`.
- Because a deferred test is INCONCLUSIVE, the stage-1 verdict of a star with no fatal FAIL is
  INCONCLUSIVE by rule 3 of `refute.core.verdict.aggregate`. That is the intended reading: the
  star was not evaluated by the full battery.
- The promotion to stage 2 follows a rule fixed in the protocol, with a cap of 3 and **no
  replacement** of a star that falls at stage 2. The promotion is recorded apart from the
  verdict.
- Every scanned star gets a light ledger entry, with `method_status` and
  `temporal_diagnostic`.

**Stage 2, only for promoted stars and controls.** Pixel files are downloaded, and the full
decisive battery runs under the same lock. Each of these gets a complete dossier.

The choice of cases depends on stage-1 results, and this is declared in every report:
publishing all N entries keeps the denominators, but it does not remove that conditioning.

## 6. Costs and caps

Estimates, not measurements: WP0 1 d; WP1 2 to 3 d; WP2 3 d; WP3 2 to 3 d; WP4 1 d; WP5 2 to
3 d; WP6 4 to 6 d; the funnel adds 2 to 3 d (hypothesis). Total 16 to 21 agent-days, against
the cap of 22. CPU and data volume per star are not extrapolated from a synthetic target: they
are measured on the controls (the full battery, a conservative bound for stage 1) before any
exploration run, and the N rule of the protocol uses that measurement. Every run writes a
`cost.jsonl` (logical-processor hours, peak memory, bytes downloaded, network time, transient
failures), as input for v0.7.

Stop and report when any cap is reached: 22 agent-days, 8 calendar weeks from 2026-10-29
(that is, 2026-12-24), 100 logical-processor hours, or 10 GB in `.cache`. No automatic
extension.

**Plan.**
1. Targeted update only: `uv lock --upgrade-package filelock --upgrade-package virtualenv`,
   with the cooldown in force, so the science stack (numpy, scipy, astropy, lightkurve,
   astroquery, matplotlib) does not move during the phase. A full `uv lock --upgrade` is not
   planned for v0.4: it would change the numerical stack under fixtures recorded with the
   current one, without a need of the phase.
2. Check the diff of `uv.lock`: only `filelock`, `virtualenv` and packages they pull in may
   change; any other change stops the update for the maintainer.
3. The pre-commit hooks (`ruff-pre-commit` v0.16.9, equal to ruff in `uv.lock`; `gitleaks`
   v8.30.1) and the GitHub Actions (pinned by SHA) stay as they are unless an alert or a need
   appears; any change follows the same 7-day rule by hand.
4. Run the whole suite after the update (JUnit XML), and the drand and lock tests explicitly.
5. When: at the start of WP1, after P1 is approved (installing new versions of packages runs
   third-party code that this machine has not run), in its own commit, well before P3.
6. Any new dependency the phase turns out to need (none is planned) is a stop: it is proposed
   to the maintainer with its cooldown check before it enters `pyproject.toml`.

## 8. Risks

- **#30.** New optional schema fields with defaults change the resolved hash of older claims.
  The new fields exist only in a new v0.4 claim schema; earlier locks verify at their tags;
  an explicit test checks the resolved hashes of the v0.1, v0.2 and v0.3 claims.
- **Selection on outcomes.** The funnel chooses cases from stage-1 results; declared, with all
  N entries published and separate denominators.
- **Multiple testing.** Exposure grows with N. The expected number of spurious promotions is a
  sum of per-star probabilities that is not estimated; the transformed curves document it
  descriptively per star. A transformed curve is not a true null.
- **Cost.** Searches on multi-year curves may cost more than estimated; the N rule and the
  injection ladder of the protocol absorb this, and the caps stop the phase.
- **Transient services.** MAST and catalog queries fail at times; retries are fixed in the
  protocol, and when they are exhausted the selection or download stops for the maintainer;
  a target is never skipped silently (#22).
