## 2. The defect, read in the code at the approval SHA

In `src/refute/packs/tess/screen_d5.py`:
- **(a)** `_noise` (lines 143 to 175) computes ONE `sigma_w` (line 149) and ONE `sigma_r` (lines 152
  to 175) for the whole curve, from the baseline. `_f1_box` takes them once (line 228), and `_pink`
  uses the same pair for both parities (line 242). A parity with larger σ is given the other
  parity's noise: P2.4 and P9.5.
- **(b)** `_wstd` (lines 88 to 93) weights by 1/σ² without normalizing the spread. A single baseline
  point with a tiny σ takes almost all the weight, so `sigma_w` (line 149) and `sigma_bin` (line
  168, through the bin weights of line 163) collapse toward 0: B6.
- **(c)** In T2 and T6, the precise point fixes its parity's weighted mean (line 241), while
  `sigma_tr` (line 242) comes from the global noise. The test is conservative there: it passes,
  but loses sensitivity.
- **(d)** `_mes` (line 314) and `_f2_u1` (line 685) use the same `_noise` and `_pink`, so the
  report-only MES series (F2, U1) share the defect.

## 3. Sources (the stored excerpts only)

From [sources/v0.5.11-screen-papers-2026-11-24.md](sources/v0.5.11-screen-papers-2026-11-24.md)
(read 2026-11-24). No paper was read again: in this phase a download is a stop.

- **K25 II.1:**
  - the box depth is the "weighted mean of all in-transit data points (entirely within half a
    transit duration from the transit center";
  - Eq. (2), `\sigma_{tr}=\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_{w}^{2}}{n}+\frac{\sigma_{r}^{2}}{N_{tr}}}`;
  - Eq. (3), `\sigma_{r}^{2}=\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}^{2}-\sigma_{\mathrm{bin,xpt}}^{2}`, with "we set
    σr=0" when negative;
  - σ_w "after masking out data within one transit duration".
- **K25 IV.4, Eq. (11):** `\mathrm{OE}_{\mathrm{box}}=\frac{|\delta_{\mathrm{odd}}-\delta_{\mathrm{even}}|}{\sqrt{\sigma_{\delta_{\mathrm{odd}}}^{2}+\sigma_{\delta_{\mathrm{even}}}^{2}}}`;
  "We fail TCEs with OEbox>3".
- **T18 and SM03** say nothing on this noise. T18 A.3.4 defines F_red only, and SM03 gives
  geometry.

**What the excerpts settle, and what they do not:**
- They settle that the denominator of Eq. (11) holds **one uncertainty per parity**,
  `\sigma_{\delta_{odd}}` and `\sigma_{\delta_{even}}`, each the uncertainty of that parity's depth.
- They settle that the depth is a **weighted mean**, and that K25's noise has the pink form of
  Eq. (2) with the red term of Eq. (3).
- They do **not** give the estimator for unequal σ. The v0.5.11 gate's reading (finding 7), "the
  noise over the corresponding transits", is not in the stored excerpts; it is neither used nor
  quoted as K25's.
- The estimator below is therefore this correction's **[choice]**. It is consistent with
  Eq. (2), Eq. (3) and Eq. (11): with every σ equal, it reduces to the current form (section 5).

## 4. The rule (normative), replacing the specification's section 5 noise and section 6 σ_par

Unchanged:
- the valid points and correction 1's input rule;
- w = 1/σ²;
- the epochs;
- the baseline d(t, 0) > T with at least 10 points;
- OE_box's form and its threshold 3;
- every `not_assessed` and `unavailable` rule not named here.

**The scale [choice].** m_b is the weighted mean of the baseline, and s² = Σ_b w (f − m_b)² / (n_b − 1),
the baseline's reduced chi-square.
- It is dimensionless, and about 1 when the σ are right.
- A single precise point barely moves it, because its own residual about m_b is near 0.
- If s² is not finite and positive, the noise is unavailable.

**The red term [choice]** (K25 Eq. (3) form, inverse-variance weighted across bins):
- The bins are as now: width T over the baseline from the first valid time, at least 2 points per
  bin, and at least 3 bins.
- For bin k: its weighted mean μ_k, its total weight W_k, and v_k = s² / W_k.
- With u_k = 1 / v_k:
  - μ̄ = Σ u_k μ_k / Σ u_k;
  - Q = Σ u_k (μ_k − μ̄)²;
  - c = Σ u_k − Σ u_k² / Σ u_k.
- **σ_r² = max(0, (Q − (M − 1)) / c),** with M the number of bins. This is a method-of-moments
  excess over the expected bin scatter; "we set σr=0" when negative is K25's.
- If c is not positive or a term is not finite, the noise is unavailable.
- **Not tested.** The tested first candidate used the unweighted variance (section 1).

**Per parity [choice]** (K25 Eq. (2) form):

σ_δ,par² = s² / W_par + σ_r² / N_par,

where W_par is the total weight of the parity's in-transit points and N_par is its number of
epochs.
- It is the propagated uncertainty of a weighted mean, scaled by s², plus K25's red term.
- **F1:** OE_box = |δ_odd − δ_even| / √(σ_δ,odd² + σ_δ,even²), and `fail` if OE_box > 3.

**The report-only MES grid (proposed, for Codex's scope decision):** each cell's σ is
s² / W_in,cell + σ_r² / N_cell. Then F2 and U1 use the same noise as F1. If Codex limits the
correction to F1, this paragraph is struck, and `_mes` keeps the current form, recorded as a
declared limit of the report-only quantities.

**Recorded metrics:** s², σ_r, W_odd, W_even, N_odd, N_even, σ_δ,odd, σ_δ,even and OE_box.
`sigma_w` is no longer recorded for F1.

## 5. Checks of the rule (arithmetic, not runs)

- **Equal σ.** With every σ equal to σ0: s² · σ0² is the baseline variance with divisor n_b − 1, so
  s² / W_par = (that variance) / n_par. That is K25's σ_w² / n, up to n_b / (n_b − 1) (about 1.0003
  for the baseline of M1's geometry).
  - If the bins also have equal counts, the weighted red term equals the unweighted one, up to the
    same divisor.
- **B6.** The precise point's residual about m_b is about 0, so s² stays near 1, and its bin enters
  Q with a residual near 0.
- **P2.4, P9.5.** W_odd is smaller by the ratio², so σ_δ,odd grows by the ratio, which is what
  the parity's noise is.
- **T2, T6.** W_odd grows by the precise point's weight, so σ_δ,odd shrinks and the test regains
  sensitivity.
