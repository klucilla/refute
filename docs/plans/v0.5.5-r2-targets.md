# v0.5.5 R2: the targets, defined without counts (for the blind review)

**Status: definitions only** (the maintainer's decisions of 2026-11-21, 15:20, item 1 (9)). Each
target's rule is stated as **what it counts, of what, against which frozen limit**; the limits'
values and every result are left out, as the writing rule asks. The frozen protocols define them
in full (the v0.5 acceptance and calibration protocol); the code that counts C1 and C2 is in the
bundle (`calib05/c1.py`, `calib05/c2.py`, `calib05/values.py`). `method_status: not_validated`.

## The synthetic families (C1)

C1 draws synthetic light curves by family from a generator: **P** (planets), **PG**, **PV**, **PA**
(planets on grazing, variable and artifact hosts), **PX** (an off-target source), and the spurious
families **SE** (single events), **V1**, **V2** (stellar variability), **EB1**, **EB2** (eclipsing
binaries), **ER** (edge ramps), **NW** and **NR** (noise: white and correlated). For each family the
**eligible set** is the curves whose shared candidate passes the gate (and, for the planet
families, matches the injected identity). Each rule is reported PASS, FAIL or NOT DECIDABLE (an
eligible set below a frozen minimum size never satisfies a rule).

| Target | What it counts | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** (spurious survival) | in each spurious family (SE, V1, V2, EB1, EB2, ER, NW, NR): SURVIVED over eligible, by its upper Wilson bound; and the SURVIVED count against the earlier battery's on the same eligible set | a frozen upper limit, and not above the earlier count; SURVIVED + WEAKENED reported beside it, with no limit |
| **D2** (planets kept) | in P: kept (SURVIVED or WEAKENED) over eligible; and REFUTED over eligible with its upper Wilson bound | at least the earlier battery's kept fraction less a frozen allowance; REFUTED under frozen limits |
| **D3** (grazing, variable and artifact hosts) | in PG, PV and PA, each: REFUTED over eligible with its upper Wilson bound | frozen limits |
| **D4** (decidability) | in P, the eligible subset above a frozen expected signal-to-noise: INCONCLUSIVE over that subset | a frozen upper limit |
| **D5** (off-target source) | in PX: REFUTED over eligible | a frozen lower limit |

## The real curves (C2)

C2 runs both the earlier ("compatibility") battery and the new one on the same authenticated real
stars, with injections:

| Target | What it counts | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **T2a** (injected planets kept) | injections into hosts negative at the original gate: among recovered gate-passing injections, those SURVIVED or WEAKENED at stage 1 | at least the compatibility battery's fraction on the same injections, less a frozen allowance |
| **T2b** (transformed curves) | on the fixed group of transformations of each type (inverted, rotated), separately: the gate-passing detections whose verdict is retained (SURVIVED or WEAKENED); only records with status OK, the gate passed and a retained verdict enter the numerator | not above the compatibility battery's count |
| **T2c** (original curves, a subset) | on the valid original curves, the development stars excluded: the SURVIVED count at stage 1 | not above the compatibility battery's count |

## The proposed adversarial family (R2, not built)

**VN (non-sinusoidal periodic variability)**, proposed in the arguments (B3.3), to be frozen before
any number with the rest of R2's freeze and generated in R3:

- **Shapes:** periodic stellar variability with no transit, of three kinds; **each curve's kind is
  drawn independently with probability 1/3**. The fundamental and harmonics 2 to 6, with amplitudes
  `a_k = a_1 · k^(−alpha)` (content beyond the harmonics the `sinusoid` test fits), and **one global
  phase `phi` drawn uniformly per curve**; the relative phases are fixed by the kind:
  (1) an asymmetric rotational modulation (sawtooth-like): `alpha` uniform in [0.8, 1.2], harmonic
  phases `k·phi`;
  (2) a multi-harmonic spot signal: `alpha` uniform in [1.0, 2.0], harmonic phases `k·phi + psi_k`,
  each `psi_k` drawn uniformly and independently;
  (3) a pulsation-like profile with a steep rise and a slow decline: `alpha` uniform in [0.5, 1.0],
  harmonic phases `k·phi + (k − 1)·pi/2`.
- **Periods:** the variability period drawn log-uniformly in **[0.7, 10] days, the range of V1 and
  V2** (`calib05/c1.py`).
- **Amplitudes:** **`a_1 / sigma_w` drawn log-uniformly in [0.5, 5], the amplitude scale of V1 and
  V2** (`calib05/c1.py`; `sigma_w` the star's white noise).
- **The search and the gate:** the frozen ones; the alignment found (the candidate's period against
  the variability period: P/2, P, 2P or none) is recorded per curve.
- **The minimum of ambiguous cases:** at least **100 eligible VN curves reaching today's ambiguous
  branch** (flagged, no small residual) with a valid domain, coverage and detectability; **below
  it, the VN safeguard is NOT DECIDABLE**, and B3.3 is not adopted.
- **Seeds:** a fixed derivation string set at R2's freeze (`refute-r3-vn:<index>`); no seed is
  drawn in R2; none is ever a confirmation seed.
- **Eligibility:** as the other spurious families: the shared candidate passes the gate.
- **The comparator:** the frozen battery and the new one on the same VN curves (section 7's pairs).
- **The record states** how many VN curves reach today's ambiguous branch under the frozen rule,
  against the minimum above.
- **Counted** like D1's families (SURVIVED, with SURVIVED + WEAKENED reported) and in section 7's
  paired rule, with at least the same number of valid pairs.
