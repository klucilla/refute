no data was read to write these designs.** Earlier unit-test activity (offline tests of the pure
functions and of the generator, `tests/v055/`) is recorded separately. The numbers below that

## What the four checks share

- **The code** is in [`docs/validation/v0.5.5/`](../validation/v0.5.5/); nothing in `src/`
  changes.
  - `r1_common.py`: the outcome rules, the futility rules, the seeds and the machine policy, as
    pure functions tested offline (`tests/v055/`).
  - `generator_v2.py`: C1 version 2, the true star drawn from the catalog's declared 5 % (the
    charter's (b)). **Every new draw of R1 uses it.** It mutates process-global module state for
    the length of one call, so it requires serial use within each worker process.
  - `ledger_v055.py` and `caps.json`: the caps up to G1, with the stop at 80 % of each, checked
    at each step's boundaries (before a run, after it, before a summary), not monitored
    continuously.
  - `pinned_hash.py`: T2a-E locates the earlier stages' records under the code hash they were
    written with (below).
- **The driver** is v0.5.4's `budgeted_v3` (unchanged), one line `v055` of 64 CPU-h (80 % of 80),
  launched through `launch_detached.py` (processors 8 to 31, BelowNormal).
- **The machine policy** (the maintainer's): processors 0 to 7 always free; on working hours
  (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00) at most 10 workers, otherwise up to 22; batches of at most
  30 minutes. **R1 uses at most 10 workers at every hour** (Codex, r0r1 round 2, the
  conservative option): "up to 22" is a ceiling, not an obligation, and a shard that passed its
  declared 1,200 s (the driver checks a shard's time only after it ends; it is not a hard
  timeout) could otherwise cross 08:00 with more than 10. So no pause is needed (`NO_PAUSE`, a
  far-future date: the driver compares a date and has no case for none). The CPU price does not
  change; the wall time is longer.
- **One lock, fixed caps and one resume** (the charter, change 4). One lock (the ledger's) covers
  the whole run (`ledger_v055.guarded_run`): the identity and resume checks, the caps, the part's
  cap, the start's record (only after every precondition passed) and the driver's run. Each
  part's cap is cumulative over its identity: its allocation less what archived attempts
  consumed, within the line's room (the driver compares it with the identity's whole
  consumption). **Every start is recorded** in the ledger (`starts`), whether or not it spent
  anything; one start beyond the first is the **one pre-approved resume** (same code, same rows,
  same fingerprint). A second resume, a new identity or any change of code or rows needs the
  maintainer.
- **The frozen identity.** Each record holds the complete code identity: `src/`'s current code
  hash and the SHA-256 of every file the check executes or reads as configuration outside `src/`
  (its code, the earlier stages' modules it calls, the driver, the frozen inputs such as round 3's
  `b1-composition.json`), under one digest. **The second stage refuses any difference in the
  complete code identity, including the current source and dependency hash**, and records the
  first's SHA-256 before it uses its futility or counts.
- **Seeds** are new strings, `refute-v055-<check>-<set>:<family>:<i>`, disjoint between checks
  and sets, and different from C1's development and confirmation strings.
- **Kept per case** (the charter, change 9): each record keeps its inputs (the whole truth), the
  battery's chain or test rows with their messages and classes, the final candidate, the
  complete parameters of the fits it makes itself, the replay record, each placebo's phase, value
  and status, and the oracle's intermediate numbers. B0's own fit, reused unchanged for the box
  and known readings, returns the radius only; that limit is kept for comparability with B0.
- **Each check is written once**: its record refuses to be overwritten.

## D2-F: D2's rule counts at the oracle, three variants

**The question.** Under the current rules, and under two variants of method that the design
questions call method improvements, not relaxations
([`v0.5.3-design-questions.md`](v0.5.3-design-questions.md):129-131), do D2's oracle-level
blockers leave room within D2's allowance (22 losses of 178; version 5 loses 37 in round 3's
forecast)?

**These counts diagnose certificate limitations and observed blockers. They are not demonstrated
lower bounds on unavoidable retention losses** (Codex, r0r1 round 1): a certificate's failure does
not exclude a warning FAIL, which keeps the planet. Counting them as losses makes "route" harder,
the conservative side for the one decisive reading D2-F gives. **D2-F has no "closed" outcome.**

**Disclosure.** Step 1's floors (8 strict, 16 by rule, 21 upper), step 7's 9 certificate failures
(plus 3 uncertain), I4's 2 information-limited planets, and the union of step 1's F_rule with
step 7's failures (20, or 22 with the uncertain, Codex's count) are known. D2-F counts on **new
planets**, so none of those planets is reused.

**The population.** Two sets, A and B, of **200 P planets each**, from C1 version 2; seeds
`refute-v055-d2f-<A|B>:P:<i>`. Eligible: v0.4's gate passed and the identity (C1's definition for
planets).

**Per eligible planet** (`d2f_floor.py`):
- **version 5**'s verdict and test rows, with round 3's frozen composition (`b1_b14.config()`),
  each row's class, detail and message kept;
- **at the oracle** (the generator's model, the true ephemeris and depth, the stellar trend known):
  - `odd_even`'s power with the transit shape as a nuisance (I4's `nuisance_bound`, unchanged) at
    the battery's own alternative on the final candidate's depth;
  - `sinusoid`'s Fréchet certificate (step 7's construction, unchanged), the smallest block over
    the applicable harmonics;
  - the same block as a **simulated joint probability**: the flag's (cos, sin) on the
    out-of-transit cadences and the box depth after the harmonics on every cadence are jointly
    Gaussian under the generator's covariance. Their exact cross-covariance is computed (the GLS
    estimators' `G1⁻¹ (X1ᵀ Σ11⁻¹ X2) G2⁻¹`). The probability that the battery's own flag rule
    fires **and** the residual is small is drawn at the worst of eight phases (20,000 draws per
    phase). **A check:** the draws' flag marginal must equal the battery's `_sinusoid_power`, and
    their small-residual marginal stage A's `p_small`, each to 0.02; otherwise the planet is UNK.

**The categories** (True, False or unknown):

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| **DATA** | a decisive test of version 5 INCONCLUSIVE with the class `not_enough_data` |
| **INFO** | `odd_even`'s oracle power below 0.95 (unknown if the bound is invalid) |
| **SIN_F** | the Fréchet certificate's smallest block below 0.93; unknown within ±0.02 of 0.95 (step 7's band) |
| **SIN_J** | the joint probability's smallest block below 0.94; unknown within ±0.01 of 0.95 |
| **RULE** | version 5's `sinusoid` INCONCLUSIVE in the ambiguous-cause branch (`AMBIGUOUS_SINUSOID`) |
| **RULE_3** | RULE, and at the oracle a large residual would not be shown with probability 0.95: `P_large = Φ((μ_box − 0.5 D)/σ_box − 3)`, with `μ_box = [G2⁻¹ X2ᵀ Σ⁻¹ μ_planet]₀` the box's GLS mean under the normalized transit approximation, omitting the generator's multiplicative stellar trend (computed, not assumed equal to the depth D; Codex, r0r1 rounds 1 and 2) |

**The counts per variant** (a planet counts if any of its categories is True; the **upper**
count also takes a planet with any unknown category):
- **current**: DATA ∪ INFO ∪ SIN_F ∪ RULE;
- **variant 2** (a simulated joint probability in place of the Fréchet bound, the 0.95
  requirement unchanged): DATA ∪ INFO ∪ SIN_J ∪ RULE;
- **variant 3** (the ambiguous branch split by evidence: a residual shown large is a PASS):
  DATA ∪ INFO ∪ SIN_F ∪ RULE_3.

**The frozen outcome per variant** (`r1_common.d2f_variant_outcome`), with the **upper** count:
- **"route under the variant"** if the Wilson 95 % upper end of the pooled fraction (A and B) is
  at most **14/178** and each set's point fraction is at most 14/178 (the count leaves 8 of D2's
  22 losses for the estimators);
- **"unresolved"** otherwise. A large count closes nothing: it is reported, never an
  infeasibility basis.

**The sample from the band.** With about 178 eligible per set (round 3: 178 of 200), the pooled
356 reach "route" only if the upper count is at most **18 of 356** (5.1 %, about 9 of 178; my
arithmetic, Wilson at 95 %). So "route" needs a count near step 1's strict 8 of 178.

**The decision-value test.**

| Outcome | Prior (opinion) | The next action at G1 |
|---|---|---|
| route under variant 2 or 3 | about 25 to 40 % | the variant enters R2's design questions with a measured count; D2's estimators are worth building |
| unresolved | about 60 to 75 % | D2 has no route shown under these variants; G1 weighs a pause of D2 |

Two outcomes are plausible and each changes the next action: **it passes the test.**

**The futility rule** (`r1_common.d2f_futile_after_first`): after set A, set B runs only if some
variant can still end "route" (set A's upper point fraction at most 14/178). Otherwise set B is
refused and the outcome is "unresolved".

**Stops:** any planet raising an error, or fewer than 150 eligible in a set, ends with
"unresolved (a stop)" for every variant.

**Its limits.** **These counts combine model-based oracle calculations and blockers observed under
version 5. A small count motivates further design; it does not establish an estimator-independent
retention margin** (Codex, r0r1 round 2). RULE_3's `μ_planet` is a transit approximation in the
columns' normalized units (`−trapezoid × sector scale`), leaving out the generator's
multiplicative stellar trend, so it is not exactly the generator's physical mean. The variants
are **measured, not adopted**: adopting one is R2's, after G1.

## D1-P: D1's SURVIVED-denial price curve, per exit

**The question.** At the planet-side price the charter fixes for each exit, can a test leave at
most D1's allowance of SURVIVED binaries **among those the locked battery leaves SURVIVED**?

**Disclosure.** B0's and step 4's oracle numbers are known at every level (corrected record:
[`pause-record-corrections.md`](../validation/v0.5.4/pause-record-corrections.md)): at α = 0.05,
B0's box leaves 14.1 and 12.5, the known star 4.6 and 4.1, step 4's settings 4.9 to 8.6, all on
**all eligible binaries** of C1 version 1. D1-P reads only **the battery's survivors**, with the
star **drawn** (version 2), which no record has computed; Codex rejected the proposal's guess that
the prior reading would lie between 4.6 and 14.1.

**The population.** Two sets, A and B, of **200 EB1 and 200 EB2 each** (C1's projection is 200
per family), from C1 version 2; seeds `refute-v055-d1p-<A|B>:<family>:<i>`. Eligible: v0.4's gate
passed (C1's definition for these families).

**Per eligible binary** (`d1p_price.py`): the locked battery's chain (`paired_chain`, exactly as
C1's runs: v0.4's gate and v0.5's verdict). For each binary v0.5 leaves **SURVIVED**, the oracle
distance `d` to the nearest planet at the found period (B0's method: the noiseless binary, the
planet's depth, impact, epoch shift and effective radius fitted, the distance under the
generator's covariance), under three readings of the catalog star:
- **prior (primary):** the planet's effective radius under the Gaussian penalty
  `((ρ − ρ_B)/σ_ρ)²`, with `ρ_B` the binary's true effective radius and `σ_ρ = 0.0527` the
  catalog's (5 % in radius and mass, to first order). `d²` is the smallest evaluated
  `M² + penalty` over **three fits**: prior-weighted, box-constrained and known-star. **Each is an
  upper bound on the minimum, so the reading leans toward "route"; disclosed.**
  - **What it is:** an oracle surrogate under an explicitly specified catalog experiment (the
    catalog taken as a measurement of the true star, with the true star drawn from the same law
    as the declared error).
  - **What it is not** (Codex, r0r1 round 1): planet-side calibration, and the interpretation of
    `Φ(z_x − d)` with fitted parameters and the battery's survivor selection, have **not** been
    established. Drawing the star does not by itself provide that demonstration. Its outcomes are
    readings for G1, never a basis for a record by themselves.
- **box (a reference reading):** B0's ±3σ box on the effective radius around the catalog's centre.
- **known (a reference reading):** the effective radius fixed at the binary's true one.
- **These are reference oracle readings under their respective stellar assumptions. No ordering
  around the primary surrogate is established** (the drawn star can fall outside the box).

**The reading** (B0's, per family, set, reading and price x): a survivor **is assigned the nominal
surrogate non-rejection score** `Φ(z_x − d)`; a binary the battery already denies SURVIVED counts
0. The thresholds below are reading rules for G1; no coverage or calibration is attributed to
them. At C1's
projection `n' = round(200 × eligible / attempted)`, with D1's allowance `k = max_count(n')`
(the Wilson upper end at most 0.10): the expected SURVIVED count, and the 5th, 50th and 95th
percentiles of the probability of at most k over 2,000 bootstrap cohorts (seeds 20261120 for A,
20261121 for B). The curve is reported at x in {0.0027, 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30}
and at the exit prices and scenarios below.

**The exit prices** (the charter's (a)):

| Exit | Price x | Note |
|---|---|---|
| warning (WEAKENED) | **0.10** | the tolerance unit; a decisive reading |
| INCONCLUSIVE | **0.05**, read **conditional on D2 and D4 met in full** | no room while D2 fails its rule (141 of 178 kept against 156); a decisive reading only under that condition |
| fatal | **scenarios only**: 0.05 − 3/178 = 0.0331, and 0.05 | **The value 0.05 − 3/178 is a historical reference scenario, not measured available room for this battery on generator v2** (3 of 178 is version 5's REFUTED fraction on round 3's D2 population); 0.05 is the frozen budget's whole. The room must be measured on the battery and population the new test would join, which D1-P does not draw. Reported, never an outcome. |

**The frozen outcome per exit (warning and INCONCLUSIVE), under the primary reading**
(`r1_common.d1p_set_reading` and `d1p_exit_outcome`):
- per set and family: **"route"** if the expected count is at most **k − 2** and the bootstrap
  5th percentile of the pass probability is at least **0.5**; **"closed"** if the expected count
  is above **k** and the 95th percentile is below **0.05**; "unresolved" otherwise;
- per exit: **"route"** if both families read "route" on both sets; **"closed"** if some family
  reads "closed" on both sets; "unresolved" otherwise.

The box and known readings are reported beside it with the same rule, as reference readings;
they decide nothing.

**The sample from the band.** With about 190 eligible per family and set (B0: 381 of 400 and 373
of 400), `k` is 10. "Route" asks for an expected count of at most 8 with a pass probability of at
least 0.5 in 95 % of cohorts, and "closed" for one above 10 with a pass probability below 0.05 in
95 % of them. The binding count is the battery's survivors (about 35 to 45 per family, by v0.5's
confirmation), each contributing at most 1.

**The decision-value test.**

| Outcome | Prior (opinion) | The next action at G1 |
|---|---|---|
| route at the warning price | about 25 to 40 % | D1 has a priced route under the surrogate: its test, and the surrogate's calibration, are worth designing in R2 |
| closed at both decisive exits | about 10 to 20 % | G1 weighs whether a class-scoped record (the charter's (c)) is worth its own demonstration; the maintainer's stop |
| unresolved | about 45 to 60 % | G1 weighs a pause of D1 or a larger check |

At least two outcomes are plausible and each changes the next action: **it passes the test.**

**The futility rule** (`r1_common.d1p_futile_after_first`): after set A, set B runs only if some
decisive exit can still end "route" (both families read "route" on A) or "closed" (some family
reads "closed" on A). Otherwise set B is refused and every exit is "unresolved".

**Stops:** any binary raising an error, fewer than 150 eligible in a family and set, or a
survivor without a distance, ends with every exit "unresolved" and no outcome.

**Its limits.** It is an oracle surrogate of non-rejection under the generator's model and a
per-planet level. It is not a battery, measures no REFUTED room (on P, PG, PV or PA), and a
"route" shows that the information exists under the surrogate, never that a realized test reaches
it. A "closed" is never a record by itself (the charter's (c) lists what one needs).

## T2a-E: a model-free census of the background error

**The question.** For T2a's 102 class-(i) injections whose `systematics` loss is the background
block, does E4's own contrast reach the block's power of 0.95 when its error is measured on the
host's own background, without a noise model?

**Disclosure.** Step 6 fitted a noise model and found every one of the 102 inadequate at the
transit's scale (0 valid). I1's median power of the 103 class-(i) cases is 0.371. A0's
background error is a median 23.2 times the white error on a selected subset (Codex's caveat).
These are known.

**The population.** I1's `i2_population`, the same 102 injections as step 6, replayed exactly as
step 6 replays them (`step6_i2.replay`, with step 6's stops: B1's verdict and tests reproduced,
the recorded `background_rise_err` to 1e-9, the block's power). Then **each replay is compared
with I1's own historical record of the injection** (read by I1's reader under I1's committed
fingerprint): class (i) with `background`, the background error to 1e-9 relative, and the block's
power to 1e-9; a difference is a stop before any placebo (Codex, r0r1 round 1). In step 6's
order; **stage 1** is the first 51, **stage 2** the other 51.

**The code hash of the earlier records.** The earlier stages' readers key their records by
fingerprints that include `src/`'s code hash, which covers `uv.lock`; the dependency update of
2026-11-20 (`5e5f40a`) changed it. T2a-E therefore **locates** those records (in its schedule
only, never around the replay or the identification of the current run) under the code hash of
commit `8653911` (`pinned_hash.py`). It refuses unless the files the code hash covers are exactly
that commit's, file for file (ignored files included), except `uv.lock`, which must be that
commit's or the approved one of `5e5f40a`.
- **The pinned hash authenticates historical record lookup. The replay runs in the current
  environment; matching recorded outputs establishes agreement only for the quantities and
  tolerances checked** (Codex, r0r1 round 2). The update changed soupsieve and tornado, which
  reach the tree transitively (astroquery and lightkurve through beautifulsoup4; lightkurve
  through bokeh), not through a direct import.
- Each summary holds both code hashes; each case records the interpreter and numerical-package
  versions used by its replay.

**Per injection** (`t2ae_census.py`):
- **The placebo error.** E4 itself (`battery_b1.a0_depth_quadratic`, unchanged, with its own
  seed) at **24 evenly spaced phase shifts** of the candidate's epoch. A shift is dropped when its
  windows come within two outer half-widths of phase 0, or of phase 0.5 when E4 excludes it. The
  sample standard deviation of the usable rises is multiplied by `sqrt((K − 1)/χ²_0.05(K − 1))`.
  **The χ² multiplier is a heuristic inflation here; 95 % coverage has not been established for
  these dependent placebo estimates.** **At least 12 usable shifts**, otherwise UNK. Each shift's
  phase, value and status are kept.
- **The white floor**, reported apart: **the minimum-variance combination of the declared E4
  window contrasts under the stated white-noise approximation** (the series' own first-difference
  scale). It is not the best performance of any estimator, and it does not model the detrending's
  effect on the noise. A non-finite or non-positive scale or variance is UNK, checked before any
  division.
- **The power**: `battery_v51_diff.background_block`, unchanged (step 6's `power`), with the
  inflated placebo error. **Freed** means a power of at least 0.95.

**The frozen outcome** (`r1_common.t2ae_outcome`), with V the valid injections:
- **`INCONCLUSIVE (too many unknown)`** if more than 20 of the 102 are UNK;
- otherwise **"an error model is worth building"** if freed ≥ 0.8 V;
- **"empirically limited for this estimator class"** if freed ≤ 0.4 V;
- **"unresolved"** otherwise.

**The sample from the band.** The population is fixed at 102 (all of them). The rule's two
thresholds are 0.8 V and 0.4 V; with V near 102 they are about 82 and 41 injections apart from
each other, so a census, not a sample, decides.

**The decision-value test.**

| Outcome | Prior (opinion) | The next action at G1 |
|---|---|---|
| empirically limited | about 55 to 70 % | T2a's route depends on R2's design questions (the background alternative); a class-scoped reading is possible, labelled so |
| an error model is worth building | about 10 to 20 % | an error model of the background is worth designing in R2 |
| unresolved or INCONCLUSIVE | about 20 to 30 % | G1 weighs a pause of T2a |

Two outcomes are plausible and each changes the next action: **it passes the test**, narrowly.
Its value is mostly in telling "limited" from "unresolved".

**The futility rule** (`r1_common.t2ae_futile_after_first`): after stage 1, stage 2 runs only if
the UNK do not already pass 20 and some completion of the other 51 can still reach a decisive
outcome. Otherwise stage 2 is refused and the outcome is read from stage 1.

**Stops** (step 6's, plus I1's record): a replay that does not reproduce B1's record, an
injection not class (i) with `background`, nothing captured, a recorded error or power that
differs from the replay or from I1's historical record, a missing I1 record, or the pinned code
hash refused: the driver stops dispatching, and the stop goes to the maintainer.

**Its limits.** It measures E4's error on the host's own background at placebo epochs, so it is
about **this estimator class**, never "any estimator" and never the 615 injections. The placebo
shifts share the host's correlated noise, so they are not independent draws; the inflation does
not correct for that.

**Left out, by the decision-value test.** The proposal's two extra counts (a replay of
`period_alias`'s 17 losses, and the hosts lacking a catalog radius or mass) change no action at
G1. They are left to R2 or R3, where D1's test on real hosts would need them.

## T3b's recount

**The question.** Has the approved frame (v0.5's C3 false-positive pool) grown enough to give a
new confirmation sample its 11 domain-eligible stars?

**Disclosure.** v0.5.4's count (2026-11-12) found 2 of the 22 unwalked stars domain-eligible, a
shortage of 9 ([`t3b-count.json`](../validation/v0.5.4/t3b-count.json)). **Availability may
change through domain-eligibility metadata without a new TOI table** (a star's TIC fields or its
SPOC products), as well as through a later table (new dispositions or catalogue membership).
**A recount of the frozen frame and the construction of an updated frame are distinct
operations** (Codex, r0r1 round 1).

**The decision-value test.** Either operation would have to find **9 more domain-eligible stars**
eight days after the last count. The proposal's opinion is a probability below 10 % within three
months; over days it is lower. Only one outcome ("still short") is plausible, and it changes no
action at G1. **It fails the test, so it does not run in R1, and no code is frozen for it.** This
deferral is not a present finding of unavailability.

**When it would run.** Only if G1 decides to write T3b's **sample-availability record**, which
needs a dated recount below 11. Then the recount is an input to that record, not a check, with a
design of its own:
- **a recount of the frozen frame** (the 63, 41 walked, 11 accepted, 22 left, and the frozen
  inputs' hashes): v0.5.4's `t3b_count.py` as it is, metadata only;
- **an updated frame** from a later TOI table: a new provenance design (the table's URL,
  retrieval date and SHA-256, the v0.4 domain unchanged) and its authorization. The old script
  does not accept a new table, since it requires the frozen inputs. The table's data-use status
  under ExoFOP's policy is **not confirmed**
  ([`sources/v0.5.4-exofop-data-use-policy-2026-11-12.md`](sources/v0.5.4-exofop-data-use-policy-2026-11-12.md));
  that is the maintainer's call at G1.

## What R1 cannot do

- Adopt a variant, decide a design question, write a draft or a record, build a test, change a
  threshold after its data, or reuse confirmation data. Each is a stop that is not delegated.
- Run before the maintainer's release, which follows the independent review.
- Pass a cap: at 80 % of any cap of v0.5.5 (`ledger_v055.check_caps`, at each step's
  boundaries), it stops. The stop thresholds are 64 CPU-h, 2.4 M review tokens, 7.2 agent-days
  and 4 GB of disk.
