# T3b's sample-availability record (2026-11-22)

**Adopted by the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-22 (09:00), item 2 (#218).** This record is made
from the draft
[`v0.5.6-t3b-availability-record-DRAFT.md`](v0.5.6-t3b-availability-record-DRAFT.md). That draft
and the earlier one
([`v0.5.5-t3b-availability-record-DRAFT.md`](v0.5.5-t3b-availability-record-DRAFT.md)) stay
unchanged, as history.

**This is not an infeasibility record.** It is a dated statement about the availability of a
sample under one frame rule. **T3b stays deferred, `INCONCLUSIVE`**: no target is relaxed, no
verdict changes, and v0.5 stays `WEAKENED`. `method_status: not_validated`.

## 1. The target

T3b: in a newly drawn sample of known false positives (eclipsing binaries), at most a frozen
number SURVIVED, with every valid, identity-qualified outcome of the group. A new confirmation
sample needs **11** domain-eligible stars never used before
([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)).

## 2. The frame rule and the domain

- **Frame rule:** v0.5's C3 false-positive pool, i.e. TOI rows with TFOPWG disposition `FP` whose
  TIC is in TESS-EB, without the targets of v0.1 to v0.4.
- **Domain:** v0.4's D-1 to D-9, with S_max 107.

- **Pool:** 63 members. The 11 targets of v0.5 were excluded, leaving 52, triaged in ascending TIC.

- **Use of the disposition:** the TOI table's disposition is used as NExScI's answer of 2026-11-22

## 4. The statement

On 2026-11-22, neither v0.5's frozen frame nor the updated frame (the TOI table of that date) can
supply the 11 domain-eligible stars that a new T3b confirmation sample needs. Each frame supplies
2, a shortage of 9. **This is a dated availability statement about one frame rule. It is not a
statement that T3b is infeasible, and it is not an infeasibility record.**

## 5. Its limits

- **One frame rule only.** Both counts use v0.5's C3 rule (FP and TESS-EB). An amended frame was
  not counted; for example, another eclipsing-binary catalogue or another disposition. Amending
  the frame would be a design decision.
- **Time-dependent.** New TESS sectors, SPOC products or dispositions can change the count. The
  statement holds for its date.
- **Not an infeasibility record.** It does not meet, and does not claim, the standard of the
  charter's (c) and amendment 1's (e): scope, a population bound, two seed sets, and so on.
- **The pool was examined** for T3b's planning in v0.5.2, so any later sample from it is
  prospective, not blinded.

## 6. What would change it

- An amended frame argued in R2 (the questions draft, D3 (b)), which is a design decision.
- Time: a recount at a later date.
