hashes, the seeds, the exclusions, the treatment of unknowns, the numerical criteria, the outcomes
and the full budget.

- a defect, or a reproduction that diverges;

## Corrections to the audit's sections 2, 3 and 6 that bear on the plan

- **D1, "the density channel alone":** the near-twin share of EB1 is a property of the generator,
  not measured in the eligible population, and not shown to drive D1's failure. A failure of the
  proposed test bounds that test only.
- **D2, "a floor of up to 21" and "a coin flip":** 21 is a sum of heterogeneous marginals, not a
  shown floor. The 3 REFUTED come from the current estimator; a warning FAIL may keep retention;
  the coin flip was not measured.
- **T2a, "certification above 20 needed":** it is one way to widen the margin, not a logical
  requirement of every PASS (B still admits 552 of 559). The 24 non-grid class-(ii) losses are
  not shown unrecoverable by their messages alone.
- **D4, "nothing needed before an acceptance":** D4 passed in forecasts and is still pending
  acceptance, including the effect of anything built.
- **I4 and E2's gap:** I4 constrains the specific nuisances, under a known trend and covariance,
  on the valid cases. The 1.44 comes from 28 finite ratios on earlier losses, not from the 177
  fresh planets. It does not show that all 25 current losses are recoverable, nor refute the
  broader hypothesis about the shape.
- **Section 6's thresholds** ("a floor of about 18", "four simultaneous estimators", "well below
  one half", "more than 3,500 CPU-h") are strategic judgments, not frozen rules or measured
  probabilities. The certification subtotal starts at about 3,448 CPU-h, with unsized parts on
  top.
