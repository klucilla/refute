# v0.5.5: T3b's preparation (the dated recount of the frozen frame; the updated frame as a design)

**The decision.** The maintainer's decisions of 2026-11-21 (13:30) at G1, item 5 (#218): "T3b,
preparation only: the dated recount of domain-eligible stars (the source and reading date of each
fact; ExoFOP and the NASA Exoplanet Archive) and the state of ExoFOP's data use, with a record in
`sources/` (#34) or marked unconfirmed. Nothing is recorded as infeasibility or availability: the
record is a stop." `method_status: not_validated`. **A count, not a selection**: nothing is drawn,
ordered by randomness, chosen, adjudicated or run; no light curve, pixel file or follow-up note is
read. Codex's conditions (conversation r2choice): the recount of the frozen frame is kept apart from
any updated frame; **the frame, the domain, the threshold and the readings are fixed here, before
the data they read** (amendment 1 (e)10); each fact carries its source, version and effective
reading date; ExoFOP's data use stays unconfirmed where no applicable document is found.

## 1. Fixed before the data

- **The frame (frozen):** v0.5's C3 false-positive pool, the approved frame: TOI rows with TFOPWG
  disposition `FP` whose TIC is in TESS-EB, without the targets of v0.1 to v0.4. It is rebuilt from
  v0.5's frozen inputs, each checked against its SHA-256 by `c3select.read_pools`:

  | Input | Source | Retrieved (UTC) |
  |---|---|---|
  | the TOI table | ExoFOP, https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/download_toi.php?sort=toi&output=csv | 2026-11-04T13:07:51Z |
  | `pscomppars` | the NASA Exoplanet Archive, its TAP service (the query in `calibration/v0.5/inputs.json`) | 2026-11-04T13:07:54Z |
  | TESS-EB | VizieR J/ApJS/258/16, its static edition (the v0.2 snapshot's content) | 2026-11-04T13:07:54Z |

  It must be **63 members, 41 walked by v0.5, 11 accepted by v0.5, 22 never walked**; anything
  else stops.
- **The domain:** v0.4's D-1 to D-9 through `pilot.triage`, unchanged, with v0.5's frozen S_max
  of 107; today's metadata: MAST's TIC catalog and MAST's SPOC 120-s light-curve and pixel-file
  listings.
- **The threshold:** **11** domain-eligible stars, the number a new confirmation sample would
  need ([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)).
- **The exclusion:** the 11 stars v0.5 accepted are earlier targets and are excluded from any new
  sample; they are not triaged.
- **The readings:**
  - **Reading A (comparability):** the 22 members v0.5's walk never reached, the same set as
    v0.5.4's count of 2026-11-12, with today's metadata.
  - **Reading B (the reading a future record would use):** every member not accepted by v0.5:
    the 22 and the 30 walked and rejected in 2026-11 (52 stars), with today's metadata, in **one
    simultaneous recount**. Reason: a star rejected by the domain in 2026-11 is not an earlier
    target and may meet the domain now (new sectors or products); counting it is the assumption
    **conservative toward feasibility** (charter (c)6). Caveat: the pool's metadata were examined
    for T3b's planning in v0.5.2, so a sample from it is prospective, not blinded
    ([`v0.5.3-design-questions.md`](v0.5.3-design-questions.md), section 4).
  - Each reading is reported as a count `k` of its stars accepted by the domain, the rejections by
    clause, and `max(0, 11 − k)`. **A shortage is a counted fact, not a record.**

## 2. The recount (to run after this design and its procedure on #218)

- **Script:** [`docs/validation/v0.5.5/t3b_recount.py`](../validation/v0.5.5/t3b_recount.py): v0.5.4's
  `t3b_count.py` provenance, logging and retry rules, unchanged, over reading B's 52 stars (which
  contain reading A's 22).
- **Metadata only:** MAST's TIC catalog and SPOC product listings; **no new access to ExoFOP**
  (the TOI table is v0.5's frozen copy); no light curve, pixel file or note is read.
- **Retries:** 5 attempts and 120 s of backoff per star; exhausted retries stop the recount; a
  failure is never a rejection.
- **Records:** per-star rows name TESS-EB members, under a restricted licence (#33), so they stay in
  `.cache/v055/t3b-recount/` with their SHA-256; the repository receives **counts only**
  (`docs/validation/v0.5.5/t3b-recount.json`), with each input's source and retrieval date and the
  recount's own UTC time.
- **Price:** 52 stars × 3 metadata calls, below 0.05 CPU-h (an estimate), charged to v0.5.5's
  charges; no CPU of the new battery version.

## 3. The updated frame (a design only; not run)

- **What it would be:** the same frame rule on a later TOI table. The NASA Exoplanet Archive serves
  a TOI table with the `tfopwg_disp` column, "updated approximately once a week based on the
  ExoFOP TOI list" ([`sources/v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md`](sources/v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md)),
  so a table could be read from the Archive's TAP service without a new access to ExoFOP.
- **What it needs before it runs:** a provenance design (the query, its URL, retrieval date and
  SHA-256; the same TESS-EB static edition; the same exclusions plus every target of v0.5), the
  v0.4 domain unchanged, and **the maintainer's authorization**: its content derives from ExoFOP's
  TOI list, and whether a TFOPWG disposition is "data supplied by other users" under ExoFOP's
  policy is **not confirmed**.

## 4. ExoFOP's data use

Re-read on 2026-11-21 (the record in `sources/` above): the policy is unchanged since 2023-03-27;
the disposition question is **not confirmed**; the TFOP publication policy and any terms for bulk
access were **not read** (unconfirmed).

## 5. What this preparation does not do

It records no availability and no infeasibility (a stop that is not delegated); it selects,
draws and adjudicates nothing; it does not build an updated frame; it changes nothing in T3b's
status (deferred, `INCONCLUSIVE`).
