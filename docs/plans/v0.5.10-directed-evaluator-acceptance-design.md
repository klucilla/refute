# The directed evaluator's synthetic acceptance: design (v0.5.10, part (b))

> **A design only** (exit criterion 3 of the [charter](v0.5.10-charter.md)). Nothing here is run,
> nothing in `src/` changes, and nothing is adopted. Exploratory; `method_status: not_validated`.
> A later phase that wants route (e) would pre-register this acceptance, with its numbers fixed
> before any run, and run it before any lock that uses the directed evaluator.

## Why it is needed

- **Route (e) depends on this evaluator.** Route (e) is a prospective audit of predicted transit
  windows in new sectors. It uses Refute's holdout evaluator in its directed mode: a claimed
  ephemeris, fixed before the data exist, checked only inside predicted and control windows.
- **The mode was never accepted.** The directed mode has been a separate pending item since
  v0.2.1 (ROADMAP.md, "Directed mode of the holdout"; corrections plan, revision 3, row B8).
- **The v0.2.1 closure names what was missing**
  (`docs/validation/v0.2.1/closing-2026-10-27.md`, lines 79 to 85):
  - the lot had **no false positives and no curves without a transit**;
  - the gauntlet and the global gate were not run;
  - **the ability to reject spurious signals was not measured**;
  - zero observed FAIL is not a zero error rate: the one-sided 95 % upper bounds of the FAIL
    fraction were 0.008 and 0.025;
  - "No text may say that the engine or the holdout is safe on this evidence."

  Each item below answers one of these.

## What is tested

The fixed-ephemeris path of `src/refute/packs/tess/holdout.py`, as it stands at the commit the
acceptance names:
- the predicted windows and their baseline bands (`_predict_windows`, `HiddenYear.window`,
  `local_baseline`);
- the control windows and the replica spans (`register_replicas`, `replica_phases`);
- the period-correction grid (`period_grid`, `best_dp_index`);
- the stacked depth (`_measure`).

The ephemeris enters as data, and no step searches for a new period.

## The synthetic cases (generated, never real TOIs)

Each case is a light curve split into a "known" part (the epoch the prediction comes from) and a
"future" part (one or more sectors read only through the evaluator's windows).

| Class | What it is | What a correct evaluator does |
|---|---|---|
| **Positives** | box or limb-darkened transits injected at the claimed ephemeris, over a grid of depth/noise (SNR per window), duration and number of windows covered | recovers the recurrence (PASS) at a rate that rises with SNR; the rate at each grid point is measured |
| **Nulls** | the same curves with **no** transit in the future part | does not report a recurrence (no PASS); measures the false-PASS rate |
| **Aliases** | a transit at P/2, 2P, P/3 or 3P, or at the claimed period with an epoch offset of half a period | does not credit the claimed ephemeris unless the windows genuinely contain the events; each alias type gets its own rate |
| **Ephemeris drift** | the true period differs from the claimed one by k·σ_P (k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 5), with σ_P from the claim | recovers inside the declared period-correction grid and refuses outside it; the boundary is measured |
| **Correlated artifacts** | red noise (a declared power spectrum), momentum-dump steps and scattered-light ramps placed at the predicted times, with no transit | no PASS from systematics; the false-PASS rate is measured per artifact type |
| **Eclipsing-binary mimics** | primary and secondary eclipses (odd/even depth difference), and a diluted eclipse | a recurrence is recorded (it repeats), and the result says recurrence, never "planet" |
| **Coverage loss** | future data with gaps covering 0 %, 25 %, 50 % and 75 % of the windows | below the declared minimum coverage the result is INCONCLUSIVE, never PASS; zero coverage is INCONCLUSIVE |

## What the acceptance fixes before any run

- **Control windows:** their number per transit window, and placement away from every predicted
  and aliased time (the replica spans of revision 4 as the starting point). The statistic is the
  stacked depth in the transit windows against the distribution in the control windows.
- **Timing uncertainty:** each window's half-width is a declared multiple of the propagated
  1-sigma, hypot(σ_T0, n·σ_P), plus the duration. A claim whose propagated σ exceeds a declared
  fraction of the period is refused as untestable.
  - The route (e) census shows why this matters (`docs/validation/v0.5.10/route-e.json`):
    eumemic's median 1-σ is about 7 minutes, the CTOIs' about 58 minutes, and the CTOIs' 90th
    percentile above a day.
- **Minimum coverage:** the fraction of predicted windows with data, and the number of covered
  events, below which the result is INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Product cutoff:** SPOC 2-minute light curves only, or FFI light curves too (TESS-SPOC 200 s).
  Each product needs its own acceptance; the cadence enters the window sampling.
- **Pass criteria of the acceptance itself**, each with its Wilson 95 % interval:
  - a minimum recovery at a declared SNR;
  - a maximum false-PASS rate on nulls, artifacts and aliases (for example, an upper bound below
    0.05);
  - correct refusal at zero coverage in every case.
- **Sample sizes:** chosen so that the false-PASS upper bound can fall below the declared ceiling.
  With zero events in n nulls, the one-sided 95 % bound is about 3/n, so 0.05 needs n ≥ 60 per
  class, and 0.01 needs n ≥ 300.

## Outputs and limits

- **Outputs:** per class and grid point, the counts, the rates with their intervals, and the
  coverage. The acceptance passes only if every pre-registered criterion is met.
- **Limits:**
  - **Recurrence, not nature.** A PASS says the signal recurred at the predicted times. An
    eclipsing binary passes too: route (e) audits recurrence, never planethood.
  - **Synthetic, not real.** The real future data may hold systematics outside the injected
    classes. The lock of a route-(e) claim keeps its own control windows on real data.
- **Cost:** a planning estimate only. A few thousand synthetic curves at the measured unit costs
  of v0.5.9 (about 60 CPU-seconds per evaluation) is a few tens of CPU-hours, to be capped in the
  phase that runs it.
