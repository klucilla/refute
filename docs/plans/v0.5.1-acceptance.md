## Conventions

- **Seeds.** Development: `int(SHA-256("refute-v051-dev:<case>:<i>")[:16], 16)`. Acceptance
  (unseen while building): `int(SHA-256("refute-v051-accept:<case>:<i>")[:16], 16)`; the second
  set of the combined gates: `refute-v051-accept2`. A test that reads an acceptance seed before its
  WP's code is frozen is a deviation, recorded in #208.
- **Binomial bands** (as v0.5): "FAILs at least at rate p" over N trials means a FAIL count at or
  above the lower 2.5 % quantile of Binomial(N, p); "false-failure rate at most a" means a count at
  or below the upper 97.5 % quantile of Binomial(N, a). N = 200 per case unless stated.
- **Noise models** (as v0.5): white Gaussian; correlated, the AR(1) of `synthetic.ar1_series` with
  `red_noise_ppm` half the white level and `tau` one hour; and a stellar trend (a sinusoid of
  amplitude uniform in [0, 2000] ppm, period uniform in [3, 10] d, as the C1 generator), alone and
  with the correlated noise. Injections enter before detrending.
- **Claim schema.** Every new parameter and test exists only with `discovery.version: 3`, in a new
  `corrections` section of the test plan (defaults = the values below). Claims of v0.1 to v0.5 give
  exactly their recorded outputs; the v0.5 battery (`discovery.version: 2`) is unchanged.
- A case passes when its "Must" holds. A **sabotage** check names a patched version of the code
  (applied inside the test, never committed) and the case it must make fail.

## A0: the empirical error of a statistic (shared by WP2, WP3 (a) and (d))

**What it is.** A statistic measured on the candidate's events (a per-event depth; a group mean;
the odd-even difference; the large-minus-core or SAP-minus-expected depth difference) is measured
again, by the same estimator on the same series, on **phase-shifted ephemerides**: the candidate's
period, duration and baseline bands, with the epoch moved by a fraction `phi` of the period, for
20 values of `phi` drawn with a seed (derived from the test name and the candidate, as WP1 of v0.5)
in [0.15, 0.35] and [0.65, 0.85], each at least one duration (in phase) from every other shift,
from 0 and from 0.5. Each shifted ephemeris has the candidate's event spacing, the same sectors and
nearly the same coverage, so the scatter of the statistic over the shifts contains the covariance
across events and sectors (correlated noise and detrending included) at the candidate's own
geometry; nothing is rescaled. A shift is valid when its events meet the statistic's own coverage
rule (the same number of measured events per group, or per event for a per-event statistic, as the
real ephemeris; otherwise it is not used). The **empirical error** of the statistic is the robust
standard deviation of its values over the valid shifts; with fewer than 10 valid shifts it is not
measured, and the test is INCONCLUSIVE (class `not_enough_data`). The error used is
`max(model error, empirical error)`. **Per-event** values (WP2) take, for each event `i`, the
scatter of the shifted event `i` (the event nearest in time); the per-event statistics of WP2 are
then validated against their false-failure bands in correlated noise at realistic spacing (rows 2.1
and 2.5), not assumed independent.

**Why.** v0.5 row 1.10 (per-event pulls sd 1.348 in correlated noise) and the WP3 diagnosis (two
measurements of the same photons treated as independent) are both errors of an assumed model; the
shifts measure the noise of the actual statistic at the actual geometry.

| # | Case | Must |
|---|---|---|

| A0.2 | group means (odd, even) and their difference on 400 curves per noise model, no transit, with 2, 3 and 4 sectors and unequal event coverage | the same bands |

## WP2: the per-event tests made decisive (#209)

The definitions of v0.5 (v0.5 acceptance, WP2) are kept; only the per-event errors change (A0).

- `single_event` (fatal): domain at least 3 covered, measured events; FAIL if the largest single
  event's SNR over the total SNR exceeds 0.88 (Kunimoto et al. 2025, III.11). Sensitivity: the v0.5
  rule (one event carries the whole signal, the others at zero, at the worst eligible event),
  simulated with the A0 errors.
- `event_consistency` (fatal): chi-square of the per-event depths about their weighted mean with
  the A0 errors; FAIL if p < 0.00135; sensitivity as v0.5 (one event off by a full depth, worst
  eligible event, non-central chi-square).

## WP3 (a): `aperture_depth` and `systematics` with the error of the difference

The rejection regions and the alternatives of v0.5 are kept. What changes is the error of the
difference, measured with A0 on the **difference series** itself:

- `aperture_depth`: the depth of the annulus (the large aperture's pixels minus the core's), and
  the large-minus-core depth difference, measured on the flux series of each; the difference's
  error is the A0 error of the large-minus-core depth difference measured at the pseudo-events.
  FAIL as v0.5 (difference at `max_sigma` with that error, and ratio at least `min_depth_ratio`).
  Sensitivity: the v0.5 simulation (alternative ratio `min_depth_ratio + 0.3`), with the core depth
  and the difference drawn from their measured errors and their measured correlation (from the
  pseudo-events).
- `systematics` (SAP): the series `SAP / median(SAP) - CROWDSAP x PDCSAP / median(PDCSAP)` per
  sector; its event depth is the SAP-minus-expected difference, its error the A0 error of that
  difference. FAIL as v0.5 (difference at `max_sap_sigma` and relative difference at least
  `max_sap_rel_diff`). **When SAP and PDCSAP are identical up to a constant factor in a sector**
  (no correction was applied there, as in synthetic data), there is no corrected flux to compare
  with, so the sub-check is **not applicable** in that sector (reported, never a PASS, never
  "detectable"); with no sector where it applies, the SAP sub-check is not applicable and the test
  rests on its other sub-checks (the registry makes SAP conditional; the dumps and background
  sub-checks stay required). A zero scatter over the shifts with a non-identical series is not
  zero error: the model error floor applies, and if both are zero or not estimable the sub-check
  is INCONCLUSIVE ("error not measurable").
- `systematics` (background): unchanged from v0.5 except its error, measured with A0 on the
  background series.

| 3a.3 | pulls of the large-minus-core and SAP-minus-expected differences on 400 transit-free windows per noise model | A0's bands |

## WP3 (b): `sinusoid` per harmonic, with the star's other variability modelled (#203)

The development diagnosis: on 75 development planets, `sinusoid` refused a PASS on 19, all with
a stellar trend; the worst harmonic's beta had median 5.75 with a trend and 1.08 without.

- **Sub-checks** P/2, P and 2P, each applicable when its period Q is below 20 d; a sub-check
  outside the domain is "not applicable" (never a PASS, never a FAIL); with no applicable
  sub-check the test is INCONCLUSIVE, class "outside the declared domain" (WP4). The registry
  declares the three as conditional with at least one required.
- **The joint model.** On the normalized, undetrended PDCSAP flux with the transits masked (one
  duration each side): per-sector offsets, the tested harmonic, and up to 3 extra sinusoids found
  by iterative prewhitening of the residuals (Lomb-Scargle peak, amplitude signal-to-noise above 4,
  frequency outside 1 / T_span of each tested harmonic); beta computed on the joint model's
  residuals (as v0.5).
- FAIL (warning) as v0.5: for an applicable harmonic, significance above 15 and semi-amplitude at
  least half the depth. Sensitivity per sub-check as v0.5 (alternative semi-amplitude = the depth),
  with the joint model's covariance.

## WP3 (d): `odd_even` with an efficient estimator (plan amendment 1)

The forecast: at the Cramer-Rao error, `odd_even` against the full depth is detectable for 98.9 %
of eligible development planets; the diagnosis: v0.5's group errors are about 20 % above it (on a
white-noise planet at SNR 10.3, 57 and 63 ppm per group against about 50), and `odd_even` alone
refused a PASS on 15 of 75 development planets (median SNR 11.3). The rule and its alternative
(#185) are kept; the estimator changes:

- each group's depth is the weighted least-squares amplitude of the candidate's trapezoid (the
  shape fitted once on all events; the group amplitudes the only free parameters) against the
  flattened curve's baseline (the transit-masked detrending), instead of a box mean against local
  baseline bands; the errors are A0's, on the same estimator at the pseudo-events.

| # | Case | Must |
|---|---|---|
| 3d.1 | white-noise planets at SNR 8, 10, 12, 20 | the estimator's group error at most 1.10 x the Cramer-Rao error (median over 200) |
| 3d.2 | 1.8 (a), (b), (c) of v0.5, each noise model | (a) FAIL at least 0.95; (b) never PASS; (c) false-failure at most 0.0027 |
| 3d.3 | group-mean pulls (A0.2) with this estimator | A0's bands |
| 3d.4 | a binary folded at half its period, depth ratios 0.5 and 0.7, SNR 15 and 25, 200 each | where the true difference is detectable (`(1 - r) depth / sigma >= 3 + 1.645`): FAILs at least at rate 0.95 (band); otherwise never PASS unless no failure is detected and the full-depth alternative is detectable; and, as non-regression, FAILs at least at the v0.5 code's rate on the same curves |

**Sabotage:** the box mean with local bands (v0.5) makes 3d.1 fail; model errors alone make 3d.3
fail in correlated noise.

## The eclipsing-binary route: `duration_excess` (warning, #10)

- **Measurement.** The total duration `T` of the candidate's trapezoid (the fit of WP3 (d)), and
  its error `sigma_T` from 20 injection-recoveries of the same trapezoid (the measured parameters,
  the same estimator) on A0's shifted ephemerides; at least 10 valid recoveries, else INCONCLUSIVE
  (`not_enough_data`).
- **Bound.** The circular-orbit maximum for a central transit (b = 0) at the found period,
  `T_max(P) = (P / pi) asin(min(1, (1 + k) / (a / R*)))`, `a / R*` from the TIC mass and radius by
  Kepler's third law, `k` the square root of the fitted depth. Its uncertainty from 2000 seeded
  draws of the radius, the mass (TIC errors, independent Gaussians truncated at positive values)
  and `k` (from the depth's A0 error); `sigma_Tmax` is their standard deviation. Missing radius,
  mass or their errors: INCONCLUSIVE (`missing_input`).
- **Decision.** Warning when `T - T_max(P) >= 3 sqrt(sigma_T^2 + sigma_Tmax^2)`: "duration X h is Y
  sigma above the circular-orbit maximum for the TIC star; an eccentric orbit, a wrong stellar
  radius or a binary at twice the period can do this". Never fatal; the report says a binary it
  flags is WEAKENED, not refuted.
- **Sensitivity rule.** Alternative `T_alt = T_max(2P)` (the same expression at twice the found
  period, with the same draws); detectable when `(T_alt - T_max(P)) / sqrt(sigma_T^2 +
  sigma_Tmax^2) >= 3 + 1.645` (the Gaussian closed form with the full uncertainty above).

| # | Case | Must |
|---|---|---|
| D.1 | pulls of `T` on 400 circular planets per noise model | A0's bands |
| D.2 | 400 circular planets, families P and PG of the C1 generator, acceptance seeds | false-warning count within the upper 97.5 % quantile of Binomial(400, 0.0027) |
| D.3 | binaries folded at half their period (C1 EB1 geometry, impact parameter 0 to 0.8), depth ratios 1.0, 0.9 and 0.7, target SNR 10, 20, 30, 200 each | per case, among the binaries whose `duration_excess` rule is detectable (the full uncertainty above): the warning at least at the lower 2.5 % quantile of Binomial(n, 0.95); the test expected to catch each case reported (`odd_even` for 0.7, `duration_excess` for 1.0 and 0.9) |
| D.4 | a star without a mass error; without a radius | INCONCLUSIVE, class "missing input" |
| D.5 | 1.8 (a), (b), (c) of v0.5 with the rule above | (a) warning at least 0.95; (b) never PASS; (c) false-warning at most 0.0027 |

**Sabotage:** without `sigma_Tmax`, D.2 fails; with the model duration error of the v0.4 trapezoid
(not accepted: coverage 0.54), D.1 fails.

## WP4: INCONCLUSIVE classes (#201, part of #205)

Every INCONCLUSIVE test result of a version-3 claim carries one class: `outside_domain` (a
declared domain excludes the candidate before data are read), `insufficient_sensitivity` (the
engine refused a PASS), `missing_input` (a required input is absent: TIC parameters, pixel data,
WCS), `not_enough_data` (zero coverage, too few events or pseudo-events). The verdict's reasons
and the C1 to C3 tallies count them apart.

| 4.3 | the tallies | four verdict counts per battery and, for INCONCLUSIVE, per class |

## Combined gates (plan, section 3)

Run once, on frozen WP2 and WP3 code (with the EB route), on acceptance seeds `refute-v051-accept`,
100 curves per family of the C1 generator (P, PG, PV, PA, SE, V1, V2, ER, NR, PX; NW reported),

eligibility:

|---|---|---|
| G1 | SE, V1, V2, ER, NR | D1: SURVIVED / eligible upper Wilson bound at most 0.10, and not above the v0.4 count |
| G2 | PG, PV, PA | D3: REFUTED / eligible at most 0.10, upper bound at most 0.20 |
| G3 | PX | D5: REFUTED / eligible at least 0.80 |
| R | P (D2, D4), EB1 and EB2 are not run here (C1' measures them) | D2 and D4 on P reported, never a gate |

rule).

reported. A failed gate: the screening and the second set as the plan's section 3 says.

errors back), WP2's decisive per-event tests, WP3 (a), WP3 (b), WP3 (d), `duration_excess`.

## Literature (read on 2026-11-06 unless stated)

  value commonly used in the pulsation literature; one example of its use, read on 2026-11-06:
  "Detection of 75+ pulsation frequencies in the delta Scuti star FG Virginis",

  criterion to an earlier paper, which was not read; that attribution is not claimed here).

  read 2026-11-03).
- Thompson et al. 2018, ApJS 235, 38, A.4.1.4 (plan, section 12).
- Dawson and Johnson 2012, ApJ 756, 122 (plan, section 12).
- Seager and Mallén-Ornelas 2003, ApJ 585, 1038 (the circular-orbit duration).
