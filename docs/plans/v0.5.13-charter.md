## 1. The question

Can battery version 5 be **frozen** for a future confirmatory test (v0.6), with targets that tell
**"not assessed"** apart from **"wrong"**?

- "Frozen" means a candidate battery whose code, configuration and thresholds are hashed, and
  whose development-data behaviour on the three axes of section 5 is recorded.
- It is never a verdict, and it adopts nothing. Adopting it for v0.6 is the maintainer's decision,
  after this work and its gate.

## 3. Part A (v0.5.13): the steps, in this order

Each step ends with a check that a record can run. A failed check returns the stop to the
maintainer. The order is binding: a step starts only after the previous step's check passed, or
after its stop was answered.

### Step 0. The meter and the "before" proofs (#228, item e)

- **The meter is a Windows job object only.** Every unit runs in one job object, which counts
  the CPU of every descendant process, including terminated ones, and the launcher's own
  overhead.
- **The offline units** run with `UV_OFFLINE=1`, and their tests with pytest's
  `--disable-socket`. **There is no AppContainer** (amendment 1 of v0.5.12 and #228).
  - This is a weaker barrier than v0.5.12's design, and it is declared: a descendant that opens
    a socket outside pytest is not blocked.
  - The units are committed. Each records its argv, and network units are named (section 8).
- **The "before" proofs,** at the approval commit, before any step that may change `src/`:
  - `golden-local` on versions 1 to 4;
  - `claim-hashes`;
  - `verify_locked_tags.py` (10 of 10).
- **The demonstration, before step 1.** A CPU burn with a child process, and a create-and-delete
  and retained-output pair across units (the disk bound of section 9). Each outcome is in the
  journal.
- **The check:** `git diff --quiet <approval> -- src/`, and the demonstration's outcomes in the
  journal.

### Step 1. #226: the BLAS threads, decided without moving versions 1 to 4

- **The question:** can the BLAS thread count be pinned so that the bytes no longer depend on the
  machine's default, **without changing any byte of versions 1 to 4**?
  - The pin is set by environment (`OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS`)
    or by `threadpoolctl`, whichever is already importable. Neither may add a dependency.
- **The named verification NR-A1,** approved by this charter as a named rerun (Process diet 2,
  item 1).
  - **Reason:** to decide #226 before any lock.
  - **Procedure:** `golden-local` and `claim-hashes` on the synthetic fixtures, under three
    settings: the default, pinned to 1, and pinned to 4. Each payload is compared byte for byte
    with the "before" payload of step 0. Provenance fields are excluded.
  - **What triggers it:** this step, once.
  - **Repetition:** none, apart from section 8's classes.
- **The rule:**
  - If the payload pinned to 1 equals the "before" payload for all of versions 1 to 4, the pin to
    1 is **proposed**. Its `src/` diff (where the runner sets the pin) goes to the stop of section
    10, with Codex's review attached.
  - **If any byte of versions 1 to 4 differs under the pin: STOP.** Nothing is pinned, and the
    decision returns to the maintainer.
- **Also recorded:** the mechanism of the historical CI difference stays the declared limit of
  F-1. This step does not claim to find it.
- **The check:** the three payload files and their comparison are committed.

### Step 2. #227: assertions against the generating model, before U1

- **New tests,** written before any code, on the #223 cases (`docs/validation/v0.5.12/step2/`).
  Each value is asserted against the **generating model's parameters**, independently of
  `tests/v0512_oracles.py`:
  - the secondary's phase, within one grid step;
  - `delta_pri` and `delta_sec`, against the injected depths;
  - MES, against depth, σ and count.
- **The probe.** The case record is committed and **pushed with the trailer `CI: order-proof`**
  before the first test run. The probe's start checks that the record is in the pushed commit
  (#228, item 1).
- **A failing assertion** is a documented finding, never an edited expectation.
  - If the oracle is wrong, the oracle is corrected in a new file.
  - If `src/` is wrong, the correction is behind the `src/` stop.
- **The check:** every model assertion passes, or each failure has its record and its stop
  answered. Counts come from a JUnit XML file kept in git with its checker.

### Step 3. #224: F1 under correlated noise, before any use of F1 on real data

The field's standard is to inject synthetic transits into real light curves. The measurement is
pre-registered here:

- **(i) Synthetic families with correlated noise:**
  - AR(1) red noise at σ_r ∈ {3e-4, 1e-3} and τ ∈ {1 h, 6 h};
  - B6 included;
  - the v0.5.12 block-aligned red offsets, and offsets that are **not** aligned with the transit
    windows or the estimator's bins.

  Each family has 3,000 equal-depth null cases and 300 unequal-depth sensitivity cases, with new
  seeds whose input identities are checked against every prior record (the v0.5.12 rule).
- **(ii) Injection into real TESS light curves,** from **development or already-exposed targets
  only** (section 6):
  - **The stars:** 20 quiet and 20 active, with SPOC 2-minute light curves.
    - The **activity index** is fixed here, before any read: A = (RMS of the flux in 2-hour bins)
      / (median `flux_err` / √(points per bin)), on the first sector read.
    - A < 1.5 is quiet, A ≥ 3 is active. The stars in between are skipped, and logged.
    - The list is drawn from the exposed, no-TOI hosts (the proposal's DEV-H) by a committed
      selection with a committed seed. Only if DEV-H has too few stars does the list take exposed
      TOI hosts (DEV-T), with their recorded transits masked.
  - **The injections:** per star, 50 equal-depth (null) and 50 unequal-depth (depth ratio 0.6)
    box transits.
    - The depths are set by the expected SNR, in three bands: 10–15, 15–30 and > 30.
    - Periods are log-uniform from 1 to 15 days; epochs avoid gaps; the seeds are fixed.
    - In total, 2,000 null and 2,000 unequal per activity class.
- **(iii) The control rule with the red depth.** The sensitivity control adds the white term only
  today. The candidate rule adds a red term for the transits per parity:
  - the form: σ_Δ² = 1/Σw_odd + 1/Σw_even + σ_r² (1/N_odd + 1/N_even);
  - the time-correlated noise form follows the field's practice. Its source is to be recorded in
    a `sources/` record before use, and is **not confirmed** in this draft.

  It is measured beside today's rule, and **adopted only by a dated correction of the
  specification with a blind review** (below).
- **The rules, unchanged from v0.5.12** (no target is relaxed):
  - the null: the Wilson upper bound of F1's rejection rate is at most 1 %, per family and per
    activity class;
  - the sensitivity: at least 281 of 300 per cell for the synthetic families. The real-curve
    cells use the same proportion (0.9367), with a Wilson lower bound reported beside it.
- **If the estimator must change** (σ_r underestimated again, or the null fails), the change is a
  **dated correction of the specification**, with tests before code and a **blind review** before
  adoption, approved separately with its purpose, number and cost. Then it is measured again on
  **new seeds and new injections**.
- **The check:** each batch's identities are frozen and committed before its first F1 call; the
  results are recorded against the fixed rules.

### Step 4. The two "not assessed" buckets, emitted by the code

- **Every `unassessed` outcome** of version 5's screen and level 2 carries a reason, emitted by
  the code from a closed list. Each reason maps to exactly one bucket:
  - **`unassessed_data`:** the data cannot support the test (no product, too few points, missing
    sectors, the σ out of correction 1's range, no centroid data);
  - **`unassessed_power`:** the data exist, but the test lacks the power to decide at the signal's
    SNR (expected SNR below the test's gate, too few transits per parity, the MES floor).
- **Tests first,** then the code behind the `src/` stop.
- The list and the map are frozen in a committed file before any calibration (Part B).
- **The check:** every reason the code can emit is in the map. A test enumerates the emitting
  branches.

### Step 5. The seal

- **The "after" proofs:** `golden-local`, `claim-hashes` and `verify_locked_tags.py`. Payloads are
  equal byte for byte, unless step 1's stop approved otherwise.
- **Full CI:** `scripts/ci_evidence.py <sha> --level full`.
- **The seal:** a pushed SHA and a journal cursor.

### Part A's exit criteria

1. #226 decided by step 1's rule. Either the pin was proposed and the payloads are equal, or the
   stop was answered.
2. #227 covered by step 2. The model assertions pass or are recorded, and the order is proved by
   a push.
3. #224 measured by step 3's pre-registered rules. If a correction was needed, it passed its blind
   review and was measured again on new cases.
4. The two buckets are emitted by the code and mapped (step 4).
5. Versions 1 to 4 did not move, and full CI holds (step 5).
6. The caps were measured and met (section 9). Every execution outside the meter is recorded at
   once with its estimated cost, and every repetition has its approval or its pre-approved class.

## 5. The targets, fixed before any data (item b)

These are frozen at this charter's approval. A change is a dated amendment that states which
results were known. They are **proposed for v0.6** and used in Part B only as development-data
checks.

### 5.1 "Not assessed" against "wrong"

- An outcome is **assessed** (a decision), **`unassessed_data`** or **`unassessed_power`**
  (step 4). Neither bucket is ever a PASS. Zero coverage is `INCONCLUSIVE`.
- **Wrong** is defined on assessed cases only:
  - a false positive that **survives**;
  - a planet that is **not retained** (refuted).
- The buckets come only from the code's reasons, never from a reading after the fact.

### 5.2 The three axes, with the "AND" rule

All three must pass, together with the strata floors and the symmetry lock.

| Axis | Statistic | Rule | Proposed value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Safety** | FPs that survive / FPs assessed | Wilson upper bound (one-sided 95 %) ≤ **X** | **X = 0.25** |
| **Sensitivity** | planets retained / planets assessed | Wilson lower bound (one-sided 95 %) ≥ **Y** | **Y = 0.80** |
| **Coverage** | assessed / (all − `unassessed_data`), per arm | Wilson lower bound (one-sided 95 %) ≥ **Z** | **Z = 0.70** |

- `unassessed_power` counts **against** coverage.
- `unassessed_data` is reported per arm, and its share is bounded by the symmetry lock.

### 5.3 Floors by stratum

- **The strata:** three bands of expected SNR, with the cut points fixed from the development
  pools' distribution in Part B, **before** any v0.6 data.
- **The floors per band:**
  - safety: the Wilson upper bound is at most 0.40;
  - sensitivity: the Wilson lower bound is at least 0.65.

### 5.4 The symmetry lock

- **The statistic:** the abstention rate, both buckets together, on planets minus that on false
  positives.
- **The rule:** the upper end of the 90 % Newcombe interval (method 10) of the difference's
  absolute value is at most **D = 0.15**.
- **What it prevents:** the battery cannot meet safety by abstaining on false positives, or meet
  sensitivity by abstaining on planets.

### 5.5 The power calculation (sized on the v0.6 sample)

The sample is route (d1)'s, from v0.5.10's census: **171 ground-discovered planets** (the strict
count) and **307 KELT false positives**, both with SPOC 2-minute data. The calculation is exact
binomial, deterministic and offline: [power.py](v0.5.13-charter-prep/power.py), with its output in
[power.json](v0.5.13-charter-prep/power.json) and [power.md](v0.5.13-charter-prep/power.md).

**The design alternatives** (power 0.80, all cases assessed):

| Rule | Passes with 80 % power when the true rate is |
|---|---|
| Safety, X = 0.25 (307) | FP survival ≤ 0.191 |
| Safety, X = 0.25 (215, 70 % assessed) | FP survival ≤ 0.180 |
| Sensitivity, Y = 0.80 (171) | retention ≥ 0.872 |
| Sensitivity, Y = 0.80 (120, 70 % assessed) | retention ≥ 0.887 |
| Coverage, Z = 0.70 (171 / 307) | assessed share ≥ 0.784 / 0.765 |
| Stratum, X_s = 0.40 (82) | FP survival ≤ 0.270 |
| Stratum, Y_s = 0.65 (46) | retention ≥ 0.819 |
| Symmetry, D = 0.15 | power 0.98 at 20 % abstention in both arms |

**The joint power.** In the stated scenario (FP survival 0.15, retention 0.90, 85 % assessed, 15 %
abstention in both arms), the joint probability of passing all rules is **0.953**. This treats the
parts as independent, which is a declared limit: they share cases.

**The declared risk.** The safety scenario is an assumption. Of the 1,128 KELT-FP rows, 307 are
single-lined spectroscopic binaries (SB1), whose transits can look like a planet's in photometry.
The type mix of the 307 usable targets is not counted here. If photometry cannot reject SB1s,
X = 0.25 may be unreachable. Part B must then report the safety axis per FP type, and the
maintainer decides before v0.6 whether the target is set per type. **No target is relaxed after
data.**

## 8. The lighter process (#228, item e)

- **The meter:**
  - the job object only, with `UV_OFFLINE=1` and `--disable-socket`, and no AppContainer
    (step 0);
  - the network units are named: `git push`; `gh` for issue comments, run lists and CI
    evidence; the `workflow_dispatch` of step 5; MAST reads for step 3 (ii); Codex and gate
    traffic.
- **The caps criterion:** "the caps were measured and met; every execution outside the meter was
  recorded at once with its estimated cost". A gap is no longer automatically a failed criterion.
  It becomes one if it is not recorded at once, or if its estimated cost breaks a cap.
- **The pre-approved repetition classes.** These may be repeated without a new approval, because
  they give no case a new value:
  - (a) checkers and verifiers (`commit-check`, `ruff`, `ci_evidence.py`, `golden-local`,
    `claim-hashes`, `verify_locked_tags.py`);
  - (b) formatting;
  - (c) infrastructure failures (a network error, a killed process, a full disk), where the unit
    produced no output value for any case.

  Each repetition is journaled with its class. **Never for a scientific measurement**: a rerun of
  a measurement batch needs its approval in #218 first. The repetition's identity is the unit's
  logical identity, not its paths or tree state.
- **The order proof:** a push with the trailer `CI: order-proof` before each probe and each
  measurement batch. The batch's start checks that its record is in the pushed commit.
- **Decision records:** a decision in #218 cites the maintainer's pasted block, and declares no
  hour. The comment's timestamp is its time.
- **Codex:**
  - one round per closed question;
  - the review of every `src/` diff, attached to its stop;
  - **the final review of the phase's commits, in a new conversation.**
- **The gate:**
  - a fresh Claude Fable session, binding, given only the gate-review protocol and the phase name;
  - Codex as second opinion, in a new thread;
  - each launched with the maintainer's approval of its purpose, number and cost.
