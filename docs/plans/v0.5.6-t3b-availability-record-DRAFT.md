## What the record would state (if the maintainer adopts it)

1. **The target.** T3b: in a newly drawn sample of known false positives (eclipsing binaries), at
   most a frozen number SURVIVED, with every valid, identity-qualified outcome of the group. A new
   confirmation sample needs **11** domain-eligible stars never used before
   ([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)).
2. **The frame rule and the domain.** v0.5's C3 false-positive pool: TOI rows with TFOPWG
   disposition `FP` whose TIC is in TESS-EB, without the targets of v0.1 to v0.4. The domain is
   v0.4's D-1 to D-9 with S_max 107.
3. **The counts, as counted.**
   - **v0.5's frozen frame** ([`t3b-recount.json`](../validation/v0.5.5/t3b-recount.json), MAST
     metadata read on 2026-11-21): **2 domain-eligible, a shortage of 9**, in both readings (A and
     B of the earlier draft).
   - **The updated frame** ([`t3b-updated-count.json`](../validation/v0.5.6/t3b-updated-count.json),
     counted on 2026-11-22). The procedure was posted on #218 before the run; the design is
     [`v0.5.5-t3b-preparation.md`](v0.5.5-t3b-preparation.md), section 3, fixed before these data.
     - **Inputs:** the NASA Exoplanet Archive's TOI table and PSCompPars, retrieved on
       2026-11-22 at 01:57 UTC, and the same TESS-EB static edition, each SHA-256 recorded.
     - **Pool:** 63 members before the exclusion of v0.5's targets, 11 excluded, so **52**
       triaged in ascending TIC.
     - **Result:** **2 domain-eligible, a shortage of 9**.
     - **Rejected by clause:** D-1 16, D-4 19, D-5 1, D-6 5, D-7 5, D-8 4.
     - **Use of the disposition:** the TOI table's disposition is used as NExScI's answer of
       2026-11-22 allows
       ([`sources/v0.5.6-nexsci-answer-2026-11-22.md`](sources/v0.5.6-nexsci-answer-2026-11-22.md)).
4. **The statement.** On 2026-11-22, neither v0.5's frame nor the updated frame (the TOI table of
   that date) can supply the 11 domain-eligible stars that a new T3b confirmation sample needs.
   Each supplies 2. **This is a dated availability statement about one frame rule, not a statement
   that T3b is infeasible.**

## Its limits (stated in the record)

- **One frame rule only.** Both counts use v0.5's C3 rule (FP and TESS-EB). An amended frame, for
  example another eclipsing-binary catalogue or another disposition, was not counted, and would
  be a design decision.
- **Time-dependent.** New TESS sectors, SPOC products or dispositions can change the count; the
  statement holds for its date.
- **Not an infeasibility record:** it does not meet, and does not claim, the standard of the
  charter's (c) and amendment 1's (e) (scope, a population bound, two seed sets, and so on).
- **The pool was examined** for T3b's planning in v0.5.2; any later sample from it is prospective,
  not blinded.
- **Acknowledgment** (for any public text that uses it): "This research has made use of the
  Exoplanet Follow-up Observation Program (ExoFOP; DOI: 10.26134/ExoFOP5) website, which is
  operated by the California Institute of Technology, under contract with the National
  Aeronautics and Space Administration under the Exoplanet Exploration Program."
