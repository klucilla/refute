## Objections

1. **High, 3.3 can pass without checking its Must.** The design treats “every emitted severity is `warning`” as evidence for “no fatal FAIL,” but `check_sinusoid` is always a warning. The frozen v0.5 rows require no fatal FAIL from **any WP3 test** for unrelated-period coexistence, and at most a warning from WP3 for rotation-period coexistence. **Change required:** evaluate the relevant WP3 outcomes on both coexistence cases and apply those fatality limits to the emitted results. Report `sinusoid` FAIL counts separately. [Design](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/validation/v0.5.1/acceptance/wp3b-design.md:78), [frozen v0.5 rows](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/plans/v0.5-acceptance.md:200)

2. **High, the 3.5 sabotage description does not match the code’s domain behavior.** Lowering `sinusoid_harmonic_max_period_days` to 10 for candidates at P=12 or 15 leaves P/2 applicable. It makes P inapplicable, but does **not** make the whole test `INCONCLUSIVE outside_domain`; `check_sinusoid` returns that only when P/2 is also out of domain. **Change required:** make the sabotage implement the frozen v0.5 candidate-domain behavior if the claim is that P≥10 becomes `INCONCLUSIVE`, or describe and assert the actual failure mechanism, the missing required P block. [Design sabotage](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/validation/v0.5.1/acceptance/wp3b-design.md:117), [implementation](C:/Projetos/refute/src/refute/packs/tess/battery_v51.py:91), [frozen sabotage](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/plans/v0.5.1-acceptance.md:374)

3. **Medium, 3.6 does not construct a box found by the engine.** It supplies a candidate at the injected sinusoid’s period, epoch, duration, and chosen depth. That tests a truth candidate, while the row says the sinusoid is “found as a box.” **Change required:** derive the candidate through the version-3 search and finalization path for this construction, then state which resulting candidate is tested. [Design](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/validation/v0.5.1/acceptance/wp3b-design.md:97), [frozen row](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/plans/v0.5.1-acceptance.md:370)

4. **Medium, 3.7 only gates on the absence of a nearby extra frequency.** It reports the fitted P-harmonic amplitude, but does not require that the harmonic carries the injected trend, as the frozen Must says. **Change required:** define a pre-run criterion for the fitted harmonic’s relationship to the injected amplitude and include it in the Must, or narrow the claim and explain why the reported fit is sufficient evidence. [Design](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/validation/v0.5.1/acceptance/wp3b-design.md:102), [frozen row](C:/Projetos/refute/docs/plans/v0.5.1-acceptance.md:371)
