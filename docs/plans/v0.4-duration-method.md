# v0.4 WP4: method specification of the transit-duration uncertainty (issue #176)

Written on 2026-10-29 **before any WP4 code**, as case 4.1 of
[v0.4-acceptance.md](v0.4-acceptance.md) requires. The values below are fixed here; the test of
case 4.1 checks the implementation against them, never against values chosen after seeing its
results.

## Estimator

`refute.packs.tess.duration`, method `trapezoid-least-squares-1`:

1. Input: the detrended light curve that the gauntlet used for the final candidate (the
   transits masked during detrending), the candidate's period `P`, epoch `t0`, BLS duration
   `T_bls` and depth, and the per-point uncertainty `sigma` of the gauntlet.
2. Window: cadences with `|dt| <= 1.5 * T_bls`, where `dt` is the time from the nearest transit
   centre `t0 + n P`.
3. Model: a symmetric trapezoid in `dt` with four free parameters: the centre offset `c`, the
   flat-bottom depth `d`, the total duration `T` and the ingress duration `tau`
   (`0 < tau <= T / 2`); flux `1 - d * shape(dt - c)`.
4. Fit: `scipy.optimize.least_squares` (trust-region reflective, bounds as above and
   `0 < T <= 3 T_bls`), residuals divided by `sigma`, start at `c = 0`, `d` = the candidate's
   depth, `T = T_bls`, `tau = 0.15 T_bls`.
5. Uncertainty: the covariance `(J^T J)^-1` at the solution, scaled by the reduced chi-square
   when it is above 1; `duration_err_hours = 24 * sqrt(cov[T, T])`.
6. Nominal interval: `T +- duration_err` is a **68 %** interval.

## Estimability criterion (case 4.2)

The duration is estimable only if all hold; otherwise the exporter writes no number and states
which rule failed:

- the fit converged (`least_squares` status > 0) and the covariance is finite and positive;
- at least **10** in-window cadences fall inside the fitted transit (`|dt - c| < T / 2`), over at
  least **2** distinct transits;
- the fitted ingress is resolved: `tau >= 1` cadence (2 minutes for 120-s data);
- the relative error is at most **0.5**: `duration_err / T <= 0.5`;
- the fitted `T` is not at a bound of the fit.

Passing the gate is not enough (case 4.3): a significant signal can still fail these rules.

## Acceptance set (case 4.1)

- Synthetic curves: white noise only, 120-s cadence, two sectors of 27 days, a trapezoid
  transit with `P = 3.7 d`, true duration `T = 2.5 h`, ingress `0.15 T`.
- SNRs (whole-curve transit SNR, set through the depth at a fixed noise of 1000 ppm): **10, 20
  and 40**.
- Seeds: **0 to 199** for each SNR (200 trials per SNR).
- The fit receives the true period and epoch and `T_bls = T` rounded to the nearest of the
  default BLS durations; it does not see the true `T`.
- Pass rule, per SNR: among the trials judged estimable, the fraction whose interval contains the
  true `T` lies inside the binomial 95 % band around 0.68 for that number of trials (the Wilson
  interval of 0.68 at `n` trials, computed by the test), and at least 150 of the 200 trials are
  estimable at SNR 20 and 40.
- Case 4.2: a transit of 3 in-transit cadences (`T` of 6 minutes at 120-s) is not estimable, with
  the reason; a curve with one transit only is not estimable.
- Case 4.3: a gate-passing synthetic signal that fails a rule above gets a justified absence.
- Case 4.4: the exporter writes the value, `duration_method: trapezoid-least-squares-1` and its
  version, or the absence with its reason, and still says that nothing was submitted.
