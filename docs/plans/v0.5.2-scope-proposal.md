## The shape of the round: two stages, with a decision between them

**Stage 1, a bounded diagnostic.** The experiments E1 to E3 below and the design of the acceptance,
on development data only. Cap: **12 processor-hours and 1.5 agent-days**, binding from the
maintainer's authorization. It ends with a stop: a concrete acceptance specification, a task-count
and cost worksheet, and the choices the experiments did not settle.

**Stage 2, the correction**, only by the maintainer's decision on that stop: code, acceptance runs,
protocol. Its caps are set then. If the remaining design cannot satisfy the unchanged targets, the
work stops and reports that outcome.

## (a) The acceptance: a criterion fixed before the code

**The problem.** A row with n = 400 passes with probability 0.950 under perfect calibration, and A0
had 1,548 decidable rows: "pass every row" is extraordinarily unlikely even for perfect pulls. A0's
597 failed rows, against about 77 expected, were still real miscalibration. Amendment 11 authorizes
replacing "pass every row"; it does not authorize accepting a defective cell.

**Each operational cell earns its own permission** (coverage stratum x sector count, for each
statistic), under every tested noise model; a cell never inherits acceptance from its neighbours.
Permission requires equivalence in **every required construction and conditional branch applicable
to that cell** (the unequal-event patterns, spacings, sector-noise arrangements, heterogeneous
coverage correlations and both secondary-exclusion branches of A0.4 to A0.8), or a separately
justified worst-case guarantee; those checks are in the simultaneous family and in the worksheet, and
aggregate calibration cannot replace them.

1. **The tolerances stay:** population absolute mean pull at most 0.1 and population pull standard
   deviation in [0.9, 1.1].
2. **Equivalence, simultaneously.** A cell is *accepted* when simultaneous confidence intervals for
   its signed mean and its scale lie entirely inside those tolerances (one one-sided test per bound,
   two per parameter's equivalence interval; Lakens 2017). Simultaneity is over every cell and
   construction, both parameters and both tails, by a studentized max-t bootstrap with centred roots
   (Romano and Wolf 2005, whose guarantee is asymptotic and rests on its distributional and
   standard-error consistency assumptions: not a finite-sample guarantee), resampling **whole
   curves**, so that shared curves, shared events and missingness are preserved. A cell whose
   interval lies outside is *miscalibrated*; a cell whose interval straddles a bound, or with too few
   measurements, is *not measured*: both leave its statistics diagnostic and its decisive test
   INCONCLUSIVE. Failing to establish equivalence is not evidence of miscalibration, and the two
   classes are reported apart.
3. **Aggregate summaries are additional checks only**, and can only reject: per family (statistic x
   noise model), the concatenated mean and the concatenated variance
   `S^2 = sum_r [(n_r - 1) s_r^2 + n_r (m_r - m)^2] / (sum_r n_r - 1)`, within the same tolerances,
   with whole-curve bootstrap intervals.
4. **The specification of stage 1 fixes, before the procedure decides any permission:** the
   familywise error level, the simultaneous family and centred bootstrap roots; the standard-error
   estimation and its treatment inside the resamples; the resampling strata and the preservation of
   shared-curve membership across rows; weighting, missing observations, retention rules and the
   minimum number of independent curves; the bootstrap replication count, its quantile uncertainty
   and the moment assumptions that apply.
5. **The sizes follow, and are the main cost driver.** For independent unit-variance Gaussian pulls
   with a simultaneous critical value near 4, 1,600 trials give an interval half-width of exactly 0.1:
   an illustrative minimum for the interval width, and **insufficient for useful certification**,
   since a true zero mean would then be certified with probability zero. For the mean alone in one
   cell, about 2,790, 3,187 and 3,553 independent pulls give a certification probability of 80, 90
   and 95 %; requiring mean and scale, or many cells, raises it, and shared events mean that pull
   counts are not independent sample sizes. The worksheet states the desired certification
   probability, the distance from the tolerance bounds, the independent-curve counts, the clustering
   and the joint mean and scale performance. Its choices are more trials, fewer cells, or leaving some
   cells not measured. The tolerances are not widened to save cost.

**Known-answer validation of the procedure, before any use.** An exact control: a **fixed linear box
contrast on raw noise**, `D = a'e` and `Z = D / sqrt(a' Sigma a)`, with Gaussian noise of known
covariance `Sigma` (every generated component, and the generator's actual gaps and sectors), and the
coefficients and masks fixed independently of the noise. It validates the inference machinery in
that control experiment, **not the correctness of errors after adaptive production preprocessing**:
detrending estimates a trend from the flux and clips flux-dependent outliers, and any stronger
analytic claim needs its own derivation. Preregistered and measured, with Monte Carlo uncertainty and
fixed success criteria, separate seeds for estimation and evaluation, and a nested run for the
bootstrap's own quantile uncertainty:

- the simultaneous confidence-region coverage and the familywise probability of **incorrect
  permission**;
- the behaviour at and just beyond each mean and scale bound, including mixtures of correct and
  defective cells;
- the correct estimator's certification probability, apart from erroneous "miscalibrated"
  classifications.

The validation exercises the actual shared-curve layout, unequal contributions, unavailable
observations and retention rules, and adds known-distribution non-Gaussian controls relevant to the
statistics. Whether the bootstrap suits the relevant tails and the clustered design is justified and
checked, not assumed.

**Sabotages.** Specified one by one before the code: direction, minimum meaningful effect (the size
that would violate the criterion the protection exists for), uncertainty by paired whole-curve
resampling, and planned power. **The baseline must satisfy its criterion and the sabotage must violate
the criterion it protects**; a detectable but harmless change is not detection. Structural sabotages
stay deterministic invariants. Replacing or dropping a required protection is an explicit design
decision for the maintainer, not a record that its effect was small. (v0.5.1: `neff_equals_n`,
`pool_sectors` and `force_phase_half` were not distinguishable from their baseline.)

**The noise unknown at run time.** Acceptance stays under every tested noise model; the correction of
the causes ((b), (c)) is what should make that attainable. A run-time noise classifier is **cut from
this round**: an observable quantity is a noisy selector, and it would need its misclassification
calibrated.

**The sector counts.** The run-time quantity is the number of sectors holding **measured events**,
not of light-curve files. The domain is bounded and declared: the counts calibrated are an explicit
list, and a count between or beyond them needs a validated interpolation or worst-case envelope
before it is grouped. Unsupported counts stay INCONCLUSIVE, and the fraction of real stars affected
is reported. Adding 5, 8 and 12 sectors raises the sector total of the affected rows from 9 to 34,
a 3.78-fold exposure under linear scaling: it enters the worksheet, and the list is a choice made
there.

## (b) A0: the same mask for real and pseudo-windows

**Experiment E1 (development seeds, inside stage 1's cap), before any design.** On A0.1's and A0.2's
curves, the same curves in four arms: as built (real masked, pseudo unmasked); no mask for either;
real unmasked and pseudo masked (the crossover); both masked.

Frozen before E1: the mask width (`transit_mask_half_width_durations`, half-width T, as the pipeline
has it, not the transit core), the grid, the treatment of the other real masks, segment boundaries,
the detrending's window, quadratic blend and sector-wide clipping scale, and coverage. **A reference
implementation masks each pseudo-window separately** on a subsample; an interleaved "comb" (which
would make it affordable) is used only if it reproduces that reference within a fixed tolerance,
because disjoint windows do not have disjoint detrending influence.

**Reported, with uncertainties, on paired curves:** the change of the numerator variance, of the
estimated variance, of the bias and of the availability. **Decision rule:** the asymmetry is supported
as the mechanism if the both-masked arm brings the numerator variance and the estimated variance into
agreement within a tolerance fixed before the run, in correlated and long noise, without moving white
noise beyond that tolerance. The crossover is reported, and its direction is read as evidence, not
required as a fixed quantitative pattern. Failure of the specified pattern rejects that quantitative
explanation; it does not show the mask has no role. The result goes to the stop of stage 1.

## (c) The matched estimator: its selection inside the calibration

v0.5.1: the shape, epoch and duration are selected on the same data the depths are measured on; the
mean vanishes without the search. The decisive `matched_diff` fails on its scatter, which that does
not explain ((b) is the first suspect).

**The null.** The relevant null is **equal-depth transits with an unknown common shape and depth**,
not noise alone: a shape selected from transits has another distribution than one selected from
noise. E2 evaluates each candidate over signal strengths, shapes, noise models and observing
geometries, and repeats every selection and error-estimation step.

**Candidates (E2, development seeds, inside stage 1's cap):**
1. **Cross-fitting, as an experiment:** a common amplitude estimand; the shape from one parity
   measures the other. Both final group estimates still depend on both folds, so the whole
   cross-fitted difference is calibrated (template uncertainty, cross-fold covariance, the search
   upstream); the existing sum over events is not assumed valid. Cross-fitting: Chernozhukov et al.
   2018; this application is unproved.
2. **The search's candidate shape, with no additional fit.** It is data-selected upstream, so it is
   not an a priori shape; it removes one selection step.
3. **The same fit repeated on pseudo-ephemerides** (the maintainer's option; Kepler's inversion and
   scrambling, Thompson et al. 2018, are the precedent for a repeated search), **as a variance
   estimate only, and only if it shows both validity and affordable precision.** Random phases change
   event counts, parity allocation, gaps and weights. Calibrating a 0.0027 tail directly is out of
   reach here: about 1,109 independent replicates give a 95 % chance of one exceedance at that rate.
   A variance route needs a separately validated tail model. Otherwise this candidate is dropped.
4. **The fallback:** `odd_even` on the box estimator with A0's errors, which must pass its own
   acceptance and sensitivity checks before it is decisive.

**Frozen before E2:** the sample sizes, the required cells, the uncertainty criteria, the minimum
availability, the power aggregation over the alternative, and a tie-breaker; separate development
evaluation seeds. **The choice rule:** among the candidates meeting the population tolerances of (a)
on the equal-depth null, the highest power against the full-depth odd/even alternative. If none
qualifies, that is the result reported at the stop.

## (d) The sinusoid in noises with a stellar trend

WP3 (b)'s failures are all in the two noise models with a trend (3.2: 37 to 50 of 200 false failures
against 2). The trend in those models is itself a sinusoid of 3 to 10 days.

**No exclusion.** The target population stays, with planets under C1-like trends in its denominators.

**Experiment E3 (development seeds, inside stage 1's cap):** map the false failures against the
frequency separation from P, P/2 and 2P and the amplitude, and evaluate a joint nuisance model where
the separation is possible. Ambiguous cases stay INCONCLUSIVE and stay in the existing denominators;
that does not by itself meet the detectability requirement. A permitted nuisance sinusoid and the
alternative that produce the same observations cannot have both a 0.0027 rejection probability and
0.95 power: if the requirements conflict, the conflict is reported at the stop, for the maintainer
to decide on the approach. A narrowed test is never called the unchanged acceptance.

## (e) D1 must not pass vacuously

D1 stays as it is (upper Wilson bound of SURVIVED / eligible at most 0.10, and not above the v0.4
count). **Added, as further conditions for a PASS to count, both required:**

- REFUTED / eligible at least the paired v0.4 battery's REFUTED / eligible on the **new round's same
  eligible set**, minus 0.10;
- INCONCLUSIVE / eligible at most the paired v0.4 value on that set, plus 0.10.

The 0.10 is an empirical non-regression margin, the same as D2's, **for the maintainer's approval**.
Otherwise D1 is FAIL, with "vacuous" in its reason. The conditions can only remove a PASS. On v0.5's
record: EB1 v0.4 REFUTED 110 of 190, EB2 70 of 186; v0.5 62 and 69, with EB2 73 of 186 INCONCLUSIVE.
On the same record, all five families that passed D1 in v0.5 satisfy both conditions (Codex's check).

## (f) The real false-positive pool

**T3b keeps its population:** TFOPWG FP disposition **and** TESS-EB membership, with its eligibility,
identity and shortage rules, and its eight valid outcomes. v0.5's pool leaves about 22 stars never
walked against about 41 walked per confirmation (about 5.9 acceptances expected, 11 needed).

**In this round:**
1. Refresh the ExoFOP TOI table and rebuild the pool under the same rule (a stop: a selection of real
   targets), with an **inventory checkpoint before any selection**: overlaps, prior-use exclusions,
   available products and plausible yield. A nominal catalog size is not an eligible-pool inventory.
2. **If the pool is short, T3b is NOT DECIDABLE for that round** (the v0.5 shortage rule). The size is
   never reduced and no star is reused; the shortfall goes to the maintainer.
3. **Other sources are candidates for a maintainer's decision, not a substitute.** The TESS Ten
   Thousand Catalog (Kostov et al. 2025: 10,001 eclipsing binaries, machine-learning screening
   followed by photometric, photocenter and human vetting) is a plausible photometric source, but a
   catalog binary did not enter the TOI process and can be an easier control. Its use would be a
   separately named feasibility measurement that settles nothing about T3b. Replacing T3b's source
   needs an explicit population-equivalence argument and the maintainer's decision. A catalog-only
   confirmation campaign is **not** proposed.
4. Gaia DR3's eclipsing-binary candidates (Mowlavi et al. 2023) are **deferred**.

Terms (`docs/plans/sources/v0.5.2-candidate-sources-2026-11-08.md`): Gaia, CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO (S1); the
T10K catalog, **unconfirmed** (S3); ExoFOP, acknowledgment and citation, with its contributor-data
provisions to be distinguished from the use of the compiled table (S4); VizieR, **unconfirmed** (S5).
Article licenses, catalog terms and host-service policies are separate questions. Any new source
enters only with a license reading, a `sources/` record, a line in `docs/third-party-data.md`, a
`DATA_LICENSE` component, and downloads with URL, date and SHA-256 (5 attempts, 120 s).

## (g) The caps from the measured cost

**Authorized now, if the maintainer agrees: stage 1 only** (12 processor-hours, 1.5 agent-days; disk
within 2 GB). A probe may stop the work or trigger a new decision; it never makes spending authorized
afterwards.

**The worksheet is a deliverable of stage 1**, for the design actually proposed: a task count and a
cost per task, with probes over **all C1 families** and representative C2 and C3 workloads (longer
curves, the nested fits), the reference and known-answer runs, the larger cells of (a), the expanded
sector list, the combs, the experiments, diagnostics, compatibility checks, prefixes and reviews, a
disk cap, and the calibration and review reserves.

**What is measured so far, as an order of magnitude only:** A0's acceptance 101.9 processor-hours at
400 per row; WP3 (b) 15.58; a C1 curve through the paired version-3 chain 110 s (family P only, the
old chain); C2 in v0.5 39.13 hours for 100 stars; C1' at 110 s is 73 hours. The 62 hours of v0.5.1's
remaining acceptances was an allocation estimate, not a measurement. The previous-chain
extrapolation of the calibration was approximately 97 processor-hours; this is neither a measured
version-4 cost nor a lower bound. The worksheet distinguishes stage-1 measurements of existing code
or prototypes from projections for unimplemented corrections, and probes of the final frozen workload
come before full acceptance and confirmation, within caps already authorized. **Caps for stage 2 are
set from the worksheet, not here.** Agent-days and reviewer tokens
likewise. **The agent-day ledger is kept this time** (`.cache/v052/cost.jsonl`, each round's wall
time on the tracking issue): its absence is a recorded deviation of v0.5.1.
