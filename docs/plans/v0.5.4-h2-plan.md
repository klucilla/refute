# v0.5.4, second half: the plan (steps 1 to 7)

**The decision.** The maintainer's decisions of 2026-11-12 (night), items 4 and 7 (#217). The
second half is the plan of the audit's section 5, steps 1 to 7
([`v0.5.4-fable-audit.md`](v0.5.4-fable-audit.md)), adjusted by the maintainer and by Codex's
check ([`v0.5.4-audit-check.md`](v0.5.4-audit-check.md)). The frozen I2 and I3
([`v0.5.4-I2-I3-designs.md`](v0.5.4-I2-I3-designs.md)) stay as a record, superseded, and never
run. Step 8 is the maintainer's own decision. `method_status: not_validated`. Development data
only.

## Every step

Before any step reads data:
1. **Its rule is frozen** in a design under `docs/plans/v0.5.4-h2-step<N>-*.md`, with the
   population, denominators, versions and hashes, seeds, exclusions, the treatment of unknowns,
   the numerical criteria, the outcomes and the full budget.
2. **Its design and code are reviewed by Codex**, up to three rounds; a divergence after three
   goes to the more conservative option, recorded.
3. **Its exact procedure is posted on #217.**

**How it runs.**
- New parts use `budgeted_v3` (`65680a3`).
- Long runs start through `launch_detached.py`, the launcher of record since the restart test
  (decision 7b).
- If a part stops by the driver's mechanics, one completion part per step is pre-approved, with
  the same code and rows, reviewed by Codex as a diff, its procedure posted first (decision 7c).
- Codex reviews each step at its end.

**Non-delegated stops:**
- an infeasibility draft;
- a cap reached, or 80 % of a token cap;
- a defect;
- a rerun that does not reproduce its record;
- a need to change `src/`;
- a dependency or third-party code;
- a real target or confirmation data.

## The stop

The second half stops at the end of steps 1 to 7, or at the drain of 2026-11-18 19:30 with the
state saved, whichever comes first. The stop report gives:
- each step's result;
- the consumption;
- a recommendation between going on and pausing, by the criteria of the audit's section 6, as
  Codex's check qualified them: those are strategic judgments, not frozen rules.

The pause is at 20:00 on 18/11; work resumes only on the maintainer's release, after the
publication of 19/11.
