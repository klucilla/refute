# Open sources for v0.5.6's part A, as read on 2026-11-21

**Why this record.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-21 (21:00), item 4 (#218): v0.5.6's plan
uses nothing that depends on `tfopwg_disp` until NExScI answers. This record lists the open sources
the plan names, each with its URL, reading date and a short literal excerpt (#34). **It is not a
legal opinion, and it decides nothing.**

## 0. MAST: SPOC two-minute light curves

- The light curves the battery reads are SPOC's two-minute products at MAST, fetched through
  `lightkurve` as in every phase since v0.1 (the data adapter of the TESS pack). The MAST pages on
  their access and use were **not re-read for this record (not confirmed here)**.

## 1. MAST: SPOC threshold-crossing events (TCEs), bulk statistics

- **Page:** "TESS - TCE Bulk Downloads",
  https://archive.stsci.edu/tess/bulk_downloads/bulk_downloads_tce.html
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200).
- **Literal excerpt:** "Download all TESS threshold crossing event (TCE) statistics for a given
  Sector or Sector range ("multi-sector") as a .csv file."
- **The DV diagnostics these statistics come from** (the TESS Science Data Products Description
  Document, EXP-TESS-ARC-ICD-0014 Rev F,
  https://archive.stsci.edu/files/live/sites/mast/files/home/missions-and-data/active-missions/tess/_documents/EXP-TESS-ARC-ICD-TM-0014-Rev-F.pdf,
  read 2026-11-21 through a search service's excerpt): difference-image centroid offsets, e.g.
  "skyOffset | Value and uncertainty of magnitude of difference image centroid offset on the
  sky". **Which of these columns the bulk CSV carries is not confirmed** until a file is read.

## 2. The NASA Exoplanet Archive: confirmed planets with their TIC

- **Page:** "Planetary Systems and Planetary Systems Composite Parameters Data Column Definitions",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_PS_columns.html
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200); the page's own date "Last updated: 4 June
  2026".
- **Literal excerpts:** `tic_id`: "Name of the star as given by the TESS Input Catalog";
  `disc_facility`: "Name of facility of planet discovery observations".
- **Public data:** "All data in the NASA Exoplanet Archive are public"
  ([the companion record](v0.5.5-archive-toi-terms-2026-11-21.md), section 1).

## 3. The TOI table's columns other than `tfopwg_disp`

- **Page:** "TESS Objects of Interest Table Data Columns",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_TOI_columns.html (read 2026-11-21; see
  [`v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md`](v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md),
  section 2).
- **Use in v0.5.6 (Refute's reading, not decided):** the TOI identifier, TIC, period, epoch,
  duration and depth, read by a query that names its columns and leaves `tfopwg_disp` out. Whether
  the rest of the TOI list falls under the same open question as the disposition is **not
  confirmed**; the question sent to NExScI on 2026-11-21 was about the disposition. Their reuse is
  part of the maintainer's approval of part A. Before that approval, the ephemeris source, its
  required columns and its matching keys are recorded. SPOC's TCE statistics (section 1) are a
  fallback only if the match to the TOI (by TIC and by the period they carry) can be made with
  approved inputs; if the fallback needs a different sampling frame, that frame goes to the
  maintainer for approval first. `tfopwg_disp` itself is used only for the purpose NExScI's reply
  explicitly covers.

## 4. Context, not used as a label

- Guerrero et al. (2021), "The TESS Objects of Interest Catalog from the TESS Prime Mission",
  https://arxiv.org/html/2103.12538v2, read 2026-11-21 through a search service's excerpt: in the
  prime mission, "the TSO examined 55,281 TCEs from 27,822 two-minute targets".
- Giacalone et al. (2020), "Vetting of 384 TESS Objects of Interest with TRICERATOPS and
  Statistical Validation", https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00691 (read 2026-11-21, the abstract page's
  excerpt). A published per-TOI table could serve as a comparison; **its terms of reuse are not
  confirmed**, so the plan does not use it.
