# ExoFOP's data use policy, as read on 2026-11-12 (for T3b)

**Why this record.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (14:00), item 6 (#217). T3b's
recommendation A would use TESS dispositions and, where needed, TFOP observing notes hosted on
ExoFOP as evidence for an independent adjudication. This record keeps the policy's terms that bear
on that use, with short literal excerpts, as the rule of #34 asks for every legal or license
statement. **It is not a legal opinion, and nothing was adjudicated.**

**The source.**
- **Page:** "ExoFOP Data Use and Professional Conduct Policy",
  https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/pcp.php
- **Read on:** 2026-11-12, a live fetch (HTTP 200).
- **The page's own date:** "Updated: 27 March 2023".

**Short literal excerpts** (each verbatim from the page):

1. **The operator:** "ExoFOP is operated by the NASA Exoplanet Science Institute (NExScI)."
2. **TFOP data:** "may be subject to a 12 month proprietary period."
3. **Before public release:** the use "must follow the guidelines outlined by the TESS Follow-Up
   Observation Program Publication Policy".
4. **Ownership:** "any data associated with a given data provider should be considered their
   property".
5. **Contact first:** "the user should contact the data owner" before uploaded data "are utilized".
6. **Acknowledgment or co-authorship:** "as negotiated bilaterally between the data user and the
   data owner".
7. **Unpublished data:** "can be cited by the contributor name and the ExoFOP URL".
8. **The standard acknowledgment** begins: "This research has made use of the Exoplanet Follow-up
   Observation Program (ExoFOP; DOI: 10.26134/ExoFOP5)".

**What it implies for T3b** (Refute's reading, for the maintainer; not decided):
- **Before any use of a user-uploaded note or file as evidence,** the policy asks that its uploader
  be contacted about its quality and use (excerpt 5). That is a human step. Refute never sends a
  message automatically, so the maintainer would do it.
- **Proprietary TFOP data** (excerpt 2) would be used only after public release, or under the TFOP
  publication policy (excerpt 3).
- **Ownership and credit** (excerpts 4, 6, 7): citation, acknowledgment or co-authorship are
  negotiated with the data owner. ExoFOP's standard acknowledgment (excerpt 8) goes in any
  published material that uses ExoFOP's services.
- **The TESS dispositions themselves** (the TOI catalogue's disposition column) are a different
  question from user-uploaded data. The policy's guidelines speak of "data supplied by other users".
  Whether a disposition counts as such data **is not confirmed** by this page.
