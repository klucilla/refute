# NExScI's answer on the use of the TFOPWG disposition (`tfopwg_disp`), received 2026-11-22

**Why this record.** The maintainer's addendum of 2026-11-21 (22:45) to the 22:30 decisions,
item 1 (#218). The maintainer asked NExScI on 2026-11-21 whether the TOI table's TFOPWG
disposition column may be used in Refute's project. This record keeps the answer under the rule of
#34 (source, date, short literal excerpts). **It is not a legal opinion.** By the decision, it
holds no name, e-mail address or message identifier.

## The answer

- **Source:** an e-mail answer from the NASA Exoplanet Science Institute (NExScI), on NExScI's
  behalf, to the maintainer's question. The maintainer forwarded it into the Claude Code session.
- **Received:** 2026-11-22, 00:25 UTC.
- **Literal excerpts** (verbatim, as the maintainer gave them):
  1. "This column is fine to use in your project."
  2. "Dispositions do not count and you do not need to ask TFOP for permission."
- **The acknowledgment to include**, verbatim:
  > "This research has made use of the Exoplanet Follow-up Observation Program (ExoFOP; DOI:
  > 10.26134/ExoFOP5) website, which is operated by the California Institute of Technology,
  > under contract with the National Aeronautics and Space Administration under the Exoplanet
  > Exploration Program."

## What it settles (Refute's reading, with the decision)

- **Use in Refute's project:** the use of `tfopwg_disp` (the column the question named) in
  Refute's project is confirmed by NExScI. This covers T3b's aggregate count and the descriptive
  use in v0.5.6. The maintainer's addendum, item 2 (T3b) and item 3 (v0.5.6), sets how it is used.
- **ExoFOP's data-use policy:** by excerpt 2, dispositions are not among the data for which TFOP's
  permission is needed.
- **The acknowledgment:** it goes into every future public material that uses ExoFOP or the TOI
  table (a public report, the site), as part of the text the maintainer approves (the addendum,
  item 4).
