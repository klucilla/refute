# Typical TESS photometric uncertainties (source record, v0.5.11 spec correction 1)

Read on 2026-11-25 by the agent, for the input range of
[v0.5.11-spec-correction-1.md](../v0.5.11-spec-correction-1.md).

**Source:** NASA HEASARC, TESS Science Support Center, "TESS Telescope Information", section
"Typical Noise Level". URL: https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/tess/telescope_information.html
(retrieved 2026-11-25; the page's `dc.date.modified` metadata reads 2026-11-17).

**Short literal excerpts:**
- "at the level of 60 ppm on hourly timescales"
- "For fainter stars with Tmag = 16, the photometric precision drops to about 1%"

**What the correction takes from it** (the agent's own arithmetic, labelled as such):
- These are 1-hour precisions of normalized flux: 60 ppm = 6e-5 and 1% = 1e-2.
- Per cadence, under white-noise scaling (√ of the number of cadences per hour; an assumption,
  not a statement of the source):
  - 2-minute cadence (30 per hour): about 6e-5 × √30 ≈ 3.3e-4 and 1e-2 × √30 ≈ 5.5e-2;
  - 20-second cadence (180 per hour): about 1e-2 × √180 ≈ 0.13 for Tmag = 16.
- So typical per-cadence σ in normalized flux lie roughly between 1e-4 and 1e-1, to an order
  of magnitude.

Nothing else on the page is used.
