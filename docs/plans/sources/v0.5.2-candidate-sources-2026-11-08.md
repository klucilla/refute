## S3. The TESS Ten Thousand Catalog

- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05631 (Kostov et al.; abstract), published as ApJS 279, 50,
  https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2025ApJS..279...50K/abstract (search result only).
- Excerpt: "we present a catalog of 10001 uniformly-vetted and -validated eclipsing binary stars".
- Excerpt: "We combed Sectors 1-82 of TESS Full-Frame Image data".
- Method, as the abstract states it: a neural network identified eclipse-like features; about 60,000
  targets were analysed by automated methods and manual inspection by citizen scientists; the 10,001
  passed ephemeris and photocenter tests and visual inspection. This does not establish that each
  entry is a TOI false positive or a spectroscopically confirmed binary.
- Its VizieR entry is listed by a search as J/ApJS/279/50 (https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/ApJS/279/50);
  the page did not load on 2026-11-08 (`ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED`).
- Excerpt (the arXiv full text, read 2026-11-08): "All our data products and results are publicly
  available as machine-readable online supplements."
- URL of the full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05631v1 (read 2026-11-08). In its positional
  comparison with known systems the article lists "∼10,000 planet candidates from ExoFOP-TESS", among
  the catalogs queried within one TESS pixel. It does not state how many of the 10,001 entries are TOIs,
  or TOI false positives: the overlap needed by T3b is **unknown** from the text.
- The article also provides about 900,000 unvetted targets as an auxiliary data set; those are not the
  vetted catalog.
- **Unconfirmed:** the catalog's license or terms of use. No statement of them was read.
