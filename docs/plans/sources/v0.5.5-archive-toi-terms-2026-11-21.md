## 1. The Archive: its data are public

- **Page:** "Exoplanet Archive User Login",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/applications/Login/
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200).
- **Literal excerpt:** "All data in the NASA Exoplanet Archive are public".
- **The TOI table is one of the Archive's tables**: "TESS Objects of Interest Table Data Columns",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_TOI_columns.html (read 2026-11-21), with the
  column `tfopwg_disp` (TFOPWG Disposition).

## 2. The Archive's and ExoFOP's own description of ExoFOP's policy

- **Source:** Christiansen et al. (2025), "The NASA Exoplanet Archive and Exoplanet Follow-up
  Observing Program: Data, Tools, and Usage", *Planetary Science Journal* 6, 186;
  https://arxiv.org/html/2506.03299v1 (the arXiv version), read 2026-11-21 through a search
  service's excerpt of the page and a live extraction.
- **Literal excerpts** (from the search service's verbatim excerpt of the page):
  1. "The NEA and ExoFOP are freely available services for the exoplanet community."
  2. ExoFOP's policy "provides direction on acknowledgment, citation and/or co-authorship for use
     of data uploaded to ExoFOP".
- **On how the Archive's TOI table is filled:** the extraction tool reported that ExoFOP takes new
  and updated TOIs from the TESS Science Office and that the Archive updates its TOI table from
  ExoFOP's holdings. **The wording is not confirmed verbatim**; the Archive's TOI column page says
  "updated approximately once a week based on the ExoFOP TOI list" (the companion record).
- **Not found:** the extraction reported no restriction in the paper on using TFOPWG dispositions
  (**not confirmed** by a full reading).

## 3. The relation, as read (Refute's reading, for the maintainer; not decided)

- **What is confirmed by the sources:** the Archive states that all its data are public, and its
  TOI table, with `tfopwg_disp`, is one of its tables; the Archive asks for its standard
  acknowledgment in published material that uses its services (the companion record).
- **What ExoFOP's policy addresses:** by its own text and by the Archive's paper, the use of data
  **uploaded to ExoFOP** (follow-up observations by its users): contact with the data owner before
  use, credit and acknowledgment in publications.
- **What stays not confirmed:** whether a TFOPWG disposition shown in the TOI table is "data
  supplied by other users" in the sense of ExoFOP's policy; the TFOP Working Group Publication
  Policy (not read); any term for automated or bulk access.
- **For a count of frame membership** (rows of the public TOI table with `tfopwg_disp = FP`,
  joined with TESS-EB, then MAST metadata; no note, file or follow-up datum of ExoFOP read; no
  per-star result published): the sources above read as public catalogue use with the Archive's
  acknowledgment if published. Whether that settles item 5's condition ("the use for a COUNT
  confirmed") was checked by Codex before any design or run.

## 4. The outcome (Codex, conversation r2t3b, 2026-11-21)

**NOT CONFIRMED for T3b's count.** Codex's reading: the Archive's table is public but derives from
ExoFOP's TOI list; the records leave open whether `tfopwg_disp` falls under "data supplied by other
users"; using metadata only and publishing only a total narrows the scope but does not settle that
question. The TFOP publication policy, read afterwards (section 2a), does not address it; what
would settle it is an explicit confirmation from TFOP or NExScI, a human step that is the
maintainer's. **So the updated frame's count does not run** (the decisions' item 5), and a draft of
T3b's availability record is prepared instead.
