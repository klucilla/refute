## 2. The NASA Exoplanet Archive's TOI table

- **Page:** "TESS Objects of Interest Table Data Columns",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_TOI_columns.html
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200); the page's own date "Last updated: 22 September
  2025".
- **Facts, with short literal excerpts:**
  1. The Archive serves a TOI table with a `tfopwg_disp` column (values APC, CP, FA, FP, KP, PC).
  2. Its source and cadence: "updated approximately once a week based on the ExoFOP TOI list".
  3. It "may be slightly out of sync with the ExoFOP".

**What it implies for an updated frame** (Refute's reading, for the maintainer; not decided): an
updated frame could read its dispositions from the Archive's TOI table, without a new access to
ExoFOP, but **its content derives from ExoFOP's TOI list**, so the data-use question of section 1
is not settled by changing the host (**not confirmed**).

## 3. The NASA Exoplanet Archive's acknowledgment

- **Page:** "Acknowledging the NASA Exoplanet Archive in Publications",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/acknowledge.html
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200); the page's own date "Last updated: 13 August
  2025".
- **The request:** a standard acknowledgment "in any published material that makes use of the NASA
  Exoplanet Archive's services", and a direct acknowledgment of a specific literature reference
  when its data are used. The page states no other restriction on the use of the TOI table
  (**no other term confirmed** from this page).
