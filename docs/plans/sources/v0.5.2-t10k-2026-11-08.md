## The article and its data page

- Article: Kostov et al. 2025, ApJS 279, 50, doi:10.3847/1538-4365/ade2d8,
  https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4365/ade2d8
- Data page: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4365/ade2d8/data
- The data page lists three machine-readable tables: Table 2 (about 900,000 unvetted targets), Table 3
