# The Archive's TOI table for v0.5.6 part A: public data and the ephemerides' origin (2026-11-21)

**Why this record.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-21 (22:30), item 3 (#218): part A reads
the TOI table's ephemeris columns through a query that names its columns; this record gives the
Archive's statement that its data are public and the ephemerides' origin (#34). It is not a legal
opinion. NExScI's answer on the disposition column is in
[`v0.5.6-nexsci-answer-2026-11-22.md`](v0.5.6-nexsci-answer-2026-11-22.md).

## 1. The Archive's data are public

- **Page:** "Exoplanet Archive User Login", https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/applications/Login/
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200).
- **Literal excerpt:** "All data in the NASA Exoplanet Archive are public, but some website
  features, including Published Data Upload and User Preferences, require a login."

## 2. The columns part A reads, and where they come from

- **Page:** "TESS Objects of Interest (TOI) Table Data Column Definitions",
  https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_TOI_columns.html
- **Read on:** 2026-11-21, a live fetch (HTTP 200); the page's own date "Last updated: 22 September
  2025".
- **Columns read** (names as on the page): `toi`, `tid`, `tfopwg_disp` (descriptive only, by the
  addendum of 22:45, item 3), `pl_orbper`, `pl_tranmid`, `pl_trandurh`, `pl_trandep`, `st_tmag`,
  `toi_created`, `rowupdate`.
- **Literal excerpts:**
  - `toi`: "TOIs are identified and numbered by the TESS Project."
  - `pl_tranmid`: "Planet Transit Midpoint [BJD]".
  - `toi_created`: "Date on which the TESS Project identified the Object of Interest."
- **The ephemerides' origin:** the TOI list is the TESS Project's; the Archive's table is "updated
  approximately once a week based on the ExoFOP TOI list" (read 2026-11-21, the companion record
  [`v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md`](v0.5.5-exofop-and-archive-data-use-2026-11-21.md)).
  **Which pipeline (SPOC, QLP or another) produced each row's ephemeris is not confirmed** from
  the column page; the frame records each row as read, with the query and its date.

## 3. Confirmed planets (the planet arm)

The Archive's PSCompPars (`tic_id`, `pl_name`, `pl_orbper`, `disc_facility`), as in
[`v0.5.6-open-sources-2026-11-21.md`](v0.5.6-open-sources-2026-11-21.md), section 2.

## 4. The acknowledgment

Every future public material that uses ExoFOP or the TOI table carries the acknowledgment given in
NExScI's answer (the addendum of 22:45, item 4).
