## ExoFOP-TESS: the TOI table and the observing notes

- Pages: https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/ and the policy page https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/pcp.php
- Downloads: `download_toi.php` and `download_obsnotes.php`, on
  `https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/`; URL, UTC time, size and SHA-256 in
  [`toi-fetch.json`](../../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/toi-fetch.json) and
  [`obsnotes-fetch.json`](../../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/obsnotes-fetch.json).
- Policy, excerpt 1: "any data associated with a given data provider should be considered their property"
- Policy, excerpt 2: "the user should contact the data owner"
- Policy, excerpt 3: "may be subject to a 12 month proprietary period"
- Page, excerpt 4: "Please include the following standard acknowledgment"

**State for the observing notes: unresolved.** The notes are contributed by named users, and the policy
asks that the owner be contacted before data are used. They were read for counts in this inventory, and no
note, username or identifier is recorded; that does not establish an exception to the policy. Operational or
published reliance on them needs documented owner authorization or an authoritative ExoFOP clarification,
which also settles the proprietary status and the acknowledgment. It goes to the maintainer at the protocol
stop.

## The TOI catalog's dispositions

- Guerrero et al. 2021, ApJS 254, 39, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.12538, read on 2026-11-09: the vetting
  table lists the disposition "EB" as an eclipsing binary with a V-shaped transit. The excerpt read does
  not say whether the label covers a nearby binary (the reading of sections 7.1 to 7.3 is Codex's, in
  `docs/plans/v0.5.2-consensus/t3b-frame-round-1-answer.md`).

- Gaia DR3 non-single-star orbital solutions: their eclipsing channel takes its input from
