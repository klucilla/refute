# Texts for files that Phase I does not edit (README.md, CLAUDE.md, ROADMAP.md)

Written 2026-10-17 on branch `claude/v0-2-1-reopen` (Phase I); **final version of 2026-10-18**
(after pieces 4 and 5 of M4 and the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-18). These files are edited
only on `main`, by Phase H, after the maintainer has seen the diff. Each text below is final as
proposed; "Replace" gives the current text and "With" the new one. The holdout texts describe the
default behaviour; one sentence in each says what the v0.2.1 corrections add when a claim switches
them on (version 2 of the corrections block).

## 1. README.md (issue #23; the wording of `issue-23-acceptance.md`, Amendment 2)

### 1a. The paragraph that starts "The sharpest attack is the **blind holdout**"

Replace:

> The sharpest attack is the **blind holdout**. Hide an entire year of observations. Fit the
> orbit using only the other years. Predict when the transits should appear in the hidden
> year, each with an uncertainty window, then read the hidden year **only** inside those
> windows, their local baselines and control windows at the opposite phase. A real periodic
> signal passes. Noise rarely does.

With:

> The sharpest attack is the **blind holdout**. Hide an entire year of observations. Fit the
> orbit using only the other years. Predict when the transits should appear in the hidden
> year, each with an uncertainty window; everything decided up to here uses only the other years
> and the hidden sectors' time spans. Then the whole hidden year is detrended once, with each
> predicted window masked out to the inner edge of its baseline band; that step is not logged,
> and its outlier clipping can drop hidden cadences. After it, the test reads the hidden year only
> inside the predicted windows, their baseline bands and control windows at the opposite phase,
> and every such read is logged. When a claim switches on the v0.2.1 corrections, replica spans
> placed from the same metadata away from the windows are masked and read in the same way, to
> measure the hidden year's noise before any window is read. A real periodic signal passes. Noise
> rarely does.

### 1b. Item 4 of "The steps in detail"

Replace:

> 4. **Blind holdout.** Each observing year is hidden in turn and must be predicted from the
>    others. The code that reads the hidden year can only open the predicted windows, their
>    local baseline bands and the phase-0.5 control windows.

With:

> 4. **Blind holdout.** Each observing year is hidden in turn and must be predicted from the
>    others. In a round that reaches the measurement, the whole hidden year is detrended once,
>    with each predicted window masked out to the inner edge of its baseline band (not logged;
>    its clipping can drop hidden cadences). After that, the code can only open the predicted
>    windows, their local baseline bands and the phase-0.5 control windows, and every read is
>    logged. With the v0.2.1 corrections switched on, replica spans placed from the metadata are
>    masked and read the same way, and are read before any window.

## 2. CLAUDE.md (issue #23), item 4 of "Engine components"

Replace:

> 4. **Blind holdout**: part of the data stays hidden; predictions are made from the
>    rest and checked only at predicted windows.

With:

> 4. **Blind holdout**: part of the data stays hidden; predictions are made from the
>    rest and checked at predicted windows. The hidden year is detrended once before any
>    logged read, each window masked out to the inner edge of its baseline band (its
>    clipping can drop hidden cadences); after that, only the predicted windows, their
>    baseline bands and the control windows are read (and, with the v0.2.1 corrections on,
>    replica spans placed from the metadata, read first), and every read is logged.
