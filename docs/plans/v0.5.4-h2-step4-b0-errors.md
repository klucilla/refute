# v0.5.4, second half, step 4: B0's oracle with stellar errors of 1, 2 and 3 %

**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (night), item 4c (#217): "passo 4: o
oráculo do B0 com erros estelares de 1, 2 e 3 %." It follows Codex's conditions
([`v0.5.4-audit-check.md`](v0.5.4-audit-check.md), step 4).
`method_status: not_validated`. Development seeds only.
**Frozen before any of its numbers is computed.**

## What B0 left

B0 (`docs/validation/v0.5.3/stageB/d1_bound.py`, `d1-bound.json`, outcome C) bounded D1 for EB1
and EB2 with two stars:
- `catalog`, radius and mass errors of 5 % each;
- `star_known`, no error.

This step fills in the stellar precision between them. It answers what precision would leave D1
room **under B0's own assumptions**. It does not say whether a realizable test reaches that
precision.

## The computation (B0's oracle, unchanged, at three new errors)

- **The population.** B0's own eligible binaries: 381 EB1 and 373 EB2, seeds
  `refute-v053-b0:<family>:<i>`.
  - They are read through B0's reviewed reader: `budgeted.assembled` on v0.5.3's ledger, through
    `i4_bound._ledger(ledger_b)`.
  - Each record's found period and epoch (`found`) is reused, so the search is not repeated.
  - **Disclosure:** these development seeds were used by B0, and its 0 % and 5 % results are
    known. The three settings are paired with them on the same curves, which is the comparison
    asked for.
- **Per binary.**
  - The curve is regenerated (`c1.generate(family, seed)`; the generator is deterministic).
  - For each setting p in (0.01, 0.02, 0.03), the star's radius and mass errors are both p.
  - The bounds are `d1_routes.effective_bounds(p, p)`, the ±3σ box on the effective radius
    `R / M^(1/3)`.
  - The nearest planet is `d1_routes.nearest_with_bounds(forecast, t, truth, found, p, bounds)`.
  - The distance is `d = sqrt(forecast.mahalanobis2(t, sector, r × trend_factor, σ_w, σ_r))`.
  - These are B0's functions, unchanged.
- **The reading, per family, setting and level α** (B0's levels 0.05, 0.01, 0.0027 and 0.00135),
  B0's own:
  - the non-rejection probability `Φ(z_α − d)`;
  - the projection `n' = round(200 × eligible / attempted)` (B0's attempted counts);
  - D1's allowance `k = forecast.max_count(n')`, the Wilson upper end ≤ 0.10;
  - the expected non-rejected count at `n'`;
  - the median and 95th percentile of the Poisson-binomial pass probability over 2,000 bootstrap
    cohorts (seed 20261111, B0's).
- **A check.** The 5 % setting is recomputed on 20 binaries, the first 10 of each family. Each
  distance must equal B0's recorded `catalog` distance to 1e-6 relative.

## The outcomes (frozen; per setting, at B0's primary level α = 0.0027)

- **"The scenario leaves a margin"** if, for both families, the expected non-rejected count is at
  most k and the median pass probability is at least 0.5.
- **"The scenario leaves none"** if, for some family, the expected count is above k and the
  median pass probability is below 0.05 (B0's outcome-A rule).
- **`INCONCLUSIVE`** otherwise.

Always under B0's per-planet level assumption, which is stronger than C1's family averages (the
audit's point 16, accepted). This is never a D1 draft, and never a general statement about D1.

## The run

- **The part.** It is `h2_step4`, under `budgeted_v3`. A shard holds 10 binaries, the families
  alternating, so that the checkpoint holds both.
- **The launch.** It runs through `launch_detached.py` (12 workers, processors 8 to 31,
  BelowNormal), with no new dispatch after 2026-11-18 19:30.
- **The allocation.** 3 CPU-h, moved from `h2_unallocated`. A pilot of one binary at one
  setting, run before the freeze with only its status and CPU printed, took 2.7 CPU-s, so the
  2,282 evaluations (754 × 3 settings + 20 checks) project to about 1.7 CPU-h. The driver's
  checkpoint stops the run above 1.5 times the allocation, and the part's hard cap bounds it.
- **B0's fingerprint** is the one its committed record states (`cc407aecdd294028`). Recomputing
  it today would hash today's code; the driver checks every shard's sidecar against it.
- **The caps** are checked for the phase only, with the rest of the plan's tokens reserved.
- **The fingerprint** binds the code, the design, B0's record and its shards' fingerprint.

**The summary** checks the caps first, and that the charges' room holds its reserve of 0.03 CPU-h.
It charges its own processor time, mostly the bootstrap, in a `finally`, under a name unique to
the invocation (`h2_step4_summarize_<UTC>-<UUID>`), as B0 did. Its record is written only after that
charge succeeded. The median and p95 over cohorts are
a forecast by resampling, not a confidence interval (B0's design).

**Stops before any record** end the script before it writes:
- the caps (`check_caps`), or the charges' room below the summary's reserve;
- a failure of the summary's own charge;
- B0's reader refusing;
- eligible counts other than 381 and 373;
- the driver's own stop.

**Stops in the record** are written to `stops`. Under any of them no aggregate is computed, and
every outcome is `INCONCLUSIVE (a stop)`:
- any binary raising an error;
- the 5 % check not run on all 20, or a distance or B0 reference that is not finite and
  positive, or differs by more than 1e-6 relative;
- a non-finite distance at 1, 2 or 3 %, checked on every binary before any aggregate.
