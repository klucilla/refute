## What is bounded

`battery_v52.sinusoid` (`src/refute/packs/tess/battery_v52.py:334-372`) gives each applicable
harmonic j (P/2, P, 2P below the 20-day domain) a sensitivity block:
`max(0, p_flag_j + p_small − 1)`, a Fréchet lower bound on the joint probability.
- `p_flag_j`: the probability that its rule flags a sinusoid of semi-amplitude equal to the
  depth (significance > 15 and amplitude ≥ 0.5 × depth), from the (cos, sin) covariance.
- `p_small`: the probability that the box depth left after the model is below half the depth by
  3σ.

A planet's `sinusoid` PASS needs every applicable block at least 0.95 (the sensitivity rule).

**The oracle.** Each quantity is taken at its information bound under the generator's model:
- the true ephemeris and depth;
- the generator's white and correlated noise (`sigma_white_ppm`; `sigma_r = sigma_w / 2` when
  correlated);
- the stellar trend known;
- the sector normalizer fixed (stage A's convention, `d2_bounds.sinusoid_bound`).

The two quantities at the oracle are:
- `p_small^bound` is stage A's and I4's formula with `sinusoid_bound` evaluated at the **true**
  ephemeris. I4 used the battery's final candidate; both are reported, and their agreement is a
  check.
- `p_flag_j^bound` is `battery_v5._sinusoid_power(cov_j, depth, depth, sp, seed)`, the battery's
  own function, unchanged.
  - `cov_j` is the 2×2 block of the inverse Gram matrix on the **out-of-transit cadences** (the
    battery's mask: beyond the half duration plus `sinusoid_mask_durations` durations), with
    columns for the sector offsets and the applicable harmonics' cos and sin, in normalized units
    (times the sector scale), under the generator's covariance (`gram`).
  - The extra frequencies the battery adds are left out: fewer columns can only give a smaller
    covariance, so this is a bound.
- **The Fréchet block at the bound:** `b_j = max(0, p_flag_j^bound + p_small^bound − 1)`.
- **The certificate fails (L_sin):** min over the applicable harmonics of `b_j` is below
  0.95 − 0.02.
  - This is the battery's own certificate at the bound. A Fréchet lower bound below 0.95 is the
    failure of this certificate, **not a universal impossibility**: marginals of 0.97 give a lower
    bound of 0.94 but admit a joint probability of 0.97 (Codex, `h2-step7` round 1).
  - Its reading: the battery's sensitivity rule, computed with its own certificate, cannot pass
    that planet even at the bound.
  - Coverage is taken as valid at the oracle (`can_fail` true), the optimistic choice for a
    bound.
- **The Monte Carlo precision.** `_sinusoid_power` uses 2,000 draws per phase (the minimum of 8).
  A minimum block within ±0.02 of 0.95, about four standard errors, is **uncertain** and counts
  as unknown.
- **Numerical validity.** The Gram matrix and each 2×2 covariance must be finite, positive
  definite and not ill-conditioned (scaled condition ≤ 1e12), and every probability finite and in
  [0, 1]. Otherwise the planet is UNK; `_sinusoid_power`'s zero for a failed Cholesky is never
  used as a power.
- A planet whose P/2 is outside the domain is the battery's `OUTSIDE_DOMAIN`, counted apart.

## D2: the population and the outcome

- **The population.** D2 version 5's 178 eligible fresh planets (`i4_bound.schedule`'s fresh
  rows, the same indices as I4), 3129 included.
- **The counts.**
  - L_sin (with the UNK);
  - I4's L_oe (`power_nuisance` < 0.95, valid) and its E;
  - step 1's DATA losses (`h2-step1-floor.json`).
  - **Sensitivity is told apart from retention.** A warning FAIL (flagged, and the dip
    attenuated) gives WEAKENED, which keeps the planet. So the recorded `sinusoid` status of each
    planet (version 5's records) is cross-tabulated with L_sin, and a planet whose certificate
    fails but whose recorded status is a warning FAIL is left out of the floor.
  - **The floor** is DATA ∪ L_oe ∪ (L_sin less the recorded warning FAILs). It is conditioned
    on the recorded statuses: leaving out only the warning FAILs already on record does not
    exclude a warning FAIL among the others, so it is no floor of unrecoverable losses. Its **upper
    version** adds every L_sin planet, the uncertain ones, I4's E and the UNK.
- **The outcomes, against D2's 22 losses (two, as Codex's condition allows):**
  - **"a conditional margin"** of 22 − floor if the upper version ≤ 22. It holds under the
    generator's model, the battery's own certificate at the bound, and with every other loss
    recovered;
  - **`INCONCLUSIVE`** otherwise. No "exhausted" outcome is drawn from this certificate.
- **Kept apart, as before:**
  - a qualified PASS, a warning FAIL and an INCONCLUSIVE are told apart, from the records;
  - the ambiguous-cause branch is RULE (step 1). The bound says nothing about it: a warning FAIL
    keeps retention, and the flag fires on the star's variability.

## T2a: the feasibility reading (diagnostic only)

T2a's hosts are real stars with no true covariance, so no information bound is computed for them.
The reading reports step 1's category sets of T2a's `sinusoid` blockers. Its outcome is
**`INCONCLUSIVE` by design**: a real limit would need a reviewed noise model.

## The run

- **The part.** It is `h2_step7`, under `budgeted_v3`, with 0.5 CPU-h moved to it from
  `h2_unallocated` before it runs. One shard holds one planet.
- **The timed pilot.** One planet, timed before the freeze with only its status and CPU printed,
  took 0.6 CPU-s. The driver's own checkpoint (about a twentieth of the shards) projects the
  rest. It stops when that projection passes 1.5 times the allocation (0.75 CPU-h), and the run
  stops at the part's hard cap (`ledger_i.hard_cap`: at most 1.5 times the allocation less the
  consumption, over the 10 % overshoot).
- **The caps.** They are checked for the phase only (`in_h1=False`), with 750,000 tokens reserved
  for the rest of the plan's reviews.
- **The fingerprint.** It binds the code, the design, `i4-bound.json` and `h2-step1-floor.json`.
- **The launch.** It runs through `launch_detached.py` (processors 8 to 31, BelowNormal), drained
  by 2026-11-18 19:30.
- **Seeds.** The Monte Carlo seed of `_sinusoid_power` is `int(sha256("refute-v054-step7:" +
  index + ":" + j))`, truncated to 32 bits.
- **The price:** about 0.05 CPU-h with the pool's overhead, by the pilot; one Codex conversation
  of about 150,000 tokens.

**The residual's Gram matrix** is validated like the flag's, and its σ must equal stage A's
`sinusoid_bound` to 1e-9 relative; otherwise the planet is UNK.

**Stops before any record:** a population other than 178, a reader's refusal, and the driver's
own stop (its `stop.json` and the ledger). Each ends the script before the record is written.

**Stops in the record** are written to `stops` before the script exits, and the outcome is then
`INCONCLUSIVE (a stop)`, never a margin:
- any planet raising an error;
- p_small compared with I4's on fewer than 90 % of the valid planets, or differing by more than
  0.05 on more than 10 % of them (a sign the oracle differs).
