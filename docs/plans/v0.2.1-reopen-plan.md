# Plan: reopening of v0.2.1 Gauntlet fixes (Phase I)

Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-17, as an explicit exception to "only the current
phase": v0.2.1 is reopened **in parallel** with v0.3 (Phase H), which stays the current phase
and has priority in everything shared. The mandate is to work until every exit criterion of
v0.2.1 (ROADMAP.md, section "v0.2.1 Gauntlet fixes") is met with evidence and the phase's
independent gate review returns `SURVIVED`, or `WEAKENED` with its issues tracked.

## Isolation from v0.3 (while v0.3 is not sealed)

- Work only in the git worktree `.cache/wt-v021`, branch `claude/v0-2-1-reopen`, with its own
  virtual environment. The branch starts at `origin/main` `80abc00` (the local `main` held a
  v0.3 commit not yet pushed, which this branch must not publish).
- Nothing under `src/`, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock` reaches `main` until v0.3 is sealed
  (the code hash of the v0.3 locks covers `src/`). `main` is merged into the branch from time
  to time. `ROADMAP.md` and `CLAUDE.md` are not edited here: proposed texts go to the
  maintainer.
- Heavy TESS work runs on logical processors 0 to 7 with at most 6 workers
  (`scripts/run_tests_affinity.ps1 -Threads 0-7 -Workers 6`); Codex also runs on 0 to 7.
- Nothing of the VM, Hyper-V, the JEA endpoint, the proxy, `calibration/v0.3`,
  `docs/validation/v0.3` or `scripts/vm`.

## Order

1. **Stage 1 (no approval needed):** the affinity option of the test script; the full suite
   in the worktree as a baseline; the audits of criteria 1, 4 and 5; issue #23 (report
   sentence and documentation); the evaluation of #33.
2. **Stage 2 (design, with Codex):** correlated-noise cases for the changes already made;
   option D commit 3b; revision 4 of the corrections plan (rows B1, B3 to B7) and the plan of
   H3b (transit times with an ingress model). Ends with the resumption amendment for the
   maintainer.
3. **Stage 3:** each correction, acceptance record first, then tests (white and correlated
   noise), then code and sabotages.
4. **Stage 4:** development campaign M4 and M5 (about 9 hours of computation per point with 6
   workers, scaled from campaign 1).
5. **Stage 5 (maintainer):** freeze, drand round, the confirmation run.
6. **Stage 6 (maintainer at every step):** calibration protocol with the coverage
   requirement, selection, lock, run, reviews, report.
7. **Stage 7:** gate review.

## The merge into `main` (only when the maintainer releases it, after the v0.3 seal)

- `main` is merged into the branch first, and the full suite runs on the result.
- **The lock verification of every lock must pass after the merge:** the v0.1 and v0.2
  historical locks by their projection (with extension 1 of the table, `docs/lock-format.md`,
  "Extensions of the table", and any later extension the maintainer approves), and **the v0.3
  locks** as their own records require. A v0.3 lock that the merge would make `TAMPERED` in its
  claim component (for example because the new plan section `gauntlet.corrections` enters a
  resolved TESS claim) stops the merge and goes to the maintainer; the table is never extended
  for it without the maintainer's approval.
- The code hash of the v0.3 locks covers `src/`, so the merge itself is the maintainer's step.
