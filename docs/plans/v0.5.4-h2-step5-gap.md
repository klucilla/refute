**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (night), item 4d (#217): "passo 5: a
decomposição da distância do E2 ao limite (tendência, janelas locais, caixa)." It follows Codex's
conditions ([`v0.5.4-audit-check.md`](v0.5.4-audit-check.md), step 5).
`method_status: not_validated`. Development data only.
**Frozen before any of its numbers is computed.**

## The question

E2, `odd_even`'s estimator (`battery_b1.check_odd_even`), reports an error on the odd-even depth
difference above the information bound. The closing record's 1.44 is the median of 28 finite
ratios on v0.5.2's losses. I4 showed that the transit shape as a nuisance does not explain it
(σ ratio median 1.00004).

This step asks which of three differences between E2 and the bound accounts for the gap:
- E2 works on **detrended** flux, so the trend is fitted;
- it works in **local windows**, with each event's own baseline;
- it measures a **box**, not the trapezoid.

## The variants (each a Fisher bound under the generator's model)

All variants use the generator's covariance (white plus the exponential correlated term per
sector, stage A's `gram`), the true ephemeris, and the odd-even contrast `c = (1, −1)` on the odd
and even depth columns. Each gives σ_v of the contrast, in depth units (stage A's convention).

| Variant | Columns and cadences |
|---|---|
| **V0, the known bound** | stage A's `odd_even_bound`: trapezoid × the known trend, sector offsets, all cadences. Its σ must equal I4's `sigma_known_template` to 1e-6 relative, a check |
| **V_trend, a free trend** | V0 with each sector's offset replaced by a quadratic in time. Where the star has a trend, the generator's flux `(1 − D·shape) × trend` gives its derivatives with respect to the trend's amplitude, phase and period, each times `(1 − D·shape)`, as nuisances |
| **V_win, local windows** | only the cadences within `h = T/2 + 1.5 T` of each transit centre (A0's half-width, `baseline_outer_durations = 1.5`); an offset and a slope per window as nuisances; the trapezoid × the known trend |
| **V_box, a box** | V0's cadences and offsets with box columns (the true duration, no ingress) in place of the trapezoid. The box contrast's response to a trapezoid contrast, r = c^T G_bb^-1 G_bt c / 2, is computed under the same model; σ_box / r is the variant's σ in trapezoid depth units |
| **V_win+box** | V_win's cadences and nuisances with V_box's columns and response |
| **V_all** | V_win+box with V_trend's trend derivatives added where the star has a trend |

**A common amplitude.** A box is not the same amplitude's bound. Its σ is divided by its response
r to the true shape. **E2's recorded error is converted by the same rule:** E2 measures a box on a
trapezoid, so its error is divided by its own response, the trapezoid's mean over the box,
`r_E2`. The common depth's leakage into the contrast is zero by the odd/even symmetry of that
mean.
- Every record keeps the raw σ, r, the converted σ and `r_E2`.
- The powers use I4's alternative with σ in trapezoid units.
- The equivalence stays an assumption, stated.

## The population and the comparison

- **The population.** D2 version 5's 178 eligible fresh planets (I4's fresh rows). E2's error is
  I4's recorded `odd_even_battery.metrics.gls_difference_err_ppm`, the battery's own replay on
  the same curve.
- **Unknowns.** A planet without that error is counted as `unknown_e2_error`. A variant whose
  Gram matrix is not positive definite or ill-conditioned (condition above 1e12), or whose σ is
  not finite and positive, is UNK for that variant. Every ratio, share and crossing reports its
  denominator.
- **Per planet and variant:** the inflation σ_v / σ_V0; for the trapezoid variants, the power at
  `odd_even`'s alternative (`gaussian_probability(alt, σ_v, max_sigma)`, I4's convention). E2's
  recorded error and `r_E2` are kept in the records but not compared (the outcome below).
- **The summary per variant:** the quartiles of the inflation and its denominator; for the
  trapezoid variants, the number of D2's `odd_even` class-(i) losses (step 1's REC) whose power
  crosses below 0.95, with its denominator.

## The outcome (frozen, after Codex's round 3, the tie-break)

**The gap's outcome is `INCONCLUSIVE` by design.**
- **Why.** E2's effective response to the contrast is unknown. The trapezoid's mean over the box
  does not include the events kept, their coverage, the final candidate, the GLS weights or the
  detrending. A sample asymmetry between odd and even can leak the common depth into the
  contrast. The box variants' response r is likewise not shown to null the common depth.
- **So no comparison with E2's error is drawn,** no explained share, and no decision from them.
  The box variants' σ is recorded but not interpreted as an equivalent bound.

**What the step reports** is the Fisher inflation σ_v / σ_V0 of each variant, with its quartiles
and its denominator. For the trapezoid variants only (V0, V_trend, V_win), it also reports the
number of D2's `odd_even` REC losses whose power at I4's alternative crosses below 0.95, with its
denominator. These are diagnostics of how much a free trend or local windows inflate the bound
under the generator's model.

**V_all** is a partial combination: the windows' offsets and slopes absorb constants and linear
terms, not a quadratic's curvature, so V_trend's quadratics are not implied by it.

**A divergence** (a median inflation below 0.99 for V_trend or V_win, which add nuisances to the
same template) is a defect for the stop. V0 must be compared with I4's `sigma_known_template`
on every planet, and must match to 1e-6 relative.

This decides nothing about D2.

**Stops before any record:** a population other than 178, a reader's refusal, the driver's own
stop. **Stops in the record**, written to `stops` before the script exits (the outcome is then
`INCONCLUSIVE (a stop)`):
- V0 not reproducing I4's `sigma_known_template` on any planet (1e-6 relative);
- a median inflation below 0.99;
- a population other than 178;
- any planet raising an error (its variants are UNK, and the outcome is then `INCONCLUSIVE`).
