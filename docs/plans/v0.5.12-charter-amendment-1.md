## b) The change

From this amendment's approval on:
- **Every unit without network runs OUTSIDE the AppContainer.** It still runs in the launcher's
  job object, so two things hold:
  - the CPU of the whole process tree, descendants included, is still counted;
  - the phase-wide disk bound (charter, step 0; Codex's round 3, item 5) still holds.
- **The network controls of those units** are `UV_OFFLINE=1` for every one of them, and pytest's
  `--disable-socket` for the units that run pytest.
- **The network block across the process tree no longer applies to them.** This is recorded as a
  **declared limit of criterion 6**, which stays **NOT met**.
- **Units with network** (GitHub operations and reviewers) do not change.
- **Unchanged:**
  - the three caps;
  - M1, M2 and the sensitivity control, with their rules and seeds;
  - steps 2 to 5;
  - the stops;
  - exit criteria 1 to 5;
  - the gate as the closure condition.
- **The implementation** is a new launcher version, `docs/validation/v0.5.12/tools/launch_v3.py`
  (rule (c): `launch_v1.py` and `launch_v2.py` are not edited).
  - It runs `{python}` from the project's `.venv`, in a job without the container, with
    `UV_OFFLINE=1`, and keeps every other rule of the earlier versions.
  - It is committed before its first unit.
