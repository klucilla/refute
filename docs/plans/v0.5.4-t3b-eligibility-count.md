# v0.5.4: T3b, the eligibility count of the approved frame's 22 remaining stars (design and price)

**The decision.** The maintainer's decision of 2026-11-12 (night), item 6 (#217): "T3b: nenhuma
adjudicação. Desenhe e precifique a contagem de elegibilidade das 22 estrelas restantes do quadro
aprovado; ela só roda se for só metadados e couber na política do ExoFOP registrada em sources/;
senão, traga na parada."

`method_status: not_validated`. **A count, not a selection.**
- Nothing is drawn, ordered by randomness, chosen, adjudicated or run.
- No light curve, pixel file or follow-up note is read.
- The count changes nothing in T3b's status: T3b stays deferred, `INCONCLUSIVE`, its gate
  permission not granted ([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)).

## The population

**The approved frame** is v0.5's C3 false-positive pool: TOI rows with TFOPWG disposition `FP`
whose TIC is in TESS-EB, without the targets of v0.1 to v0.4. It has 63 members. v0.5's selection
walked 41 of them, in its seeded order, until 11 were accepted; 22 were never walked
([`t3b-inventory-r2.json`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-inventory-r2.json)).

**The 22 stars** are rebuilt from v0.5's frozen inputs
([`calibration/v0.5/inputs.json`](../../calibration/v0.5/inputs.json)) and records:
- the TOI table retrieved 2026-11-04, `pscomppars` and the TESS-EB static edition, read by
  `c3select.read_pools`, which checks each against its frozen SHA-256;
- the files of `known_targets`, which must equal their bytes at v0.5's freeze commit;
- v0.5's selection record, which must equal its committed bytes.

The frame must be 63 members, 41 walked and 11 accepted, with exactly 22 left; anything else
stops. No input is fetched again.

## The procedure

1. **The list.** Rebuild the pool and remove every TIC that v0.5's ledger walked. The rest must be
   exactly 22 stars, or the step stops. They are sorted by TIC ID; the order has no effect, since
   every star is triaged.
2. **The triage.** Each of the 22 goes through v0.5's own triage, unchanged:
   - `pilot.triage(tic, DOMAIN, s_max=107)`, with the v0.4 domain D-1 to D-9 and v0.5's frozen
     S_max of 107;
   - the TIC fields come from MAST's TIC catalog (`astroquery.mast.Catalogs.query_criteria`);
   - the SPOC 120-s light-curve and target-pixel listings come from MAST's search
     (`lightkurve.search_lightcurve` / `search_targetpixelfile`);
   - these are metadata queries only, the same calls as v0.4's and v0.5's walks;
   - each star's triage runs under `with_retries`, with 5 attempts and 120 s of backoff. The
     failed attempts are logged; exhausted retries stop the count, and a failure never becomes a
     rejection.
   - **No walk stops at 11:** all 22 are triaged.
3. **The records.** Each search is logged on its own to `.cache/v054/t3b-count/` with the
   per-star evidence, failed attempts included: the TIC query and the two MAST searches, each
   with its context, its UTC time, and either the SHA-256 of a canonical text of its answer or
   its error. Any stop writes the star's partial log first. `inputs.json` itself must equal its
   bytes at v0.5's freeze commit.
   - Per-star rows name TESS-EB members, and TESS-EB is under a restricted licence (#33), so
     **the repository receives counts only**.
   - The per-star evidence stays in `.cache`; it is archived to the private Drive with a SHA-256
     manifest if kept.
4. **The result** is a count `k` of the 22 accepted by the domain, with the rejections tallied by
   clause.
   - With v0.5's 11 accepted, the frame's domain-eligible total is 11 + k of 63. It joins v0.5's
     queries of 2026-11 with today's, so it is not one simultaneous recount of the 63.
   - The domain triage is v0.5's acceptance step (11 of 41 accepted), so `k` is the number the
     frame can still offer at that step. Against the 11 more that a new confirmation sample would
     need ([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)), `k ≥ 11` or a counted
     shortage `11 − k` replaces the forecast of 4.0 to 5.9.
   - **A shortage is a counted fact, not an infeasibility record** (that is a non-delegated stop).

## Metadata only, and ExoFOP's policy

- **No new remote access to ExoFOP.** The queries go to MAST's TIC catalog and MAST's product
  search. No TFOP note, uploaded file or ExoFOP page is read.
- **The TOI table** (hosted by ExoFOP, retrieved 2026-11-04) is read only from v0.5's frozen copy,
  to rebuild the frame that v0.5 already approved and used.
  - Whether the TOI table's disposition column is "data supplied by other users" in the sense of
    ExoFOP's policy is **not confirmed**
    ([`sources/v0.5.4-exofop-data-use-policy-2026-11-12.md`](sources/v0.5.4-exofop-data-use-policy-2026-11-12.md)).
  - This count adds no use of notes or uploads: it reads the same frozen bytes for the same
    frame. Reusing frozen bytes does not settle that question; it stays **not confirmed**
    (Codex, `h2-t3b` round 1).
- **Not a selection of real targets.** No star is chosen for any run, and no order or seed is
  applied. If Codex's review finds the count to be a selection, it does not run, and it is
  brought to the stop.

## The price

- **CPU:** 22 stars × 3 metadata queries, each a few seconds, below 0.05 CPU-h (an estimate,
  not measured). It runs in one process, with no pool and no driver part.
- **Network:** 66 logical calls without a failure. With every retry used, up to 330 calls and
  2h56 of backoff, plus the latency.
- **Disk:** below 1 MB in `.cache`.
- **Tokens:** one Codex review of this design and its script, about 100,000 to 150,000 tokens.
- **Agent time:** about 1h.

## What would stop it

It stops, for the maintainer, in any of these cases:
- a rebuilt list other than 22 stars;
- a frozen input whose SHA-256 differs;
- a TIC column missing (`sel.SelectionError`);
- a query that fails after the transient rule;
- Codex finding the count to be a selection, or to need any non-metadata access.
