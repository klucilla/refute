# v0.5.7 The TOI's own signal: plan

Opened on 2026-11-23 by the maintainer's decision received at 10:41 local (#218, comment
7102888515), under the amended advance rule: v0.5.6 met its exit criteria. The basis is the
independent recommendation, stored unchanged:
[v0.5.7-consensus/independent-recommendation.md](v0.5.7-consensus/independent-recommendation.md).
Its sections 3 and 5 are summarized here, and its recounts are exploratory.

**Limits label:** 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'. `method_status: not_validated`.
**Everything in this phase is exploratory.** Its outputs are never verdicts, never candidates and
never a disposition of any TOI.

## Goal and scope

**Goal.** Run the v0.5 battery's tests, unchanged and with the limits label, on the TOI's own
ephemeris instead of the search's strongest signal, and publish what each test says.

- **In scope:**
  - the same 540 TOIs as v0.5.6: part A's 240 (`docs/validation/v0.5.6/part-a-draw.json`) and part
    B's 300 (`calibration/v0.5.6/part-b-draw.json`), already exposed;
  - each TOI is run with the test plan its v0.5.6 run used (part A: `calibration/v0.5/claim.yaml`;
    part B: `calibration/v0.5.6/claim.yaml`), stage 2, with no catalog test;
  - a paired comparison of search mode (the v0.5.6 records) against TOI-seeded mode (this
    phase);
  - a report and a dataset per TOI and per test.
- **Out of scope:**
  - any change in `src/`, in rules, thresholds, sensitivity refusals or aggregation;
  - new targets;
  - the #59 fixes;
  - more sectors or another cadence.
- **The phase's rule** (the decision, item 4): no rule, threshold or sensitivity refusal changes.
  If INCONCLUSIVE does not fall (door 4), that is recorded and nothing is adjusted.

## TOI-seeded mode (the design decision received at 11:01 local, #218, comment 7103371874)

**Recorded correction.** The opening's item 2 ("the period fixed, the epoch held in a window") is
**revoked** by the maintainer. It conflicted with the phase's rule (the battery unchanged), as
Codex's design round found (`v0.5.7-consensus/design-round-1-answer.md`, blocking item 1). This
plan said "fixed mode"; it now says **TOI-seeded**, never "fixed". The doors' quotations below keep
the recommendation's words ("fixed mode"), which are read as TOI-seeded.

- **The seed:**
  - the TOI's period (`period_days`) and duration (`duration_hours`);
  - the TOI's epoch (`epoch_bjd`), moved to the best offset in a window of ± one TOI duration.
- **The seed's window:**
  - a weighted box of the TOI's duration, on a grid centred on the propagated epoch, in
    two-minute steps that never pass the window's edge (a window wider than the phase uses
    [−P/2, P/2) only);
  - the offset with the largest positive depth SNR is kept if that SNR is at least 3.0 (a
    heuristic for the start, not a significance); otherwise the propagated epoch is used and the
    record says so.
  - **Why this width:** v0.5.6 matched an epoch within half a duration. Some cases with the right
    period were off by less than one duration (TOI-5300.01: 1.21 h against 1.96 h), but others
    were off by much more (TOI-4201.01: 13.9 h against 2.18 h). The window is a start only: the
    battery refits the epoch over the whole phase anyway.
- **The battery, unchanged.** `attack_candidate` uses the seed for its detrending mask and the
  start of its fit. It then refits, before any test:
  - the period, within one refinement step;
  - the epoch, over the whole phase;
  - the duration, the depth and the SNR.

  Nothing holds the period or the epoch inside the battery (option B is the maintainer's stop),
  and `src/` does not change (option C waits for the next phase).
- **Every record keeps** (the decision, item 2):
  - the TOI's period and the battery's final period, their factor and their difference;
  - the largest epoch residual across the data, in units of the TOI's duration;
  - the number of TOI transits covered by data;
  - `stayed_on_toi_ephemeris`, by v0.5.6's matching rule: factor 1 within 0.001, and the final
    epoch within half a TOI duration;
  - the seed's window and its offset;
  - the provenance of the inputs against the v0.5.6 record;
  - a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the limits label, and `coverage_state`
    (`measured` or `unavailable`).
- **The inputs are bound to v0.5.6:**
  - The downloaded light-curve and pixel-file sectors must equal the v0.5.6 record's. Otherwise
    the target is not analyzed (`INPUTS_DIFFER`).
  - With equal sectors, the SHA-256 of the files are compared where the history has them, giving
    a provenance of `same`, `different` or `unknown`.

## The four doors (exactly as in section 3 of the recommendation; exploratory, never verdicts)

The text is quoted from `independent-recommendation.md`, lines 79 to 82, in translation. The
counting rule under each is fixed here, before any result.

**Two versions of every door** (the design decision, item 2), with the same thresholds:
- **total:** all records;
- **stayed:** only the records that stayed on the TOI ephemeris.

**The cohort** comes from the frozen draws. A door is marked only when the records are exactly the
540 TICs (no duplicate, no extra, the same part and arm), and every OK record has a valid outcome
and a status for every decisive test. Until then, the door is "incomplete".

1. **Identity resolved.** "In fixed mode, among confirmed planets with the SNR gate passed, REFUTED
   falls from 22 (9 in A, 13 in B) to at most 4 of 270."
   - *Counted:* the 270 planet TOIs (part A's planet arm and part B's planet stratum).
   - *Found when:* at most 4 TOI-seeded records have `outcome` REFUTED with `snr` PASS.
   - The report also shows the planet records that are not OK, and an upper bound (the observed
     count plus those). A door that passes only because they count as zero is flagged.
2. **A discriminating test.** "Some fatal test fails in at least 10 FPs and in at most 1 planet (the
   FPs come only from part B)."
   - *Counted:* for each test, its FAILs whose severity is fatal on that record, among part B's 150
     FP records and among the 270 planets. A warning FAIL never counts.
   - *Found when:* some test reaches at least 10 FPs and at most 1 planet.
   - The planet records that are not OK are shown, with an upper bound, as in door 1.
3. **Outside the search's domain.** "Among the 60 TOIs with a period outside 0.5 to 20 d, at least
   20 % end with an outcome other than INCONCLUSIVE."
   - *Counted:* every one of the 540 TOIs whose TOI period is below 0.5 d or above 20 d. A record
     that is not OK counts as INCONCLUSIVE.
   - *Found when:* the fraction is at least 0.20.
4. **Decidability.** "INCONCLUSIVE in planets falls to 50 % or less." The recommendation expects it
   **not** to be found.
   - *Counted:* among the 270 planets; a record that is not OK counts as INCONCLUSIVE.
   - *Found when:* the fraction is at most 0.50.

## The pilot and the stop

- **The set:** 10 TOIs that matched in search mode: the first 3 matched planet records and the first
  2 matched others (open or FP), of each part, in draw order.
- **Reproduced:** a TOI is reproduced when the TOI-seeded mode gives the same outcome and, for
  every test, the same status as its v0.5.6 record. Each row also shows its provenance and whether
  it stayed on the TOI ephemeris.
- **The stop:** fewer than 9 reproduced, a pilot row without a terminal record, or a stop of the
  pilot's batch means **STOP**, and the maintainer decides.
- **Going on:** otherwise the run goes on to the 540, and only while the runner, the report script,
  the plans, the draws and the v0.5.6 bundles keep the SHA-256 the pilot recorded.
- **No new Codex round:** the pilot is the check (the decision, item 3).

## Exit criteria

1. The runner, the doors (both versions) and the report script are committed before the first
   result.
2. The pilot reproduces at least 9 of 10, test by test.
3. The 540 records exist. Each carries a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the limits
   label and `coverage_state`.
4. The exploratory report holds the paired table and marks each door found or not found, in both
   versions.
5. One Codex round (the design, before the pilot; held, then decided by the maintainer) and a short
   gate review.

## Budget and machine

- **Approved by the decision:**
  - about 13 CPU-h, with a cap of 20;
  - about 180 GB downloaded;
  - a disk peak of 20 GB;
  - pixel files deleted per target, with a manifest;
  - light curves deleted at the end.
- **Machine use:** in business hours (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00), at most 10 workers and 2
  downloads; otherwise up to 20 workers and 6 downloads. BelowNormal priority, CPUs 8 to 31,
  batches of up to 30 minutes, resumable.
- **The operational limits** (Codex's design round, blocking item 5):
  - one exclusive run lock;
  - CPU charged as the larger of the records' CPU and the workers' measured process CPU, plus the
    controller and any unclean end;
  - every cache under `.cache/v057`, so that the disk peak covers it;
  - the in-flight marker written before each dispatch;
  - `clean` deletes only the light curves the manifests list, after a terminal state, with a log;
  - no target admitted after the deadline.
- **Deadline:** 10 calendar days, to 2026-12-03. If it is exceeded, a short report follows, with
  no new round.

## Risks and early detection (section 5 of the recommendation)

| Risk | Early detection |
|---|---|
| A TOI-seeded candidate changes the tests' semantics (SNR gate, prewhitening, period domain) | The paired pilot of 10 TOIs |
| INCONCLUSIVE does not move, and a rule is tempted | Door 4 is written before running; a proposed threshold stops the phase |
| Too few unexposed planets for a later confirmation (47) | Counted when the next phase opens |
| A per-TOI output read as a disposition | Aggregate tables only in public text; per-TOI outputs only in the labelled dataset |
| Too much process | One page, one Codex round, two stops, 10 days |
