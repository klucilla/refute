# v0.5.7 The TOI's own signal: plan

Opened on 2026-11-23 by the maintainer's decision received at 10:41 local (#218, comment
7102888515), under the amended advance rule: v0.5.6 met its exit criteria. The basis is the
independent recommendation, stored unchanged:
[v0.5.7-consensus/independent-recommendation.md](v0.5.7-consensus/independent-recommendation.md).
Its sections 3 and 5 are summarized here, and its recounts are exploratory.

**Limits label:** 'WEAKENED; does not meet D1, D2, D4, T2a'. `method_status: not_validated`.
**Everything in this phase is exploratory.** Its outputs are never verdicts, never candidates and
never a disposition of any TOI.

## Goal and scope

**Goal.** Run the v0.5 battery's tests, unchanged and with the limits label, on the TOI's own
ephemeris instead of the search's strongest signal, and publish what each test says.

- **In scope:**
  - the same 540 TOIs as v0.5.6: part A's 240 (`docs/validation/v0.5.6/part-a-draw.json`) and part
    B's 300 (`calibration/v0.5.6/part-b-draw.json`), already exposed;
  - each TOI is run with the test plan its v0.5.6 run used (part A: `calibration/v0.5/claim.yaml`;
    part B: `calibration/v0.5.6/claim.yaml`), stage 2, with no catalog test;
  - a paired comparison of search mode (the v0.5.6 records) against fixed mode (this phase);
  - a report and a dataset per TOI and per test.
- **Out of scope:**
  - any change in `src/`, in rules, thresholds, sensitivity refusals or aggregation;
  - new targets;
  - the #59 fixes;
  - more sectors or another cadence.
- **The phase's rule** (the decision, item 4): no rule, threshold or sensitivity refusal changes.
  If INCONCLUSIVE does not fall (door 4), that is recorded and nothing is adjusted.

## Fixed mode (the adviser's addition, set before the pilot)

- **The period** is the TOI's (`period_days`) and stays fixed.
- **The epoch** is the TOI's (`epoch_bjd`), refined only inside a window of **± one TOI duration**
  around its propagated transits.
  - **Why this width:** v0.5.6 counted an epoch as matched within half a duration. The cases with
    the right period and the epoch outside that tolerance were off by less than one duration (for
    example 1.21 h against 1.96 h). A full duration covers that drift while staying far from
    phase 0.5. The frozen frame holds no epoch uncertainties, so the width cannot rest on them.
- **The refinement:**
  - a box of the TOI's duration is slid over the detrended curve, in steps of two minutes;
  - the offset with the largest positive depth signal-to-noise is kept, if that value is at least
    3.0;
  - the offset found is recorded per TOI;
  - if nothing qualifies, the record says so and the TOI is attacked at the propagated epoch.
- **What the battery does next:** the battery's own `attack_candidate` runs unchanged. Its own
  finalization sets the depth, the SNR and the final epoch. Every record keeps:
  - the window;
  - the refined offset;
  - the final ephemeris;
  - whether the final epoch left the window.

## The four doors (exactly as in section 3 of the recommendation; exploratory, never verdicts)

The text is quoted from `independent-recommendation.md`, lines 79 to 82, in translation. The
counting rule under each is fixed here, before any result.

1. **Identity resolved.** "In fixed mode, among confirmed planets with the SNR gate passed, REFUTED
   falls from 22 (9 in A, 13 in B) to at most 4 of 270."
   - *Counted:* the 270 planet TOIs (part A's planet arm and part B's planet stratum).
   - *Found when:* at most 4 fixed-mode records have `outcome` REFUTED with `snr` PASS.
2. **A discriminating test.** "Some fatal test fails in at least 10 FPs and in at most 1 planet (the
   FPs come only from part B)."
   - *Counted:* for each fatal test, its FAIL count among part B's 150 FP records and among the 270
     planets.
   - *Found when:* some test reaches at least 10 FPs and at most 1 planet.
3. **Outside the search's domain.** "Among the 60 TOIs with a period outside 0.5 to 20 d, at least
   20 % end with an outcome other than INCONCLUSIVE."
   - *Counted:* every one of the 540 TOIs whose TOI period is below 0.5 d or above 20 d. A record
     that is not OK counts as INCONCLUSIVE.
   - *Found when:* the fraction is at least 0.20.
4. **Decidability.** "INCONCLUSIVE in planets falls to 50 % or less." The recommendation expects it
   **not** to be found.
   - *Counted:* among the 270 planets; a record that is not OK counts as INCONCLUSIVE.
   - *Found when:* the fraction is at most 0.50.

## The pilot and the stop

- **The set:** 10 TOIs that matched in search mode: the first 3 matched planet records and the first
  2 matched others (open or FP), of each part, in draw order.
- **Reproduced:** a TOI is reproduced when the fixed mode gives the same outcome and, for every
  test, the same status as its v0.5.6 record.
- **The stop:** fewer than 9 reproduced means **STOP**, and the maintainer decides. Otherwise the
  run goes on to the 540.

## Exit criteria

1. The runner, the doors and the report script are committed before the first result.
2. The pilot reproduces at least 9 of 10, test by test.
3. The 540 records exist. Each carries a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the limits
   label and coverage.
4. The exploratory report holds the paired table and marks each door found or not found.
5. One Codex round (the design, before the pilot) and a short gate review.

## Risks and early detection (section 5 of the recommendation)

| Risk | Early detection |
|---|---|
| A fixed candidate changes the tests' semantics (SNR gate, prewhitening, period domain) | The paired pilot of 10 TOIs |
| INCONCLUSIVE does not move, and a rule is tempted | Door 4 is written before running; a proposed threshold stops the phase |
| Too few unexposed planets for a later confirmation (47) | Counted when the next phase opens |
| A per-TOI output read as a disposition | Aggregate tables only in public text; per-TOI outputs only in the labelled dataset |
| Too much process | One page, one Codex round, two stops, 10 days |
