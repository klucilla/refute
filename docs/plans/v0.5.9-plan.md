## The question

When the v0.5 battery enters through the TOI's ephemeris, with a bounded and declared refit, and
its output is checked on a declared set of local perturbations, do the cases that changed class in
v0.5.8 stabilise, and at what cost in decidability and CPU?

## What runs

The script is `docs/validation/v0.5.9/prototype_run.py`. It imports v0.5.7's `seeded_run.py`, v0.5.8's
`stability_report.py` and the battery's functions; none of them is edited, and nothing in `src/`
changes. If the prototype needed a change in `src/`, the work would STOP.

**Per TOI:**
- **One download**, by the adapter, unchanged. Every file is hashed (SHA-256), and the inputs are
  bound to the TOI's v0.5.6 record by `seeded_run.provenance`: other sectors are refused. The
  record also says whether the files are byte-identical to v0.5.8's.
- **Two entry modes on the same bytes,** E and S, in all 24 TOIs. Each work unit checks the SHA-256
  of every input file against the download's record before reading anything.
  - **E, the TOI's ephemeris** (`entry_fit`): a bounded two-pass refit.
    - The window: the period within ±0.001 relative of the TOI's, and the epoch within ±half a TOI
      duration of the TOI's epoch propagated to the pivot. This is v0.5.6's identity rule, used as
      the bound of the fit.
    - The steps: the battery's fine period step (`finalize_candidate`: D·P/(6·span)/10, at most
      2,000 per side, a coarser step recorded when it applies); epoch steps of D/20
      (`final_oversample`); 21 durations over 0.5 to 1.5 D (`final_duration_steps`).
    - Pass 1 masks the TOI's ephemeris; pass 2 masks pass 1's fit; both search the same window,
      anchored on the TOI.
    - The objective: the likelihood of a weighted box (0.5·SNR², positive depths only), with the
      battery's per-point noise.
    - **Unresolved entry:** no positive box in the window, or the optimum on the period or epoch
      edge, gives INCONCLUSIVE with the reason. A missing or invalid TOI ephemeris gives the same.
  - **S, the battery's own search** (`search_fit`): `search_period`, then the battery's own refit
    (`attack_candidate`'s mask, detrend and `finalize_candidate`), unchanged. No search candidate
    gives INCONCLUSIVE with the search's reason.
- **The base output of each mode** (`evaluate_fixed`): the battery's tests at that mode's final
  ephemeris, held fixed. Then the coverage and sensitivity rules, deferral and the decisive
  aggregate, as in v0.5.7 and v0.5.8.
- **Six local probes around a decided base** (`probe_ephemerides`), one axis at a time, in a frozen
  order:
  - p1, p2: the epoch ± D/20, the fit's epoch step;
  - p3, p4: the period ± D·P/(6·span)/10, the battery's fine period step;
  - p5, p6: the duration ± D/20, one step of the 21-point duration grid.
  - They are **local probes**, not a resolution argument: the steps are the fit's own grid steps.
    Each probe record holds the step actually used.
  - An INCONCLUSIVE base runs no probe: the consensus rule only demotes.
- **The consensus output** (`consensus`): a decided base keeps its class only if all six probes are
  recorded, OK and give that class. Otherwise it is INCONCLUSIVE, naming the first disagreeing probe
  in the frozen order. A probe that is missing, not OK, or of another class disagrees.

### Codex's first three blocking items, at prototype level

1. **An explicit path** (`explicit_path`). Only discovery version 2 runs, with the corrections off
   and neither the version-3 nor the version-4 sections. Any other plan is refused with its reason.
   No schema is read or written, so no rule of versions ≥ 3 is inherited.
2. **A fixed evaluation** (`evaluate_fixed`).
   - `attack_candidate` runs unchanged, except its refit step: `pipeline.finalize_candidate` is
     replaced, in the worker process only and only for that call, by a remeasurement at the given
     ephemeris. That remeasurement (`box_stats`) gives the depth, its error, the SNR and the
     transits covered.
   - **Frozen:**
     - the detrending mask at the evaluated ephemeris (the corrections are off, so no
       prewhitening);
     - the likelihood objective;
     - the epoch pivot: the cycle nearest the middle of the light curve, for both modes;
     - the box convention: half-open, at least three points;
     - the tie rule: the first maximum in period, then epoch, then duration order;
     - the steps, computed from the raw light curve's span.
   - **Shared by E and S:** the same files, the same test plan (the one each TOI's v0.5.6 run
     used), stage 2, no catalog test.
   - The tests cover end-of-baseline drift and gaps: `entry_grids` on a 4,000-day span, and the
     fixed evaluation and both entries on a synthetic planet observed in two sectors about two
     years apart (`synthetic.scenario_data("planet")`).
3. **The centroid's numerical failures** (`guarded_centroid`).
   - The guard catches:
     - a singular or non-positive-definite sky or combined covariance;
     - an overflow;
     - a negative or non-finite quadratic form, in the observed statistic or in `sky_power`'s
       simulations.
   - Each gives `centroid_shift: INCONCLUSIVE`, with the reason and zero coverage, never ERROR. The
     guard is installed only during that test's call, and restored after it.
   - Tested on stubs and inside the real `check_centroid_v5` with a singular covariance.
   - **Zero coverage and the consensus:** a probe whose outcome changes because a test lost its
     coverage disagrees, like any other change of class. No other coverage condition is added.

**Not done here (blocking items 4 to 7):** they concern a locked claim, which this phase does not
have. The version-5 specification draft answers each of them (see "Deliverables").

**A confound, stated before any result.** The battery's simulation seeds read the candidate's
period, epoch and depth (`power.candidate_seed`). A probe moves the ephemeris, so it also moves the
simulations' seeds. The prototype does not separate the two. A disagreement is therefore the
ephemeris and the seed together. A target-keyed seed is an item of the version-5 specification,
not of this prototype.

## The questions, written before any result

These are exploratory marks: counts with their Wilson 95 % intervals, never verdicts and never doors
with a threshold. Their counting rules are fixed in `prototype_report.py`, committed with this plan.
- **Completeness.** Nothing is marked until there is exactly one record per TOI and mode (48), each
  with a status.
- **A pair** is one TOI's E record and S record. A record that is not OK makes its pair neither
  stable nor decided.
- **Q1, the 14 changes** (13 changes and the reverse case): how many pairs give the same class in E
  and S:
  - without consensus (both bases);
  - with consensus (both consensus outputs).
  
  Each count is split into both decided and both INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Q2, the 8 controls:** how many keep their recorded class in both modes, without consensus and
  with consensus.
- **Q3, the two numerical ERRORs:** do all four records (2 TOIs × 2 modes) end with a status other
  than ERROR and a reason? Any guarded numerical failure is listed.
- **Q4, decidability:** per mode, the decided outputs over the 24 and over the 14, without and with
  consensus. The difference is the cost of the consensus in decidability.
- **Q5, cost:**
  - CPU seconds of the fit, the base evaluation and each probe axis;
  - the fetch's CPU and the bytes per TOI;
  - the ledger's totals.

## Records

- **One record per work unit** in `.cache/v059/run/records`. The units are the download, the base of
  each mode and each probe.
- **The 48 mode records** (24 TOIs × 2 modes) are built by `prototype_run.py bundle` into
  `docs/validation/v0.5.9/prototype-records.json`. Each has:
  - a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the label, `method_status: not_validated`,
    `coverage_state`, and provenance;
  - the input files' SHA-256;
  - the base output and the consensus output;
  - the fit (resolved or not, the window and the passes) and its identity: v0.5.6's matching rule
    on the final fit, by `seeded_run.ephemeris_record`;
  - each probe's output, step and differing tests, and the first disagreeing probe;
  - the measured costs (CPU of the fetch, the fit, the base evaluation and each probe; bytes).
- **The unit records** go to `prototype-units.json` if it stays within 5 MB. Otherwise they are
  archived to the Drive with a SHA-256 manifest, and the repository keeps the mode records with
  every unit file's SHA-256 (the maintainer's rule on raw outputs).
- **The report:** `prototype_report.py` writes `prototype-report.json` and `prototype-report.md`.

## Caps and machine

| Cap | Value | How |
|---|---|---|
| Downloads | 15 GB | A download unit is admitted only if the bytes downloaded so far, plus a reservation for every download in flight (1.25 times the TOI's v0.5.8 bytes, at least 0.3 GB), plus its own stay within 14 GB. The estimate is about 7 GB. |
| CPU | 8 CPU-h | `seeded_run`'s charge: the larger of the records' and the workers' measured CPU, plus the controller's and any interruption's bound. No unit is admitted past the cap; a running unit finishes. The estimate is about 4 to 6 CPU-h. |
| Disk peak | 10 GB | Measured under `.cache/v059` every 2 s. Admission is held at 8.5 GB of measured disk plus reservations; a sample over 10 GB stops admission. |
| Deadline | 2026-12-07 23:59 local | No unit is admitted after it. |

- **The machine:** CPUs 8 to 31 (`pinrun`), BelowNormal priority. Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00:
  at most 10 workers and 2 downloads; otherwise up to 20 workers and 6 downloads.
- **Batches:** admission stops after 28 minutes; a unit's wall estimate is at most 25 minutes.
- **Resumable:** one record per unit, a recorded unit never runs again, the run lock is held by the
  operating system, and an unclean end is charged by an upper bound.
- **Downloads:** at most 3 attempts per TOI within one approved attempt, waiting 60 s and 180 s;
  every attempt is recorded. A download that still fails is an ERROR, not rerun.
- **Files:** pixel files are deleted when a TOI's last unit is recorded, with a manifest; light
  curves at the end (`clean`), by literal path with their SHA-256, inside `.cache/v059` only.
- **No ledger of caps beyond the run's own:** the run is under 5 CPU-h in the estimate, but the
  run's ledger (`prototype-ledger.json`) records every batch anyway.
- **No dependency changes** in this phase.

## Deliverables

1. **The report,** with Q1 to Q5 marked and the costs measured, as a draft for the maintainer's
   approval.
2. **A draft specification of battery version 5,** [docs/plans/battery-v5-spec.md](battery-v5-spec.md).
   It answers each of Codex's 7 blocking items, including:
   - the symmetric paired claim (E and S on every drawn TIC, the same bytes, flips counted both
     ways, abstentions and losses apart);
   - the thresholds with their Wilson interval convention declared, and the power calculation;
   - a lock rule without self-reference (the lock commit and tag first, its identity registered
     in a following commit).
3. **A model of pair yield and cost,** to size a locked PC panel in the next phase, with a disjoint
   PC reserve for the prospective reading. Nothing is drawn now.

## Exit criteria

1. The consensus record and correction 2 of the v0.5.8 report are committed.
2. The plan, the control rule, the seed, the script and the report script are committed, and the
   pushed SHA is checked with `gh api`, before the run.
3. The 48 records (24 × 2 modes) exist, each with a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the
   label, coverage and provenance.
4. The report holds Q1 to Q5 marked, with the measured costs.
5. The version-5 specification answers the 7 blocking items.
6. One Codex round on the round's work, and a short gate review.

## Order and stops

1. **Commit A:** this plan, the scripts (with the control seed), the tests, the consensus record and
   correction 2. Push.
2. **The draw** (`prototype_run.py draw`) writes the set. **Commit B:** the set. Push.
3. **One Codex round** on the plan, the set, the control rule and the scripts, resuming thread
   `01a20a4f-3ec2-7cb2-8a0a-5990041bd6d7`. Its blocking items are applied. If it returns REFUTED, a
   second round runs in the same thread.
4. **Commit C:** what the round changed, and its answer, stored unchanged in the consensus record.
   Push, and check the SHA with `gh api`.
5. **The exact procedure is posted on #218, then STOP.** The run starts only with the maintainer's
   recorded approval.

**Stops that stay the maintainer's:**
- running the run;
- any change in `src/`;
- locks and drand rounds;
- reruns beyond the approved set;
- public text and the site;
- Zenodo (a token, a draft or a publication);
- third-party code;
- dependencies;
- changing old targets;
- deletions outside `.cache`.
