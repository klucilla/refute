## The question

When the v0.5 battery enters through the TOI's ephemeris, with a bounded and declared refit, and
its output is checked on a declared set of local perturbations, do the cases that changed class in
v0.5.8 stabilise, and at what cost in decidability and CPU?

## What runs

The script is `docs/validation/v0.5.9/prototype_run.py`. It imports v0.5.7's `seeded_run.py`, v0.5.8's
`stability_report.py` and the battery's functions; none of them is edited, and nothing in `src/`
changes. If the prototype needed a change in `src/`, the work would STOP.

**Per TOI:**
- **One download**, by the adapter, unchanged. Every file is hashed (SHA-256), and the inputs are
  bound to the TOI's v0.5.6 record by `seeded_run.provenance`: other sectors are refused. The
  record also says whether the files are byte-identical to v0.5.8's.
- **Two entry modes on the same bytes,** E and S, in all 24 TOIs. Each work unit checks the SHA-256
  of every input file against the download's record before reading anything.
  - **E, the TOI's ephemeris** (`entry_fit`): a bounded two-pass refit.
    - The window: the period within ±0.001 relative of the TOI's, and the epoch within ±half a TOI
      duration of the TOI's epoch propagated to the pivot. This is v0.5.6's identity rule, used as
      the bound of the fit.
    - The steps: the period window's two ends are exact trials, with n equal steps per side,
      n = ceil(half-width / fine), where fine is the battery's fine period step
      (`finalize_candidate`: D·P/(6·span)/10). So the step is at most fine and no trial lies
      outside the window; n is at most 2,000, a coarser step being recorded when it applies.
      Epoch steps of D/20 (`final_oversample`), with exact ends at ±D/2; 21 durations over 0.5
      to 1.5 D (`final_duration_steps`).
    - Pass 1 masks the TOI's ephemeris; pass 2 masks pass 1's fit; both search the same window,
      anchored on the TOI.
    - The objective: the likelihood of a weighted box (0.5·SNR², positive depths only), with the
      battery's per-point noise.
    - **Unresolved entry:** no positive box in the window, or the optimum on the period or epoch
      edge, gives INCONCLUSIVE with the reason. A missing or invalid TOI ephemeris gives the same.
  - **S, the battery's own search** (`search_fit`): `search_period`, then the battery's own refit
    (`attack_candidate`'s mask, detrend and `finalize_candidate`), unchanged. No search candidate
    gives INCONCLUSIVE with the search's reason.
- **The base output of each mode** (`evaluate_fixed`): the battery's tests at that mode's final
  ephemeris, held fixed. Then the coverage and sensitivity rules, deferral and the decisive
  aggregate, as in v0.5.7 and v0.5.8.
- **Six local probes around a decided base** (`probe_ephemerides`), one axis at a time, in a frozen
  order:
  - p1, p2: the epoch ± D/20, the fit's epoch step;
  - p3, p4: the period ± D·P/(6·span)/10, the battery's fine period step;
  - p5, p6: the duration ± D/20, one step of the 21-point duration grid.
  - They are **local probes**, not a resolution argument. The offsets are **declared formulas
    of the final fit**, the same for both modes. For E they can differ from the steps its own
    window used (a coarsened period step, or the TOI's duration in place of the fit's); those are
    recorded in the fit's window. Each probe record holds the offset actually used.
  - An INCONCLUSIVE base runs no probe: the consensus rule only demotes.
- **The consensus output** (`consensus`): a decided base keeps its class only if all six probes are
  recorded, OK and give that class. Otherwise it is INCONCLUSIVE, naming the first disagreeing probe
  in the frozen order. A recorded probe that is not OK, or of another class, disagrees. A probe
  that was never recorded, or that a resource stop ended (`STOPPED_AT_CAP`, `CPU_ALLOWANCE`,
  `DOWNLOAD_ALLOWANCE`, `WORKER_LOST`), is not a disagreement: that consensus is `INCOMPLETE`,
  kept apart.

### Codex's first three blocking items, at prototype level

1. **An explicit path** (`explicit_path`). Only discovery version 2 runs, with the corrections off
   and neither the version-3 nor the version-4 sections. Any other plan is refused with its reason.
   No schema is read or written, so no rule of versions ≥ 3 is inherited.
2. **A fixed evaluation** (`evaluate_fixed`).
   - `attack_candidate` runs unchanged, except its refit step: `pipeline.finalize_candidate` is
     replaced, in the worker process only and only for that call, by a remeasurement at the given
     ephemeris. That remeasurement (`box_stats`) gives the depth, its error, the SNR and the
     transits covered, all from the same membership: finite points with a positive noise, in the
     half-open box.
   - **The S base is a prototype output.** S uses the battery's historical search and refit, then
     the prototype remeasures at the pivoted final ephemeris; its class is not v0.5.6's.
   - **Frozen:**
     - the detrending mask at the evaluated ephemeris (the corrections are off, so no
       prewhitening);
     - the likelihood objective;
     - the epoch pivot: the cycle nearest the middle of the light curve, for both modes;
     - the box convention: half-open, at least three points;
     - the tie rule: the first maximum in period, then epoch, then duration order;
     - the steps, computed from the raw light curve's span.
   - **Shared by E and S:** the same files, the same test plan (the one each TOI's v0.5.6 run
     used), stage 2, no catalog test.
   - The tests cover end-of-baseline drift and gaps: `entry_grids` on a 4,000-day span, and the
     fixed evaluation and both entries on a synthetic planet observed in two sectors about two
     years apart (`synthetic.scenario_data("planet")`).
3. **The centroid's numerical failures** (`guarded_centroid`).
   - The guard catches:
     - a singular or non-positive-definite sky or combined covariance;
     - an overflow;
     - a negative or non-finite quadratic form, in the observed statistic or in `sky_power`'s
       simulations.
   - Each gives `centroid_shift: INCONCLUSIVE`, with the reason and zero coverage, never ERROR. The
     guard is installed only during that test's call, and restored after it.
   - After Codex's round 1: every covariance is checked finite, symmetric and positive definite;
     in the simulations, both quadratic forms (heterogeneity and significance) of every draw are
     checked before the rule reads them. Only a failure raised by a guard, at the operation it
     checks, becomes INCONCLUSIVE; any other exception propagates unchanged.
   - Tested on stubs, inside the real `check_centroid_v5` with a singular covariance, and on the
     real `sky_power` simulation path.
   - **Zero coverage and the consensus:** a probe whose outcome changes because a test lost its
     coverage disagrees, like any other change of class. No other coverage condition is added.

**Not done here (blocking items 4 to 7):** they concern a locked claim, which this phase does not
have. The version-5 specification draft answers each of them (see "Deliverables").

**A confound, stated before any result.** The battery's simulation seeds read the candidate's
period, epoch and depth (`power.candidate_seed`). A probe moves the ephemeris, so it also moves the
simulations' seeds. The prototype does not separate the two. A disagreement is therefore the
ephemeris and the seed together. A target-keyed seed is an item of the version-5 specification,
not of this prototype.

## The questions, written before any result

These are exploratory marks: counts with their Wilson 95 % intervals, never verdicts and never doors
with a threshold. Their counting rules are fixed in `prototype_report.py`, committed with this plan.
- **Structural validity** (its own field): the run reached a terminal state recorded in the bundle
  (`complete`, `stopped` or `abandoned`), and there is exactly one row per TOI and mode (48), each
  with a status. Without it nothing is marked.
- **Completeness, per question:** each question is marked only if every TOI it reads is complete:
  no row without a record, no required unit missing, **no unit ended by a resource stop**, no
  `INCOMPLETE` consensus. A recorded scientific or code failure with its reason (an ERROR, an
  unresolved entry) is complete. A resource stop is never counted as a disagreement or a
  demotion; its cost stays in Q5. The incomplete TOIs are listed.
- **A pair** is one TOI's E record and S record. A record that is not OK makes its pair neither
  the same class nor decided.
- **Q1, the 14 changes** (13 changes and the reverse case), without consensus (both bases) and with
  consensus (both consensus outputs):
  - **the headline:** pairs with the same *decided* class;
  - beside it: both INCONCLUSIVE (joint abstentions), and the same class of either kind;
  - apart: the same counts over the pairs whose two fits stayed on the TOI ephemeris.
- **Q2, the 8 controls:** how many keep their recorded class in both modes, without consensus and
  with consensus (for the two INCONCLUSIVE controls, INCONCLUSIVE).
- **Q3, the two numerical ERRORs:** do all four records (2 TOIs × 2 modes) end with a status other
  than ERROR and a reason? Were they evaluated (an unresolved entry or a unit not run is reported
  apart)? The base's `centroid_shift` status and message, and every guarded numerical failure with
  its reason, are listed.
- **Q4, decidability:** per mode, the decided outputs over the 24 and over the 14, without and with
  consensus. The difference is the cost of the consensus in decidability.
- **Q5, cost:**
  - CPU seconds of the fit, the base evaluation and each probe axis (the whole unit and the
    evaluation apart);
  - the fetch's CPU and the bytes per TOI (the manifest's and the metered transfer);
  - the ledger's totals.

- **The 48 mode records** (24 TOIs × 2 modes) are built by `prototype_run.py bundle` into

  - a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the label, `method_status: not_validated`,

## Caps and machine

| Cap | Value | How |
|---|---|---|
| Downloads | 15 GB | Each download unit has a byte allowance (1.25 times the TOI's v0.5.8 bytes, at least 0.3 GB). **The supervisor** reads, every second, the bytes of the TOI's files on disk that were not there before the unit (cache hits excluded; a partial file that vanished counted at its largest size), and ends the worker as soon as they reach the allowance (`DOWNLOAD_ALLOWANCE`). The worker also meters its own completed products and refuses a product once the allowance is reached. A unit is admitted only if the bytes charged so far, plus every running unit's allowance, plus its own stay within 14 GB; a charge over 15 GB ends every worker. The overshoot is bounded by one second of transfer per download unit (at most 2 run at once in business hours, 6 otherwise). A download unit without a result is charged its whole allowance. Limits, stated: bytes received but never written to a file, and the catalog queries, are not metered. The estimate is about 7 GB. |
| CPU | 8 CPU-h | **One charge**, used for admission, supervision and settlement: per dispatch, its journaled CPU (the larger of the worker's own and the supervisor's last reading plus one second), plus the controller's CPU of every batch. Each unit runs in its own process, one thread (BLAS and OpenMP threads set to 1), with a CPU allowance (4 times its estimate, at least 10 minutes) enforced by the supervisor and by the worker itself (it ends its own process past it, even if the controller died). A unit is admitted only if the charge plus every running unit's allowance plus its own stays under the cap less 0.02 CPU-h; at that line the supervisor ends every worker (`STOPPED_AT_CAP`). A dispatch without a result is charged its whole allowance. |
| Disk peak | 10 GB | Measured under `.cache/v059` every second. Admission is held at 8.5 GB of measured disk plus the download allowances running; a sample over 10 GB ends every worker. The caps are checked again after every drain. |
| Deadline | 2026-12-07 23:59 local | No unit is admitted after it. |

- **The CPU risk, stated before the run** (Codex's round 1): with the runner's conservative
  estimates, the downloads and bases total about 2.9 CPU-h, and all possible probes up to about
  10 CPU-h more; the probes run only for decided bases. The run can therefore stop at the cap.
  Units are admitted in the set's order (the changes first, then the reverse case, the numerical
  ERRORs and the controls), and each question is marked only if its TOIs are complete. The cap is
  not raised by this phase.

- **The machine:** CPUs 8 to 31 (`pinrun`), BelowNormal priority. Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00:
  at most 10 workers and 2 downloads; otherwise up to 20 workers and 6 downloads.
- **Batches:** admission stops after 28 minutes; a unit's wall estimate is at most 25 minutes.
- **The supervisor** (Codex's round 2): one process per unit, started and ended only by the
  controller, by its own handle (never by name). Every dispatch is journaled in its own file
  before the process starts and updated every second; a unit's record, then its charge, are
  written before it leaves the journal. A failure of the supervisor itself ends every worker,
  settles it and stops the run.
- **Resumable and idempotent:** a recorded unit never runs again; the run lock is held by the
  operating system. Before any dispatch, every unsettled dispatch is settled: a worker of an
  earlier batch still alive (the same PID and creation time) refuses the run; a result file
  becomes its record; otherwise the dispatch is charged its whole CPU and byte allowances and its
  unit runs again within the same approved run.
- **The run's files are pinned before the first dispatch:** the runner, the report script, the
  set, the plan, every imported file, every file under `src/`, `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`; a
  resume or a bundle with any of them changed is refused. Each unit checks the SHA-256 of every
  input file, and parses **the exact bytes of the adapter's manifest** whose SHA-256 it checked
  (the star and neighbor data the tests read).
- **Links:** a cache root that is a link or a junction (the data, the records, the journal, the
  results, the deletion logs and every cache folder the runner sets) is refused before any folder
  is created and before every download.
- **Downloads:** at most 3 attempts per TOI within one approved attempt, waiting 60 s and 180 s;
  every attempt is recorded. A download that still fails is an ERROR, not rerun.
- **Files:** pixel files are deleted when a TOI's last required unit is recorded, with a manifest;
  light curves at the end (`clean`), by literal path with their SHA-256, inside `.cache/v059`
  only. `clean` runs only on a `complete` run, or on a `stopped` run that the maintainer has
  explicitly ended (`abandon <reason>`, irreversible: `run` then refuses to resume it).
- **The unit records above 5 MB** are staged in `.cache/v059/run/units.json` for the Drive. The
  copy must be a distinct file under `K:/Meu Drive/Pessoal/Projetos/refute`; `verify-archive
  <copy>` checks it against the bundle's SHA-256 and the staged source's, and writes the receipt
  (destination and both hashes). `clean` refuses while staged records have no matching receipt.
- **No ledger of caps beyond the run's own:** the run is under 5 CPU-h in the estimate, but the
  run's ledger (`prototype-ledger.json`) records every batch anyway.
- **No dependency changes** in this phase.

## Deliverables

1. **The report,** with Q1 to Q5 marked and the costs measured, as a draft for the maintainer's
   approval.
2. **A draft specification of battery version 5,** [docs/plans/battery-v5-spec.md](battery-v5-spec.md).
   It answers each of Codex's 7 blocking items, including:
   - the symmetric paired claim (E and S on every drawn TIC, the same bytes, flips counted both

   - a lock rule without self-reference (the lock commit and tag first, its identity registered

   PC reserve for the prospective reading. Nothing is drawn now.
