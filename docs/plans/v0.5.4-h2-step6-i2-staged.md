## The question

I1 found 103 of T2a's 169 `systematics` losses in class (i). In 102 of them the `background`
block (E4: the background rise in transit) has a power below 0.95. **This step's first stage
asks:** under a noise model fitted to each host's background series, could a GLS estimator of a
common in-event step with E4's own windows and baselines reach the block's power for these
injections? Or is the background
information-limited for them?

## The population

**The 102 injections** are I1's `i2_population` (`i1-diagnosis.json`; 54 hosts), each I1 class (i)
with `background` non-detectable. Each is replayed exactly as I1 replayed it (`i1_diagnose`'s T2a
rebuild and attack, unchanged), and the replay must reproduce I1's record.

**The capture.** During the replay, the `systematics` sub-check's inputs are captured by wrapping
`battery_b1.check_systematics` before version 5's activation installs it. The wrapper calls the
original unchanged. It records:
- the sectors A0 uses (`a0_aux`, else `aux`);
- the candidate, the plan and the seed.

Inside the same activation it recomputes:
- `g_bkg` with E4's operator (`battery_b1.a0_depth_quadratic`, seed + 3);
- `g_sap` with version 4's A0 (`a0_v4.a0_depth`, seed + 4).

`g_bkg` must reproduce the recorded `background_rise_err` (1e-9 relative). `g_sap` is checked
jointly: the block's power recomputed at the recorded error must equal the recorded power.

## The noise model (one family)

1. **The series.** On each host, the series is E4's own preprocessed background series: the
   additive detrender with the transit mask, exactly as `a0_depth_quadratic` builds it. That is
   E4's internal transformation, preserved. The model is fitted on the cadences outside every
   event's window.
2. **Binning.** The series is binned to a width of one third of the candidate's duration, with at
   least two cadences per bin, within each sector. Each bin keeps its integer index.
3. **The family**, per host:
   - a per-sector constant, **profiled out by GLS** at each likelihood evaluation. The filter is
     linear, so the innovations of the series and of a constant give the constant exactly;
   - an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process (σ_r, τ);
   - white noise of variance σ_w² / n for a bin of n cadences, with σ_w per cadence.

   It is fitted by Gaussian maximum likelihood, with the constants profiled out, through a Kalman
   filter on the irregular bins: the project's own code, numpy and scipy only.
   - **Starts:** σ_w per cadence at s = 1.4826 × the binned values' MAD × √(mean cadences per
     bin); σ_r at s / (2 √(mean cadences per bin)); τ at the larger of the duration and three
     bins.
   - **Bounds:** σ_w and σ_r within e^−12 to e^6 (σ_r e^−14 to e^6) of that scale; τ from one bin
     to 30 days.
   - **Convergence:** scipy's L-BFGS-B success, otherwise UNK.
   - **The binned OU is an approximation:** a bin's mean is not an OU sample. It is accepted only
     for τ ≥ 3 bins; otherwise the injection is UNK.
4. **Adequacy at the transit's scale.** A Box-Pierce statistic on the standardized innovations,
   with each lag's own pair count: `Q = Σ n_k r_k²`, lags up to `L = max(5, ceil(3 × duration /
   bin))` (three durations), df = L − 3.
   - The lag-k pairs are taken only between bins of the same sector exactly k bins apart, never
     across a gap or a sector.
   - Fewer than 50 bins, a null denominator, or fewer than 30 pairs at any lag is UNK. The pair
     counts are recorded.
   - Adequate if p ≥ 0.01, otherwise UNK. **This p is a heuristic diagnostic.** It replaces the
     frozen I2's lag 20 on 2-minute cadences (Codex's point 6).
5. **The cadence-level covariance** is σ_w² I plus σ_r² exp(−|Δt|/τ) within each sector.

## The bounds (two, both with E4's windows)

At each event E4 measured (`g_bkg`'s epochs, centres `t0 + epoch × period`), each window is
rebuilt as `quadratic_box` builds it, including its constant fallback. A window that cannot be
rebuilt makes the injection UNK, so E4's events are kept exactly.

**A unit response is required, through E4's transformation on the real background.**
- The response is `T(b + a·s) − T(b)`: the real background b with an in-event step s of size a,
  3 times the recorded error, through E4's own detrender. NaNs are kept, and the two series'
  times and sectors must be identical.
- Divided by a, each window's operator must return 1 ± 0.01 on it.
- So must the best E4 combination, with its own weights, and the GLS estimate, which must also
  be finite.
- Each operator must null its own baseline (a constant, and also t and t² for a quadratic) to
  1e-8 of the sum of its weights' absolute values.
- Any two windows sharing cadences make the injection UNK, since a contrast is checked only
  against its own baseline (Codex's round 3, the conservative option).
- Otherwise the injection is UNK.

**σ_E4op** keeps E4's per-event operator c_j.
- The covariance of the event outputs is V_jk = c_j^T Σ c_k, with the white noise of any shared
  cadence included.
- The best linear combination gives σ_E4op = (1^T V^-1 1)^(-1/2).

**σ_full** is the GLS error of a common in-event step, with each window's own E4 baseline as
nuisances: a quadratic, or a constant where E4 fell back. It runs on the window cadences, under Σ,
through the banded OU precision.

**Validity.** Both σ must be finite and positive, with their matrices positive definite and not
ill-conditioned (scaled condition ≤ 1e12); otherwise UNK.

**Scope.** These bound a GLS estimator of a common step with E4's windows and baselines, under
each host's fitted covariance, whose uncertainty is not propagated. They are not "any estimator",
they say nothing about the 615 injections, and they are never a draft.

## The power and the count

- **The power.** For each bound, the background block's power is `battery_v51_diff.background_block`
  unchanged, with `g_bkg.error` replaced by the bound and `g_sap` as recomputed.
- **"Freed"** means a power ≥ 0.95 under σ_full: the information exists, under the fitted model.
  The count under σ_E4op is reported apart.
- **Valid** injections are those reproduced, adequate and computed. UNK is everything else.

## The outcomes (frozen)

- **`INCONCLUSIVE`** if UNK > 20 of the 102.
- Otherwise, with F the freed count and V the valid count:
  - **"an estimator is worth designing"** if F ≥ 0.8 V: the information exists for most;
  - **"the full bound, with FAIL controls, is worth designing"** if F ≤ 0.4 V: the background
    is information-limited for many. This is the audit's 40 of 102 rule, scaled to the valid
    count;
  - **`INCONCLUSIVE`** otherwise.
- **Against T2a's margins:**
  - from step 1's per-case detail, the T2a losses whose every decisive blocker is the
    `systematics` blocker of a freed injection are counted as recoverable;
  - the losses left are compared with 7 (B), with 2 outside the 5 REFUTED, and with 49 (C,
    conditional on the selector's certification, never granted).

  These are readings, not forecasts.
- **Never a draft:** no FAIL control exists here.

## The run

- **The part.** It is `h2_step6`, under `budgeted_v3`, one injection per shard, launched through
  `launch_detached.py` (12 workers, processors 8 to 31, BelowNormal), with no new dispatch after
  2026-11-18 19:30.
- **The allocation** is 6 CPU-h, moved from `h2_unallocated`. A pilot of one injection, run
  before the freeze, took 191 CPU-s, so the 102 project to about 5.4 CPU-h. That pilot ended at
  the adequacy check, so it does not measure the bounds' cost, which is not yet measured. A
  second pilot of two injections ran (165 and 88 CPU-s), and its statuses are disclosed below. The driver's
  checkpoint projects the real cost. The driver's
  checkpoint stops the run above 1.5 times the allocation, and the part's hard cap bounds it.
- **Disclosure.** The pilots printed their statuses, so they showed three injections' outcomes
  before the freeze: all three UNK, "inadequate at the transit's scale" (the first under the
  round-1 fit, the next two under the round-2 fit). No other number was seen. These injections
  are counted like every other. Three of three inadequate makes `INCONCLUSIVE (too many
  unknown)` a likely outcome, stated before the run.
- **The caps** are checked for the phase only, with the rest of the plan's tokens reserved.
- **The summary** checks the charges' room, charges its own CPU under a unique name, and writes
  its record only after that charge.

**Stops before any record:** a population other than 102; a reader's refusal; the driver's own
stop; the caps.

**Stops during the run.** These raise inside the shard. The driver stops dispatching, closes its
batch, and the script records the stop with its reason and shard, and the part's final
consumption, in the ledger and in the driver's folder, before it exits:
- a replay that does not reproduce I1's record;
- an injection not class (i) with `background`;
- nothing captured;
- a recomputed `background_rise_err` that differs (1e-9 relative);
- the block's power recomputed at the recorded error differing from the recorded power;
- any error; every failure carries the injection's identity and the exception's type.

**The summary** lists each injection with its status, whether it is freed, and its other
blocking tests (from step 1's per-case detail).
