# Refute v0.5 core: a route to PASS or to defensible infeasibility records (read-only clone, commit 8653911)

Nothing was written or run beyond read-only inspection; no network. I propose; I decide nothing. `method_status` stays `not_validated`. All statements concern development data. Probabilities are my opinion, not measurements. "My arithmetic" marks numbers I derived and did not run.

## Executive summary (10 lines)

1. Under the current test rules, an all-PASS confirmation is very unlikely (below 2 %, opinion): T2a and D2 are bound by rules, D1 by a price on the planet side.
2. D1 is one trade-off curve: the frozen rule counts SURVIVED only and sets no target on WEAKENED (`docs/plans/v0.5-acceptance.md:420-424`), so D1 is feasible only at a planet-side price, and an unconditional infeasibility record is logically unavailable.
3. New finding: every C1 star is exactly solar while its catalog entry declares 5 % errors (`calib05/c1.py:77-80`, `:366`; `synthetic.py:203-225`). "Stellar precision" in C1 is a label, and the admissibility convention that decides D1's bound was never fixed.
4. Step 4's "13.8/13.7 against 10" holds at level 0.0027, a B0 convention; at the frozen REFUTED budget of 0.05 the same record leaves 5.4 and 4.9 (`h2-step4-b0-errors.json:33-38, 131-136`).
5. D2 is on a knife-edge: its rule-made floor is 16 to about 22 of 22 allowed losses, before any estimator shortfall.
6. D4 is solved in development (0.057 and 0.122 against 0.30); it only needs a confirmation of whatever battery is locked.
7. T2a conflicts structurally with the sensitivity rule on real noise; it has no route under current rules and no estimator-free infeasibility argument on real hosts.
8. T3b is a sample-availability problem (2 stars available, 11 needed); a counted availability record is the one record supportable today.
9. Program: a charter with no CPU, then four cheap three-outcome checks (50 to 80 CPU-h), then a gate; only then a prospective battery version, acceptance and confirmation (1,800 to 4,500 CPU-h, 8 to 13 M tokens, 6 to 10 weeks).
10. A pause is again rational if the cheap checks close D1 at every admissible price, or put D2's floor at the allowance, or free under 40 % of T2a's background cases while the prospective route stays closed.

## 1 to 3. Per target: binding constraint, route to PASS, route to an infeasibility record

### D1 (EB1, EB2)

**1. Binding constraint: (a) and (c) together, then (b).**

- **Where it stands.** v0.5's confirmation left 35 of 190 EB1 and 40 of 186 EB2 SURVIVED (`calibration/v0.5/runs/c1-confirmation/report.json`, `families.EB1.v05.counts`, `families.EB2.v05.counts`), against an allowance of 10 each (`stageB/README.md:55-56`). So at least 25 of 35 and 30 of 40 current survivors must lose SURVIVED.
- **(c) The rule counts SURVIVED only.** `c1.py:641-649`; the acceptance text adds "SURVIVED + WEAKENED is reported beside it, with no target" (`v0.5-acceptance.md:420-422`). A binary leaves the count by a fatal FAIL, a warning FAIL or any INCONCLUSIVE (`core/verdict.py:260-276`).
- **(c) Planet-side prices differ by exit.** REFUTED is capped at 0.05 on P and 0.10 on PG, PV, PA (`c1.py:664-669, 682`). INCONCLUSIVE is capped by D2's retention and D4 (`c1.py:654, 667, 685-690`). WEAKENED has no cap anywhere (`c1.py:654`).
- **(c) `odd_even`'s declared alternative is 1.0 times the depth** (`calibration/v0.5/claim.yaml:246`; `params.py:312`; `battery_v52.py:151`). EB2's relative odd/even difference is 0.105 to 0.667 (`c1.py:289`), so a sensitivity-qualified PASS is coherent with every EB2 member.
- **(c) The generator's star.** Durations come from a star of exactly 1 M_sun and 1 R_sun (`c1.py:77-92`). The target carries a catalog star of 1.0 ± 0.05 in radius and mass (`c1.py:366`; `synthetic.py:203-225`). The truth therefore sits at the catalog's centre for every curve.
- **(a) Information.** With B0's ±3σ box on that catalog star, the oracle leaves 29.2 and 35.5 at level 0.0027 and 14.1 and 12.5 at 0.05 (`stageB/README.md:63-68`). With the star taken as known it leaves 4.6 and 4.1 at 0.05.
- **(b) Estimator gap.** Step 3's realized test rejects 44 % of EB1 and 27 % of EB2 at a planet rejection of 8.5 % (`h2-step3-i3.json:154-159`), against an oracle rejection above 92 % at 5 % under the box.

**2. Most promising route to PASS: a warning-severity likelihood test, priced first.**

- **Why warning severity, on principle.** C1 generates circular orbits and exact stars only (`c1.py:77-92`), so it cannot measure a density test's false refutations of eccentric planets or of stars with wrong catalog values. A fatal severity cannot be validated on C1. This argument does not depend on any result. (The astrophysical premise is my background knowledge, unconfirmed in the repo.)
- **What must be shown first: check D1-P, the SURVIVED-denial price curve.**
  - It reuses B0's oracle distances, which are level-free. One run gives the minimum binary SURVIVED fraction at every planet-side price x in {0.0027, 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30}.
  - Three star readings: B0's box (strict), a Gaussian penalty at the declared 5 % (primary, not yet computed), and star known (generous).
  - Two disjoint fresh seed sets of at least 400 attempted per family, plus the locked battery's verdict per binary, so that the curve is also read among current survivors.
  - **Frozen thresholds, per price x.** "Route" if the expected count is at most k − 2 and the bootstrap 5th percentile of the pass probability is at least 0.5, on both sets, under the primary reading. "Closed" if the expected count exceeds k and the 95th percentile is below 0.05, on both sets, under the reading the charter declares admissible. Otherwise unresolved.
  - A zero-CPU reading of B0's, B0b's and step 4's existing distances sizes it first; that reading is never a draft.
- **What must be built, only on a route.** A GLS likelihood ratio of planet against binary at P and 2P under a fitted correlated-noise model, on mask-detrended flux, with the catalog star as a prior. It needs a declared alternative and a sensitivity block; step 3's test had none (`v0.5.4-h2-step3-i3-population.md:46-48`).
- **Expected effect size.** Under the box, the oracle at 0.05 leaves 12 to 14 against 10 or 11 (`b0b-record.md:30`). The price needed is probably 0.10 to 0.20 (my extrapolation, unconfirmed). Under the primary reading it lies between 4.6 and 14.1 at 0.05; unknown.
- **Probability (opinion).** 20 to 35 % for a development forecast that meets D1 at a WEAKENED price of 0.20 or less; 15 to 30 % through confirmation. For a fatal-class route under the primary reading, 10 to 20 %.
- **Cost.** D1-P: 35 to 45 CPU-h (B0 measured 2.45 CPU-h per 800 binaries, `stageB/README.md:16`; about 15 CPU-h per 800 for battery verdicts, `v0.5.4-h2-step2-i3-increment.md:39-41`), 0.3 to 0.45 M tokens, 2 agent-days. The build: 60 to 120 CPU-h, 1.5 to 2.5 M tokens, 5 to 8 agent-days.
- **Main risks.**
  - Overfitting to the generator: a trapezoid shape, a solar star at the catalog centre, AR(1) noise with a fixed 1-hour time scale (`c1.py:47, 210`).
  - A WEAKENED rate of 10 to 20 % on true planets empties SURVIVED of meaning; P had 8 of 186 WEAKENED in v0.5 (`report.json`, `families.P.v05.counts`).
  - Under today's aggregation the new test's own INCONCLUSIVE blocks the verdict (`core/verdict.py:268-270`), which costs D2, D4 and T2a.
  - Real hosts without a catalog radius or mass (`types.py:61-64`) would be INCONCLUSIVE. The count among T2a's hosts is unconfirmed.

**3. Route to an infeasibility record.**

- **An unconditional D1 record cannot be rigorous.** A battery that returns WEAKENED on every curve meets D1, D2, D3 and D4 as written. So "no battery can meet D1" is false under the frozen rules alone.
- **A class-scoped record can be rigorous.** Its form: "D1 cannot be met by any admissible battery that denies SURVIVED to at most x of the planets". It needs two things fixed before any number, on principle and by the maintainer: the admissible price per exit, and how a test may use the catalog star (box, prior or point value).
- **The exact criterion.** On two disjoint fresh seed sets, at the admissible price of every exit, D1-P returns "closed" under the admissible star reading, with the C1' draw's own sampling included. Fatal exit: x at most 0.05, the whole frozen budget. INCONCLUSIVE exit: x at most D2's 0.10 tolerance. Warning exit: the price declared in the charter.
- **Why B0, B0b and step 3 did not establish it.**
  - B0 controls the level per planet over a worst-case box, a stronger requirement than the family-average rules (`stageB/README.md:44-50`). It is two-outcome and binary-side only.
  - B0b required both 0.0027 and 0.05; at 0.05 the pass medians were 0.452 and 0.102 (`b0b-record.md:36-42`). The families' order reversed between seed sets (`:53-56`).
  - Step 3 asked D1 "by this test alone" on all eligible binaries (`v0.5.4-h2-step2-i3-increment.md:36-46`), although the battery already denies SURVIVED to 155 of 190 and 146 of 186. By design it could return no negative result (`:45-46, 92`).
- **EB1's near-twin share.** The ratio is uniform on [0.2, 1.0] (`c1.py:265`), and 246 of 381 eligible EB1 fold at half the true period (`stageB/README.md:54`). About 5 % of the family has a ratio above 0.96, where the odd/even difference is under 0.6σ even at SNR 30 (my arithmetic). That share alone equals the allowance, so EB1 rests on the density channel.
- **`odd_even`'s alternative against EB2.**
  - The detection z-score is about SNR × (1 − r)/(1 + r), so roughly half of EB2 lies under 3σ for any odd/even estimator (my arithmetic, white noise).
  - Lowering the alternative to 0.105 would need SNR near 88, outside C1's 7.1 to 30 (`v0.5-acceptance.md:371`). At 0.667 it would turn about 30 % of P INCONCLUSIVE.
  - So the INCONCLUSIVE exit through `odd_even` is incompatible with D2. That is a rigorous lemma for a record.
- **Step 4.** Its outcomes were frozen at 0.0027 only (`v0.5.4-h2-step4-b0-errors.md:49-55`). At 0.01 the 1 % star gives 10.0 and 9.5; at 0.05 all three settings leave 4.9 to 8.6 (`h2-step4-b0-errors.json:27-38, 93-98, 125-136, 191-196`). So step 4 shows no floor at the frozen budget. Its scenarios also have no source in C1, where the truth is always the catalog's centre.
- **Can a structural argument ground a record?** Yes for a class-scoped record, no for an unconditional one. To be rigorous it needs:
  - indistinguishability defined by a Neyman-Pearson bound, since exact twins have measure zero and EB2 has none;
  - the null restricted to planets inside the generator's support, with the mimic planets shown to be typical P members (they have impact 0 to about 0.65 by my arithmetic, inside P's 0 to 0.8, `c1.py:239`);
  - all three exits and their prices;
  - population-level control instead of a per-planet level;
  - two fresh seed sets and the confirmation draw's own sampling;
  - a written inequality that a reviewer can check without the code.
- **Consistency check of that argument (my arithmetic).** A member at twice the found period mimics a planet's duration when its impact is at least 0.61 with the star known, or at least 0.27 inside the box. That is 24 % and 67 % of members. Times the half that odd/even cannot see, it gives about 12 % and 33 % before the ingress shape, the same order as B0's 6 % and 19 %.

**2. Route to PASS.**

- **First: check D2-F, a rule-floor census on fresh seeds at the oracle.**
  - Three variants: the current rules; the Fréchet bound replaced by a simulated joint probability with the 0.95 requirement unchanged; the ambiguous branch split by evidence, so that a residual shown large is a PASS.
  - About 600 fresh P planets with the locked battery's verdicts.
  - **Frozen thresholds.** "Route under variant v" if the 95 % upper limit of the floor is at most 14 of 178-equivalent. "Closed under the current rules" if the 95 % lower limit is at least 23 on two seed sets. Otherwise unresolved.
  - Cost: 10 to 25 CPU-h (0.039 CPU-h per curve at three batteries, `b1-stop-report.md:169`; step 7 took 0.06), 0.3 M tokens, 1.5 agent-days.
- **Then build.** A maximum-likelihood `odd_even` contrast on a fitted trapezoid with a fitted noise model, within about 1.15 of the bound and with a calibrated error (E2's mean and tail were UNRESOLVED, `closing-record.md:233`). A sinusoid residual by exact likelihood, available on at least 80 % of curves (E3 delivered 15 %, `closing-record.md:158-161`).
- **Expected effect.** Recovering every one of the 16 pure-sensitivity losses (`h2-step1-floor.json:45`) leaves 21 against 22: a coin flip under the current rules. The second and third variants would move the floor toward 8 to 12 (unconfirmed).
- **Probability (opinion).** 5 to 15 % under the current test rules; 40 to 55 % in a prospective version with the two method changes.
- **Cost.** 60 to 150 CPU-h, 2 to 3 M tokens, 6 to 10 agent-days. The estimators' calibration is about 206 CPU-h as an illustration (`closing-record.md:183`).
- **Risks.**
  - A noise model fitted to the generator's AR(1) is an oracle on C1 and wrong on real hosts; T2a shows the same `odd_even` losses (43, `closing-record.md:106`).
  - The alternative scales with the measured depth (`battery_v52.py:151, 363`), which is biased high near the detection threshold.
  - B1.2's gain did not carry to fresh planets (81 to 86, then 142 to 141; `closing-record.md:156-157`).

**3. Route to an infeasibility record: the evidence points away from one.**

- **Criterion.** On two fresh sets, the 95 % lower limit of the planets no admissible battery can keep must exceed the allowance. The count is the union of data limits, the Fisher limit for `odd_even`, the joint probability for the sinusoid, and the false FAILs.
- **Evidence.** The strict floors are 8 and 11 of 178 against 22. Under the current rules the floor is near the allowance, so "unresolved" is the likely outcome. D2's terminal states are PASS or pause.
- **Why steps 1 and 7 did not settle it.** They read known samples. A Fréchet lower bound is not an impossibility (`v0.5.4-h2-step7-sinusoid.md:43-45`). The upper count is not a bound (`v0.5.4-h2-step1-floor.md:84-87`). The two floors were never joined.

## 4. Dependencies and order

|---|---|---|
| D1, any new decisive test | D2, D4, T2a | its INCONCLUSIVE blocks (`core/verdict.py:268-270`) |

**Order.** Charter first. Then D1-P and D2-F, which say whether the core has any route under the current rules. Then T2a-E and the T3b recount. Build nothing before the gate. If building, start with the `odd_even` estimator, which serves D2 and T2a, then the sinusoid, then D1's test, then the background, then certification.

## 5. The three open design questions (principle only; no proposal to decide them now)

- **What they are.** Whether a warning test's INCONCLUSIVE blocks; the background alternative of 0.8 at 0.95; the sinusoid's small-residual requirement (`v0.5.3-design-questions.md:14-137`).
- **Cost of deciding now.**
  - The effects are known: D2 would pass 98 against 97 without non-sinusoid INCONCLUSIVE blockers (`:6-11`), and most T2a losses are warning INCONCLUSIVEs (169 and 95).
  - A decision toward change cannot be told from moving the goalposts by an outside reader. A decision to keep costs nothing but forecloses T2a.
- **A prospective route exists and is defensible, in my opinion, with a residual cost.** It would need all of:
  - a new battery version, with the historical locks reproducing unchanged (`:57-60`);
  - the arguments frozen before any number of that version;
  - an independent review by a reviewer who receives the questions and the code semantics but not the target forecasts;
  - both variants pre-registered and both reported, the principled one as primary;
  - fresh confirmation sets, never yet drawn (`:141-153`);
  - the disclosure that the results were known;
  - the spurious-family targets (D1 for SE, V1, V2, ER, NR; T2b) as the adversarial check that the change is not a relaxation.
- **Two adjacent items I found are closer to method than to rule.** A simulated joint probability in place of the Fréchet bound, which the design questions already call "a method improvement, not a relaxation" (`:129-131`), and the evidence split of the ambiguous branch. They still belong to the same prospective route.

## 6. Process changes that would cut the most waste

1. **A decision-value test before any run.** A check runs only if at least two outcomes are each plausible and each changes the next action. Steps 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 could not, by design, return a draft or a negative result.
2. **A futility rule.** Step 6's pilots showed three of three UNK before the freeze (`v0.5.4-h2-step6-i2-staged.md:146-150`), and it still ran for 3.78 CPU-h.
3. **Size the sample from the band first.** At 141 curves, step 3's strict budget held only with one FAIL or none (`pause-record.md:75-77`).
4. **Fixed caps and one pre-approved resume, same code and rows.** I4 and step 4 each needed two completion scripts with reviews.
5. **Price before freeze.** I2 and I3 were frozen at 95 to 380 and about 860 CPU-h against a 25 CPU-h half (`h1-stop-report.md:52-57`).
6. **Spend review tokens where rules freeze.** v0.5.4 used about 2.4 M tokens for 29 CPU-h (`pause-record.md:242-247`). One round is enough for mechanical completions.
7. **One ledger of floors and margins per target, with units.** It would have avoided the 27/26 against 26/25 comparison and the unjoined floors.
8. **Redesign the acceptance sizing before paying for it.** The inherited 2,824 to 3,446 CPU-h is the largest cost in the line.
9. **Always keep messages and inputs per case.** I1 reran 6.4 CPU-h to recover them.

## 8. Errors and blind spots in the newer records

1. **Level-specific claims read as general.** `pause-record.md:63-64` and `:112-113`, and `ROADMAP.md:66-67`, say no precision is enough and that even the known star leaves 11.9 and 11.2. ## Overall assessment

- **Is resolving the core realistic?** Not as an all-PASS under the current test rules. A mixed resolution is realistic: D4 by PASS, T3b by an availability record or an amended frame, D1 by a priced warning-class PASS or a class-scoped record, and D2 and T2a only through a prospective battery version.
- **The crux is the charter.** Two conventions were never fixed: the admissible planet-side price of denying SURVIVED, and how a test may use the catalog star. Without them, neither "PASS" nor "infeasible" is well defined for D1.
- **Timescale.** One to two weeks to the gate after R1, for under 80 CPU-h. Six to ten weeks to a confirmation if every gate passes. The most likely end is a mixed state at G1 or after R3.
