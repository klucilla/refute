# Corrections to the v0.5 line's pause record (after v0.5.5)

**Accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-21 (20:00, #218), item 1.** The adopted record,
[`v0.5.5-v05-line-pause-record.md`](v0.5.5-v05-line-pause-record.md), keeps its bytes: a correction
is a new record. This file replaces the wording of two resuming triggers of its section 5. Nothing
else changes: no rule, target, lock or verdict, and the advancement rule does not change.
`method_status: not_validated`.

## Why

Codex's final round on the adopted record (conversation "pause", 2026-11-21) found that two triggers
inherited from the draft did not state the condition the cited design or record requires. The
maintainer adopted the record with three named corrections only, so the record kept the draft's
wording and these two corrections were left for the maintainer's decision.

- **Trigger 4 (D1).** The record asks for a D1-P price that reads "route" in all eight cells on
  **a new seed set**. One seed set gives four cells (two views by two families). R1's frozen design
  counts the eight cells across **two** seed sets (two views, two families, sets A and B):
  [`v0.5.5-r1-designs-revision-1.md`](v0.5.5-r1-designs-revision-1.md), section 3 ("route" needs
  the eight expected cells, `d1p_exit_outcome`).
- **Trigger 3 (T2a).** The record cites v0.5.4's noise-model condition in short form ("a noise model
  under which at least 82 of the 102 class-(i) injections are valid by step 6's requirements"). The
  full condition is in [v0.5.4's pause record](../validation/v0.5.4/pause-record.md) ("What would
  warrant reconsidering T2a", item 1), and is now written out in full.

Codex's answers, unchanged: [`pause-round-1-answer.md`](v0.5.5-consensus/pause-round-1-answer.md),
[`pause-round-2-answer.md`](v0.5.5-consensus/pause-round-2-answer.md) and
[`pause-round-3-answer.md`](v0.5.5-consensus/pause-round-3-answer.md).

## The corrected wording

**Trigger 3 (T2a, by new data)** reads:

> new TESS sectors or a measured background or SAP series for the T2a hosts under which a recount
> by v0.5.4's step-1 method (categories unchanged, a new script frozen before its data) gives
> `F_strict` ≤ 7 under configuration B; or a noise model of the background series, in a new design
> frozen before its data, under which at least 82 of the 102 class-(i) injections are valid by all
> of step 6's requirements (v0.5.4's triggers, unchanged; their full wording is in
> [v0.5.4's pause record](../validation/v0.5.4/pause-record.md)).

**Trigger 4 (D1)** reads:

> the maintainer's choice between a new priced test (designed under charter (a) and (d)) whose
> D1-P price reads "route" in all eight cells across two new seed sets for a decisive exit, and a
> class-scoped record under charter (c) and amendment 1 (e).

Where the adopted record and this file differ on these two triggers, this file holds. The note under
trigger 4 and the pointer under trigger 3 in the adopted record stay as history.
