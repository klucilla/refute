## Q1. Charter

1. **SHOULD-FIX, (a) WEAKENED 0.10** (charter:53, 66-69). Stricter than the frozen rules, which have no WEAKENED cap (c1.py:641-656). What a skeptic would object to:
   - It reuses a number, not a unit. D2's 0.10 margin and D3's 0.10 are battery-wide (c1.py:654-682); the charter applies 0.10 per new test, with no cumulative cap. P already had 8 of 186 WEAKENED in v0.5 (report.json, families.P.v05.counts).
   - P's own unit is 0.05, and step 2 halved allowances to leave room for the rest of the battery (v0.5.4-h2-step2-i3-increment.md, "The budgets", about :64-76).
   - Moving from 0.05 to 0.10 lowers non-rejection, so it favours "route".
   - Not shown to be goalpost moving: the plan guessed 0.10 to 0.20 was needed and the charter took the low end; D1-P also reads 0.05 as a decisive exit and prints the whole grid (designs:243-252). Fix: state a battery-wide cap and the per-test/battery unit shift.
2. **SHOULD-FIX, (a) REFUTED exit** (charter:51, 60-62). "Budget less the battery's REFUTED fraction" is looser than step 2's strict budget (r_P ≤ 0.025, upper end ≤ 0.05), which that document said may be tightened, "never loosen[ed] after a result". The charter cites step 2 for INCONCLUSIVE only. Disclose and say why the strict half does not bind. D1-P keeps fatal as scenarios only (designs:252), which limits the effect now.
3. **NOTE, (a) INCONCLUSIVE 0.05** (charter:52, 63-65). Reuse verified (step 2: i_P(≥15) ≤ 0.05). Step 2 also had r_P + i_P ≤ 0.05 on all P, which the charter replaces by "D2 and D4 in full". The condition is conservative and correctly coded as conditional (d1p_price.py:81-84).
4. **SHOULD-FIX, (b) v2 is a population change** (charter:36-38, 86-100). The frozen acceptance design fixes "a/R_* ... for the star (mass 1.0, radius 1.0)" (v0.5-acceptance.md:343-348). Direction:
   - harder than v1 with the exact star, and it makes the planet-side cost of any density test real;
   - generally easier than B0's ±3σ box, B0's stated requirement (stageB/README.md:44-50, 63-68), with no strict ordering;
   - near-neutral for the locked battery, whose only stellar test tolerates 1.5 times the maximum duration (calibration/v0.4/claim.yaml:135);
   - nearly inert for D1-P's primary reading, whose penalty is centred on the binary's true ρ_B (d1p_price.py:122-125, 180).
   The charter should say which generator a future confirmation of the frozen targets uses, and ask for a v1/v2 bridge on the same seeds.
5. **SHOULD-FIX, disclosure.** The general statement (charter:23-29) is good. Missing: a list of earlier conventions superseded (B0's box and α = 0.0027 primary, all eligible binaries; step 2's strict budgets) with the direction each change moves each outcome.
6. **SHOULD-FIX, (c)** (charter:124-137). Sound, and item 6 has the right direction. Gaps:
   - no evidential level is fixed for the population bound;
   - freezing a record's thresholds before its data is not listed;
   - a record scoped "under the current test rules" is rule-made infeasibility, while the advance rule says "infeasible with the available data" (ROADMAP.md:17-20). Say that such a record does not by itself discharge the advance rule;
   - "admissible" is defined by prices adopted after results; a record must say so.
7. **NOTE, (d)** (charter:147-160). Safeguards are adequate for opening. For R2: say how the reviewer is kept blind, and require D2, D4 and T2a (which count INCONCLUSIVE) to be reported under both aggregations.
8. **NOTE, (e)** is sound, but R1 does not meet (e)1 and (e)3 for D2-F, or (e)5 for the caps (below).
9. **NOTE.** The plan, this review and possibly the advisor share one model family. Codex is the only cross-model check.
