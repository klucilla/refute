## Goal and scope

**Goal.** Measure how many outputs and test statuses of the v0.5 battery change when the same TOI
signal is refitted from another seed, and publish that per test.

- **Stage 0** (no doors; the committed records only): a committed script that reproduces the table
  of the recommendation's section 1, or records the difference.
- **Stage 1:** a TOI-seeded rerun, paired with each TOI's v0.5.6 record, on TOIs already exposed.
  - **The set,** fixed before running:
    - the 107 matched and decided v0.5.6 records (28 SURVIVED, 53 WEAKENED, 26 REFUTED);
    - 60 of the 257 matched INCONCLUSIVE records, drawn with a committed seed;
    - a second seed, shifted by one two-minute step, on the 107 only, and only if Codex's design
      round finds that it needs no change of the mode. **Not run:** the round found that it
      changes the mode (it can move the seed beyond the window and overrides the fallback to the
      propagated epoch; `v0.5.8-consensus/design-round-1-answer.md`, item 3).
- **Out of scope:** `src/`, new targets, the #59 fixes in `src/`, the half period, prospective PCs,
  and any change of a threshold or of a seed rule.
- **The phase's rule:** nothing changes in the v0.5 battery or in any verdict. **Any proposal of a
  new threshold ends the phase.**

## The four doors (exactly as in section 3; exploratory, never verdicts)

The text is quoted from `independent-recommendation.md`, lines 81 to 84, in translation. Each door
is counted **in total** and **only over the records whose provenance is `same`**. The counting
rules are fixed here, before any result. A reseeded record that is not OK counts against
stability (it changes class, and its statuses change).

1. **Stable output.** "At most 5 of the 107 decided change output class."
   - *Counted:* the 107. A record changes class when its outcome differs from its v0.5.6 outcome.
2. **Flip predictable from the records.** "At least 80 % of the statuses that change were within
   0.02 of the power threshold or within 0.5 sigma of the statistic's threshold." The
   recommendation expects it **not** to be found.
   - *Counted:* every test whose status changes, over the 167.
   - *A change is near* when, in the v0.5.6 record, some sensitivity block of that test has
     |probability − required_probability| ≤ 0.02, or the message's statistic is within 0.5 of its
     threshold. The statistic is parsed by a fixed list of patterns, `<value> sigma (<= | > <threshold>)`.
   - Unparseable changes count as not near.
3. **A fit with more than one optimum.** "In at least 5 % of the 167, the refitted SNR differs by
   more than 10 % from the recorded one."
   - *Counted:* |SNR reseeded − SNR v0.5.6| / SNR v0.5.6 > 0.10.
   - A record that is not OK, or has no candidate, counts as not differing.
   - *Found when:* at least 9 of the 167 (5 % is 8.35).
4. **The claim's events.** "Of the 17 FPs and 1 planet REFUTED and matched in part B, at least 15
   FPs stay REFUTED and at most 2 planets end REFUTED."
   - *Counted:* the 17 FPs stay REFUTED in the reseeded run.
   - *Planets:* among part B's planet records in the set, at most 2 reseeded records are REFUTED.

Every rate is reported with its Wilson 95 % interval.

## Exit criteria

1. The scripts, the draw of the 60 and the doors are committed before the first result.
2. Stage 0 reproduces the table of section 1, or records the difference.
3. The stage-1 records exist, with a machine `started_utc`, `mode: exploratory`, the limits label,
   coverage and provenance.
4. A report with flips per test and per output, Wilson intervals, and every door marked.
5. One Codex round on the design, and a short gate review.
