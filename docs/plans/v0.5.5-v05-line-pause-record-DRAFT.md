**no method route** (a better computation of the same rule) with decision value by the recorded
evidence (conversation r2method, 2026-11-21, in the consensus record). This draft states what that
leaves.

## 3. Why no principled route is left with the current data

- **Inside the current rules:** R1's checks were built to tell "route" from "unresolved" at the
  oracle (D1-P, D2-F) or to measure an estimator class (T2a-E). None read "route"; D2-F and D1-P were
  futile after their first set; T2a-E read "empirically limited".
- **By a prospective version:** R2 argued every open design question from the verdicts' semantics,
  under two independent blind reviews. The principled answers keep the current rules (B1 (a′), B2 (a),
  B3.1, B3.2 Fréchet, the frozen ambiguous branch); the one rule change with a plausible effect (B3.3)
  could not meet its conditions and stays a variant.
- **By a method:** Codex examined the joint probability, the `odd_even` estimator, the background
  error's estimate, D1's separation and B3.3 against the recorded evidence: none with demonstrated
  decision value.

## 4. Why no infeasibility record has a basis

- **The standard** (charter (c), amendment 1 (e)): a declared scope; a population bound at one-sided
  95 % on **each of two new seed sets**; every exit at its price; both sides of the trade-off; the
  confirmation draw's sampling; assumptions conservative toward feasibility; thresholds frozen before
  the data; a statement checkable without the code; Codex's, an independent and the maintainer's
  reviews.
- **R1 was not built to that standard**: its checks ran on one set each (set B refused by futility),
  read route certificates and estimator classes, not population bounds on unavoidable losses; D2-F's
  counts "are not demonstrated lower bounds on unavoidable retention losses" (its design).
- **Amendment 1 (e), item 11:** a record "under the current test rules" shows that **those rules**
  cannot meet a target (a rule-made infeasibility); it says so in its first paragraph and **does not,
  on its own, discharge the advancement rule**.
- So the evidence supports a **pause**, not an infeasibility record. Writing either is the
  maintainer's.

## 5. The triggers for resuming (each verifiable)

Each names evidence that can be checked; none authorizes a run, a design or a rule change, which
remain the maintainer's.

1. **D2, by method:** a method proposal (a better computation of the same rule) argued from scratch
   under charter (d), frozen before its data, under which D2-F's frozen code on **a new seed set**

4. **D1:** the maintainer's choice between a new priced test (designed under charter (a) and (d)) whose
