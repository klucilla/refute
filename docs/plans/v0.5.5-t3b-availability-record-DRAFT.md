# DRAFT: T3b's sample-availability record (not a record)

**This is a draft only.** The maintainer's decisions of 2026-11-21 (15:20), item 5 (#218): the use
of the NASA Exoplanet Archive's TOI table for a count is **not confirmed**
([`sources/v0.5.5-archive-toi-terms-2026-11-21.md`](sources/v0.5.5-archive-toi-terms-2026-11-21.md)),
so the updated frame was not counted, and this draft was prepared. **Writing an availability
record is a stop that is not delegated**; this text records nothing, changes nothing in T3b's
status (deferred, `INCONCLUSIVE`) and is not public text. `method_status: not_validated`.

## What the record would state (if the maintainer adopts it)

1. **The target.** T3b: in a newly drawn sample of known false positives (eclipsing binaries), at
   most a frozen number SURVIVED, with every valid, identity-qualified outcome of the group; a new
   confirmation sample needs **11** domain-eligible stars never used before
   ([`t3b-status.md`](../validation/v0.5.2/stage2/t3b-status.md)).
2. **The frame and the domain.** v0.5's C3 false-positive pool (TOI rows with TFOPWG disposition
   `FP` whose TIC is in TESS-EB, without the targets of v0.1 to v0.4), rebuilt from v0.5's frozen
   inputs (the TOI table retrieved from ExoFOP on 2026-11-04; `pscomppars` from the NASA Exoplanet
   Archive and TESS-EB's static edition, the same day; each SHA-256 checked); v0.4's domain D-1 to
   D-9 with S_max 107.
3. **The counts, as counted** ([`t3b-recount.json`](../validation/v0.5.5/t3b-recount.json), MAST
   metadata read on 2026-11-21, with the design fixed and pushed before the data):
   - reading A, the 22 members v0.5's walk never reached: **2 domain-eligible, a shortage of 9**
     (the same count as v0.5.4's of 2026-11-12, [`t3b-count.json`](../validation/v0.5.4/t3b-count.json));
   - reading B, the 52 members not accepted by v0.5, one simultaneous recount: **2
     domain-eligible, a shortage of 9**.
4. **The statement.** On 2026-11-21, the approved frame cannot supply the 11 domain-eligible stars a
   new T3b confirmation sample needs: it supplies 2. **This is a dated availability statement about
   one frame, not a statement that T3b is infeasible.**

## Its limits (stated in the record)

- **One frame only.** An updated frame (a later TOI table) was **not counted**: its data use is not
  confirmed; an explicit confirmation from TFOP or NExScI would allow it, and it is the
  maintainer's to seek.
- **Time-dependent.** New TESS sectors, SPOC products or dispositions can change the count; the
  statement holds for its date.
- **Not an infeasibility record:** it does not meet, and does not claim, the standard of the
  charter's (c) and amendment 1's (e) (scope, a population bound, two seed sets, and so on).
- **The pool was examined** for T3b's planning in v0.5.2; any later sample from it is prospective,
  not blinded.

## What would change it

- A confirmation of the TOI table's data use, then an updated frame counted under a design fixed
  before its data.
- An amended frame argued in R2 (the questions draft, D3 (b)), a design decision.
- Time: a recount at a later date.

## What it would ask the maintainer to decide

Whether to adopt this as T3b's availability record (a non-delegated stop), to seek the data-use
confirmation first, or to leave T3b deferred.
