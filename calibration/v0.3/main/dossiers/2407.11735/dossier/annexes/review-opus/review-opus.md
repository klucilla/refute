## Summary (two sentences)
The dossier is intact and its three claim verdicts (`INCONCLUSIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`) follow from docs/verdicts.md: the build ladder stopped at its first rung with a Refute error ("version 1 does not build a Dockerfile"), so no environment was built, no data was read and no run executed. The three transcriptions match Table 1 of arXiv 2407.11735v2 and the README of the pinned commit. Two problems remain. The ladder stopped at a rung that Refute itself cannot run, although the `requirements.txt` rung was applicable. And the claim, as locked, could never have produced anything other than `INCONCLUSIVE`, because two base gates and one attack were declared beforehand to have no evidence route.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity.** `refute.exe check-dossier` printed "core: intact against its seal" and "OK: manifest intact", exit 0. My SHA-256 of each file matches MANIFEST.sha256 and SEAL.json: claim_file.json and claim_input.json are both dcea7ed1…, which equals `dossier.json.claim_file_sha256`; the log is 9776dc01…; profiles/build.json is bb82f461…; MANIFEST.sha256 is d1d82431…, as listed in SEAL.json. profiles/build.json `claim_sha256` and `execution_id` match dossier.json `provenance`.
- **Transcription against the paper (arXiv 2407.11735v2, Table 1, row "ProSub (ours)").** C1: closed-set accuracy, CIFAR-10 4,000 labels, CIFAR-100 OOD, "94.50±0.05", matches. C2: AUROC at 1,000 labels, "0.92±0.00", matches. C3: AUROC at 4,000 labels, "0.93±0.00", matches. Section 4 states "mean and std over three runs using EMA", which matches `reported_n_seeds` 3, `kind: sd` and the tag `test/accuracy_ema`. The paper sets K = 2^19 steps, K_p = 5·10^4 and w_self = 10 for CIFAR-10 ID; the argv has `--trainsteps=524288 --pretrainsteps=50000 --ws=10.0`. The AUROC tag `ood/auroc_subspacescore` matches the paper's subspace score (Sections 3 and 4.3). The Table 1 caption itself does not name the score, so this is a reasonable reading rather than a quote.
- **Command against the repository.** The README at 8a28cf3 gives the same create_datasets, create_split (`--seed`, `--size`) and prosub_ossl flags as the argv, with the seed substituted and with `--size`/`--nlabeled` 1000 for C2. The extraction path `cifar10.{seed}@N+cifar100/ProSub_archWRN-28-2_batch64_lr0.03_momentum0.9_nclass10_wd0.0005_wpl1.0_ws10.0_wsub1.0/summaries` matches data.py (`"{}.{}@{}+{}"`), base.py (`experiment_name` sorts the kwargs; `rerun` defaults to "") and the flag defaults in prosub_ossl.py. create_datasets.py fetches exactly the two MATLAB URLs recorded in `test_plan.datasets`, with no checksum, which explains why an allowed patch is used for the byte origin.
- **Repository version.** Commit 8a28cf3 is the latest commit; I checked via the GitHub API: author and commit date 2024-09-26T15:17:39Z, "Update README.md". This matches `version_basis: latest_at_draw` and `build.exclude_newer` "2024-09-26".
- **Verdict rules.** `environment_build` is `INCONCLUSIVE` with cause `refute_error`, with 0 of 1 attempts completed. Under docs/verdicts.md ("Attribution" and rule 1), that gives `INCONCLUSIVE` and not `NOT_REPRODUCIBLE`, which is correct. No attack ran (`attacks_not_executed` are listed). Under "The result of a paper", `profile.headline` "3 of 3 drawn claims: INCONCLUSIVE" and `has_negative: false` are correct, so no right of reply is triggered. No PASS anywhere has zero coverage.
- **Order of approvals.** The transcription was approved on 2026-10-18 and the claim at 2026-10-19T13:11:54Z. The engine started at 15:46:42Z (`provenance.times`), and the source and dataset retrievals date from 2026-10-19 01:48–02:52Z, all before the run. The time correction in `definitions.transcription_approval_time` is disclosed.
- **Text.** Nothing in the dossier overstates a result, calls the paper reproduced, or comments on the authors. The `statement` fields restate the paper's numbers as claims.
