## Summary (two sentences)
The dossier is intact, the three drawn claims match Table 1 and Section 5.1 of arXiv 2305.19518v2, and all three `INCONCLUSIVE` verdicts follow rule 1 and the default attribution rule of docs/verdicts.md. The objections are about the procedure, not the verdicts. The locked claim could never have reached a decided verdict, and the only base run that executed (C2) most likely failed because of how Refute built the environment, not because of the artifact, although the dossier records the cause as "undetermined".

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity:** `refute.exe check-dossier <dossier>` printed "core: intact against its seal" and "OK: manifest intact", exit 0. `claim_file.json` and `claim_input.json` both hash to `ff1ac093…3424`, which matches `dossier.json` `claim_file_sha256`, `provenance.claim_input.sha256` and `claim_sha256` in both `profiles/*.json`. The log, lock, pyvenv and sources hashes in `provenance` match `MANIFEST.sha256` and `SEAL.json`. `execution_id` `0715eb05…` is the same in `dossier.json` and both profiles.
- **Transcription against the paper (v2, Table 1 and Section 5.1):**
  - C1: CIFAR-10, 35% PMD, LRA-diffusion (CLIP) 96.54 ± 0.13 against LRA-diffusion (SimCLR) 88.76 ± 0.24. The quoted sentence "incorporating the CLIP encoder into our method significantly improves test accuracy over our LRA-diffusion (SimCLR)" is present in Section 5.1.
  - C2: CIFAR-100, LRA-diffusion (SimCLR), 35% PMD: 61.39 ± 0.15.
  - C3: CIFAR-10, LRA-diffusion (CLIP), 35% PMD + 60% U: 87.21 ± 0.71.
  - Metric (accuracy %), split (test) and direction match. The CLIP model ViT-L/14 matches Section 5.1 and the script's `--CLIP_type` default.
- **Command and code (repository at 231e587):**
  - The argv matches the README CIFAR example (`--noise_type … --fp_encoder … --nepoch 200 --warmup_epochs 5`), apart from `--device` (see objection 5).
  - The noise-label files `noise_label/cifar10-1-0.35.npy`, `cifar100-1-0.35.npy` and `cifar10-1-0.35_U_0.6.npy` are present in `setup.patches.clone_manifest`.

  - No patches were applied (`patches: []`, `touched: {}`), and the clone and patched manifests are identical.
