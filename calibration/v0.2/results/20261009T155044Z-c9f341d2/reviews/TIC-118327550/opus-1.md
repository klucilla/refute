## Summary
The dossier for TOI-244 b (TIC 118327550) is intact and internally consistent. The period is recovered (relative error 2.8e-6) and ten of eleven gauntlet tests pass on sensible inputs. The `REFUTED` verdict rests on one hidden-year round (2023), which missed `min_snr = 5.0` by 0.03 (SNR 4.969). In that round the hidden data show a 998 ± 201 ppm dip at the predicted time, consistent with the train depth, so the evidence suggests an underpowered test rather than a failed prediction.

## Assessment: SERIOUS OBJECTIONS
Integrity and the deterministic computation hold, and the verdict follows the locked rules. However, the single fatal FAIL comes from an underpowered round in which the hidden data contain the predicted transit at a consistent depth (998 ± 201 ppm). The FAIL depends on a 2-degree-of-freedom scatter error and on missing the threshold by 0.03 in SNR. The evidence does not support reading `REFUTED` as a failed blind prediction for this target.
