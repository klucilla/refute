## Summary
Integrity holds and the `REFUTED` verdict is still supported by `centroid_shift`, which on its own is enough for REFUTED. The second fatal failure, `holdout_by_year`, does not hold up: in 2 of its 3 rounds it tested a different signal (about 0.52 d) on hidden data that was never detrended, and the third round failed only because it lacked the statistical power to pass.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity:** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` printed `OK: manifest intact`. `sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256` passed for every file. `claim.yaml` (80a48356…) and `claim.lock.json` (98f37ef0…) are byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.yaml` and `self_claim.lock.json` at `cc9d104`. All 6 attachments match the repository at that commit and the lock. `integrity.json` is PASS, with claim c9f341d2… and code 2cd8b5c1… matching between lock and run and equal to the `verdict.json` provenance. `git diff --stat cc9d104 HEAD -- src` is empty, so the code I read is the code that ran.
- **Target and data:** the light-curve and pixel files are `…-0000000154459165-…` SPOC 120 s products for sectors 34/61/88 (2021/2023/2025), which matches `target.json.mast`. ExoFOP sectors 7 and 8 come from the QLP full-frame images, not SPOC 2-min data. Tmag is 10.4259 in both TIC and ExoFOP. TIC R = 0.787 ± 0.052 R☉ and M = 0.79 M☉ agree with ExoFOP (0.79). The neighbor cone of 120″ returned 348 sources, and `nearby_contamination` (warning) correctly lists sub-pixel neighbors that could produce the signal (TIC 831946954 at 1.9″, 831946953 at 4.5″, 154459164 at 10.3″). The 1839 pixel-file cadences dropped in sector 61 are explained as matching the light curve.
- **Signal:** P = 3.1471376 d is within 8.9e-6 of ExoFOP's 3.1471655 d. Propagating the epochs gives a difference of about 0.002 d over 239 cycles. `plots/phase.png` shows a clean box of about 1040 ppm.
- **Other tests:** odd/even 0.75σ (11 odd / 13 even transits); secondary eclipse 0.72σ over 20 windows; `period_alias` BIC gain of 210 for a box on top of a sinusoid, not near any listed systematic period; systematics: 0 of 25 transits near momentum dumps, SAP vs PDCSAP 0.94σ, background −0.09σ; `eb_catalog` found no match in 2.19M rows (it is excluded from the "flagged" definition anyway).
- **Holdout blindness:** every `access_log` entry is a registered window or baseline band inside the hidden-year span (`HiddenYear._registered` enforces this). No read falls outside the predicted windows and baselines. In 2021 and 2025, however, those windows and baselines cover the whole hidden year, as described above. Windows wider than the transit decided all three rounds: 2021 and 2025 at about 0.82–0.89 d against the candidate's 0.131 d transit; 2023 only slightly wider (0.155 d against 0.131 d).
- **Text:** the report and `export/candidate_parameters.md` call nothing a confirmed discovery, state that nothing was submitted, and label the target "already catalogued; it is not a new candidate".
