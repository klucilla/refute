## Summary
The dossier is intact, and its claim, lock and code hashes agree. The `WEAKENED` verdict follows correctly from the locked rules. However, the fatal `centroid_shift` test passed for the wrong reason: it measured a 35-sigma offset of the transit source away from the target, pointing toward the bright 9.5" neighbor that ExoFOP names as the real source of the transits, and still reported the source as "consistent with the target" only because of the 0.5-pixel floor. The evidence in the dossier supports the blend alternative over an on-target signal, so the `WEAKENED` result understates how strongly the data argue against this claim for this known false positive.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` printed `OK: manifest intact`. `integrity.json`: status PASS, claim `c9f341d2…` and code `2cd8b5c1…` match the lock, and all 6 attachment hashes match. `claim.lock.json` is byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.lock.json` at `cc9d104`. The dossier's `claim.yaml` has the same raw SHA-256 (80a48356…) as `self_claim.yaml` at the lock commit `e176ac3`. `attachments/targets.yaml` and `PROTOCOL.md` match the lock hashes and the files at `e176ac3`. The only change between `e176ac3` and `cc9d104` is the lock file, RUNS.md and LOCK_HISTORY.md. I could not re-hash the FITS files: they are not in the dossier, and downloading was not allowed.
- **Target and data.** `target.json` shows TIC 294179389, TOI-1310.01, TFOPWG disposition FP, source ExoFOP, retrieved 2026-10-09. The product is SPOC 120-s PDCSAP with the default bitmask. Sectors 55, 75, 76, 81, 82 and 83 match the MAST light-curve and pixel-file searches. ExoFOP also lists sectors 14–16, but MAST returned no SPOC 2-min products for them (QLP detection), which agrees with the protocol filter. Stellar values (TIC v8: R 1.323, M 1.084, Tmag 10.5742) match the ExoFOP row. Pixel cadences dropped to match the light curves are reported for each sector.
- **Signal.** The period 2.6794214 d is within 3.2e-6 relative error of the published value. The ephemeris epoch is consistent with the published epoch. The duration (2.55 vs 2.99 h) and depth (6917 vs 7468 ppm) differ in ways expected from a box fit. SNR is 258.
- **odd_even** (1.46 sigma; 26 odd and 27 even transits; `plots/odd_even.png`) and **secondary_eclipse** (-0.28 sigma over 55 windows; `plots/secondary.png`): the inputs are sensible and the results follow from them.
- **plausibility** (1.07 R_Jup, duration ratio 0.72) follows from the TIC inputs, assuming the target is the host.
- **period_alias** (BIC gain 17793) and **systematics** (SAP depth 4340 ppm vs the 4303 ppm expected from CROWDSAP; 1 of 54 transits near a momentum dump; background -0.4 sigma) follow from their inputs.
- **eb_catalog**: 2,189,061 catalog rows scanned, and no entry within 21" for this target (`targets.yaml` scan: tess_eb 0, gaia 0).
- **Holdout 2022 round.** Window half-widths are 0.058 d against a half-box of 0.053 d, which is tight. All transit-window accesses match registered windows: 11 transit and 10 control windows, enforced by `HiddenYear._registered`. Control SNR is 0.80 against hidden SNR 69.8. Both rounds pass the issue-#2 timing rule (0.0018 and 0.066 d, both below the 0.106 d duration).
- **Text.** `report.md` and the export carry the "not a confirmed discovery / nothing submitted" disclaimers and the scope statement. The export states "Calibration target: already catalogued; it is not a new candidate." Nothing is called a discovery. The only overstatements are the centroid message (objection 1) and the holdout wording (objection 5).
