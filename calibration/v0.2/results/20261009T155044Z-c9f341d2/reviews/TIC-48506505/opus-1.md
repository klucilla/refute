# Independent review: TIC-48506505 (Kepler-447 b), verdict `REFUTED`

## Summary
The dossier is intact and the data match the target: the full-data search finds the catalogued 7.794 d signal, and every per-signal test except the holdout passes on it. The verdict's only fatal failure, the 2024 holdout round, did not test that signal. The train-only search on 2021 and 2022 locked onto a 0.5177 d, 10.4 h, 202 ppm feature that looks like red noise. Its predicted windows overlap and cover the whole hidden year, so the REFUTED verdict comes from a search artifact, not from evidence against the claimed signal.

## Checks with no objection

- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` returns `OK: manifest intact`. `integrity.json` shows status PASS and matching locked and current hashes: claim `c9f341d2…` and code `2cd8b5c1…`. Lock commit `e176ac3`, run commit `cc9d104`. The SHA-256 of all six attachments equals the values in `claim.lock.json.attachments` (recomputed with sha256sum).
- **Target and ephemeris.** The found period of 7.7943034 d differs from the published 7.79430132 d by 2.65e-7. The found epoch 2459397.42211 equals the published T0 propagated 568 cycles, within -1.17 min (propagated sigma about 1.49 min). The published values have source and retrieval time recorded in `target.json` (NASA Exoplanet Archive pscomppars, Lillo-Box et al. 2015, retrieved 2026-10-09T15:00:52Z).
- **Product and sectors.** SPOC 120 s PDCSAP is used, with bitmask `default` (17087). There are 10 light curves and 10 target pixel files covering sectors 40, 41, 53, 54, 55, 74, 75, 80, 81 and 82. These match the MAST record in `target.json.mast`, and each file has a hash in `data/manifest.json`. `data_quality_warnings` is empty.
- **SNR gate.** SNR 25.8 over 30 transits.
- **odd_even.** 2734 ± 211 against 3067 ± 190 ppm, 1.17 sigma, with 14 and 15 transits.
- **secondary_eclipse.** -44 ± 139 ppm over 30 windows.
- **Pixel tests (no sign of a blend at or beyond about 0.5 px).**
  - `centroid_shift`: the dimming source is offset 0.18 px from the target (0.9 sigma, chi2 25.5 for 20 dof) over all 10 sectors and 166980 cadences. Cadences dropped to match the light curves are reported per sector.
  - `aperture_depth`: depth falls in the larger aperture (ratio 0.61), as expected for a source on the target.
  - `systematics`: the SAP depth of 2618 ppm matches CROWDSAP × PDCSAP (2601 ppm), and the background rises by only 0.4 sigma in transit.
- **nearby_contamination (warning).** The one "viable" neighbor, TIC 1717172551 at 14.35" (about 0.68 px), would need a 90% eclipse. The centroid result excludes it: a source at 0.68 px would exceed the 0.5 px floor at many sigma. The warning severity is appropriate.
- **period_alias.** Adding the box improves the BIC by 264, and the period is not near a known systematic period.
- **eb_catalog.** 2,189,061 catalog rows scanned, no match within 21". The snapshot hashes match the protocol.
- **Holdout isolation.** In all three rounds, every transit-window read equals a registered window to within 5e-7 d. Every baseline read has the documented width (gap 0.25 and outer 1.5 train durations). No read falls outside the hidden-year sector spans. The 2021 and 2022 rounds were decided with windows 1.3–1.5× the train duration and about 0.85–0.9 h full width, close to the transit. The phase-0.5 control windows are by design and were reported, not judged. Only the 2024 round had windows wider than the transit (objection 3).
- **Text.** Neither `report.md` nor `export/candidate_parameters.md` calls this a discovery or a confirmation. Both state that nothing was submitted and that this is a catalogued calibration target. The scope note is present.

## Assessment: SERIOUS OBJECTIONS

The deterministic code produced `REFUTED` correctly under the locked rules, and this review does not change the verdict. But the evidence shows that the single fatal failure behind the verdict came from the 2024 round's training search choosing a different, sub-gate signal (0.518 d, SNR 7.05, ephemeris chi-square 165). Its windows covered the entire hidden year. That round neither tested nor contradicted the claimed 7.79 d signal, which the dossier shows present in 2024. These points should be tracked as a holdout design issue: no check that the train candidate passes the gate or is consistent with a transit, and a timing guard that scales with the train duration. The report should also be corrected so it no longer implies that this round was a blind test of the claimed signal.
