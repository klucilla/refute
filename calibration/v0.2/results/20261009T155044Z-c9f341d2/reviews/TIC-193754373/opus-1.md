## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` prints "OK: manifest intact". These all agree on claim SHA `c9f341d2…` and code SHA `2cd8b5c1…`: `claim.lock.json`, `integrity.json` (status PASS, no tampered components), `verdict.json.provenance` and `REPRODUCE.md`. `git diff --stat e176ac3 cc9d104` touches only `calibration/v0.2/LOCK_HISTORY.md`, `RUNS.md` and `self_claim.lock.json`, so no code changed between the lock and the run. The `claim.yaml` file hash equals `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.yaml` at cc9d104.
- **Data.** All 14 file hashes in `data/manifest.json` (7 LC and 7 TPF) match the cached FITS files. The FITS headers give TICID 193754373, RA 306.4221, Dec 45.9711 and TESSMAG 11.4923 in every sector. Sectors [41, 55, 56, 75, 76, 82, 83] match MAST availability at selection (`target.json.mast`). The product is SPOC 120-s PDCSAP with the default bitmask, and no pixel-file cadences were dropped. CROWDSAP 0.66–0.73 in the headers matches the reported median of 0.703.
- **Period.** 3.9540629 d vs published 3.9540709 d, relative error 2.0e-6 (tolerance 1e-3, alias "P"). The published values are sourced to the NASA Exoplanet Archive pscomppars / Yee et al. 2025, retrieved 2026-10-09T15:00:52Z.
- **snr / odd_even / secondary_eclipse.** SNR 163 over 46 transits. Odd and even depths are 6581 ± 74 and 6688 ± 99 ppm (0.87 sigma, 23 and 22 transits). Secondary at phase 0.5 is 23 ± 59 ppm over 45 windows. The plots (`odd_even.png`, `secondary.png`, `phase.png`) agree, and the transit shape is flat-bottomed with short ingress.
- **plausibility.** Companion radius 1.33 R_Jup, consistent with the archive `pl_rade` of 14.6 R_earth (about 1.30 R_Jup). Duration ratio 0.74.
- **systematics.** The SAP depth (4630 ppm) matches CROWDSAP × PDCSAP (4666 ppm), 0.56 sigma. The in-transit background rise is 0.2% of the depth. 1 of 46 transits is near a momentum dump.
- **period_alias.** Box ΔBIC is 19449 over 5125 in-transit cadences, and 3.954 d is not within 1% of any listed systematic period.
- **eb_catalog.** No match within 21" across 2,189,061 scanned rows, with snapshot hashes recorded.
- **Blind holdout.** Every hidden-year access in `holdout_rounds[*].access_log` is a registered transit or control window or its baseline band. `HiddenYear` raises on any other access. Transit windows are 3.73–3.99 h wide: not wider than the transit (found 3.78 h, published 4.27 h). `max_timing_sigma_days` is ≤ 0.0027 d (≤ 4 min), far below one duration. All 3 rounds pass: hidden SNR 28.4, 63.1 and 97.5, while the phase-0.5 control gives SNR 0.7, 0.9 and -0.5. Of the predicted windows, 7/7, 13/14 and 25/26 contain data.
- **Text.** The report and the export sheet both state that the result is not a confirmed discovery and was not submitted. The export notes "already catalogued; it is not a new candidate". The scope limits are stated, and nothing claims a discovery.

The pixel tests cannot separate the 14.7" neighbor, the centroid significance is overstated, and the text has inaccuracies, all listed above.
