## Summary
The `REFUTED` verdict for TIC 350445771 (TOI-258.01, a calibration false positive) follows from the evidence. It rests on a significant secondary eclipse at phase 0.5 (13.9 sigma, depth ratio 0.206). The blind hidden-year controls detect that eclipse again on their own, and the integrity, data and holdout checks hold. My objections are about wording and about questions the tests cannot settle; none of them would change the verdict.

## Checks with no objection

- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` printed `OK: manifest intact`. `sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256` passed for all 28 entries.
  - `claim.yaml` is byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.yaml` at the lock commit e176ac3, and `claim.lock.json` is identical to the lock file at cc9d104.
  - All six attachment SHA-256 values match both the lock and git: targets 5db2b047…, PROTOCOL f7c24655…, selection_log 9d49cc2c…, eb_catalog 4ce34194…, eb_catalog_scan 70abfa45…, retrieval bd3bc670….
  - `integrity.json` shows status PASS with the same claim hash (c9f341d2…) and code hash (2cd8b5c1…) as the lock.
  - `git diff --stat e176ac3 cc9d104` touches only `calibration/v0.2/{LOCK_HISTORY.md, RUNS.md, self_claim.lock.json}`. No code changed between lock and run.
- **Target and data.**
  - The star's RA 85.46748 / Dec −56.74054 in `data/manifest.json` matches the ExoFOP position 05:41:52.18 / −56:44:25.6 in `target.json`, and Tmag is 11.0932 in both.
  - The manifest holds 60 files: 30 light curves and 30 target pixel files. Every filename contains 350445771, they cover the same 30 sectors as the MAST check in `target.json`, and the cadences add up to 447815, the number used.
  - Stellar parameters come from TIC 20190415 (R 1.99 ± 0.10 Rsun, M 1.32 Msun) with source and retrieval date recorded. Published values come from ExoFOP, retrieved 2026-10-09T15:01:37Z.
  - The sectors missing from the 2-minute data (S14–60) agree with the ExoFOP 2-minute sector list (1–13, 61–65).
- **Secondary eclipse (decisive).** 586 ± 42 ppm at phase 0.5 over 199 windows. The dip is visible and centered in `plots/secondary.png`. The hidden-year controls reproduce it independently (2018: 671 ppm at SNR 8.5; 2019: 798 ppm at SNR 8.5). For a primary with Rp/Rs ≈ 0.053 around a 6475 K star, a 0.2 depth ratio is far beyond planetary thermal emission (Teq ≈ 1870 K). Detrending did not mask the secondary, so any bias would make it shallower, not deeper.
- **Odd/even.** 2863 ± 85 against 2821 ± 66 ppm, 0.39 sigma (`plots/odd_even.png`). Together with the unequal secondary, this rules out a 2P equal-eclipse alias.
- **Wider neighbors.** TIC 350445770 (32 arcsec, Tmag 10.69) and TIC 350445762 (46 arcsec, Tmag 10.83) are excluded as the source. `aperture_depth` gives a large-to-core depth ratio of 0.48 at −26 sigma, so the depth falls as the aperture grows, and the inferred centroid offset of 0.24 px is far from 1.5–2.2 px.
- **Other tests.**
  - `period_alias`: a box model is needed on top of a sinusoid (ΔBIC 4889), and 3.19 d is not within 1% of any listed systematic period.
  - `systematics`: the SAP depth of 2008 ppm matches the 2032 ppm expected from CROWDSAP (0.50 sigma); 4 of 191 transits fall near momentum dumps; the background shows no rise in transit.
  - `plausibility`: 1.03 RJup, or about 1.17 RJup using the published depth, and a duration ratio of 0.24.
- **eb_catalog.** TESS-EB matched by TIC ID at 3.1904288 d, a relative difference of 8.6e-5 from the found period. It does not drive the result: under `pass_criteria.flag_excluded_tests: [eb_catalog]`, `secondary_eclipse` alone still gives REFUTED, so the "flagged" status holds without catalog leakage.
- **Blind holdout.**
  - Every logged hidden-year read is a registered transit or control window or its baseline band. Windows were placed from the train-only ephemeris: in the 2018 round, for example, window[0] has `t_pred` 1326.8043 and half-width 0.0290 d, and its access is [1326.7753, 1326.8333].
  - Full window widths are 1.29–1.39 h against train box durations of 1.20–1.25 h. The extra width is only the 3·σ_t timing margin, about 3–4 min per side, and the windows are narrower than the roughly 1.83 h eclipse. No round was decided with windows wider than the transit.
  - Period-correction scans used 3 steps. All five rounds are conclusive: 2024 has 6 windows with data, above `min_predicted_transits` 2, at SNR 13.9.
- **Text.** The report and the export carry the "never a confirmed discovery" and "nothing submitted" disclaimers and the scope statement. The verdict is about the claim, and nothing describes a discovery.
