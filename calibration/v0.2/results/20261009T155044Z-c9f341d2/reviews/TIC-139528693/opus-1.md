## Summary
The dossier for TIC 139528693 (WASP-78 b, calibration planet) is intact and internally consistent. The period is recovered to 5.4e-7 relative error, every fatal test passes on sensible inputs, and the blind holdout is clean. The `WEAKENED` verdict rests only on a 4.0-sigma, 168 ppm dip at phase 0.5. The dossier's own numbers make that dip consistent with a planetary occultation, which the gauntlet cannot tell apart from an eclipsing-binary secondary. I found a few minor problems with the centroid significance, a misleading holdout metric, the export sheet and the report wording.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` gave `OK: manifest intact` (exit 0). The claim SHA-256 `c9f341d2…` agrees across `claim.lock.json`, `integrity.json` (status PASS, no tampered components), `verdict.json.provenance` and `report.md`. The code SHA-256 `2cd8b5c1…` also agrees everywhere. `git diff cc9d104 HEAD -- src` is empty, so the code I read is the code that ran.
- **Target and data.** The archive `tic_id` "TIC 139528693" (WASP-78 b, pscomppars, source and retrieval date recorded) matches. Sectors [5, 31, 32, 106, 107] and years [2018, 2020, 2026] match the selection-time MAST check in `target.json`. Each sector has an LC and a TP file with a hash, and 78164 cadences were used. The product is SPOC 120-s `pdcsap_flux` with the default bitmask. Stellar parameters come from TIC v20190415, retrieval date recorded. Median CROWDSAP is 0.98.
- **Period and SNR.** Found 2.1751812 d against published 2.17518 ± 5e-6 d (alias P). The global ephemeris (2.1751845) is within 1 sigma of the published period. The found epoch maps onto the published one at 218 cycles with a residual of about 5 min. SNR is 204.6, with 52 transits with data.
- **Odd/even.** 0.15 sigma, and the plot shows matching depths.
- **Aperture depth.** The ratio is 0.95 (−2.1 sigma), so the depth does not grow with a larger aperture, as expected for an on-target source. The transit is detected at 62 sigma in both apertures.
- **Nearby contamination.** No TIC source within 21". The brightest nearby source (Tmag 14.66 at 42") would need a ~10% eclipse and would produce a centroid offset of about 2 px; the measured offset is 0.13 px.
- **Period alias, systematics, EB catalog.** `period_alias`: the BIC gain for transit-plus-sinusoid is 22754, and the period is not near a known systematic period. `systematics`: 1 of 52 transits is near a momentum dump, SAP and PDCSAP depths agree within 0.24 sigma, and the background rise is 0.05 sigma. `eb_catalog`: 2,189,061 rows scanned, no match within 21", and `eb_catalog.csv` does not list this TIC.
- **Blind holdout.** All transit-window reads in `access_log` correspond exactly to registered `windows` (my script check), and every read lies within the hidden sectors' time spans. Window widths are 0.192–0.194 d, which is 1.10–1.11 times the 4.2 h box and *narrower* than the published 4.968 h transit. No round was decided with windows wider than the transit; the holdout plots show the transit filling the window. Train-only predictions differ from the global ephemeris by only −1.5 to +2.1 min. Hidden SNRs are 62.9, 110.5 and 70.2, while the phase-0.5 controls are 2.0–2.9. The large timing chi² in two rounds (211 and 303) is absorbed because `fit_linear_ephemeris` scales the covariance by reduced chi².
- **Report text.** The report states the scope, says it is "nothing here is a confirmed discovery", and says nothing was submitted. The export sheet says "already catalogued; it is not a new candidate."
