## Summary
I could not refute the `REFUTED` verdict for TOI-653.01 (TIC 33153766, calibration role false_positive). The two fatal failures, `centroid_shift` and `aperture_depth`, are each strongly supported. Converted to sky coordinates, the per-sector centroid offsets point the same way in all four sectors and on two different cameras. My objections are minor and concern a test that passed on uninformative input (`eb_catalog`), holdout mechanics and report wording. None of them affects the verdict.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity:** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` gives "OK: manifest intact". HEAD fe2718c has no `src`, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock` changes since cc9d104. `integrity.json`: status PASS, locked and current claim SHA `c9f341d2…` and code SHA `2cd8b5c1…` equal. All six attachment hashes match `claim.lock.json`. `claim.lock.json` is byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.lock.json` at cc9d104. Raw `claim.yaml` equals the file at e176ac3. All 8 FITS files in the cache hash exactly to `data/manifest.json`.
- **Data:** the TIC, TOI and Tmag (8.5234) match the ExoFOP row in `target.json`, and `fp_reason` is "TFOP FP; retired as NEB". The product is SPOC 120-s PDCSAP with default bitmask, sectors 9/35/36/89 in 2019/2021/2025, and the LCs and TPFs cover the same sectors. TIC stellar parameters (R 3.055 Rsun, M 1.08) agree with ExoFOP (3.13 ± 0.09). The WCS puts the target near the centre of each 11×11 TPF (pixel 4.2 to 4.6) and inside the SPOC aperture of 20 to 27 px. Dropped cadences are reported (711/317/1253/0).
- **centroid_shift FAIL is not a single-sector artifact:** S9 (about 5σ), S36 (about 7.5σ) and S89 (about 30σ) are each significant on their own. In sky coordinates all sectors agree on a NE source (PA 23 to 65 deg) across camera 3 (S9/35/36) and camera 2 (S89).
- **aperture_depth FAIL:** core 135 ± 34 ppm (3.9σ) against large aperture 910 ± 14 ppm, ratio 6.7, 20.8σ. Combined with the per-sector depth scatter, this points to a source outside the core.
- **odd_even** (0.78σ), **secondary_eclipse** (−1.4σ) and **period_alias** (BIC gain 3585): inputs and results are sensible. **systematics:** SAP depth 527 ppm against 517 ppm expected from CROWDSAP 0.857; background and momentum dumps are clean. **plausibility:** 0.74 R_Jup follows from the diluted depth.
- **Holdout reads:** no transit-window read falls outside a registered window. Window full widths are 1.08 to 1.39 transit durations (D/2 + 3σ_t, as the code specifies), and every round needs `min_snr` 5 at SNR 18 to 37.
- **Text:** no discovery language. The report carries the scope and "never confirmed" disclaimers, and the verdict is stated as being about the claim.

## Assessment: OBJECTIONS
All objections are minor and none challenges the `REFUTED` verdict. Items 1 to 4 are worth tracking as gauntlet or report improvements.
