## Summary
The dossier is intact, and every pass/fail threshold was applied as locked. However, the only fatal failure, the 2019 holdout round, comes from a timing artifact and not from a missing signal. The 2024-only training ephemeris has a period 13.4 sigma away from both the all-data and the published period. Its claimed uncertainty is too small because three transit times leave one degree of freedom, so the errors cannot be inflated. As a result every predicted 2019 window sits about 1.38 d (about 3.4 window half-widths) away from where the transits fall, and they never contained a transit.

## Assessment: SERIOUS OBJECTIONS
The REFUTED verdict rests entirely on one holdout round. In that round the train-only ephemeris is 13 sigma off, its uncertainty could not be inflated with dof = 1, and the predicted windows missed the transits by about 1.38 d. Under a realistic timing uncertainty the round would have been INCONCLUSIVE. Meanwhile each year alone detects the signal at SNR about 21, and the combined period matches the published value.
