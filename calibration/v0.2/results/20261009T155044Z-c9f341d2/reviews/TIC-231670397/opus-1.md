## Summary
The dossier for TIC 231670397 (WASP-73 b, calibration role: planet) is intact and internally consistent. The `SURVIVED` verdict follows from the recorded inputs and thresholds, and I found nothing that makes a test pass for the wrong reason. My objections are about how one test result is worded and about limits of what the tests can rule out; none of them would change a test status.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` returns "OK: manifest intact". `integrity.json`: status PASS, locked claim/code SHA-256 equal current (c9f341d2…, 2cd8b5c1…), no tampered components. `claim.lock.json` is byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.lock.json` at cc9d104 (sha256 98f37ef0…), and `claim.yaml` is identical to the repo copy. All 6 attachment SHA-256s match the lock. `git diff e176ac3 cc9d104` touches only RUNS.md and the lock file, so the code is unchanged.
- **Timing order.** Selection: archive 15:00:52Z, MAST 15:03:48Z. Lock 15:29:55Z. Data and star retrieved 15:38:38Z, run 15:50:44Z.
- **Data hashes.** All 16 files (8 LC + 8 TPF) in `data/manifest.json` match the SHA-256 of the cached FITS files in `.cache/lightkurve/mastDownload/TESS`.
- **Target, product and sectors.** TIC 231670397 = WASP-73 b in `targets.yaml` and `selection_log.md` (ACCEPTED). Product is SPOC 120 s PDCSAP with the default bitmask. Sectors are 1, 27, 67, 94, 95, 102, 103, 104 (5 years), matching the MAST query in `target.json` (8 LC, 8 TPF). Tmag 9.86 is the same in both sources. CROWDSAP is 0.99, and no TIC source lies within 21 arcsec.
- **Signal.** P = 4.0873003 d vs published 4.08722 ± 0.00022: relative error 1.97e-5, 0.37σ. Depth 3357 vs 3300 ppm; duration 5.25 vs 5.59 h; SNR 278.
- **Each test's input supports its result.** odd_even: 20/20 transits, 0.49σ. secondary: 43 windows, 3 ± 14 ppm. aperture_depth: core 3393 ± 66 vs large 3249 ± 38 ppm, no growth with aperture. period_alias: ΔBIC 45600, 4.087 d is not within 1% of the listed systematic periods. systematics: 1 of 43 transits near momentum dumps, SAP/PDCSAP 0.68σ, background −1.1σ. eb_catalog: 0 TESS-EB and 0 Gaia-DR3 rows near the target, coverage 2,189,061 rows. Every test reports non-zero coverage.
- **Blind holdout.** Every access-log entry (28/24/22/48/70 entries across the 5 rounds) maps to a registered transit window, or to a control window at phase 0.5 of the global ephemeris; none is unregistered. Window half-widths are 2.54–2.65 h, which is *narrower* than half the transit (about 2.6–2.8 h), as `holdout_2026.png` shows with ingress and egress in the excluded gap. No round was decided with windows wider than the transit. Train-only predictions sit within 0.2–1.35 min of the global ephemeris. Hidden SNR is 39–119, controls are between −0.13 and 0.89 SNR, and all 5 rounds are conclusive.
- **Text.** `report.md` and `export/candidate_parameters.md` state that nothing is a confirmed discovery and nothing was submitted. A scope disclaimer is present. Nowhere is the signal called a discovery or confirmed.
