# Independent review: TIC-197650870 (TOI-1481.01, calibration role false_positive), verdict REFUTED

## Summary
The dossier is intact (check-dossier passes and the claim, code and lock hashes agree), and the REFUTED label for the signal that was analyzed is supported by the period_alias test. However, that signal is not TOI-1481.01's catalogued 2.185 d signal. It is the star's roughly 2% quasi-sinusoidal variability at about 1.677 d, which the masked detrending turned into deep artificial "transits". So one of the two fatal failures (plausibility), several passes and warnings, and the whole blind holdout rest on artifacts, and the "flagged false positive" credit is earned on a different signal.

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity:** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` printed "OK: manifest intact". `integrity.json` has status PASS, locked equal to current claim SHA-256 `c9f341d2…a8` and code SHA-256 `2cd8b5c1…752`, and all 6 attachment hashes match the lock. `verdict.json` provenance matches `claim.lock.json` and REFUTED's commits (lock e176ac3, run cc9d104).
- **Target and stellar parameters:** the TIC 197650870 / TOI-1481.01 row is from ExoFOP (retrieved 2026-10-09T15:01:37Z), TFOPWG FP, TESS disposition EB. The TIC parameters used (R 4.019 ± 0.244 Rsun, M 2.06, Tmag 10.32, TIC 20190415) match the ExoFOP row.
- **Product and sectors:** SPOC 120 s PDCSAP, sectors 56, 57, 76, 77 and 83 (2022 and 2024), each with a target pixel file, and the file hashes are in `data/manifest.json`. TOI sectors 16 and 17 are FFI-only and correctly excluded by the product rule. Sector 77 is partial (11137 cadences; 1737 pixel-file cadences dropped to match the light curve, and this is reported).
- **period_alias FAIL:** sensible. It uses its own 5.03 d detrend, and the sine model beats sine plus box by ΔBIC 10.7. This agrees with `full.png` and with the TFOPWG comment. It alone justifies REFUTED for the analyzed 1.677 d signal, also without eb_catalog.
- **eb_catalog PASS:** the single Gaia DR3 EB extracted (source 2001350757319471232, P = 0.395 d) is about 116 arcsec away, well outside 21 arcsec. TESS-EB has no entry for this TIC.
- **Holdout 2024:** correctly INCONCLUSIVE, because the timing sigma of 18.76 h exceeds the 12 h duration, and its `access_log` is empty (no hidden read).
- **systematics PASS:** 76 momentum dumps; 4 of 73 events near a dump; SAP/PDCSAP difference 0.4 sigma; no background rise.
- **Text:** no discovery language. The report and export say "not a new candidate", "nothing has been submitted" and "never a confirmed discovery", and the scope paragraph is accurate.

## Assessment: SERIOUS OBJECTIONS
REFUTED is defensible for the 1.677 d variability signal through period_alias. But the dossier never analyzes TOI-1481.01's catalogued 2.185 d signal, and this target is still counted as a flagged false positive. The plausibility failure, the secondary_eclipse warning severity and the contamination screen all come from a 6% depth created by the masked detrending. The 2022 holdout round read the whole hidden year with windows wider than the transit, so it was not blind.
