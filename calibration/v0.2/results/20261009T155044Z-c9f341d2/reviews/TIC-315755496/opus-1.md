# Independent review: TIC-315755496 (TOI-2053.01, calibration role: false_positive), verdict `REFUTED`

## Summary
The dossier is intact and the deterministic rules were applied correctly. However, the only fatal failure behind `REFUTED` (holdout_by_year, 2024) is a marginal miss of the threshold (SNR 4.87 against 5.0), and the hidden-year data in that round actually show the predicted dip at the predicted depth. The tests that should catch this target's real false-positive mechanism (a blend from a star less than one pixel away, or a transit duration far too short for a 5.3 R_sun host) either pass because they cannot resolve it, or do not exist in the gauntlet.

## Checks with no objection

- **Integrity.** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` returns "OK: manifest intact". `claim.yaml` and `claim.lock.json` are byte-identical to `calibration/v0.2/self_claim.yaml` and `self_claim.lock.json` at cc9d104 (SHA-256 80a48356… and 98f37ef0…). All 6 attachment SHA-256 hashes match `claim.lock.json`. `integrity.json` reports `status: PASS`, with locked claim and code hashes equal to current. The lock has `git_dirty: false`, and `src/` shows no diff between cc9d104 and HEAD.
- **Target identity.** Manifest RA/Dec 310.33153 / 82.43527 match the ExoFOP 20:41:19.53 / +82:26:07.09, and Tmag 10.5008 matches. Recovered period 4.2779837 d against the published 4.27808 d gives a relative error of 2.25e-5 (alias P), and the epoch matches within about 3 min.
- **Data.** 7 SPOC 2-min LC files and 7 TPFs (sectors 40, 47, 52, 53, 73, 74, 78; years 2021–2024), exactly as in `targets.yaml` → `mast`. Each has a SHA-256 and a MAST URL. ExoFOP's sector 24 is a QLP/FFI detection with no 2-min product, which is consistent. CROWDSAP 0.937. There are large cadence gaps in S73/S74 (5472/6564 TPF cadences dropped), and the 2023 holdout used only 3 of 6 windows. Both are recorded honestly.
- **SNR** 24.2 ≥ 7.1 over 35 transits. **odd_even** 705 vs 712 ppm, 0.07σ (16/19 transits). **secondary_eclipse** 64 ± 59 ppm, 1.1σ, ratio 0.09.
- **period_alias.** BIC gain of 506 for the box; 4.278 d is not within 1% of 4.567 d or any other listed systematic period.
- **systematics.** 0 of 35 transits near a momentum dump; SAP depth 659 ppm vs expected 664 ppm (0.08σ); background rise is 12.5% of depth at 0.07σ.
- **eb_catalog.** 0 TESS-EB and 0 Gaia rows within 21″ (`eb_catalog_scan.json` per_target TIC-315755496: 0/0). Snapshot hashes are recorded.
- **Holdout blindness.** Every `transit-window` access in every round equals a registered window (0 unregistered reads). Control windows sit at exactly phase 0.5 of the train ephemeris. Baseline reads are the declared bands (0.25–1.5 durations outside each window). Hidden-year detrending uses hidden flux outside the windows, masked only by train-predicted windows; this is documented in `holdout.py`. `run_holdout` rejects the global candidate. No round exceeded `max_timing_sigma_durations` (the largest σ_T is 0.0101 d against a 0.092 d duration).
- **Text.** The report carries the "never a confirmed discovery / nothing submitted" disclaimer and the scope statement, describes the verdict as about a claim, and nowhere calls the signal a planet or a discovery.

## Assessment: SERIOUS OBJECTIONS

`REFUTED` follows correctly from the locked rules. But its only fatal driver is a near-threshold holdout miss whose own data show the predicted signal. The blend and stellar-density evidence that does point against the signal is either unresolvable by the fatal pixel tests or not tested at all. This review does not decide the verdict.
