# Independent review: TIC-101395259 (TOI-623.01, calibration role `false_positive`), dossier `c3-dossiers`

## Summary
The dossier is intact and its INCONCLUSIVE verdict follows from the locked aggregation rule: `period_alias` is a fatal decisive test, and its PASS was refused because the sensitivity simulation fell short (43/50 vs 45 required). The reason for the INCONCLUSIVE is on Refute's side, though. The data themselves exclude the alias alternative by a wide margin. Without that refusal the verdict would be WEAKENED, never REFUTED, because no decisive test addresses the alternative the target's own catalog record names: a stellar companion on the target (SB1).

## Checks with no objection
- **Integrity:** `uv run --frozen refute check-dossier <dossier>` → "OK: manifest intact", exit 0. `integrity.json`: status PASS; claim `48acfe24…0210` and code `15a9c256…bf8e` equal the lock (`claim.lock.json`), `verdict.json.provenance` and `REPRODUCE.md`; 7 attachments match; `git_dirty` false. Lock commit dbc67a1 is an ancestor of run commit 49d823a, and `git diff --stat` between them shows no change in `src/`, `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock` or `calibration/v0.5/claim.yaml`.
- **Target and data:** TIC 101395259 is consistent across `target.json`, `claim-targets.yaml`, `data/manifest.json` and the TESS-EB match (by TIC id). Product: SPOC 2-min PDCSAP, default bitmask. Light curves and TPFs cover sectors 8, 9, 35, 62, 89 and 99, equal to the MAST lists (`listed_sectors_after_s_max` empty). ExoFOP lists 8, 9, 35 and 62, and 89 and 99 are later. Each file has a SHA-256 and a URL. The used-cadence fractions are 0.71–0.98. Stellar parameters come from TIC v8 with a retrieval date (R = 1.489 ± 0.071, M = 1.34, Tmag 10.19), consistent with ExoFOP. Period agrees with ExoFOP to 1.2e-6 relative error, and the epoch agrees within 2 minutes.
- **snr / odd_even / duty_cycle / edge_events / single_event / event_consistency:** inputs are sensible and results follow from the metrics (15–16 events; odd 6 vs even 9; chi2 16.8/14).
- **nearby_contamination:** 35 TIC sources within 120"; none within 21". The nearest (35.5", Tmag 16.17) could give at most ~4000 ppm even if fully eclipsed, so it cannot make the signal. CROWDSAP 0.99.
- **aperture_depth:** depth ratio large/core 0.94 (-3.7 sigma). A ratio that falls with the larger aperture is consistent with a source on or near the target, and sensitivity to ratio 1.5 is 1.0.
- **systematics:** SAP and PDCSAP depths are equal (0.88 sigma), there are no transits near momentum dumps, and the background does not rise in transit.
- **eb_catalog:** the FAIL is correctly downgraded to a warning (a TESS-EB match under the target's own TIC at period 7.57462 d, same photometry). The three Gaia-DR3-EB rows in `attachments/eb_catalog.csv` are at dec ≈ -50°, so they belong to other targets.
- **Verdict aggregation:** `core/verdict.py:aggregate` places INCONCLUSIVE (rule 3) ahead of the warning-only WEAKENED (rule 4). The reason string names only `period_alias`, which is correct because the other INCONCLUSIVEs (`centroid_shift`, `holdout_by_year`) are diagnostic.
- **Blind holdout:** I checked every `access_log` entry in all 5 rounds (24/12/14/14/14 entries) by script. Each entry is centred within 1e-3 d of a predicted transit (`windows[].t_pred`) or of its ±P/2 control. No read falls outside a predicted window, its baseline band or a control window. Full transit windows are 2.26–2.43 h, narrower than the 3.0 h transit (trapezoid) and the 3.085 h published duration, so no round was decided with windows wider than the transit. Baseline exclusion half-widths (1.68–1.79 h) are beyond the 1.5 h half-duration (see `plots/holdout_2025.png`). Mask half-width = 1.0 × duration (`battery.py`, `holdout.py`). The 15 hidden windows with data add up to the 15 measured events. The holdout is diagnostic and does not affect the verdict.
- **Text:** the report states that the method is `not_validated` and that "nothing here is a confirmed discovery". It defines SURVIVED as limited, notes that a PASS does not mean an effect is absent, and labels the role as `false_positive`. It nowhere calls the signal a planet or a discovery.
