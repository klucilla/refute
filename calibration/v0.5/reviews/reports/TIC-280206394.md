## Summary
The `INCONCLUSIVE` verdict for TIC 280206394 (TOI-677 b) follows the locked aggregation rules, and the integrity, data and holdout checks hold. The verdict was set by the `sinusoid` test's period limit (P ≥ 10 d gives INCONCLUSIVE before any data are read), not by anything in the light curve, and the two warning FAILs underneath it are weak and partly contradicted by the dossier's own pixel evidence.
