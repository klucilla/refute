## Summary
The dossier for TIC 286355915 (TOI-1637.01, an eclipsing-binary false-positive control) is intact. Its `WEAKENED` verdict follows from the declared rules: there are two warning failures, `secondary_eclipse` and `eb_catalog`, and no fatal test failed. The evidence points more strongly than `WEAKENED` suggests towards a low-mass stellar companion, which the gauntlet cannot test, and one of the two warnings is guaranteed by how the target was selected.
