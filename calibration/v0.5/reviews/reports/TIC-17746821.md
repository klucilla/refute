## Summary (two sentences)
The dossier for TIC 17746821 (HAT-P-50 b, a planet control in C3) is intact, recovers the published signal at P = 3.1220093 d (relative error 5.1e-7), and its `WEAKENED` verdict follows from one warning-level test: `nearby_contamination` FAILs on TIC 17746820, a Tmag 16.11 star 7.0" away that would need a 48% eclipse. I found nothing that undermines a decisive test. I have four minor objections, about an unexplained Sector 7 anomaly, two unexplained diagnostic numbers in the report, an unused shape argument against the 7" neighbour, and a published depth left out of the report.
